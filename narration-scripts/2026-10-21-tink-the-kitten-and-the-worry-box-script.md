# Read-aloud script — "Tink the Kitten and the Worry Box"

Tone: warm, soft, bedtime-slow. Slightly hushed, like telling a secret.
Pace: unhurried; pause a beat between paragraphs. Let the worry-heavy lines feel heavy, and the floating-bubble lines feel light.

**Opening** — cozy and welcoming: "In a little blue house at the edge of a garden…"
**The worries gather** — a touch serious but gentle, never scary: each worry small, "like a pebble."
**"That box is getting so heavy"** — whispered, sleepy.
**Grandmother arrives** — warm and bright, voice lifts a little: kind, crinkly eyes.
**"Would you like to show me one?"** — tender, inviting.
**Worries floating away** — light, almost smiling; let "like a soap bubble" sparkle.
**The feather** — softest voice of all, nearly a whisper.
**Closing** — "Sleep now, little Tink." very gentle, then a pause: "The end. 🌙"
