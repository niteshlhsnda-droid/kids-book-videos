# Narration Script — Hoofy the Camel and the Helpful Hump

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime storyteller. Gentle smile in the voice throughout; never scary.

- **Opening** ("Long ago, at the edge of a wide golden desert..."): dreamy, soft. Pause after "cool, clear pond."
- **The friends at work**: bright and cheerful, a touch of rhythm to the list — grain, water, plow.
- **"And right beside them lived a camel named Hoofy"**: slow down, a little playful tease in "But Hoofy never helped at all."
- **Hoofy's three "Humph"s**: each one lazier and grumpier than the last. Pause half a beat after each "Humph" so it lands.
- **"All day long, Hoofy did nothing..."**: drowsy, drifting.
- **The tired friends**: tender, a little sad — the listener should feel their tired paws.
- **The Spirit of the Desert arrives**: hush the voice; calm, moonlit, magical. "soft as a desert breeze" — barely above a whisper.
- **The hump grows**: wonder, not fright. "It did not hurt, not one bit" — reassuring.
- **Hoofy's apology**: shy, small, sincere.
- **"And from that day on..."**: warm, happy — the story's turning. Slow the last paragraph right down.
- **Final line** ("to remind us that helping others makes every journey lighter"): the softest line of all; let it fade like a yawn.
- **Moral**: say it like a gentle secret, then stop.
