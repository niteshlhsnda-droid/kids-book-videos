# Narration Script — Mole and Ratty and the River Picnic

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Mole and Ratty and the River Picnic."
- **Opening (spring-cleaning)** — soft and homely. Slow down on "till it shone." A tiny pause after "Spring cleaning can wait."
- **"Today I am going out"** — a little brighter, with happy determination.
- **Pop out of the ground** — playful and quick on "pop!" Then slow and dreamy for the meadow: "wide and bright, full of buttercups and daisies."
- **The river** — wonder rising. "Shush, shush, shush" — soft and silvery, like water. Pause before "It was the river!"
- **Ratty appears** — Ratty's voice is friendly and bright. "Hello! I am Ratty." — warm, welcoming.
- **Drifting along** — gentle and gliding. "Bright as a jewel" — let the words sparkle. Slow down on "like a flying spark."
- **Rowing** — playful. "Wobble!" and "splash!" — quick and light, not scary. Mole's "Oh no!" — small and worried.
- **Ratty's laugh** — warm and sunny, never mean. "Not a bit of it" — calm and kind. Slow down on "sharing means learning together."
- **The picnic** — happy and cosy. "Fat red strawberries" and "lemonade that fizzed in the sun" — let the words taste good.
- **Golden sunset** — slow and golden. "Ribbons of pink" — soft and dreamy.
- **"Come again tomorrow"** — Ratty's voice is warm and sure, like a promise.
- **Closing** — slowest of all. Let the river sparkle. Let the dreams drift. Long pause.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Mole." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
