# Narration Script — "Teju and the Friendly Shadow"

**Voice:** warm, gentle bedtime narrator. Slow, unhurried pace throughout — this is a story for falling asleep to. Smile in the voice at the funny moments; soften to almost a whisper by the final paragraph.

**Overall directions:** Pause briefly at each paragraph break. Linger on the playful lines ("super secret spy!") with a conspiratorial, delighted tone. The night-time scenes should feel cozy and safe, never scary. The goodnight line is spoken very softly, like a lullaby.

---

- **Opening (sunny, bright):** Warm and cheerful. "On a bright sunny morning…" — light, skipping rhythm, matching Teju running.
- **"Teju stopped. The something stopped too."** — A little mystery. Slow down slightly; whisper "It won't find me here!" as if sharing a secret.
- **"It follows me everywhere!"** — Playful alarm, not real fear. Lift on "super secret spy!" with a grin in the voice.
- **Mama's entrance:** Mama's voice is warm and calm, a gentle laugh on "What is all this running?" Her explanation — "That is your shadow, my love" — is tender and reassuring.
- **Shadow games:** Light and giggly. "Hello, shadow!" — bright and delighted.
- **Evening march:** Slow the pace a little; the long shadow like a "sleepy giant" should sound dreamy.
- **Night scene:** Softer, quieter. "Mama!" — a small worried call, not a scream. The off/on game ("Off — gone. On — back.") is curious and gentle. "But I'm scared of the dark!" — small and honest, spoken with sympathy.
- **Shadow puppets:** Cozy and magical. The rabbit hops, the bird flaps — each with a tiny lift of wonder. The silent shadow-dog gets a soft chuckle.
- **Next day's dance:** Energy returns, joyful. "He jumped, he danced" — bouncy rhythm.
- **Final paragraph:** Slow right down. Soft, sleepy, contented. A long pause before the goodnight line.
- **"Sleep now, little Teju."** — Barely above a whisper. Let it hang for a beat, then stop. Do not rush off the ending.
