# Narration Script: The Brave Little Firefly

*Companion to `stories/the-brave-little-firefly.md`. Written for a calm, warm read-aloud voice. Total runtime: about 4 minutes.*

**How to use this script:** Read the **bold narrator lines** aloud. Follow the *[bracketed cues]* for pacing, tone, and pauses. The *(parentheses)* are acting notes — don't read those.

---

*[SCENE 1 — Soft music fades in. Gentle, warm tone.]*

**In a green, green forest, where the trees tickled the clouds, lived a little firefly named Flick.**

*(Say "green, green" slowly, like a song.)*

**Flick had a tiny light on his tail. It could glow… and glow… and glow.**

*[Pause 2 seconds.]*

*[SCENE 2 — Tone turns a little worried.]*

**But Flick was afraid of the dark.**

**"Oh no, oh no," Flick whispered every evening, when the sun went to sleep. "It is too dark. Too, too dark!"**

*(Whisper this part, like a secret.)*

**So Flick hid under a big leaf… and waited for morning.**

*[SCENE 3 — A gentle tapping sound. Brighter tone.]*

**One night, his friend Mira the mouse came tapping. Tap-tap-tap.**

*(Tap your fingers lightly as you read.)*

**"Flick! Flick!" she squeaked. "I lost my way home. It is so dark, I cannot see my toes!"**

*(High, squeaky voice for Mira.)*

**Flick peeked out from under his leaf. The forest was dark… dark… dark.**

**"I am scared too," Flick said in a small, small voice.**

*[SCENE 4 — Warm, encouraging tone. This is the turning point.]*

**But then he looked at Mira's worried face. And something warm wiggled inside him.**

**"Hold my wing," Flick said. "I will try."**

**Flick took one deep breath — in… and out… — and let his little light GLOW.**

*(Take a real deep breath with the listeners here.)*

**First it glowed a little. Then a little more. Then — twinkle, twinkle! — it glowed bright and warm, like a tiny lantern.**

*(Sound delighted, like it's magic.)*

*[SCENE 5 — Happy, adventurous tone.]*

**"Oh!" said Mira. "I can see the path! I can see my toes!"**

**Step by step… glow by glow… Flick lit the way through the dark forest.**

**Past the sleepy flowers. Over the giggly stream. All the way to Mira's cozy little home.**

**"Thank you, Flick!" Mira hugged him tight. "Your light is the bravest light I know."**

*[SCENE 6 — Slow down. Soft, sleepy bedtime tone.]*

**From that night on, Flick still felt a little scared when the sun went to sleep.**

**But now he knew a secret: being brave does not mean you are not scared. Being brave means you glow anyway.**

*(Say the secret slowly — this is the moral.)*

**And every evening, when the dark came, Flick flew up, up, up — and lit the forest with his brave little light.**

**Twinkle, twinkle. Glow, glow, glow.**

*[Pause 3 seconds. Music fades out.]*

**The end. Goodnight. 🌙**

---

### Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room; a closet full of clothes works great as a free sound booth.
- Keep the microphone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Smile while you read — listeners can hear a smile.
- Leave the pauses in! Silence helps little kids imagine the pictures.
