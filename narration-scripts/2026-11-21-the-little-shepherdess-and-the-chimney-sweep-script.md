# Read-Aloud Script — The Little Shepherdess and the Chimney Sweep

Warm, slow bedtime voice throughout. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Let the wonder land; never rush the rooftop scene.

**Paragraph 1 — Opening (warm, cozy):**
Slow and soft, like settling into a chair by the fire. Emphasize "cozy," "crackled," and "kindest smile." A gentle smile in the voice.

**Paragraph 2 — Best friends (tender):**
Light and happy. A little lift on "very best of friends." Pause after "chimney tops."

**Paragraph 3 — The Mandarin speaks (low, creaky):**
Drop the voice a little for the Mandarin — slow, creaky, old. Pause before his line so children lean in. "Dark cupboard" — quiet, a little ominous, but not scary.

**Paragraph 4 — Her heart sinks (soft, sad):**
Slower. Gentle sadness on "her heart sank." Then a warm, reassuring whisper for the chimney sweep's line: "Don't be afraid."

**Paragraph 5 — Midnight (hushed, tiptoe):**
Whisper-quiet, conspiratorial. "When the clock struck twelve" — soft chime in the voice. Tiptoe rhythm on "across the rug, past the sleeping cat."

**Paragraph 6 — Up the chimney (building wonder):**
Climbing rhythm, a little faster, then a big pause before "— oh! — there was the sky." Let the sky arrive like a surprise. Wonder and awe on "washed in moonlight."

**Paragraph 7 — The wide world (bright, joyful):**
Pointing, excited — the sweep showing off his town. A delighted little laugh on "her laugh sounded like a tiny bell."

**Paragraph 8 — The cold wind (cooling, tender):**
Voice cools and slows on "cold wind blew." Soft shiver on "shivered." The shepherdess's whisper is small and homesick. The sweep's line — "Then home is where we belong" — is warm, steady, and sure.

**Paragraph 9 — Climbing home (relieved, golden):**
Gentle descent, then warmth rising like morning light. The Mandarin's line is slow, kind, and grandfatherly: "Very well. Stay together, little friends."

**Paragraph 10 — Home again (content, sleepy):**
Slow, drowsy, content. Let the last line settle like a blanket.

**Goodnight line:**
Very soft, almost a lullaby. A long pause before it. "Sleep now, little shepherdess, safe beside your friend."
