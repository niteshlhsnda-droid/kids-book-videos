# Narration script — Pippa the Pangolin and the Lantern Walk

Voice: warm bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Slow, gentle pace. Soft smile in the voice throughout.

## Read-aloud directions

- **Opening (sal forest, river)**: soft, dreamy. Pause after "lived a little pangolin named Pippa." Let the name land.
- **Curling into a ball**: hushed, protective. Slow down on "tucking her nose under her tail."
- **Lantern Walk announcement**: a small lift of wonder on "Once a year… the firefly glade." Then a wistful drop: "that she could go too."
- **Chirpy the cricket**: bright but tiny voice feel — quicker, cheerful. Slow and tender on his key line: "Then let them look… Your scales are the prettiest thing in the forest."
- **Mama's walk**: humming warmth; soften on "glowed like copper pennies."
- **The bridge / freezing**: tension — slightly faster, then a full stop at "Pippa froze." Drop to a whisper for "She curled into a ball beneath the bridge and would not come out."
- **Glow the glow-worm**: smallest, kindest voice. Gentle pause before "Would you like me to sit with you?"
- **Uncurling**: "Slowly — very slowly — Pippa uncurled." — deliberate, each word its own breath.
- **The glade / fireflies**: wonder, crescendo on "thousands of them, like a second sky come down to the grass." Joyful lift on "Hold up your lanterns!"
- **"She's beautiful."**: whispered with delight, like the mice.
- **Warm honey**: melt on "It felt like warm honey."
- **Resolution**: deep calm, slowing. "She had been seen — and the forest was kind." — let it breathe.
- **Goodnight**: the softest line of all. "Sleep now, little Pippa." — barely above a whisper, then a long pause.
- **The end.**: gentle final, smile audible.
