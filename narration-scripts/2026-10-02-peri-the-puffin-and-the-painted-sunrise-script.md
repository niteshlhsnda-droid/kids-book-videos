# Narration script — "Peri the Puffin and the Painted Sunrise"

Voice: warm Meta AI narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle bedtime pace, unhurried.

## Tone map (paragraph by paragraph)

1. **Opening / burrow** — cozy, snuggly. Slow, warm. Linger on "cozy burrow" and "sparkling far below".
2. **Peri's look** — light, fond. A little smile in the voice on "round orange beak".
3. **Sunset ritual** — dreamy and slow. Mother Puffin's line: soft, wise, maternal.
4. **The worry** — shift slightly darker, quieter. "Cold little stone" — let it land, brief pause after. The two questions: hushed, genuinely worried.
5. **Sneaking out** — hushed, tiptoeing rhythm. Quicken very slightly on "waddled down the winding cliff path".
6. **Calling the sun** — a small, brave, pleading call. Project a little, then fall quiet.
7. **Old crab** — creaky, kindly old voice. Slow and certain. "Ten thousand mornings" — weighty.
8. **Peri insists** — small but determined. Childlike stubbornness, not defiance.
9. **Keeping watch** — heavy-lidded, drowsy delivery. "I am watching" — fading, barely a whisper.
10. **Sammy the seal** — smooth, calm, low. A lullaby in speech. Pause before and after "It needs your dreaming."
11. **Sunrise** — build wonder gradually: pink (soft) → gold (brighter) → the full bloom (full, glowing warmth). "Painted just for her" — tender.
12. **Peri gasps** — breath of wonder. Bright, relieved, joyful.
13. **Mother's wing** — the warmest moment. Mother Puffin: gentle, certain. Let "Morning knows the way" ring, then a beat of silence.
14. **Heading home** — slowing down, sleepier with each sentence. "Closed her bright curious eyes" — almost a whisper.
15. **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Peri." — the softest line in the whole story. Long pause after.
16. **The end** — "The end." — barely there, then silence.

## Pauses
- One beat after each paragraph; two beats after the worry questions, after "I am watching," and after Mother's "Morning knows the way."
- Never rush the sunrise paragraph — it is the emotional peak.

## Emphasis (never shouted)
- "cold little stone", "ten thousand mornings", "I am watching", "It needs your dreaming", "painted just for her", "Morning knows the way", "warm and light as a feather", "Sleep now, little Peri."
