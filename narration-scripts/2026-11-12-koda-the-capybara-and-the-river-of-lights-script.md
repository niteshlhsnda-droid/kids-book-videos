# Narration Script — Koda the Capybara and the River of Lights

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Koda the Capybara and the River of Lights."
- **Opening (river, reeds, fireflies)** — soft and dreamy. Slow down on "blinking like stars above the water."
- **Counting the lights** — playful and quiet. Count "One, two, three" slowly, like counting fireflies for real. A tiny pause after "on and off."
- **Festival of Lanterns** — a little brighter, with wonder. "Ribbon of stars" — let the words glow.
- **Bea appears** — soften. "Peep. Peep." — small, shivery, high and quiet, like a tiny duckling. Pause before "It was Bea."
- **Bea is lost** — Bea's voice wobbles, small and worried. Koda's reply is calm and warm, like a hug. Slow down on "The river was dark."
- **"Do not worry"** — Koda's calmest, warmest voice. "Its little candle glowed gold" — warm and golden.
- **The gust of wind** — a quick, soft "whoosh!" — not scary, just a surprise. Then quiet again. Bea's "Now we are lost" — small and sad.
- **Koda remembers** — slow, thoughtful. "Hold on tight" — a gentle whisper.
- **The moonflowers** — wonder rising. "Flicker, glow!" — bright and delighted, then settle back to calm.
- **Mama found** — joyful but soft. "Mama! Mama!" — happy little peeps. "The whole river glowed gold" — warm and full.
- **Closing** — slowest of all. Let the river hum. Let the lanterns drift. Long pause.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Koda." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
