# Script — Mabel the Mole and the Moon Garden

**Tone:** Warm, gentle, storytelling voice — like a grandparent by the fire. Slow, calm pacing throughout; lower volume and slower pace for the goodnight line.

**Overall pace:** Unrushed. Let the pictures breathe; pause briefly at each beat.

---

*Soft, welcoming* — Mabel the Mole and the Moon Garden.

*Gentle, cozy* — Under the roots of a big old oak tree, in a tunnel as round as a teacup, lived a small mole named Mabel.

*Quiet, content* — Mabel loved her quiet burrow. She kept her tunnels neat and her favourite smooth pebbles lined up in a row. At night she sometimes peeped out at the garden above, then hurried back down again.

*Playful, a little patter of rain* — One rainy night, a tiny seed came tumbling down through the rain, plop, right into Mabel's tunnel. It was small and brown and very lost.

*Soft, curious whisper* — Mabel picked it up with both paws. "Where did you come from, little one?" she whispered. The seed said nothing at all. It only lay still in her paw, waiting.

*Tender, careful* — Mabel tucked the seed into a soft leaf until the rain stopped. Then she carried it up, up, up, dug a small hole in a sheltered corner beside the oak tree, laid the seed gently inside, and patted the earth over it. "There," she said. "This can be your home."

*Hopeful, steady* — Every night Mabel came to visit. She brought water in a leaf cup and cleared away fallen twigs. And one evening she saw it: a tiny green sprout, no taller than her nose, pushing up through the soil.

*Delighted, gentle* — Mabel built a low wall of smooth pebbles all around the sprout, to keep it safe. "Grow slowly," she whispered. "There is no hurry at all."

*Darker, worried — stormy* — But one night a wild wind bent the little sprout sideways, and rain splashed down until it drooped low. Mabel held a big leaf over it like an umbrella until the storm passed, but her arms were tired, and her heart felt heavy.

*Brightening, footsteps quickening* — Just then, small feet came pattering through the wet grass. Wren flew down with a soft feather. Tansy carried a cool drink of water. Sable the snail, slow and steady, brought a fresh green leaf to lay over the sprout like a blanket.

*Warm, open-hearted* — "May we help?" asked Wren. "There is room for all of us here," said Mabel, and she moved her pebbles a little wider, so everyone had a place.

*Peaceful, rhythmic* — Night after night, the four friends tended the plant together. Wren sang soft songs. Tansy kept the soil damp. Sable polished the pebbles. And the sprout grew taller and stronger every night.

*Wonder, hushed awe* — Then, on the night of the full moon, the plant opened a great white flower, round as the moon itself, glowing softly in the dark. "It is a moonflower," whispered Wren. "And it grew because you gave it a home."

*Warm, full-hearted* — The friends sat together under the moonflower, sharing the sweet night air. The garden that had once felt big and lonely now felt full and warm.

*Slowest, softest, like a lullaby* — Sleep now, little Mabel, keeper of the moon garden.

*Gentle close* — The end.
