# Narration Script — Kubo the Koala and the Sleepy Tree

Read slowly and warmly, like a bedtime story. Gentle pace throughout; pause briefly at each paragraph break. Let the sleepy, tender moments land — never rush.

## Title
"Kubo the Koala and the Sleepy Tree." — warm, inviting, a soft smile in the voice.

## Opening (the gum tree at sunset)
Calm, dreamy tone. Paint the picture slowly: the tall tree, the soft meadow, the little koala.
- "In a tall gum tree at the edge of a soft green meadow…" — slow, gentle.
- "a little koala joey named Kubo" — warm and fond.

## Kubo's description
Cozy and affectionate, like describing someone you love.
- "the roundest ears, the softest grey fur" — soft emphasis on each.

## Friends arrive
Light, happy energy. Each friend gets a distinct, playful voice.
- "Hide-and-seek!" — Kiki, bright and excited.
- "Moon-spot hide-and-seek!" — even brighter.

## Kubo's yawn
Slow down. Let the tiredness come through.
- "a great, wide yawn" — stretch the words slightly, then pause.
- "His eyelids felt heavy, like warm stones." — heavy, drowsy delivery.

## Willa's kindness
Soft and caring — Willa is gentle.
- "Just rest your eyes for one little breath." — tender, quiet.

## Kiki's laugh startles Kubo
Kiki's laugh: big and hearty, then immediately soften.
- "Ha-ha-ha-ha!" — joyful, then a beat of silence.
- "I wanted to play" — Kubo, small and wobbly, a little sad. Do not overdo it.

## Bindi's gentle plan
Warm and reassuring.
- "Let's play the quiet game." — Bindi, kind and thoughtful.
- "Rest your sleepy bones, Kubo. We'll be right here." — Willa, like a lullaby.

## Kubo drifts off
The softest part of the story. Barely above a whisper.
- "It sounded like a lullaby made just for him." — slow, fading.

## Waking under the stars
Warm and content, waking up loved.
- "twinkling like spilled sugar" — wonder, soft smile.
- "Nobody had left. Nobody was cross." — gentle reassurance, steady.

## Goodnight
- "Sleep now, little Kubo." — the softest line of all. Pause after.
- "The end." — whispered, then let the silence sit.
