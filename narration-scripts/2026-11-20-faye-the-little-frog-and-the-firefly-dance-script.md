# Narration Script — Faye the Little Frog and the Firefly Dance

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Faye the Little Frog and the Firefly Dance."
- **Opening (the pond)** — soft and dreamy. "The water wore the moon like a silver button" — slow, like a lullaby beginning.
- **The firefly dance** — gentle wonder. "Spelling golden shapes in the dark" — hushed, as if watching something magical.
- **Faye's wish** — small and wistful. "Oh, how I wish I could dance with them" — a quiet little sigh of longing.
- **The first splash** — playful, not scary. "One, two — but on the third hop her foot slipped, and splash!" — light and quick, then a soft splash. "Made the little minnows giggle" — warm and funny.
- **"Again"** — quiet determination. "Again" — very soft, very brave. The dragonfly's advice — kind and gentle, like a teacher. "Land like a falling leaf" — slow and soft.
- **Halfway across** — bright and proud. "And there she was, halfway across the pond!" — a little lift of delight. Then tired — slow down. "So she sat down to rest a little while" — sleepy and calm.
- **The old turtle** — slow, warm, and wise. "Even the river stops to rest in the quiet pools" — the slowest line in the story, like a deep breath.
- **The last hop** — building excitement. "And hopped — hop! — and landed, soft as a falling leaf" — a quick hop, then a soft landing. "Faye! You came!" — delighted, twinkling.
- **The dance** — joyful and glowing. "She did not dance the highest, and she did not dance the fastest — but she danced" — warm and proud, like a hug in words.
- **Goodnight with the firefly** — the sleepiest part. "Warm as a night-light" — barely above a whisper. "Same time tomorrow?" — soft and hopeful.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Faye." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
