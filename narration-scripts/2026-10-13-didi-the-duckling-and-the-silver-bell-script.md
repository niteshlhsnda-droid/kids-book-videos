# Read-aloud script: Didi the Duckling and the Silver Bell

**Voice:** warm, unhurried bedtime narrator. Slow, gentle pace throughout; never rush.

**Opening** ("The sun had dipped low…"): soft and dreamy, like a lullaby. Pause after "warm honey."

**Discovery** ("Didi was the smallest duckling…"): brighten slightly with curiosity on "bumped something smooth and round." Pause before "and came up with a small silver bell."

**The secret** ("She slipped it under her wing…"): quieter, a little conspiratorial — the listener is in on the secret. "Tink" — tiny, flat, slightly disappointed.

**Morning polish** ("In the morning she polished…"): a touch of hope on "until it gleamed," then let the hope fall on "Still dull." Mama's line is gentle, never scolding.

**Mama's question** ("Where did you find it, little one?"): warm and kind. Pause after Didi's mumble — let the silence carry the weight.

**The sad bell** ("All day Didi carried the bell…"): slow down; each "Tink. Tink." a little quieter and sadder.

**Fog** ("Then the evening fog came rolling…"): lower the voice, add a hint of mystery. Old Heron's "Oh dear, oh dear" is worried but not frightening — a gentle grandfatherly wobble.

**The return** ("Her wings felt heavy as stones…"): Didi's line is small and brave — she's nervous but honest. Pause before Old Heron's reply. His thanks is warm and kind.

**The ringing** ("Ting-a-ling! Ting-a-ling!"): let the joy bloom — brighter, musical, like real bells. Smile while reading it.

**Goodnight** ("That night, Didi snuggled…"): fade to a whisper. "Sleep now, little Didi." — the softest line in the whole story. Then a long pause, and "The end." like a kiss on the forehead.
