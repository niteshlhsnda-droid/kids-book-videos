# Script — Prince Milo and the Little White Cat

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator. Read softly and calmly throughout, like a parent telling a story at lights-out.

**Pacing:** about 120–130 words per minute. Pause 2 seconds between paragraphs; 1 second between sentences. Never rush the ending.

## Tone cues

- "Once upon a time" — slow, dreamy, hushed, like opening a picture book.
- King / proud older brothers — a little pompous but gentle; never harsh. Milo's lines — soft and sincere.
- Snowdrop — high, tinkly, tiny, like a silver bell. Smile as you read her.
- "tiny acorn" ... "hazelnut ... cherry stone ... tiny seed" — build delight with each reveal; each smaller than the last, whispering with wonder.
- "the king clapped his hands with joy" — warm, celebratory, then soften again immediately.
- Final transformation — hushed, magical, sparkling.
- Moral — gentle and clear, as if speaking to one child.
- "Sleep now, little listener." — a whispered sigh, the last words before sleep.

## Emphasis

- Stress Milo's kindness every time it appears: "thank you", "shared his bread", "thanked everyone here, even me."
- Slow down on the nesting reveal (acorn → hazelnut → cherry stone → seed → puppy) — this is the wonder beat of the story.

## Pauses

- After "it is far too wet to travel tonight" — cozy pause, like tucking in a blanket.
- After "no bigger than a thimble" — hold 2 full seconds before the king's reaction; let the wonder land.
- Before "Sleep now, little listener." — a long, soft breath out.
