# Narration Script — The Emperor's New Clothes

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "The Emperor's New Clothes."
- **Opening (Emperor Felix)** — soft and dreamy. Slow down on "curled like a silver ribbon." A tiny pause after "What shall I wear today?"
- **The travellers arrive** — bright and playful. The weavers' voices are smooth and smiling. "Only wise and clever people can see it" — said with a twinkle, a little mysterious.
- **The empty looms** — a gentle, busy rhythm. "Clicked and clacked and hummed a little tune" — light and musical. "The looms stayed quite empty" — said simply, letting the silliness land.
- **Marta looks** — kind and worried. "Oh dear" — small and nervous. "I cannot see a single thread" — a whisper of worry. "How beautiful!" — bright and brave, a little too quick.
- **The emperor looks** — slower, heavier. "His cheeks turned pink" — a little embarrassed. "Very fine. Very fine indeed" — trying hard to sound sure.
- **Parade day dressing** — hushed and silly. "Which was no suit at all" — a quiet, knowing smile in the voice. "Trying to look pleased" — gentle and funny.
- **The parade** — grand and cheerful. Trumpets and banners — a little lift in the voice. The crowd's cries are bright and hearty. "Nobody wanted to be the one who could not see it" — soft, understanding.
- **Ivy speaks** — small, clear, and honest. Ivy's whisper is tiny and brave. "The emperor isn't wearing any clothes at all!" — plain and true, like a child stating a fact. The father's gasp — quick and surprised. The giggle — light as a bubble.
- **The laughter spreads** — warm and golden. "Like warm honey" — slow and sweet. "Like sunshine after rain" — glowing. The emperor's big laugh — the biggest, warmest laugh of the whole story. "Ivy is right!" — joyful and free.
- **The closing reflection** — slow and sleepy again. "How silly and wonderful it all was" — a warm smile. "The one with frayed sleeves that felt like a hug" — soft as a blanket. "Saying what is true can make a whole town laugh together" — quiet and true, let it sit.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Felix." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
