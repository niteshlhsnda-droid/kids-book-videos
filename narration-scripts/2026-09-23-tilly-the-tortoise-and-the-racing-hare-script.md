# Read-aloud script — "Tilly the Tortoise and the Racing Hare"

Read slowly and softly, like a bedtime whisper-story. Let the room go quiet between sentences.

**Pace:** Gentle and unhurried throughout. Pause ~1 second at every paragraph break.

**Tone:** Warm and cozy. Smile through the funny bits; soften your voice for the evening ending.

**Emphasis cues:**
- Onomatopoeia — *plip… plop… plip… plop…* — say in a tiny, pattering rhythm, each syllable a slow little step. Do NOT rush it.
- *Zoom! Zip! Zap! Zoom!* — say with a giggly burst of energy, then settle back to calm.
- Hare's laughing lines ("A race? With you? Ha!") — cheerful and silly, like a playful big brother.
- Tilly's lines ("I may be slow, but I keep on going.") — steady and kind, never boastful.
- The race scene — build a little excitement at "GO!" then slow right back down for Tilly's walk.
- *Zzzzz…* — a long, soft sleepy buzz; let it trail off.
- Final lines (the sunset under the oak tree) — your softest voice, almost a whisper. Let the moral land gently: "Slow and steady, step by step, finishes the race."

**Pauses:** A breath after "And at last — step — Tilly's little foot touched its roots." A longer pause before the final paragraph, so the ending feels like tucking in.
