# Script — Runa the Red Panda and the Lantern Promise

**Tone:** Warm, gentle, storytelling voice — like a grandparent by the fire. Slow, calm pacing throughout; lower volume and slower pace for the goodnight line.

**Overall pace:** Unrushed. Let the pictures breathe; pause briefly at each beat. Wind, rain, and splash sounds are playful but soft — never startling.

---

*Soft, welcoming* — Runa the Red Panda and the Lantern Promise.

*Gentle, cozy* — In a green bamboo forest, where the leaves whispered in the evening breeze, there lived a little red panda named Runa.

*Warm, fond* — Runa had soft rust-red fur, a bushy striped tail, and round ears that twitched. She lived in a cozy hollow tree above a silver pond.

*Warm, golden* — Every evening, Runa lit the big lantern on the hill above the pond. Its warm golden light helped all the forest animals find their way home in the dark.

*Friendly, earnest* — One evening, her friend Tobo the tiny toad hopped up the hill. "Runa," he said, "tonight I must cross the pond to sing my little sister to sleep. Will you keep the lantern burning until I come back?"

*Steady, sincere* — Runa nodded and held out her paw. "I promise," she said. "The lantern will burn until you are home again."

*Light, splashy* — Tobo hopped away, splash-splash, across the pond, and Runa sat beside the lantern.

*Shifting, breezy, then pattering* — But then the wind began to blow. The bamboo swayed, dark clouds rolled over the moon, and pitter-pat, pitter-pat, the rain began to fall.

*Sudden whoosh, then hushed* — Whoosh! A gust of wind blew the lantern out. The hill went dark.

*Worried, quiet* — Runa's heart thumped. "Oh no," she whispered. "Tobo is out there, and my promise is in the dark."

*Trying, fizzling, a little frustrated* — She tried again. Strike, fizzle. Strike, fizzle. The matches were wet, and the wind kept blowing.

*Tempted, then resolving* — Runa wanted to run inside and hide from the rain. But she thought of Tobo, hopping alone in the dark, looking for her light. She took a deep breath.

*Quick, purposeful* — She remembered the dry hollow log at the bottom of the hill, where she kept an old ember box. She scampered down and carried it back up under a roof of big leaves.

*Soft, careful, hopeful* — In the shelter of the log, she cupped her paws around the ember and blew softly until a tiny flame danced up. She lit a dry candle and lifted it to the lantern.

*Bright, triumphant, warm* — Glow, glow, glow! The lantern burst back to life, and its golden light spilled over the hill, over the pond, all the way to the far shore.

*Joyful, relieved* — On the far shore, Tobo looked up and saw the light. "Runa kept her promise!" he cried, and hopped home, splash-splash, along the golden path.

*Warm, grateful* — When Tobo reached the hill, wet and tired but smiling, he said, "Thank you, Runa. Your light showed me the way."

*Cozy, winding down* — Runa wrapped him in a soft dry blanket, and they sat together under the lantern as the rain grew quiet.

*Soft, starry* — The clouds drifted away, and the stars came out, one by one.

*Sleepy, tender* — Runa yawned a big red-panda yawn. Tobo curled up beside her, warm and safe.

*Slowest, softest, like a lullaby* — Sleep now, little Runa, keeper of the lantern light.

*Gentle close* — The end.
