# Narration Script — "Mira and the Starlit Coins"

Read-aloud directions for the warm Meta AI voice (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Overall tone: soft, gentle bedtime whisper-warmth, unhurried pace. Short pauses at paragraph breaks (…), slightly longer at beat changes.

**Opening (beat 1–2):** Cozy and soft, like tucking someone in. Smile in your voice. — "In a small cottage at the edge of a great forest lived a little girl named Mira…"

**Beat 3 (old man):** Warm and tender. Slow down on "pressed it into his trembling hands." Let the old man's smile land.

**Beat 4 (little boy):** Playful and sweet. A tiny lilt of delight on "He giggled and hugged her knees…" Pause after "songbird's."

**Beat 5 (small girl):** Gentle care. Soft, careful rhythm on "buttoning each button with gentle fingers." Let "her tears turned into laughter" bloom warmly.

**Beat 6 (skirt, night air):** A touch of hushed wonder, slightly slower. Quietly marvel at her generosity — don't explain it, just feel it. Hum the lullaby line softly.

**Beat 7 (tiniest child):** The softest moment of the story. Barely above a whisper: "She did not hesitate…" Long pause after "warm at last."

**Beat 8 (stars fall):** Build wonder gradually. Voice brightens with each sentence. "One star twinkled, wobbled, and fell —" (pause, delight) "and as it touched the ground, it turned into a small golden coin!" Sparkle here.

**Beat 9 (coin rain):** Joyful, lilting. "She spread her mother's shawl wide and caught as many as she could, laughing in wonder."

**Beat 10 (resolution + goodnight):** Settle back down, slow and drowsy. "She shared them with everyone who needed anything, just as she always had." Then the goodnight line, extra soft: "Sleep now, little Mira." Pause. "The end. 🌙"

Emphasis words (lift lightly, never shout): trembling, giggled, gently, hesitate, wonderful, gleaming, shared, Sleep.
