# Script — "Pooh Bear and the Very Tight Place" (2026-10-12)

## Read-aloud directions

**Overall tone:** warm, drowsy-soft bedtime voice; slow and unhurried throughout.
Save the gentlest, sleepiest delivery for the last three paragraphs.

**Pacing:** natural pauses at paragraph breaks (count to two silently). Slightly
slower than conversational — this is a lullaby story.

**Characters:**
- **Narrator:** warm and soft, your calmest reading voice.
- **Pooh:** a little low and dreamy, unhurried — like he's thinking aloud.
- **Rabbit:** bright and quick, warm with laughter, not shrill.
- **Piglet:** small and squeaky — raise the pitch a touch on "A week!".
- **Christopher Robin:** kind and steady, the voice of reason.
- **Eeyore:** very slow and flat, gentle — never sad, just Eeyore.

**Key moments:**
- "One soft morning…" — breathe the opening; let the listener settle in.
- The honey-eating line ("then another… then a bigger one…") — relish the
  rhythm, a tiny pause at each "…" like spoonfuls disappearing.
- "oh dear. His tummy would not fit." — drop the voice, comedic hush, let kids
  giggle.
- "He wiggled. He waggled." — bouncy, playful staccato.
- "POP!" — a bright little burst, then melt back to soft.
- "Sleep now, Pooh Bear." — the softest line of the whole story. Whisper-warm,
  then "The end." barely above a breath.
