# Narration Script — Gerda and the Snow Queen

Read slowly and warmly, like a bedtime story. Soft, gentle voice throughout. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Let the winter scenes feel sparkling and calm, never frightening; let Gerda's journey feel brave and hopeful.

## Directions

**Paragraph 1 — The little town and the rose garden**
Warm, wonder-filled tone. Linger on "red rooftops" and "a garden full of roses". A gentle, picture-painting pace.

**Paragraph 2 — Summer play**
Light and happy. "Best friends forever" — say it like a promise between two children. Slow down on "pinky-promised beneath the biggest rosebush."

**Paragraph 3 — Winter arrives, the Snow Queen appears**
A touch of sparkle and mystery. "Shimmered like fresh snow" — soft and glittery. The Snow Queen is beautiful and lonely, never scary.

**Paragraph 4 — The invitation**
Dreamy, musical. The Snow Queen's line — "Come and see my palace of ice" — in a voice like wind chimes. Pause on "so quiet there, all alone" — a little sad.

**Paragraph 5 — Kai flies away**
Gentle motion, like a sleigh gliding. "Far to the north" — let it drift away softly.

**Paragraph 6 — Kai forgets**
Quiet and sad, but not frightening. Slow down on "He forgot the roses. He even forgot his best friend." Let the loss land softly.

**Paragraph 7 — Gerda sets out**
Brave and determined. "I will find Kai" — firm and warm. "Best friends do not give up" — the heart of the story, said with quiet strength.

**Paragraph 8 — The old woman's house**
Cozy and warm. "Warm soup and a soft bed" — like a hug. The old woman's advice — "Follow the river" — wise and calm.

**Paragraph 9 — The princess and the golden sleigh**
Bright and hopeful. "Warm boots, a golden sleigh, and a black raven" — a little adventure feeling, still gentle.

**Paragraph 10 — The palace of ice**
Hushed wonder. "Shining blue in the moonlight" — slow and sparkling.

**Paragraph 11 — Kai alone in the cold hall**
The quietest, coldest moment. "His hands cold and his smile gone away" — soft and tender, never frightening.

**Paragraph 12 — Gerda arrives**
Warmth rushes back in. "I'm here, Kai" — whispered, full of love. "I found you" — relief and joy.

**Paragraph 13 — The cold melts away**
Tender and magical. Pause after "the cold melted away." Let "remembered the roses, the summer sun, and his best friend" bloom, one memory at a time.

**Paragraph 14 — Best friends again**
Joyful but soft. "Kai!" — a happy little gasp, then a warm hug in the voice.

**Paragraph 15 — The Snow Queen smiles**
Gentle and wistful. The Snow Queen is not punished — she simply smiles, because love warms even an ice palace.

**Paragraph 16 — Home again**
Warm and sleepy. "Safe and warm, side by side, dreaming of summer" — slow down, let the listener drift toward sleep.

**Closing — "Sleep now, little Gerda."**
Very soft, like a lullaby. Then a gentle pause. "The end." — warm and final, with a smile.
