# Narration script — "Fern the Fox Cub and the Twinkling Star"

Read-aloud directions for the warm narrator voice. Overall pace: slow and
lullaby-soft throughout; never rushed. Gentle smile audible in the voice.

## Directions

- **Title:** say softly, like opening a picture book: "Fern the Fox Cub and
  the Twinkling Star."
- **Den scene:** warm, snuggly tone; linger on "cozy" and "cream-white tip."
- **Daytime vs. night:** bright and playful for the daytime list; then the
  voice grows small and quiet for "Fern felt very small" — let the shadows
  sound a little sad, never scary.
- **Mama's arrival:** reassuring, warm; "starlight" is said like a promise.
- **"But Mama, it will be dark":** tiny, shy whisper.
- **Stepping out:** hushed wonder; slow down for the fireflies "blinking hello."
- **The first star:** pause a full beat before "The first star." Then Mama's
  song — sing it, don't say it, in a low, gentle lullaby voice, slow and even.
- **The wink:** delighted surprise, a soft giggle behind "Hello, star."
- **Stars popping out:** growing wonder, voice brightening like the sky.
- **Owl and moon:** drowsy, sleepy warmth.
- **The cozy blanket line:** the emotional heart — say it slowly, like tucking
  the listener in.
- **Grandma's burrow:** warm and homey; "honey cakes" with a smile.
- **Goodnight to the star:** a breathy whisper.
- **"Sleep now, little Fern.":** barely above a whisper, tender — addressed to
  Fern.
- **"The end. 🌙":** the softest close, then a beat of silence.

Pause ~1 s between paragraphs; ~2 s before the goodnight closing.
