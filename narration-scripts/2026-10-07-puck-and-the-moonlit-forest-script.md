# Narration Script: Puck and the Moonlit Forest

*Companion to `stories/2026-10-07-puck-and-the-moonlit-forest.md`. Written for a calm, warm read-aloud voice. Total runtime: about 4 minutes.*

**How to use this script:** Read the **bold narrator lines** aloud. Follow the *[bracketed cues]* for pacing, tone, and pauses. The *(parentheses)* are acting notes — don't read those.

---

*[SCENE 1 — Music soft. Warm, wonder-filled tone, like opening a secret door.]*

**Under a silver moon lived the tiniest fairy in all the Moonlit Wood. His name was Puck.**

*(Say "Puck" with a tiny smile in your voice — he's the hero.)*

**Puck was no bigger than a buttercup. He wore a leaf-green cap with a crooked tip, an acorn-cup coat, and his little wingtips sparkled with firefly dust. The fairy queen, Titania, loved to dance under the moonbeams, and King Oberon loved to laugh at Puck's jokes. And oh — Puck loved jokes.**

*[SCENE 2 — Tone turns playful and mischievous.]*

**On moonlit nights, Puck tied the fireflies' lantern-tails into knots, and hid Old Owl's round glasses in the mushroom patch. "Naughty Puck!" the rabbits would whisper — but nobody could stay cross with him for long.**

*(Gentle mischief in your voice — he's cheeky, not mean.)*

*[SCENE 3 — Oberon speaks: warm, kingly, unhurried.]*

**One still night, Oberon called, "Puck! The moonflowers are fast asleep, and Titania's dance cannot begin until their petals open. Fly and fetch one petal from the tallest moonflower, so I may wake them gently."**

**"A petal-fetching adventure!" cried Puck, and off he zoomed.**

*(Zoom the words a little — whoosh him off.)*

*[SCENE 4 — Build the sneeze. Slow down, then burst.]*

**The tallest moonflower stood on the hill, white and glowing. Puck tugged — and a sneezy little wind tickled his nose. "A-choo!" went Puck.**

*(Make the "A-choo!" big and sudden, then laugh softly under it.)*

**Whoosh! The wind scattered all twelve of the moonflower's petals across the forest.**

**One landed in Old Owl's nest, one plopped into the stream, and one stuck to a spider's cobweb. One even landed on the nose of Pip the sleepy hedgehog.**

*[Pause 1 second. Tone turns quiet and serious — the grin fades.]*

*[SCENE 5 — Puck's grin fades. Soft, small voice.]*

**Puck's grin faded. The fairies would be waiting. Titania's dance would be late. His sneezy mess had to be mended.**

*[SCENE 6 — Lila is bright and kind; Pip is shy and sleepy.]*

**Just then Lila the little bunny hopped up. "We'll help," she said, twitching her ears. Pip carefully lifted the petal off his nose and held it up. "Me too," he said shyly.**

*(Pip's "Me too" is barely a whisper — he's sleepy.)*

**But Puck shook his head. "Thank you, friends," he said softly. "This mess is mine to mend."**

*(Puck is brave here, not sad. Small and determined.)*

*[SCENE 7 — The mending. Gentle, careful pacing — each rescue is delicate.]*

**Puck flew to the owl's nest. "Forgive me, Old Owl," he whispered, lifting the petal gently. The owl blinked, then winked.**

*(The wink is warm — the owl forgives him instantly.)*

**Puck scooped the floating petal from the stream, freed the cobweb petal without shaking a single silk thread, and thanked the spider politely. Lila and Pip pointed out the petals hidden in the ferns.**

**One by one, twelve silver petals found their way back into Puck's little hands.**

*[SCENE 8 — Wonder blooms. Slow, golden, spellbound.]*

**Oberon laid them upon the sleeping moonflowers. Slowly — petal by petal — the flowers opened, spilling moonlight like honey. Titania began her dance, twirling through the silver glow, and all the forest clapped.**

*(Let "slowly — petal by petal" unfold like the flowers do.)*

*[SCENE 9 — Oberon's line is the warm heart of the story.]*

**Oberon smiled at Puck. "You mended your mischief gently, little one. That is a finer trick than any joke."**

*(Say this like the kindest compliment in the world.)*

**Puck straightened his crooked cap, feeling warm all the way down to his toes.**

*[SCENE 10 — Bedtime now. Slow right down. A lullaby voice.]*

**Then he curled up inside the tallest moonflower and pulled a soft petal over himself like a blanket. The moon kept watch.**

*[Pause 2 seconds. Barely above a whisper.]*

**Sleep now, little Puck.**

*[Pause 3 seconds. Let the music carry the ending.]*

**The end.**
