# Read-aloud script — "Kavi the Kingfisher and the First Dive"

**Overall tone:** warm, gentle, unhurried bedtime narration. Slow pace, soft smiles in the voice. Never rush the funny moments.

## Direction notes

- **Title:** read brightly, like opening a picture book — "Kavi the Kingfisher and the First Dive."
- **Opening paragraph:** dreamy and calm; linger on "smooth as glass."
- **"He could not dive."** — small pause before this line; say it softly, kindly, not sadly.
- **Adult kingfishers diving:** playful energy on the *splash!*; make the dives sound fun, never scary.
- **"Maybe tomorrow," he whispered** — genuinely whispered, tender.
- **Dee's entrance:** light, airy voice for Dee; her backstory ("I was afraid to fly over the water") slow and confiding.
- **Beak dips:** delight on the *Blip.* and *Blip-blip.* — tiny, ticklish sounds.
- **The hop and splash:** a beat of held breath before "Then, without quite deciding to, Kavi hopped." Then big joyful *SPLASH!* — the happiest sound in the story.
- **"You did it! You did your very own first dive!"** — cheerful, celebratory, Dee's voice bubbling.
- **"hop, splash, giggle, repeat"** — rhythmic, lilting, like a little song.
- **Closing:** slow right down. "Sleep now, little Kavi." — a warm whisper. Pause. "The end. 🌙" — softest of all, let the music carry it out.
