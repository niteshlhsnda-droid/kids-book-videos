# Read-aloud script — "Princess Amara and the Little Pea"

Voice: warm, gentle bedtime narrator. Slow, unhurried pace throughout. Pause briefly at each paragraph break.

- **Opening (soft, cozy):** "Once, in a castle on a green hill…" — gentle and warm, like tucking a blanket in. Linger on "always came home alone" — a little wistful, never sad.
- **The stormy night (dramatic, but never scary):** Quicken just slightly for the rattling rain and lightning. "Just as the castle clock struck nine" — a hushed little thrill. Amara's "Please, may I come in?" — small, polite, a touch shivery.
- **Warm welcome (kind, cozy):** Warm soup, dry clothes, a seat by the fire — slow and comforting. The queen's wondering thought — quiet and curious, like a secret.
- **The pea test (mischievous, hushed):** "A tiny green pea… no bigger than a fingernail" — said with a smile in the voice. Count the twenty mattresses and twenty quilts slowly, stacking them up like a tower, "fluffy as clouds."
- **Tossing and turning (gentle, sleepy worry):** "She tossed. She turned." — short, soft, restless. "Still, the little hard lump poked and prodded her" — a small sigh, never frightened.
- **Morning (bright, delighted):** "Oh, not at all" — Amara's voice, tired but cheerful. The queen's secret smile — warm and knowing. "Only a true princess" — the heart of the story, said softly and slowly.
- **The garden walk (sunny, glad):** Brighten up. "I could feel that pea all night" — Amara laughs, light and happy. Let the roses and the sunshine warm the voice.
- **Spring wedding (content, glowing):** Slow and satisfied. The little green pea in its glass box — a fond, smiling image. "No peas, no poking" — a gentle chuckle. "Just gentle rain on the roof" — soft as a lullaby.
- **Goodnight (sleepy, very soft):** Slow right down. "Sleep now, little Amara" — a whisper, softer still, like a lullaby.
