# Narration Script — Polo the Little Penguin and the First Swim

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Polo the Little Penguin and the First Swim."
- **Opening (bottom of the world)** — soft and dreamy. Slow down on "the snow sparkles blue under the moon." A tiny pause after "there was one thing Polo had never done."
- **Never been swimming** — quiet and small. "So big, so deep, and so very dark" — let the dark sound big, but not scary. "Not today" — a small whisper.
- **Pink and gold sky** — a little brighter, hopeful. "Thump, thump, thump" — slow, like a drum, one beat at a time.
- **Finn appears** — Finn's voice is quick and twinkly. "Hello up there!" — bright and bubbly. Pause before "The water is full of twinkles."
- **Suki pops up** — playful. "Sploosh!" — quick and light, with a splash of laughter. "Warm as a hug" — soft and inviting.
- **The first hop** — quick on "splash!" then a big, happy sneeze on "Achoo!" Polo's "Too cold! Too big!" — small and shivery, not frightened.
- **Mama's feathers** — the warmest, slowest part so far. "I remember my first swim" — gentle and close. "Just one" — soft, like a secret. Slow down on "silver light."
- **The big splash** — build it up: "One small hop... two small hops..." — then a bright, joyful "SPLASH!" "And Polo was swimming!" — happy and proud.
- **Under the aurora** — gliding and twinkling. "Silver spirals" — let the words spin. "It was singing" — warm and full.
- **Coming home** — slowest of all. "Droplets flew off like tiny stars" — soft and sleepy. Let the sea whisper. Long pause.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Polo." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
