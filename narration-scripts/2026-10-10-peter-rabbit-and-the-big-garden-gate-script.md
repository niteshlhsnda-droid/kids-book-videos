# Read-aloud script — "Peter Rabbit and the Big Garden Gate"

Voice: warm bedtime narrator. Gentle, unhurried pace throughout. Let each scene
breathe — a soft pause (a slow breath) between paragraphs.

## Direction notes

- **Tone:** cozy and motherly-warm. Nothing scary — even Mr. McGregor is
  gruff-but-kindly, like a grumpy neighbor, not a villain.
- **Opening (burrow intro):** smile in the voice; paint the cozy picture.
- **Mama's warning:** softer, a little slower — a mother's gentle care, not a
  scolding. Let the words "must not go into Mr. McGregor's garden" land with
  quiet weight.
- **Peter's temptation:** playful and bright, a touch of mischief in "one tiny
  peek."
- **The feast:** delighted and bouncy, but keep it calm — savoring, not wild.
- **The chase:** pick up pace slightly; a breath of excitement, not fear.
  "Stop, you rascal!" — firm and gruff, like Mr. McGregor, then release.
- **The escape home:** quick, then a long exhale as Peter reaches the burrow.
- **The ending:** slow right down. Mama's welcome is all warmth. The chamomile
  tea scene should feel like sinking into a soft pillow.
- **Goodnight line:** "Sleep now, little Peter." — the softest line of all,
  almost a whisper. Then a full breath before "The end."

## Emphasis cues

- Whiskers / pom-pom tail: light, fond amusement.
- "must not go into Mr. McGregor's garden": gentle firmness.
- "one tiny peek": conspiratorial whisper.
- "fast as the wind": swift, airy.
- "She did not scold him": linger — this is the heart of the story.
- "Sleep now, little Peter.": tender, final, quiet.
