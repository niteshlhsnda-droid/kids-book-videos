# Read-aloud script — "Misu the Velvet Rabbit and the Nursery Magic"

Tone: warm, soft, bedtime-slow. Tender and wonder-filled, like a lullaby in words.
Pace: unhurried; pause a beat between paragraphs. Let the shabby-loved lines feel cozy, and the magic lines glow.

**Opening** — bright and cozy: Christmas morning, "tucked into the top of his stocking…"
**Forgotten** — a little wistful, never sad: "the fancier toys… looked down their noses at him."
**Sage's wisdom** — slow and deep, an old friend's voice: "Real isn't about how you're made, little one…"
**"Misu," he murmured** — softest, sleepy; the hug line almost a whisper.
**Loved and shabby** — warm and proud: "He was loved, and that was everything."
**The toy box** — gentle ache, quiet: "No one will love me now…"
**The flower and the fairy** — wonder rising: "something wonderful happened."
**"You are loved, Misu"** — glowing, magical: "love has made you Real."
**The garden** — happy and free, a smile in the voice: "He was Real at last."
**Closing** — "Sleep now, little Misu, real and loved, under the kind spring moon." very gentle, then a pause: "The end. 🌙"
