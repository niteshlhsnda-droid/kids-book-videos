# Narration Script — The Three Little Pigs and the Brick House

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "The Three Little Pigs and the Brick House."
- **Opening (green valley)** — soft and sunny. Slow down on "buttercups nodded." A tiny pause after "build a house of your own."
- **Pip's straw house** — quick and bouncy, like Pip. "My house is done!" — bright and proud, with a little laugh.
- **Posy's stick house** — just as quick and cheerful. "In a wink" — light and fast.
- **Plum's brick house** — slower, steady, careful. "He laid each brick straight" — let the words sit like bricks. "A house should be strong enough to keep us safe" — warm and wise.
- **The wolf arrives** — a little lower and slower. "Little pig, little pig, let me come in!" — sly, not scary. "Not by the hair of my chinny-chin-chin!" — brave and small. The huffing — big breathy puffs. "And down it came" — quick, then a pause.
- **The stick house** — the same rhythm as the straw house, a little faster. "They ran to Plum's house" — quick little feet.
- **The brick house** — build it up: "So he huffed and puffed" — big and blustery — then a happy pause: "but the brick house did not move at all."
- **The apple tree** — sly and playful. "Meet me tomorrow at five" — cunning. "Plum tossed an apple far away" — quick and clever, with a smile.
- **The chimney** — quick and funny. "Splash!" — bright and sudden, with a laugh underneath. "He never came back again" — relieved and final.
- **Building together** — warm and slow again. "Brick by brick" — steady, like laying bricks. "The house was warm and safe" — the softest, sleepiest line so far. Let the dreams of apples and sunshine drift.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Plum." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
