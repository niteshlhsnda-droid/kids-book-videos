# Narration Script — Nori the Little Seahorse and the Gentle Current

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Nori the Little Seahorse and the Gentle Current."
- **Opening (seagrass meadow)** — soft and dreamy. Slow down on "swayed like slow ribbons." A tiny pause after "watched the other fish zoom past."
- **The zooming fish** — quick and bouncy, like the fish. "He went nowhere fast" — with a gentle, funny sigh. "I am the slowest swimmer in the whole wide sea" — small and sad, not loud.
- **The current** — a little faster, a little breathless. "It lifted Nori right off his stem" — swept up. "His heart thumped" — quiet and worried.
- **Marlow arrives** — slow, old, and calm. Marlow's voice is deep and sleepy. "The sea does not always ask us to hurry" — warm and wise, let it sit. "Sometimes it asks us to drift and hold on tight" — the softest, most important line so far.
- **Bubbles appears** — a tiny, trembling voice for Bubbles. "I am Bubbles, and I am scared" — small and shaky. Nori's "Hold my tail" — brave and kind.
- **The current grows stronger** — tense but not scary. "Hold on" — firm and warm. "We will wait together" — steady, like a promise.
- **Holding on** — slow and rocking, like a lullaby. "They waited" — a long, quiet pause. "The sound faded to a soft hush" — almost a whisper.
- **The little lights** — wonder in the voice. "The little lights are coming out" — bright and hopeful. "Twinkling a path" — gentle and sparkling.
- **Home again** — warm and relieved. "Thank you, Nori!" — bright and happy, a little laugh. "You are the best drifter in the sea!" — proud and joyful.
- **The closing reflection** — slow and sleepy again. "He was exactly the swimmer Bubbles had needed" — warm, like a hug.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Nori." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
