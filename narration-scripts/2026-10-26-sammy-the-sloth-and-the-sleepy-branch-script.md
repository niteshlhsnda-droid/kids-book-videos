# Read-aloud script — "Sammy the Sloth and the Sleepy Branch"

**Overall tone:** warm, drowsy, lullaby-slow. Imagine telling this story under a leafy tree at bedtime, your voice getting softer as Sammy gets sleepier.

**Pacing:** slow and even, with a soft pause at every paragraph break. Let the last third of the story drift — Sammy is settling down, and so is the listener.

**Voice:**
- Narration: warm, low, soothing. Gentle and unhurried throughout.
- Sammy the sloth: slow, soft, and sleepy, with a big yawn on "Yawn! Yawn! Yawn!"
- The giraffe: tall and kind, a gentle smile in her voice.
- The parrots: bright and chattery, but not loud — a cheerful squawk.
- Tula the toucan: breezy and friendly, swooping in with energy.
- Hana the hummingbird: tiny, humming, like a soft little song.
- The jungle friends (final whisper): all together, hushed and tender.

**Emphasis & pauses:**
- Paragraph 1 (jungle introduction): paint the picture slowly — "green and leafy jungle", "soft grey-brown fur". Calm and cozy.
- Paragraph 2 (the yawn): a real, slow yawn on "Yawn! Yawn! Yawn!" Then quiet: "I need a good branch for a bed."
- Paragraph 3–4 (giraffe's branch): polite and gentle. "crunch, crunch, crunch" soft, like leaves. Pause after the giraffe's kind refusal.
- Paragraph 5 (parrots): a playful burst of squawks, then Sammy's whispered "Too noisy for sleeping."
- Paragraph 6–7 (Tula and the waterfall): Tula bright and breezy; the waterfall builds — "splash, splash, SPLASH" — then drops back to quiet for "Too splashy."
- Paragraph 8 (elephants): heavy, happy, wobbly — "trumpeting toots", "shaking the whole tree". Sammy's "Too shaky" with a wobble.
- Paragraph 9 (Hana arrives): tiny and humming, a lift of hope. "Do not worry, Sammy" — soft and reassuring.
- Paragraph 10 (the quiet branch): slow right down. "up, up, up" gentle; "like a cradle in the trees" very soft.
- Paragraph 11 (settling in): the sleepiest part. "The leaves closed over him like a green blanket." Barely above a whisper.
- Paragraph 12 (goodnight chorus): hushed, all together — "Goodnight, Sammy. Sleep tight."
- Final line: "Sleep now, little Sammy." — the softest line of all. Then a breath of silence before "The end."
