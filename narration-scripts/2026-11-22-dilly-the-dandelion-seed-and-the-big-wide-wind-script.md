# Read-Aloud Script — Dilly the Dandelion Seed and the Big Wide Wind

Warm, slow bedtime voice throughout. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Let the wonder land; never rush the flying scene.

**Paragraph 1 — Opening (warm, sunny):**
Slow and soft, like settling into a sunny meadow. Emphasize "sunny," "fluffy," and "snowflakes." A gentle smile in the voice on "the littlest seed was Dilly."

**Paragraph 2 — Home on the stem (content, cozy):**
Light and happy. A little lift on "the whole meadow." Pause after "sang as it ran." Dilly's line — "I never want to leave" — is soft and sure.

**Paragraph 3 — The wind arrives (breezy, playful):**
The wind's whisper is light and dancing. A twirl in the voice on "tiny white parachutes." Let the sisters' laughter sparkle, then settle.

**Paragraph 4 — Dilly is scared (soft, small):**
Slower. Gentle worry on "so big and so wide." Dilly's whisper is small and honest. Pause after each question so children feel it with her.

**Paragraph 5 — Mama Dandelion (warm, reassuring):**
Drop into a warm, motherly voice — slow, golden, safe. Emphasize "never forgets" and "carry you gently." The last line — "the earth will tuck you in" — is a soft promise.

**Paragraph 6 — Ruby the ladybug (bright, friendly):**
Ruby is cheerful and kind, a little quicker. A warm shine on "red wings shining." Her advice — "Close your eyes, little Dilly, and trust the wind" — is gentle and encouraging.

**Paragraph 7 — The big gust (building excitement):**
A bouncy rush on "big, bouncy gust." A crisp little "Pop!" Then a joyful lift — "oh! She was flying!" — bright and delighted. Twirling rhythm on "twirling like a tiny dancer."

**Paragraph 8 — Floating (dreamy, wide-eyed):**
Slow and dreamy, floating. Wonder on "soft and sure" and "glow like warm honey." Let "the whole world twinkled below her" shimmer and settle.

**Paragraph 9 — Landing softly (gentle, moonlit):**
Voice cools and slows as the moon climbs. "Soft as a feather" — barely above a whisper. The rain is tiny and tender. The blanket line is warm and snug.

**Paragraph 10 — Snuggling in (sleepy, content):**
Slow, drowsy, content. A hopeful little lift on "In the spring, she would grow tall and green." Then let the last line drift down like a yawn.

**Goodnight line:**
Very soft, almost a lullaby. A long pause before it. "Sleep now, little Dilly, tucked in the soft, dark earth."
