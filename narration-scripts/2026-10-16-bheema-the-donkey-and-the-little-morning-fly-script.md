# Script — Bheema the Donkey and the Little Morning Fly

**Tone:** Warm, gentle, storytelling voice — like a grandparent by the fire. Slow, calm pacing throughout; lower volume and slower pace for the goodnight line.

**Overall pace:** Unrushed. Let the pictures breathe; pause briefly at each beat. Animal sounds (moo, bark, crow, caw, sneeze) are playful but soft — never startling.

---

*Soft, welcoming* — Bheema the Donkey and the Little Morning Fly.

*Gentle, cozy* — In a little village where the lanes smelled of warm earth and fresh bread, there lived a donkey named Bheema.

*Warm, sleepy smile* — Bheema was soft and grey, with long ears that flopped when he walked, and a smile that was always a little bit sleepy. Of all the things in the world, Bheema loved sleep the best.

*Calling, then softening* — Every morning, Ramu the washerman called, "Bheema! Time to carry the clothes to the river!" But Bheema lay curled in his hay, dreaming of soft clouds, and heard not a word.

*Sympathetic, a little sigh* — So Ramu sighed and carried the heavy bundle all by himself, down to the river and back. When Bheema woke at last, with the sun already high, he felt small and sorry inside.

*Hopeful, earnest* — One evening, Bheema said to Gauri the cow, "I can never wake up on time, and Ramu does all the work alone. Will you wake me in the morning, please?" "Of course I will," said Gauri.

*Big and playful, then hushed* — At sunrise, Gauri stood beside the hay and mooed her biggest, loudest moo: "MOOO!" But Bheema just snored on, smiling in his sleep.

*Encouraging, bright* — "Never mind," said Bheema at noon, rubbing his eyes. "Moti, will you try?" Moti wagged his tail. "I will bark and bark until you wake!"

*Big and playful, then hushed* — The next morning, Moti barked his loudest, bravest bark: "WOOF! WOOF! WOOF!" But Bheema just snored on, dreaming of clouds.

*Cheerful, then hushed* — Then Cheenu the rooster puffed out his chest and crowed, "Cock-a-doodle-doo!" Kalu the crow cawed, "Caw! Caw! Caw!" as loud as he could. But Bheema just snored on.

*Quiet, sad, slow* — That evening, Bheema sat in his hay, feeling very sad. "Nobody can wake me," he said. "Not the moo, not the bark, not the crow, not the caw."

*Light, tiny, quick* — Just then, a tiny fly came buzzing by. Her name was Flick, and she was so small you could hardly see her. Light as a whisper, she landed on the tip of Bheema's nose.

*Building, then a big happy sneeze* — "Aah... aah... Aah-choo!" went Bheema, with the biggest sneeze you ever heard. His eyes popped open. He was awake!

*Surprised, delighted* — "Did you wake me?" asked Bheema. "I did!" said Flick. "Your nose tickled, and out came a sneeze."

*Warm, hopeful* — "Will you wake me every morning?" asked Bheema. "Every single morning," said Flick. And she did.

*Bright, joyful morning* — Next morning, Flick landed on Bheema's nose, tickle, tickle, "Aah-choo!" and Bheema was up before the sun. He carried the bundle to the river beside Ramu, and Ramu laughed.

*Warm, full-hearted* — Gauri mooed. Moti barked. Cheenu crowed and Kalu cawed, and the whole village smiled.

*Soft, cozy, winding down* — That night, Bheema curled into his hay with a full, happy heart. Flick settled on a straw beside him.

*Slowest, softest, like a lullaby* — Sleep now, little Bheema, and wake to a brand-new day.

*Gentle close* — The end.
