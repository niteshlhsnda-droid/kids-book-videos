# Read-aloud script — Jojo the Jackal and the Thumping Drum

Warm, slow bedtime voice. Pace: unhurried, ~130 wpm. Pause briefly between paragraphs.

## Tone cues

- Opening ("Under the roots of an old banyan tree…"): soft, cozy, sinking into the story.
- "But one night, just as his eyes were fluttering shut, he heard it.": slight lift, then a beat of silence.
- "THUMP. Thump. Thump.": low, hollow drum-like taps — three slow beats, then softer echo. Do this twice in the story (same rhythm both times).
- "What is THAT?": whispered, wide-eyed, a little tremble on "THAT".
- "If I am very quiet and very still…": small, hiding-under-blanket voice.
- Mother's lullaby paragraph: warmest tone of the story; slow down; "low and soft, humming and humming" almost a hum.
- "a bigger part of him wanted to know": gentle lift — courage arriving.
- Discovery ("In the middle of the clearing stood an old war drum…"): wonder, quiet "aha".
- "No monster! No giant!": bright, relieved, a real giggle on "he giggled".
- "It is only the wind, playing the drum like a musician!": playful, musical.
- Ending ("Jojo trotted home feeling tall and warm inside…"): slow, drowsy, voice sinking.
- "Sleep now, little Jojo.": the softest line — barely above a whisper, a smile in it.
- "The end. 🌙": gentle full stop. Let the last word fade.

## Emphasis

- Stress the onomatopoeia: THUMP (loudest) → Thump → thump (trailing off).
- "only the wind" — the relief word; say it like a secret shared.
- Never moralize the ending — let the goodnight line land warm and simple.
