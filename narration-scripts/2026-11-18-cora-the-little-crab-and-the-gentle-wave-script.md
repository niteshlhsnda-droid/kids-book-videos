# Narration Script — Cora the Little Crab and the Gentle Wave

**Voice:** warm, slow bedtime narrator (avocado_v2:MAI_01). Gentle pace throughout, like telling a story to a sleepy child. Pause briefly between paragraphs. Never rush the goodnight line.

## Directions

- **Title** — read warmly, with a little smile in the voice: "Cora the Little Crab and the Gentle Wave."
- **Opening (the rock pool)** — soft and sunny. Slow down on "sparkled like glass." A tiny pause after "clicked when she was happy."
- **Afraid of the waves** — a little quieter, a little worried. "Crash, crash, crash" — low and rumbly, but not scary. "I will stay right here, where it is safe" — small and snug.
- **The tide goes out** — dreamy, peachy-pink evening. "Tap, tap, tap" — light and quick. "I am lost! The sea has gone away!" — small and frightened.
- **Sandy appears** — gentle and kind. "I am Sandy" — wobbly and soft. "Cora's heart went thump-thump" — slow and worried. "But Sandy needed help" — warm, like a little decision being made.
- **Cora helps** — brave and busy. "Do not worry" — bright and kind. "We will wait for the sea together" — steady, like a promise.
- **The rumble** — tense but not scary. The sun sinking — slow and sleepy. "Oh no" — a worried whisper. "It will wash us both away!" — trembling, but small.
- **The gentle wave** — wonder in the voice. "And it was not a crash at all" — surprised and relieved. "Curling over the rocks like a kind hand" — soft and slow. "Hold on!" — firm and warm.
- **Riding the wave** — rocking, like a lullaby. "Cora opened one eye... then the other" — slow, with a pause. "We are swimming! We are flying!" — bright and happy, a little laugh.
- **Home again** — warm and relieved. "Sandy! You came home!" — joyful. "She was brave, even though she was scared" — proud and gentle. "Then the sea is your friend too, Cora" — the softest, most important line so far.
- **The closing reflection** — slow and sleepy again. "The waves were not too big — they were just the sea, saying hello" — warm, like a hug. "Cora waved a little claw at the moon" — a smile in the voice.
- **Goodnight line** — "Sleep now, little Cora." — the softest line of all, almost a whisper, like tucking a child in.
- **"The end."** — quiet, final, warm. Then silence.
