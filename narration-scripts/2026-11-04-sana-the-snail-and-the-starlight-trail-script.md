# Read-aloud script — "Sana the Snail and the Starlight Trail"

Voice: warm, gentle bedtime narrator. Slow, unhurried pace throughout — this is a story about slowness, so never rush. Pause briefly at each paragraph break.

- **Opening (soft, dreamy):** "In a quiet garden…" — slow and hushed, like the evening itself settling down. Linger on "soft and purple."
- **Pond Party (warm excitement, still gentle):** Lift slightly on "Tonight was the Pond Party" — a happy secret, not a shout.
- **Tiko (bouncy, playful):** A quick, springy voice for the frog: "boing, boing, boing!" then an eager "Come on, Sana!"
- **Ria (bright, quick):** Light and bouncy: "hop, hop, hop!" and "Hurry, Sana!" — cheerful, never impatient.
- **Sana's reply (calm, kind):** Soft and steady. "I'm coming as fast as I can." — no sadness, just her own pace.
- **Lumi in trouble (small, worried):** A tiny frightened voice: "Help! Oh, help!" Then Sana's whisper: "Don't worry… I'll be very, very careful." — extra slow and reassuring.
- **The freeing (gentle triumph):** Build a little warmth as each thread loosens. Lumi's thanks sparkles: "Thank you, Sana!" — bright and grateful.
- **Clouds and darkness (hushed, a little serious):** Lower the voice; the garden has gone dark. Pause before "Don't be afraid."
- **The crossing (steady, confident):** Slow, careful rhythm, one step at a time — match Sana's pace. "Step by careful step…"
- **Cheers (joyful, warm):** A happy chorus feel: "Hurrah for Sana! Hurrah for Lumi!"
- **The pond (content, glowing):** Warm and satisfied. Slow down on "that felt even better than hurrying" — the heart of the story, said softly.
- **Goodnight (softest of all):** Barely above a whisper. "Sleep now, little Sana." Then a pause, and "The end. 🌙" — let the last words fade like a firefly's light.
