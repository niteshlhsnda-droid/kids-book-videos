# Narration Script — Chiku the Monkey and the Jamun Tree

Read slowly and warmly, like a bedtime story. Gentle pace throughout; pause briefly at each paragraph break. Let the funny and tender moments land — never rush.

## Title
"Chiku the Monkey and the Jamun Tree." — warm, inviting, a soft smile in the voice.

## Opening (riverbank, Chiku's tree)
Calm, dreamy tone. Paint the picture slowly: the wide river, the tall tree, the little monkey.
- "On the bank of a wide, slow river…" — slow, gentle.
- "small brown monkey named Chiku" — warm and fond.

## Morning jamuns
Light, happy. Chiku is content.
- "soft and sweet, that stained his little fingers purple" — playful, a small smile.

## Kumo arrives
Kumo's voice: big, slow, friendly — a gentle giant, never scary.
- "Good morning… My name is Kumo." — deep but kind.
- Chiku's "Catch!" — bright and cheerful.

## Friendship grows
Warm and easy. A steady, happy rhythm.
- "Thank you, little friend" — Kumo, grateful and warm.

## Rani's greedy thought
Shift slightly darker but still gentle — this is a worry, not a fright.
- "If the jamuns are this sweet…" — Rani, wistful, a little sly. Do not make her sound wicked.
- "Bring me Chiku's heart." — quiet, serious. Pause after this line.

## Kumo refuses, then agrees
Kumo, torn and sad.
- "I cannot do that. Chiku is my friend." — firm, loyal.
- "But Rani would not eat or smile…" — soft, sad. Pause.

## The invitation
Kumo sounds cheerful on the surface, but a little strained underneath.
- "Rani has made a grand supper." — bright, with a hint of nervousness.

## Mid-river confession
Slow down. Quiet, honest, sad.
- "Chiku… I must tell you the truth." — barely above a whisper. Pause before the next line.

## Chiku's quick thinking
Quick, bright, clever — the turning point. A lift in energy, but not loud.
- "Oh, Kumo!" — sudden, as if a lightbulb went on.
- "Monkeys always leave their hearts in their trees." — quick, confident, a little cheeky.

## The dash back and the rescue
Quick, breathless, relieved.
- "Kumo turned around and swam fast…" — pick up the pace a little.
- "safe and sound" — exhale, relief.

## Forgiveness
Gentle and warm again. Everyone is sorry, everyone is forgiven.
- "My heart stays right here with me…" — Chiku, kind and steady, not angry.
- "I am sorry, Chiku" — Kumo, low and truly sorry.
- "I am sorry as well" — Rani, soft, from far away.

## Ending
Slow right down. Drowsy, cozy, stars coming out.
- "full of sweet jamuns and happy thoughts" — warm, sleepy.

## Goodnight closing
Very soft, like a lullaby, a touch slower than everything before:
- "Sleep now, little Chiku." — tender, then a gentle pause.
- "The end." — soft, final, then silence.
