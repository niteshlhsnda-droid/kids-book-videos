Akbar and Birbal: The Crows in the Kingdom

A clever Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in the great land of India, there ruled a mighty emperor named Akbar. His palace shone white in the sun, and flags fluttered from its golden domes.

Emperor Akbar had a minister named Birbal — the cleverest and kindest man in all the land. Whenever the emperor had a hard question, he would call, "Birbal! Birbal!" — and Birbal always had an answer.

One sunny morning, Akbar sat on his golden throne in the grand court. The courtiers fanned themselves. The peacocks cried out in the garden. Through the tall windows, Akbar could see black crows hopping on the palace walls — caw, caw, caw!

Akbar stroked his beard and smiled a twinkly, teasing smile. "Birbal," he said, "tell me — how many crows are there in my kingdom?"

What would YOU say? One hundred? One thousand? Hmm…

The courtiers gasped. "What a question! No one could ever know that!"

But Birbal did not even blink. "Ninety-five thousand, four hundred and sixty-three, Your Majesty," he answered without a moment's pause.

"Ninety-five thousand, four hundred and sixty-three?" Akbar leaned forward. "And how would you prove it, my clever Birbal?"

Birbal bowed low and said, "Your Majesty, send your men to count them. If they find more than my number, then some crows have come visiting from the neighboring kingdom. And if they find fewer, then some of our crows have gone visiting there."

For a moment, the whole court was silent.

Then Akbar burst out laughing. "Ha, ha, ha!" The courtiers laughed too — even the grumpiest old minister chuckled. The parrots in the garden squawked, as if they were laughing too.

"You have answered me again, Birbal!" said Akbar, clapping his hands. He gave Birbal a bag of gold coins and a soft pat on the back. Birbal bowed again, his eyes twinkling.

That evening, when the court lamps were lit and the crows flew home to their nests, Birbal sat in his garden and told the story to his little ones. They laughed and laughed, and the stars twinkled above them, soft and sleepy, while fireflies blinked like tiny lamps among the flowers.

And somewhere on a palace wall, a crow tucked its head under its wing and went to sleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A quick wit can answer even the hardest questions.
