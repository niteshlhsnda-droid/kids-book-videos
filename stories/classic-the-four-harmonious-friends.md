# The Four Harmonious Friends

*A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, at the edge of a green forest, there grew a little mango tree — so little that it was only as tall as a blade of grass.

Four friends lived near the little tree.

Piku the partridge found a tiny seed and tucked it gently into the soft earth.

Bunty the hare came hopping by with a little pot of water. "I will water the seed every day," he said, "so it can grow strong."

Minku the monkey came swinging down from the branches. "I will pull away the weeds," he said, "so nothing crowds our little tree."

And Appu the elephant came walking slowly through the trees. "I will shade it from the hot sun," he said, "so it will not feel thirsty."

Day after day, the four friends looked after the little tree.

Spring came, and the rain fell soft on the little leaves. Summer came, and the sun shone warm on the little branches. The little tree drank the rain and stretched toward the sun, a little taller every day.

Piku pecked the earth soft around it. Bunty watered it every morning. Minku pulled the weeds. Appu shaded it with his big, kind ears.

And the little tree grew… and grew… and GREW!

First it was as tall as a hare. Then it was as tall as a monkey. Then it was as tall as an elephant! And then — oh my! — it was the tallest tree in the whole forest, with branches full of golden mangoes!

The four friends danced with joy. But then they looked up, and their smiles faded.

"The mangoes are too high!" cried Bunty. "I cannot reach them!"

"Neither can I," said Minku, jumping as high as he could. "They are far above my head!"

"Even I cannot reach them," said Appu, stretching his trunk up, up, up. "They are too high for us all."

Piku the partridge fluttered up to the lowest branch and looked down at her friends. Then she had a wonderful idea!

"Friends!" she chirped. "We grew this tree together. So let us pick its mangoes together!"

Appu the elephant knelt down low. Minku the monkey climbed onto Appu's back. Bunty the hare climbed onto Minku's shoulders. And Piku the partridge flew to the very top!

"Reach, Piku, reach!" cried her friends.

Piku stretched her little beak — and pluck! She picked a golden mango!

"One for Appu!" she chirped, dropping it down.

Pluck! "One for Minku!"

Pluck! "One for Bunty!"

Pluck! "And one for me!"

And then the four friends sat under their tall, tall tree and ate the sweetest mangoes in the whole world — together.

For a seed planted by one friend, watered by another, weeded by a third, and shaded by a fourth, grows into a tree that feeds them all.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Little kindnesses grow tall — and friends who help each other can reach anything.*
