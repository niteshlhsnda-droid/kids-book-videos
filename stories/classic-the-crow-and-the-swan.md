# The Crow and the Swan

*A gentle Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green forest beside a sparkling blue lake, there lived a little crow named Kaku.

Kaku had shiny black feathers, bright black eyes, and a happy, cawing voice that went, "Caw, caw!" all through the day.

Every morning, Kaku flew down to the lake to drink. And every morning, he saw the beautiful white swan named Hansa gliding across the water, graceful and white as a cloud.

"Oh," sighed Kaku, "how I wish I were white like Hansa. Her feathers are so white and lovely. My feathers are only black, black, black."

One morning, Kaku had an idea. "I know!" he said. "I will wash and wash in the lake, and my feathers will turn white, just like Hansa's!"

So Kaku splashed into the lake. Splash, splash, splash! He washed his wings. He washed his tail. He washed his little black head. He washed and washed until the sun was high in the sky.

Then he climbed onto a rock and looked at his feathers.

They were still black. Black as night, black as the soft dark earth. No matter how hard he scrubbed, not one feather turned white.

"Oh no," said Kaku, and his wings drooped. "I washed and washed, but I am still black."

Hansa the swan glided over to the rock, her white wings shining in the sun.

"Dear Kaku," she said kindly, "why are you so sad?"

"I washed and washed," said Kaku, "but my feathers will not turn white. I wish I were beautiful like you."

Hansa smiled a gentle swan smile. "Oh, Kaku," she said, "look at your feathers. They are not just black. In the sunlight, they shine blue and purple, like the night sky full of stars."

Kaku looked down at his wing. There, in the sunlight, his black feathers did shine — blue and purple, like stars!

"And your voice," said Hansa, "makes everyone smile. Every morning, when you sing, 'Caw, caw!' the whole forest wakes up happy. The birds sing along. Even the sleepy old owl smiles."

Kaku blinked. "Really?" he said. "My voice makes everyone smile?"

"Really," said Hansa. "You do not need white feathers to be beautiful. You are beautiful just as you are, little crow."

Kaku felt warm all over, from his beak to his toes. He spread his wings and looked at them again. They shone blue and purple in the sun, just like stars.

"Thank you, Hansa," he said. "I think my black feathers are rather fine, after all!"

From that day on, Kaku never wished to be white again. Every morning he flew to the lake, sang his happy song, "Caw, caw! Caw, caw!" and the whole forest sang along with him.

And when the sun set and the stars came out, Kaku looked up at the sky and thought, "The night sky is dark and full of stars — just like my feathers."

He tucked his head under his wing, safe in his nest, and dreamed of starry skies and singing songs.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: You are beautiful just as you are — love being yourself.*
