# Sadie the Squirrel and the Secret Stash

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a tall oak tree in the middle of the wood, there lived a busy little squirrel named Sadie.

Sadie loved autumn most of all. Autumn meant crunchy leaves, golden sunshine, and best of all — acorns! Big, fat, shiny acorns, falling from the oak tree like little brown presents!

All autumn long, Sadie gathered acorns. She gathered them in the morning. She gathered them in the afternoon. She tucked them into her cheeks until her cheeks were as round as two little apples!

And then she hid them. She hid acorns under the big rock. She hid acorns behind the old log. She hid acorns under the pile of crunchy leaves.

"I have the very best hiding places," said Sadie proudly. "Nobody will EVER find my acorns!"

But when winter came, and the snow covered the wood in a soft white blanket, Sadie had a problem.

She could not remember where she had hidden her acorns!

"Was it under the big rock?" she said, digging in the snow. "No acorns here!"

"Was it behind the old log?" she said, peeping behind it. "No acorns here either!"

"Was it under the crunchy leaves?" she said, rustling through them. "Oh no! No acorns anywhere!"

Sadie sat down in the snow and sighed a big, sad sigh. "What shall I do? I have so many acorns, and I cannot find even one!"

Just then, her friend Ollie the owl swooped down and landed beside her. "Hoo-hoo!" he said. "What is the matter, Sadie?"

"I hid all my acorns," said Sadie, "and now I cannot find them!"

Ollie the owl blinked his big round eyes. "Perhaps," he said, "you need a map."

"A map!" cried Sadie. "What a wonderful idea!"

So Sadie found a big, smooth leaf and a little berry for ink. And she drew a map! She drew the big rock, with three little dots for the acorns underneath. She drew the old log, with five little dots behind it. She drew the pile of leaves, with four little dots beneath it.

"Now I will never forget!" said Sadie happily.

She followed her map — step, step, step — and dug under the big rock. There were the three acorns! She peeped behind the old log. There were the five acorns! She rustled through the leaves. There were the four acorns!

Sadie had twelve acorns! More than enough for the whole winter!

But as she looked at her map, Sadie had another idea — an even better idea.

She ran to find her friend Hazel the hedgehog, who had no acorns at all.

"Hazel," said Sadie, "I have a map! And I have twelve acorns! Would you like to share them with me?"

Hazel's eyes lit up like two little stars. "Oh, Sadie! Really? Thank you!"

So Sadie and Hazel shared the acorns — six for Sadie, and six for Hazel. And they ate them together, warm and cosy in Sadie's oak tree, while the snow fell softly outside.

And Sadie learned something wonderful that winter: a secret stash is nice, but a shared stash is even nicer.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing what you have makes winter warmer for everyone.*
