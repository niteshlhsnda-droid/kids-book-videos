Juno the Jellyfish and the Glowing Garden

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Deep under the sea, where the water was soft and blue, lived a little jellyfish named Juno.

Juno was not big, and she was not fast. But she had a very special gift: she glowed.

When Juno was happy, she glowed a soft pink. When she was sleepy, she glowed a gentle blue.

And every evening, Juno tended her favorite place in the whole ocean — a little garden of glowing sea flowers.

She watered them with swirls of water. She sang them a tiny song: "Grow, grow, little glow..."

And the flowers glowed back at her — pink, and gold, and soft, soft green.

One night, as Juno was tucking her flowers in for the night, she heard a tiny sniffle.

Sniff... sniff...

It was a baby turtle named Tiko. He was lost. His eyes were big and teary.

"I cannot find my home," sniffled Tiko. "It is so dark, and I do not know the way."

"Do not worry," said Juno softly. "My garden will light your way."

Juno glowed her brightest, happiest pink. And all her sea flowers glowed too — pink, and gold, and soft, soft green.

"Follow the flowers," said Juno. "They will lead you home, one glow at a time."

So Tiko swam slowly, from flower to flower, glow to glow.

Past the pink flowers. Past the gold flowers. Past the soft, soft green flowers.

And there — at the end of the glowing path — was Tiko's home! His mother was waiting with open flippers.

"Tiko!" cried his mother. "You are home!"

Tiko hugged his mother tight. Then he turned to Juno and said, "Thank you, Juno. Your garden saved me."

Juno glowed so happily that the whole garden shone like a tiny underwater sunrise.

"You are welcome, little friend," she said. "That is what gardens are for — to light the way home."

Then Juno swam back to her garden, tucked herself into a soft sea flower, and glowed herself gently to sleep.

Glow... glow... glow...

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A small light, shared kindly, can guide a lost friend all the way home.
