# The Faithful Parrot

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green mango grove stood the grandest fig tree anyone had ever seen.

Its branches were wide like open arms. Its figs were fat and sweet and purple as twilight. And in a cozy hollow near the top lived Paro, a bright green parrot with a golden beak.

Every morning Paro woke to the smell of ripe figs. "Thank you, dear tree," he would chirp, and feast. Every evening he sang the tree his happiest songs, and the leaves danced along — shiver-shiver-shiver.

Years floated by like soft clouds. Then one summer, no rain came. The stream shrank to a silver thread. The fig tree's leaves turned brown and crisp, and one by one they fluttered down.

No figs grew that year. Not one.

"Fly away with us, Paro!" cried the mynahs. "There are mango trees by the river, heavy with fruit!"

"Come, come!" called the sparrows. "Why stay in a dry old tree?"

But Paro only shook his bright green head. "This tree fed me when I was small," he said. "It shaded me in the hot sun. It listened to all my songs. I will not leave my friend now."

So the other birds flew away, and Paro stayed.

Each morning he searched the ground for fallen figs from better years and shared half with the tree's roots. Each evening he fetched water in his beak, drop by drop, from the faraway stream, and poured it gently at the tree's feet.

"Drink, dear tree," he whispered. "I am here."

The tree could not speak, but deep in its trunk, something warm glowed.

One still night, a soft silver mist drifted through the grove — gentle as a sigh. It curled around the old fig tree and settled on its roots like a blessing.

When Paro woke at dawn, he could not believe his eyes.

Tiny green leaves — hundreds of them! — were uncurling along every branch. And among the leaves hung figs, round and golden, glowing like little lanterns in the morning sun.

Paro laughed and twirled in the air. "Thank you! Oh, thank you!" he sang, and the whole grove sang with him.

From that day on, the fig tree never went hungry again, and Paro never left its side — for loyalty, little one, is the sweetest fruit of all.

And tonight, as the round moon rises over the mango grove, Paro tucks his head under his wing in the old fig tree — and dreams sweet dreams of golden figs.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Gratitude and loyalty are the sweetest treasures — stay true to those who care for you.*
