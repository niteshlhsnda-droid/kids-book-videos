# The Night Bakery

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Rory the raccoon woke up to a wonderful smell — warm bread, sweet honey — drifting through his window. His tummy rumbled with curiosity. Where was it coming from?

He followed his nose outside. Down the lane, a tiny shop glowed golden, with a wooden sign that read: THE NIGHT BAKERY. The door was round, like a cookie.

Rory peeked inside. An old owl in a floury apron was kneading dough with her wings. "Welcome, little one," she hooted. "I bake for dreamers."

"Bake for dreamers?" asked Rory.

"Every night, I bake moon-buns," the owl explained. "They are warm and soft. Children eat them in their dreams, and the buns make dreams sweet."

Rory's eyes went round with wonder. "Can I try one?" he asked.

"Of course," said the owl. "But first, you must help. Moon-buns need patience."

Rory watched her knead — push, fold, push, fold — the dough soft as a cloud. He helped shape the little buns, and his paws got all floury.

"Bake soft, bake slow, watch the moon-buns glow," sang the owl.

They slid the buns into the warm oven. The bakery filled with golden light, and the buns began to glow softly, like tiny moons. Rory felt warm all over.

"Bake soft, bake slow, watch the moon-buns glow," sang the owl again.

Ding! The buns were ready. The owl gave Rory the warmest one. He took a bite — it tasted like honey and starlight, the yummiest thing ever.

"Bake soft, bake slow, watch the moon-buns glow," sang the owl one last time.

Suddenly Rory's eyelids grew heavy. The warm bun had made him drowsy. The owl wrapped him in a floury apron like a blanket and rocked him gently.

"Time for you to dream, little baker," she hooted. "The moon-buns are done. Your dreams are waiting."

Rory closed his eyes and dreamed of a bakery in the clouds. He baked star-shaped cookies and shared them with all his friends.

Sleep now, little Rory.

The end. 🌙
