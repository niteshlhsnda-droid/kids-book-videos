# Nori the Little Seahorse and the Gentle Current

In a soft green meadow under the sea, where the seagrass swayed like slow ribbons, lived a little seahorse named Nori. Nori was small, with a curly tail and a tiny snout. Every morning he wrapped his tail around a tall stem of seagrass and watched the other fish zoom past.

The silver fish zoomed. The yellow fish zoomed. Even the baby clownfish zipped around like suns. Nori tried to zoom too, wiggling his tiny fins as fast as he could. He went nowhere fast. He drifted in a slow, sleepy circle and bumped into his stem. "Oh," sighed Nori. "I am the slowest swimmer in the whole wide sea."

That afternoon, a strong current came rolling through the meadow. It lifted Nori right off his stem and carried him away, over the coral and past the pink anemones. Nori tumbled through the blue water. His heart thumped. He tried to swim back, but his fins were no match for the rushing water.

Then, drifting beside him, came a very old turtle named Marlow. He moved like a slow, sleepy cloud. "Hello, little one," said Marlow. "The current is strong today." "I am lost," said Nori. "And I am too slow to swim home." Marlow smiled. "The sea does not always ask us to hurry," he said. "Sometimes it asks us to drift and hold on tight."

Just then, Nori heard a tiny sniffle. Under a purple shell, a baby clownfish was hiding, all alone. "I lost my anemone," whispered the clownfish. "I am Bubbles, and I am scared." "Do not be scared," said Nori, and he wrapped his tail around a smooth grey rock. "Hold my tail."

The current grew stronger. It pushed and pulled at Nori, and his tail slipped from the rock. Bubbles squeaked, and Nori held him tighter. "Hold on," said Nori. "We will wait together."

Nori curled his tail around the rock again, tight as a hug, and Bubbles curled close beside him. Together they rocked in the water, slow and steady, while the current rushed past. They waited. The water grew calm again. The sound faded to a soft hush.

"Look," said Marlow, pointing with one old flipper. "The little lights are coming out." Tiny glowing plankton floated through the water like stars, twinkling a path through the dark blue sea. Marlow drifted ahead, slow and sure, and Nori and Bubbles followed the lights home.

There was the seagrass meadow. There was Mama Seahorse, waiting on her tall stem. And there, beside her, was Bubbles' anemone, waving its pink arms. Bubbles darted home with a happy wiggle. "Thank you, Nori!" he called. "You are the best drifter in the sea!"

Nori wrapped his tail around his own stem and swayed in the moonlight. He was not the fastest swimmer in the sea. But he was exactly the swimmer Bubbles had needed.

Sleep now, little Nori.

The end. 🌙
