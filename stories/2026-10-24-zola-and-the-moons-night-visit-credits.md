# Credits — Zola and the Moon's Night Visit

- **Original title:** The Moon and the Villagers
- **Author:** Eric Ouma
- **Illustrator:** none credited (fresh illustrations generated for this adaptation — no source art reused)
- **Source URL:** https://www.africanstorybook.org/read/downloadbook.php?id=52803&a=1&d=0&layout=landscape
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), © African Storybook Initiative 2026
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm bedtime language for ages 4–7. The original is a short first-paragraphs story about the moon visiting a lakeside village; this adaptation adds a child protagonist (Zola), her shy humming, and the moon carrying her tune back to the sky, keeping the original's gentle wonder and nighttime setting. All illustrations are newly generated originals.
