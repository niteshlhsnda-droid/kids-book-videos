# Dolly the Dolphin and the Deep-Sea Lullaby

*A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep down in the blue sea, where the water is soft and quiet, lived a little dolphin named Dolly.

Dolly was a happy little dolphin. All day long she leaped and splashed and played with her friends, chasing bubbles and racing the little silver fish.

But when night came, Dolly had a problem. She could not sleep.

"Click, click, click!" went the shrimps. "Splash, splash!" went the waves above. "Gurgle, gurgle!" went the bubbles from the deep, dark rocks.

"Oh dear," whispered Dolly, hiding under her soft seaweed blanket. "The sea is too noisy tonight. How will I ever fall asleep?"

Then Dolly had an idea. "If the sea is full of noisy sounds," she said, "maybe I can find some soft sounds instead."

So Dolly swam down, down, down into the deep, quiet sea, to collect soft sounds for a lullaby.

First, she swam to a big, gentle whale. The whale was fast asleep, and with every breath he made a long, low hum.

"Hummm," went the whale. "Hummm."

"That is a lovely soft sound," said Dolly. "Thank you, Mr. Whale." And she tucked the slow hum into her lullaby.

Next, Dolly swam past a tiny shrimp, sitting on a pink rock and tapping its little claws very, very softly.

"Tick-tock, tick-tock," went the shrimp, as gentle as a tiny clock.

"That is a lovely soft sound," said Dolly. "Thank you, little shrimp." And she tucked the tick-tock into her lullaby.

Then Dolly swam to the tall seaweed garden, where the seaweed swayed slowly from side to side, like a mummy rocking a baby.

"Swish, swish," went the seaweed, soft as a whisper.

"That is a lovely soft sound," said Dolly. "Thank you, dear seaweed." And she tucked the soft swish into her lullaby.

At last, Dolly swam to a sleepy old turtle, yawning a big, slow yawn, his fins moving like slow, sleepy wings.

"Shoosh, shoosh," went the turtle's fins through the water.

"That is a lovely soft sound," said Dolly. "Thank you, Mr. Turtle." And she tucked the slow shoosh into her lullaby.

Now Dolly had all her soft sounds. She swam home, curled up under her seaweed blanket, and began to sing.

"Hummm," she sang, like the whale. "Tick-tock," she sang, like the shrimp. "Swish, swish," she sang, like the seaweed. "Shoosh, shoosh," she sang, like the turtle.

Her lullaby floated out across the sea, soft and slow and sweet. The shrimps stopped clicking, the waves stopped splashing, and even the bubbles stopped gurgling.

All over the deep blue sea, the fish closed their eyes. The crabs tucked in their claws. The whale hummed along, and the turtle smiled in his sleep.

"Shh," whispered the sea. "Dolly is singing. It is time to sleep."

Dolly yawned a tiny dolphin yawn, closed her eyes, and drifted off to sleep. And all through the night, the whole ocean slept soundly, wrapped in Dolly's gentle song.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Soft, gentle sounds — and a kind song — can help the whole world rest.*
