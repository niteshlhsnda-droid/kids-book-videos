# Tenali Rama and the Elephant

*A timeless Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in the kingdom of Vijayanagara, there lived a wise and merry king named Krishnadeva Raya.

In his court lived Tenali Rama, the cleverest man in the whole kingdom. Whenever the king had a puzzle, Tenali Rama always found the answer — and always with a smile.

One day, a neighbouring king sent Krishnadeva Raya a wonderful gift — a huge, grand elephant!

The elephant was as tall as a house. His trunk was as long as a tree branch. His ears flapped like great grey fans.

"What a splendid elephant!" said the king. "He is the biggest elephant I have ever seen! But tell me, my friends — how much does he weigh?"

The king's friends looked at one another. How could anyone weigh an elephant? There was no scale big enough in the whole kingdom!

"We could guess," said one of the king's friends. "Perhaps he weighs as much as a hundred men?"

"Or a thousand sacks of rice?" said another.

The king shook his head. "I do not want a guess. I want to KNOW. Who can weigh my elephant?"

Everyone was silent. Everyone, that is, except Tenali Rama, who was smiling his clever smile.

"I can weigh the elephant, Your Majesty," said Tenali Rama.

The whole court gasped. "How?" cried the king.

"Give me one day," said Tenali Rama. "And bring the elephant to the river."

The next morning, everyone gathered at the river. Tenali Rama had a big flat boat waiting there, and a pile of smooth grey stones on the bank.

"Now," said Tenali Rama, "let us walk the elephant onto the boat."

The elephant stepped onto the boat — step, step, step — and the boat sank lower and lower into the water.

"Mark the water!" called Tenali Rama. He painted a bright red line on the side of the boat, exactly where the water touched it.

Then the elephant was walked back onto the bank. The boat floated up again, light and empty.

"Now," said Tenali Rama, "let us fill the boat with stones, until the water touches the red line again."

So the king's friends carried stone after stone onto the boat. The boat sank lower… and lower… and lower… until — splash! — the water touched the red line!

"Stop!" cried Tenali Rama. "Now the boat carries the same weight as the elephant!"

All that was left was to weigh the stones, one by one, on the market scales. Tenali Rama added up all the numbers.

"Your Majesty," he said, bowing low, "your elephant weighs exactly as much as these stones!"

The king laughed and clapped his hands. "Tenali Rama! You are the cleverest man in my kingdom! No scale could weigh an elephant — but your clever thinking did!"

And from that day on, whenever anyone in Vijayanagara had a puzzle too big to solve, they would say, "Ask Tenali Rama — he will find a way!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever idea can solve even the biggest puzzle.*
