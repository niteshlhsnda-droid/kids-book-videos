# The Goodnight Zoo

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Miko the meerkat could not sleep. His burrow was too quiet, and he missed the sounds of the zoo — and his animal friends.

He tiptoed out of his burrow into the little zoo at the edge of Willowmere Wood, silver with moonlight. "I will say goodnight to everyone," he decided. "Then I will feel sleepy."

First he visited Gina the giraffe, who was already lying down — which looked very funny — with her long neck curled like a slide. "Goodnight, Gina," Miko whispered.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," yawned Gina.

Next he visited Lenny the lion, who opened his huge mouth wide. Miko felt a little scared — but it was only a yawn! A great big sleepy yawn.

"Goodnight, Lenny," Miko whispered.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," rumbled Lenny softly.

Then he visited Penny the penguin, tucked into a pile of soft snow with her flippers folded like hands in prayer. "Goodnight, Penny," Miko whispered.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," peeped Penny.

Miko visited the sleepy elephants, the drowsy zebras, and the yawning hippo, whose yawn was the biggest of all. Each animal was cozy in bed, and Miko felt sleepier with every goodnight.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," he whispered to the whole zoo.

He scurried back to his own burrow, which did not feel too quiet anymore — it felt peaceful, like the rest of the zoo. He curled into his warm sand bed.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," he whispered to himself.

His mother tucked the blanket around him. "Did you say goodnight to everyone?" she asked.

"Everyone," said Miko proudly — and then he yawned the biggest meerkat yawn ever, his eyes closing all by themselves.

"Goodnight, sleep tight," his mother whispered one last time.

The zoo was silent now. Gina dreamed of tall trees. Lenny dreamed of soft clouds. Penny dreamed of snowflakes. And Miko dreamed he was the goodnight keeper of the whole zoo — he had said goodnight to every friend, and now it was his turn to dream.

Sleep now, little Miko.

The end. 🌙
