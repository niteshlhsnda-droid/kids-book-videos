# The Two Fish and the Frog

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a round green pond, under the lily pads, lived two little fish and one bouncy frog.

The first fish was named Willow. She was quiet and thoughtful, and she always planned ahead.

The second fish was named Bubbles. He was quick and clever, and he loved solving problems at the very last minute.

The frog was named Hoppy. He was small and springy, and he trusted Willow with all his heart.

One sunny morning, a tall heron landed by the pond with important news.

"Listen, little ones," said the heron. "The fishermen are coming to this pond at sunrise tomorrow. Swim away tonight, while the moon is high."

Willow did not wait. "Thank you, Heron," she said. And splash-splash-splash, she swam down the little stream to the big, safe river.

Hoppy hopped right after her. "If Willow is going, I am going!" he cried. Plip-plop, into the stream he went.

But Bubbles stayed behind. He blew a big, shiny bubble and laughed.

"I am the cleverest fish in the pond," he said. "When the fishermen come, I will think of something. I always do."

That night, the moon rose round and white. Willow and Hoppy curled up safe in the river reeds and fell fast asleep.

Bubbles stayed awake, all alone in the quiet pond. For the first time, his clever plans felt very far away.

At sunrise, the fishermen came with their long nets — swish, swish, swish through the water.

Bubbles' heart went thump-thump-thump. Then he remembered his clever idea!

He turned on his side and held very, very still — as still as a fallen leaf.

One fisherman scooped him up, looked at him, and said, "This one is no good. Back you go!" And plop — Bubbles was tossed back into the pond.

Bubbles did not wait one more minute. Splash-splash-splash, he swam down the little stream to the big, safe river, where Willow and Hoppy were waiting.

"I was clever," Bubbles said softly, "but you were wise, Willow. Next time, I will plan ahead — like you."

And from that day on, whenever the heron brought news, Bubbles was always the very first to pack his little fins and go.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever trick can save you once — but planning ahead keeps you safe always.*
