# The Lion and the Jackal

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a warm, golden forest, where the tall grass swayed, there lived a great lion named Leo.

Leo was the king of the forest, with a soft golden mane.

Every morning, Leo walked through his forest, and all the animals bowed and said, "Good morning, King Leo!"

But Leo had a secret sadness. He was big and strong, and yet he felt alone.

"Everyone bows to me," Leo sighed to the evening sun, "but no one plays with me. No one talks to me, just for fun."

Now, in that same forest, there lived a small, clever jackal named Jai.

One soft evening, Jai heard Leo sighing, all alone on his big, flat rock. Jai scampered up, quick and light.

"Good evening, King Leo," said Jai, with a friendly smile. "Why do you look so sad?"

"I am the king," said Leo, "but I have no friend to share my stories with. My roar is big, but my heart feels small and lonely."

Jai thought and thought. His bright eyes twinkled.

"Then let us have a feast!" said Jai, happily. "A feast for all the forest! We will share stories and sweet mangoes."

Leo smiled widely. "A feast sounds lovely, Jai. But where will we find enough food for everyone?"

"Leave it to me," said Jai, with a clever wink. "I know a secret."

The next morning, Jai scampered deep into the forest and followed a trail of busy bees, buzz, buzz, buzz, to a hidden mango grove.

The mango trees were full of ripe, golden mangoes, sweet as honey. Jai scampered back to Leo, quick as a flash.

"King Leo!" he cried. "I found a grove full of mangoes, enough for everyone!"

Leo roared a happy roar, and all the animals came running.

But then, oh dear! A great fallen tree lay right across the path to the grove, blocking the way.

The animals sighed. "No one can move that tree," said a deer, sadly. "It is far too heavy."

Jai thought and thought. His bright eyes twinkled again.

"We do not need to move it," said Jai. "We can go around it, through the soft grass, if we follow the little stream."

So Jai led the way along the stream, and Leo walked behind him, helping the little animals over the stones.

Soon, they all reached the mango grove, and oh, what a feast they had! Leo shared the sweetest mangoes, and everyone laughed and told stories.

Leo laughed a deep, happy laugh. His heart felt warm and full.

"Thank you, Jai," said Leo, softly. "You are small, and you are clever, and you are my dearest friend. You helped the whole forest today."

Jai smiled and wagged his tail. "That is what friends are for, King Leo," he said. "Big or small, we all help each other."

As the sun set, the animals walked home together, full and happy.

Then Leo curled up on his rock, and Jai curled up beside him, warm and safe.

The stars came out, one by one, twinkling softly. Leo closed his eyes, and he was not lonely anymore.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Big or small, a clever, kind friend makes the whole forest happier.*
