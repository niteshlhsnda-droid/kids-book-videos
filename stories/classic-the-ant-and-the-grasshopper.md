# The Ant and the Grasshopper

*A timeless Aesop fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

All through the warm summer, a little grasshopper named Gigi sang in the sunny meadow.

She sang in the morning, she sang at noon, and she sang in the golden evening light. Her song went like this: "La la la, the sun is warm, the sky is blue, and life is sweet!"

Down in the grass, a busy line of ants marched back and forth, back and forth. They were carrying grains of wheat to their snug little home under the old oak tree.

"Come and sing with me!" called Gigi one sunny day. "The sun is shining! Why work so hard?"

A little ant named Anita stopped, wiped her brow, and smiled up at her. "We are getting ready for winter, Gigi," she said. "When the snow comes, there will be no grain in the fields. We must store food now, while the sun shines."

"Winter is a long way off!" laughed Gigi, and she danced a happy jig. "La la la! I will think about winter when winter comes!"

So Gigi sang all summer long, while the ants worked all summer long. They carried wheat and seeds and crumbs, and tucked them safely into their pantry, deep under the oak tree.

At last, the leaves turned gold and red, and a chilly wind began to blow. Gigi sang her last summer song, and then the first snowflakes began to fall — soft, white, and cold.

Soon the whole meadow was covered in snow. There was no green grass to nibble, no warm sunshine to sing in. Gigi shivered in the cold.

"Brrr!" said Gigi, hugging herself. "I am so cold, and so hungry! What shall I do?"

She hopped through the snow to the oak tree and knocked, very softly, on the ants' little door.

"Who is it?" called Anita.

"It is me, Gigi," said the grasshopper, her teeth chattering. "I am cold and hungry. In the summer I only sang, and I never stored any food. Could you spare a little crumb?"

Anita looked at her friends. The ants had worked hard all summer, and their pantry was full. But they had kind hearts.

"Come in, Gigi," said Anita warmly. "Sit by our fire and warm your feet. We have plenty to share."

So Gigi came inside, and the ants gave her warm soup and a soft blanket. Gigi's feet stopped shivering, and her tummy stopped rumbling.

"Thank you, thank you," said Gigi. "You were wise to work in the summer. I was foolish to only sing. But you shared with me anyway."

"You sang us beautiful songs all summer," said Anita, smiling. "Your songs made our work lighter. And friends help each other — that is what friends are for."

Then Gigi had an idea. "May I sing for you now?" she asked. "A winter song, to thank you?"

And so, all winter long, Gigi sang sweet songs by the ants' fire, while the snow fell softly outside. The ants tapped their feet and hummed along, and the little home under the oak tree was the cosiest place in the whole meadow.

When spring came again, Gigi did something new. She still sang — oh, how she sang! — but in the mornings, she helped the ants carry seeds too.

"Sing and work, work and sing," laughed Gigi. "That is the happiest way of all!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Work a little while the sun shines, and share kindly with friends in need.*
