Bobby's Bubble Bay

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Bobby the beaver did not want a bath. "Baths are boring," he said, crossing his arms with a stubborn pout.

His father smiled. "Then let us not take a bath," he said. "Let us sail to Bubble Bay instead."

"Bubble Bay?" asked Bobby, curiosity bubbling up.

Father filled the tub with warm water and poured in bubble soap. Soon the tub was full of foam, like a bay full of clouds, and Father placed a toy whale in the water.

"Welcome to Bubble Bay, Captain Bobby!" said Father.

Bobby climbed in. The water was warm and soft, bubbles floated everywhere, and the toy whale bobbed on the waves. Bobby felt his stubbornness melting away.

"Splish, splash, bubbles pop, worries float and never stop," sang Father.

Bobby splished with his paws. Splash! Water droplets sparkled. He made the whale dive under the bubbles, then pop up with a whoosh. Bobby giggled.

"Splish, splash, bubbles pop, worries float and never stop," sang Father again.

Bobby scooped up a handful of bubbles and blew them gently. They floated up, up, up, each bubble carrying a tiny worry away. Pop! There went the grumpy mood. Pop! There went the wiggles.

Bobby washed his ears and his tail. The warm water felt wonderful. He did not feel stubborn anymore — he felt relaxed and happy.

"Splish, splash, bubbles pop, worries float and never stop," sang Father one last time.

Father wrapped Bobby in a fluffy towel, warm from the dryer. Bobby felt like a cozy burrito, his fur clean and soft, smelling of bubble soap.

He put on his pajamas — extra soft on clean skin — and climbed into bed, his body warm and tingly.

Sleep now, little beaver. Bubble Bay has washed your day away. Dream of sailing warm and foamy seas.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A warm bath can wash a grumpy mood away, along with the day's dust.
