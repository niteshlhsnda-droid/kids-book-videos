# Why the Sun and Moon Live in the Sky

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

A long, long time ago, the Sun and the Moon lived down here on the earth, just like you and me.

They were the very best of friends. The Sun was warm and golden, and he loved to shine. The Moon was soft and silver, and she loved to glow. Every day they played together in the green fields, and every night they watched the stars come out, side by side.

One day, the Sun had a wonderful idea. "I will build a big, beautiful house," he said, "with a wide door and room for all my friends. Then we can have a lovely party!"

So the Sun built his house. He built it big and strong, with a roof of golden straw and windows that sparkled in the light.

When the house was ready, the Sun said to the Moon, "Dear friend, please come and visit me! Bring your wife, the Water, too. We will have tea and cakes and a happy afternoon together."

The Moon smiled. "We would love to come," she said.

So the Moon and the Water came to visit. The Water was kind and gentle, and she flowed along in a soft, silver stream.

"Welcome, welcome!" said the Sun, opening his wide door. "Come in, come in!"

The Water stepped inside — and oh! As she came in, she grew a little bigger. Then a little bigger still. She rose up to the Sun's knees. Then up to his waist. Then up to his shoulders!

"My friend!" cried the Sun. "You are getting so big! My house is filling up!"

"I am sorry," said the Water in her soft, bubbly voice. "But this is what I do. I keep growing and growing when I visit."

The water rose higher and higher until it touched the roof. The Sun and the Moon climbed up onto the roof, and the water rose up after them, splashing gently at their toes.

"Up we go!" laughed the Sun. "Let us climb a little higher!"

So the Sun and the Moon climbed up, up, up into the bright blue sky, and the Water stayed below, sparkling in the sunshine down on the earth.

And that is why the Sun and the Moon live in the sky to this very day. They are still the very best of friends.

In the daytime, the Sun shines warm and golden over the fields. At night, the Moon glows soft and silver over the sleeping world. They take turns, one after the other, so that the earth is never dark and never alone.

And far below them, the Water sparkles in the rivers and the seas, waving up at her two old friends in the sky.

Then, when the stars come out, the Sun tucks himself into a soft golden cloud, the Moon hums a quiet lullaby, and the whole world drifts off to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: True friends always find a way to stay close, even when things change.*
