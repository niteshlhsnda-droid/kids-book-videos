Kira the Panda and the Bamboo Lullaby

A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a quiet bamboo grove, where the tall green stalks swayed in the soft evening breeze, there lived a little panda named Kira.

Kira had soft black-and-white fur, round little ears, and a very big problem.

She could not fall asleep.

Every night, Kira tossed and turned in her cosy bamboo bed. "I am too awake," she would sigh. "The moon is up, the stars are out, but my eyes just will not close!"

One night, Kira decided to take a little walk. She padded softly through the bamboo grove — pat, pat, pat — listening to the leaves rustle above her.

Rustle, rustle, rustle. The bamboo leaves whispered in the wind.

"Shhh, shhh, shhh," sang the bamboo, ever so softly. "Shhh, shhh, shhh."

"It sounds like a lullaby," whispered Kira.

As she walked, she met her friend Rio the red panda, sitting on a mossy rock and munching a bamboo shoot.

"Hello, Kira," said Rio. "Why are you awake?"

"I cannot sleep," said Kira. "My eyes will not close."

"Here," said Rio, sharing his bamboo shoot, "have a little midnight snack. Sweet dreams always start with a full tummy."

Kira took a nibble. Crunch, crunch. It was sweet and fresh.

A little further on, Kira met Glimmer the firefly, glowing soft and gold in the dark.

"Hello, Kira," said Glimmer. "Why are you awake?"

"I cannot sleep," said Kira. "My eyes will not close."

"Then follow my light," said Glimmer, "and I will show you the sleepiest spot in the whole grove."

So Kira followed Glimmer's gentle glow — twinkle, twinkle, twinkle — through the tall bamboo, until they came to a soft, mossy hollow where the moonlight fell like silver rain.

There sat Niko the little monkey, humming a soft, sleepy song. "Hmm, hmm, hmm…"

"Hello, Kira," said Niko. "Why are you awake?"

"I cannot sleep," said Kira. "My eyes will not close."

"Then sit with us," said Niko, "and listen."

So Kira sat down in the soft moss. Rio curled up beside her. Glimmer glowed softly above them. Niko hummed his sleepy song. And all around them, the bamboo leaves rustled their gentle lullaby — shhh, shhh, shhh.

The moon smiled down through the bamboo. The stars twinkled, one by one. And Kira's eyes — her big, round, wide-awake eyes — began to feel heavy.

"Shhh, shhh, shhh," sang the bamboo.

"Hmm, hmm, hmm," hummed Niko.

And Kira the panda yawned a great big panda yawn, curled up in the soft moss, and fell fast asleep, with her friends all around her.

When morning came, Kira woke up, stretched, and smiled. She had slept all night long, right there in the bamboo grove, under the whispering leaves.

And from that night on, whenever Kira could not sleep, she would tiptoe out to the mossy hollow, listen to the bamboo's lullaby, and drift off to dreamland — shhh, shhh, shhh.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A quiet walk, kind friends, and a soft song can help anyone fall asleep.
