Henry the Hedgehog and the Hushed Garden

A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a quiet garden, behind a little stone wall, there lived a hedgehog named Henry.

Henry was a happy hedgehog with a round brown body and a very pointy nose, and he loved to snuffle.

Snuffle, snuffle, snuffle went Henry, sniffing at flowers and fallen apples. But he was a very noisy snuffler, and his little nose woke everyone up.

"Shh!" whispered Basil the mouse, looking out of his hole. "Henry, please be quiet. I am trying to sleep."

"Sorry!" said Henry, but then he snuffled again. Snuffle! Snort!

"Oh dear," said Shelly the snail, pulling her head into her shell. "That snuffle is louder than thunder."

Henry felt sad. He did not want to wake his friends. He wanted to be a good neighbour.

"I will learn to be quiet," he said. "I will learn to tiptoe and whisper, just like the soft night wind."

That evening, Henry practised in the garden. He lifted his little feet very, very slowly. Step. Step. Step.

He whispered, "Snuffle," so softly that only a flower could hear him.

"Well done, Henry!" said Basil, smiling. "You are as quiet as a feather."

Henry practised every night, tiptoeing round the pond and whispering past the roses, until all the animals in the garden were sleeping peacefully.

One night, the moon was round and bright, and Henry had a wonderful idea.

"Let us have a midnight parade," he whispered to Basil. "A quiet parade, where everyone tiptoes together."

"Yes!" said Basil. "A hushed parade!"

So Henry led the way, tiptoeing softly down the garden path. Step. Step. Step.

Behind him came Basil the mouse, tiptoeing on his tiny pink feet. Behind Basil came Shelly the snail, gliding softly along the path.

Then came Freddie the frog, hopping very, very quietly, and Wren the bird, fluttering softly above them.

They tiptoed past the roses, round the pond, and under the big apple tree, where the moonlight danced on the leaves. Nobody woke up, and nobody made a sound.

At the end of the path, they sat in a soft, grassy circle and looked up at the stars.

"That was lovely," whispered Shelly. "You are the quietest hedgehog in the whole world, Henry."

Basil yawned a tiny yawn. "Time for sleep," he whispered. "Goodnight, everyone."

"Goodnight," whispered Henry, Shelly, Freddie, and Wren, all together.

They tiptoed home, one by one, and snuggled into their warm, cosy beds.

The garden was hushed and peaceful. The moon smiled down kindly, and the bright stars twinkled softly overhead.

Henry curled up in his little nest of soft leaves and closed his eyes. He was proud of his soft, quiet feet, and proud of his quiet midnight parade.

From that night on, Henry always tiptoed and whispered after dark, and the garden always slept peacefully.

And whenever the moon was round and bright, the animals gathered for another hushed midnight parade, led by Henry, the quietest hedgehog of all.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Soft, quiet feet and a gentle whisper can help everyone sleep peacefully.
