# The Crow and the Snake

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, in a tall green mango tree, there lived a clever crow named Kiki.

Kiki had built a soft, round nest from twigs and leaves, high up in the branches.

In the nest sat his wife Mimi, keeping three little speckled eggs warm and safe.

Every morning, Kiki sang a happy song as the sun came up.

"Good morning, sun! Good morning, tree!" he cawed.

But one day, Kiki heard a soft, slithery sound from deep inside the tree.

Sssss! Sssss! A long, shiny snake was sliding up the trunk, toward the nest.

"Oh no!" cried Kiki. "That snake wants to eat our little eggs!"

Kiki flapped his wings and cawed as loudly as he could.

"Shoo! Go away, snake! Shoo!"

But the snake only smiled a slow, sleepy smile and curled up in a dark hole in the tree.

"I will wait," hissed the snake. "I am in no hurry at all."

Kiki and Mimi were very worried.

"We must find a clever plan," said Kiki. "A plan that keeps our eggs safe and hurts nobody at all."

Just then, Kiki heard music and happy voices from the palace garden below.

The king and queen were walking in the garden, under the big mango trees.

The queen wore a beautiful necklace of shiny gold beads that sparkled in the sun.

Kiki had a bright idea!

"Watch me, Mimi," he said. "I have a plan!"

When the queen sat down to rest under a tree, Kiki swooped down — whoosh!

He picked up the shiny necklace in his beak and flew up, up, up to the mango tree.

"My necklace! My necklace is gone!" cried the queen.

"Do not worry," said the king. "My guards will find it."

The king's guards looked here, and they looked there.

Then one guard looked up and saw something shiny near the hole in the mango tree.

"There it is!" he cried. "The crow has taken it to his tree!"

The guards ran to the tree with long sticks and loud voices.

"Come out! Come out!" they shouted.

The snake heard the noise and peeked out of his hole.

"Oh no!" hissed the snake. "So many people! I must go far, far away!"

And the snake slid quickly down the tree and slithered away into the deep, dark forest, far from the mango tree.

The guards climbed up and found the shiny necklace, safe and sound.

"Your necklace, my queen!" said the guard, bowing low.

The queen smiled. "Thank you, kind guards," she said.

That evening, Kiki and Mimi sat in their nest, safe and happy.

The three little eggs were warm and safe under Mimi's soft wings.

"Your clever plan worked!" said Mimi. "The snake is gone, and nobody was hurt at all."

Kiki smiled and sang his happy song once more.

"Good night, sun! Good night, tree!" he cawed softly.

And soon, the whole mango tree was quiet and still, under the twinkling stars.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever mind can solve a big problem without a fight.*
