# The Cat and the Mice

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a quiet old farmhouse, lived a great family of mice.

They lived behind the kitchen wall, in a cosy little home with tiny beds and a tiny table. Every evening, they scampered out to nibble crumbs of bread and cheese.

But there was one big problem. The problem had whiskers, soft paws, and a very hungry tummy.

Her name was Mimi the cat.

Every time the mice peeked out of their hole, there sat Mimi, washing her face and waiting.

"We cannot go out at all!" squeaked young Mina. "Mimi is always there!"

Grandma Nora, the oldest and wisest mouse, twitched her whiskers. "We must be clever," she said. "Let us watch and wait."

So the mice watched Mimi from their little hole, day after day.

Then one morning, something very strange happened.

Mimi was not sitting by the hole, washing her face. Mimi was hanging by her back paws from a hook on the wall, as still as still can be.

"Look!" squeaked Mina. "Mimi is not moving at all! She must be fast asleep — or maybe she has gone away to cat heaven!"

All the little mice clapped their tiny paws. "Hooray!" they squeaked. "We can go out and play!"

But Grandma Nora did not clap. She peered out carefully with her little round eyes.

"Wait, my dears," she said softly. "Look closer. Do you see Mimi's tail? It is twitching, just a tiny bit. And do you see her ear? It is listening, just a little."

The mice looked again. Sure enough — Mimi's tail gave the tiniest twitch. Her ear gave the tiniest wiggle.

"She is pretending!" cried Mina. "She is playing a trick on us!"

"Exactly," said Grandma Nora. "A cat who hangs still is still a cat. Let us stay right here, safe and sound, until Mimi gives up and goes away."

So the mice stayed in their cosy home. They ate the crumbs they had saved, they told stories, and they waited.

Mimi hung and hung, until her paws grew tired and her tail grew bored. At last, she dropped down with a thump and wandered off to the garden.

"Now!" said Grandma Nora, laughing. "Now we can go out!"

The mice scampered out into the kitchen, nibbled their crumbs, and danced a happy little dance around the table leg.

And from that day on, whenever something looked too easy, young Mina would say, "Wait — is that tail twitching?" And all the mice would laugh and look twice.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Look twice before you trust something that seems too good to be true.*
