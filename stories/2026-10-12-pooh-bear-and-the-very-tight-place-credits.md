# Credits — "Pooh Bear and the Very Tight Place" (2026-10-12)

- **Original title:** *Winnie-the-Pooh*, Chapter 2 — "In Which Pooh Goes Visiting and Gets Into a Tight Place"
- **Author:** A. A. Milne (1882–1956)
- **Illustrator:** Fresh watercolor illustrations generated for this adaptation (the original E. H. Shepard illustrations were not reused).
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/67098/
- **License:** Public domain in the USA — first published 1926; public domain since 1 January 2022.
- **Adaptation note:** The chapter was retold from scratch in warm, simple language for ages 4–7 (~430 words) as an original bedtime retelling; no text from the source was copied, and no Disney-specific depictions or designs were used.
