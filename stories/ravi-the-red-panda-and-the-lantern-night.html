Ravi the Red Panda and the Lantern Night

An original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

High in the misty mountains, in a forest of tall bamboo, lived a little red panda named Ravi.

Ravi loved lanterns. He loved their warm glow, their soft light, and the way they made the dark forest feel cosy.

But Ravi had a problem: he was afraid of the dark. Every night, when the sun went down, Ravi hid in his tree hollow and waited for morning, missing all the beautiful night-time things.

One evening, the forest announced the Lantern Night Festival — a night when every animal hung a glowing lantern from the trees, and the whole forest shone like a sky full of stars.

"Oh, I want to go!" said Ravi. "But... it is dark. What if I get lost?"

His best friend, Meera the mouse, squeaked kindly, "You will not be lost, Ravi. We will go together, and I will carry a lantern too. Two lanterns are twice as bright!"

So when the evening came, Ravi took a deep breath, held Meera's paw, and stepped out of his hollow.

Wow! The forest was magical! Lanterns hung from every branch — red ones, golden ones, green ones — swaying gently in the breeze like sleepy fireflies.

"Look, Ravi!" said Meera. "There is the lantern parade!"

A line of animals walked slowly through the trees, each carrying a lantern: rabbits, deer, owls, and even old Tortoise Uncle, whose lantern was the biggest of all.

Ravi's paws stopped trembling. His eyes grew wide with wonder. "The dark is not scary at all," he whispered. "Not when it is full of light and friends!"

At the end of the parade, the animals gathered in a clearing and hung their lanterns in one great tree — the Lantern Tree. Ravi hung his own little red lantern on the lowest branch, and it glowed warm and proud.

Then everyone sang the Lantern Song, soft and slow, and one by one the little animals yawned and snuggled down for the night, under the gentle light of a hundred lanterns.

Ravi yawned too, and he was not afraid at all. "Thank you, Meera," he whispered. "Tonight, the dark felt like a hug."

And from that night on, Ravi never hid from the dark again. Whenever night came, he lit his little red lantern and thought, "The dark is just the daytime, wearing its starry blanket."

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The dark feels less scary when you share its light with a friend.
