# The Jackal and the Two Otters

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Down by the sleepy river, where the water went *shhhh* over the smooth stones, lived two otter brothers. Nikki was the bigger one, and Bhola was the smaller one, and they loved each other very, very much.

One golden evening, they went splashy-splashing after one big, shiny, silvery fish.

*Plip! Plop! Splish-splash!*

Nikki chased it left. Bhola chased it right. And together — *flip-flop!* — they caught it.

"HOORAY!" they both squeaked, dancing a soggy little dance on the riverbank. "What a fish! What a BIG, silvery, shimmery fish!"

Then Nikki held the fish up. Bhola held the fish up too. And at the very same time, they both said —

"I want the BIG half!"

"I saw it first!" said Nikki, with a big huffy *huff*.

"I splashed it toward you!" said Bhola, with a small grumpy *grump*.

"MY half should be bigger!" said Nikki.

"MY half should be bigger!" said Bhola.

And they both crossed their little arms — *humph!* — and turned their backs — *mph!* — and sat very, very still.

Just then, along the riverbank came Jinx the jackal — pointy ears, swishy tail, and one big cheeky grin.

"Well, well," said Jinx. "What is all this *humph* and *mph* about, little friends?"

Nikki and Bhola explained. "We caught this fish together, but we cannot agree on the BIG half. Will you divide it for us?"

"Me?" said Jinx, and his tail swished happily. "Of course! I am very, very fair. Watch."

He took the fish and held it up to the light.

"Hmmm," he said. "This half is a TEENSY bit bigger." And he took a little *nibble-nib* from that side.

"There. Even now," he said.

But then he squinted at the other side. "Oh dear. NOW this half is a teensy bit bigger." And he took a little *nibble-nib* from THAT side.

"Nibble-nib here… nibble-nib there…" Jinx said, humming a happy little tune. "These nibbles are my judge's snack. Very official. Very fair."

Nikki watched. Bhola watched. Their big, shiny fish was getting smaller… and smaller… and smaller…

Nibble-nib. Nibble-nib. Nibble-nib.

"STOP!" cried Nikki.

"STOP!" cried Bhola. "We'd rather share! We'd rather share!"

Jinx stopped. He looked at the two otters. Then he looked at the two tiny bites left. And he laughed — not a mean laugh, oh no — a warm, rumbly laugh.

"See, my little friends?" he said kindly. "Sharing from the start would have filled both your tummies. But quarrelling only fed ME!"

And with that, he gave them back the last two bites. "One for Nikki. One for Bhola. Nice and fair."

"Thank you, Jinx," said the otters together, their eyes round and shiny like the moon on the water.

Jinx bowed low, his swishy tail sweeping the sand. "Anytime," he said. "Now go and share, little friends. That is the yummiest thing of all."

Nikki munched his bite. *Munch-munch.* Bhola munched his bite. *Munch-munch.* And even those two tiny bites tasted wonderful, because they were shared with love. They both smiled the biggest, sleepiest smiles.

"We'll never quarrel over food again," Nikki promised.

"Not ever, ever," Bhola promised, and they bumped their round heads together with the softest little *bonk* — and giggled sleepily.

That night, the river sang its soft *shhhh, shhhh* lullaby. The moon rose like a big silver cookie in the sky. And the two otter brothers curled up together on the warm riverbank, tails wrapped around each other like cozy blankets, drifting off to dream of shiny fishes and shared dinners.

And from somewhere in the dark, Jinx the jackal yawned a sleepy little yawn and whispered, "Sweet dreams, little friends."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing with love is better than quarrelling over more.*
