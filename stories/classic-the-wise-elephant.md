# The Wise Elephant

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a big, green forest lived a very old elephant named Ellie. She was the biggest animal in the forest, and she was also the wisest.

Ellie had seen many summers and many winters. She knew where the sweetest fruit grew. She knew where the coolest water flowed. And she knew the songs of every bird in the forest.

All the animals loved Ellie. When they had a problem, they came to her. When they felt worried, they came to her. And Ellie always listened, calm and kind.

One morning, Ellie lifted her big trunk and sniffed the air. Then she listened very carefully. The birds had gone quiet. The wind smelled of rain.

"A big storm is coming," said Ellie, in her soft, deep voice. "We must all go to the safe high meadow. Come, follow me."

The little rabbits hopped after her. The monkeys swung down from the trees and walked behind her. The deer came running, and the tiny mice scurried along too.

"But Ellie," said a small rabbit, "the high meadow is so far away. My little legs are tired already."

"Do not worry," said Ellie with a smile. "I will go slowly, and we will rest on the way."

As they walked, the sky grew dark. The wind began to blow, and the trees swayed from side to side.

"Stay close to me," said Ellie. "Hold on to each other's paws. We are all in this together."

When the smallest mouse began to cry, Ellie gently lifted him onto her broad back. "You can ride with me," she said. "You are safe up here."

Soon the little animals were all snuggled together on Ellie's back — the mouse, a baby rabbit, and even a tiny bird with a hurt wing.

At last, they reached the high meadow. It was dry and warm, with soft grass and tall trees to keep out the wind.

Then the storm came. Rain poured down, and thunder rumbled across the sky. But high up in the meadow, the animals were safe and dry.

Ellie shared her food with everyone. She broke off sweet branches for the deer. She gave soft leaves to the rabbits. She even found berries for the little birds.

"Thank you, Ellie," said the animals. "You kept us safe."

When the storm was over, the sun came out, warm and bright. A beautiful rainbow arched across the sky.

The animals cheered and danced in the soft grass. Then they all walked home together, safe and happy, behind their wise old friend Ellie.

That night, the forest was quiet and calm. The little animals curled up in their warm nests, thinking of Ellie's kindness, and drifted off into sweet, peaceful sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A wise and kind heart keeps everyone safe.*
