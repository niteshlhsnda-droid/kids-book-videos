Nasruddin and the Lost Donkey

A funny Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a little village with whitewashed houses and one crooked lane, there lived a kind, funny man named Nasruddin.

Nasruddin had a little grey donkey with soft ears and a gentle bray. Every morning, Nasruddin and his donkey walked to the market together, and every evening they walked home together, side by side.

One sunny morning, Nasruddin went out to feed his donkey — but the stable was empty!

"Oh dear," said Nasruddin, scratching his beard. "Where has my little donkey gone?"

He looked behind the house. He looked under the apricot tree. He looked in the garden. No donkey anywhere!

So Nasruddin put on his big turban, picked up his walking stick, and set off to search the whole village.

"Has anyone seen my donkey?" he called out, as he walked down the crooked lane.

The baker shook his head. "I have seen bread, and I have seen buns, but I have not seen your donkey."

"Has anyone seen my donkey?" Nasruddin asked the potter, who was spinning his wheel, round and round.

"Not today," said the potter. "I have seen only pots."

Nasruddin walked on, tap, tap, tap, with his stick on the dusty road. He asked the shepherd in the field. He asked the weaver at her loom. He asked the children playing marbles in the square.

"No, no, no," they all said. "We have not seen your donkey."

Nasruddin thanked them all kindly and walked on, for he would not stop until his little donkey was found.

Nasruddin sat down on a stone wall and sighed. "Oh, my little donkey, where can you be?"

But Nasruddin was not one to give up. He stood up, dusted off his robe, and walked on, all the way to the river at the edge of the village.

And there — oh, there he was! There stood his little grey donkey, munching sweet green clover by the riverbank, munch, munch, munch, as happy as could be.

"There you are!" cried Nasruddin. "You naughty little donkey! I have searched the whole village for you!"

The donkey lifted his head, twitched his soft ears, and gave a happy little bray. "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

Nasruddin looked at his donkey, and then he looked at the sky, and then he began to laugh. He laughed and laughed until tears of joy ran down his cheeks.

"Oh, thank goodness I was not riding you," he chuckled, "or I would be lost too!"

The donkey nuzzled his hand, and Nasruddin patted his soft grey neck.

"Come along, my friend," he said. "Let us go home."

So they walked home together, side by side, as the sun began to set and the first stars twinkled in the soft evening sky. The donkey trotted along, clip-clop, clip-clop, and Nasruddin hummed a happy little tune.

And when they reached home, Nasruddin gave his donkey a warm supper and a soft bed of hay, and he whispered, "Sleep well, my little friend. Tomorrow we shall walk to the market together, just as always."

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A kind laugh can find what worry cannot.
