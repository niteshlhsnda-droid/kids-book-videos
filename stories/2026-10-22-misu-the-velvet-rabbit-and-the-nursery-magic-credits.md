# Credits — Misu the Velvet Rabbit and the Nursery Magic

- **Adapted from:** *The Velveteen Rabbit* (full title: *The Velveteen Rabbit, or How Toys Become Real*)
- **Author:** Margery Williams (1881–1944), first published 1922 (Doubleday & Co., New York)
- **Illustrator:** original 1922 illustrations by William Nicholson (not reused — all illustrations for this story were newly generated)
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11757 (Project Gutenberg eBook #11757, "The Velveteen Rabbit", released 2004, listed in Project Gutenberg's Children's Literature bookshelf)
- **License:** Public domain (published 1922; copyright expired — free to copy, adapt, and re-use)
- **Adaptation note:** Freely retold in ~430 words of simple, warm language for ages 4–7 — the scarlet-fever episode and the burning of the toys were softened into a gentle "toy box" setback; the nursery-fairy becoming-real ending is kept, with the lesson (being truly loved makes you real) shown through the story, never stated as a moral.
