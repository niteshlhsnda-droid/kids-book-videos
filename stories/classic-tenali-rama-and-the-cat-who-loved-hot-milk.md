# Tenali Rama and the Cat Who Loved Hot Milk

*A witty Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in the shiny palace of King Krishnadeva Raya, there lived a soft white cat named Moti.

Moti was the queen's very special cat. She had a pink nose, pink-pink paws, and a tail like a fluffy feather duster.

Every morning, a little silver bowl of milk was placed before her. But oh dear — Moti would only sip it when it was *piping* hot! Do you know how hot that is? Bubble-bubble, steam-rising hot!

The moment the milk was hot enough, Moti would go *pat-pat-pat* with her paw — and CLATTER! Over went the bowl. Milk splashed all over the marble floor.

Every single morning, the same thing happened. Splash! Splat! Slurp! — and then a great big milky puddle.

The servants wiped the floor. The queen sighed. The king tiptoed around the puddles in his golden slippers.

At last the king grew tired of it. "Tenali Rama!" he called out. "This cat must learn some manners — or else!"

Now, "or else" sounded a little scary. But Tenali Rama just folded his arms and smiled his clever smile. He would not scold Moti. He would not scare her. He would *watch* her.

So the next morning, Tenali Rama sat down on the cool floor and watched. Moti sniffed the rising steam. She went *pat-pat* with her paw. The tall bowl wobbled — wobbled — tipped!

"I see," said Tenali Rama, rubbing his chin. "The bowl is too tall, and the milk is too hot."

The morning after that, he watched again. *Pat-pat.* Wobble, wobble, tip! Milk everywhere.

Then Tenali Rama snapped his fingers. "Aha! I know what to do!"

He called the palace carpenter, a kind old man with sawdust on his cap. Together they made a brand-new bowl — wide and low, like a little pond. Its bottom was smooth and heavy, and no paw in the whole world could tip it over.

Tenali Rama poured in the milk — not piping hot this time, just *warm*, like a cozy blanket feels on a sleepy evening.

He set the bowl down and stepped back. Moti crept up on silent feet. Sniff-sniff. She gave the bowl one little *pat* — and it did not move! Not even a wobble!

Then she gave it another *pat* — still nothing!

Moti purred — *purr-purr-purr* — and lapped up every single drop. Not one splash. Not one puddle. The floor stayed dry and clean.

The king watched it all from his big chair. And then he laughed a big happy laugh. "Ha-ha! You solved with kindness what my orders could not!"

The queen stroked Moti's soft back and whispered, "Good girl, Moti."

That night, the palace was quiet. Moti curled up beside the queen's chair, warm and full, and purred herself to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Gentle cleverness solves what scolding cannot.*
