# The Dream Boat

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Mimi the mouse had a paper boat that she sailed in her bathtub every night, in her little house at the edge of Willowmere Wood. It was white with a blue sail. But tonight, something felt different — the boat glowed faintly.

Curious, Mimi touched the sail. It felt warm, like sunshine. "Are you magic?" she whispered.

The little boat bobbed, as if nodding, and a tiny ladder dropped from its side — just Mimi's size.

"Climb aboard, sailor," squeaked a tiny voice. It was a cricket wearing a captain's hat! "Tonight we sail to Dreamland."

Mimi felt excited and a little nervous, but the cricket smiled kindly, so Mimi climbed the tiny ladder. The boat was bigger inside than outside, with a cozy blanket and a pillow.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket captain.

The boat sailed out of the bathtub and floated into the air! Mimi gasped. Below her, her bedroom grew small; above her, the stars grew close.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket again.

They sailed past the moon, and the moon waved hello. They sailed past silver clouds that felt soft as cotton. Mimi felt drowsy — the gentle rocking was lovely.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket one last time.

They arrived at Dreamland — a shore made of pillows, where trees grew candy stars. Mimi stepped off the boat, so sleepy she could barely stand.

The cricket tucked her into the pillow sand. It pulled a cloud over her like a blanket. "Sweet dreams, sailor," it whispered. "I will sail you home by morning."

Mimi closed her eyes as the boat rocked gently beside her. She dreamed of sailing silver seas — the bravest little sailor in the world.

Dream of moonlit waves and starry skies.

Sleep now, little Mimi.

The end. 🌙
