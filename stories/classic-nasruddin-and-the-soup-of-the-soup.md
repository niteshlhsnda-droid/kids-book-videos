# Nasruddin and the Soup of the Soup

*A Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a little village, lived a wise and funny man named Nasruddin.

One day, a kind neighbour named Hassan brought Nasruddin a fine fat duck as a gift.

"Thank you, Hassan!" said Nasruddin. "Tonight I will make a delicious duck soup!"

And so he did. The soup was rich and warm and smelled wonderful. Nasruddin and his family ate it happily, and there was even some left over.

The next day, a man Nasruddin had never seen before knocked on his door.

"I am a friend of Hassan's friend," said the man. "I heard you make wonderful duck soup. May I taste some?"

Nasruddin laughed and shared the leftover soup with him. The man thanked him and went away smiling.

The next day, another stranger knocked. "I am a friend of the friend of Hassan's friend," he said. "May I taste the famous duck soup?"

Nasruddin shared what little soup was left, and the man went away smiling.

The next day, yet another stranger knocked. "I am a friend of the friend of the friend of Hassan's friend!" he said. "I have come for the duck soup!"

Nasruddin smiled a clever smile. He went to his kitchen, filled a bowl with hot water, and brought it to the stranger.

"Here is your soup," said Nasruddin proudly.

The stranger sipped it and frowned. "But this is only hot water!" he said. "Where is the duck?"

Nasruddin twinkled. "My friend, your soup is the soup of the soup of the soup of the duck! Hassan brought the duck, his friend tasted the soup, his friend tasted the soup of the soup — and you, my friend, get the soup of the soup of the soup!"

The stranger blinked. Then he laughed and laughed, until his sides ached.

"You are right, Nasruddin!" he said. "I came asking for something that was never really mine. Thank you for the laugh instead!"

And he went away smiling, with a story to tell all his friends.

Nasruddin sat down, chuckled to himself, and said, "A good laugh is better than a bowl of borrowed soup."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever joke can teach a lesson better than a scolding.*
