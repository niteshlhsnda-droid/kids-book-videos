# The Wise Goat and the Lion

*A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a green valley full of soft grass and sweet-smelling flowers, there lived a clever little goat named Bunty.

Bunty had a white beard that bobbed when he walked, two little horns that curled like the moon, and a brain that worked very, very fast.

Every morning, Bunty trotted down to the old stone well to drink cool, clear water. He loved the sound of the bucket going plop, and he loved the way the water sparkled in the sun.

One hot afternoon, Bunty was drinking at the well when he heard a deep, rumbly voice behind him.

"Well, well, well," said the voice. "What have we here? A tasty little goat!"

Bunty turned around slowly — and there stood a great big lion, with a golden mane and big, hungry eyes!

Bunty's heart went thump, thump, thump. His little legs wanted to run away. But Bunty knew that a lion can run faster than a goat. So he took a deep breath and thought, fast, fast, fast.

Then Bunty smiled, as if he had just heard the best joke in the world.

"Oh, hello, Lion!" he said in a cheerful voice. "You are just in time! But please — do not roar too loudly. There is a terrible monster living in this well!"

The lion blinked. "A monster? In the well?"

"Oh yes!" said Bunty, nodding very fast. "A great big monster with a huge, roaring mouth! It roars louder than any lion in the whole forest. It roared at me just this morning, and I nearly jumped out of my skin!"

The lion frowned. "Nobody roars louder than me," he growled. "Let me see this monster!"

Bunty stepped aside. "Go on, then," he said politely. "Lean over the well and say hello."

The lion leaned over the edge of the well and looked down. There, in the clear water below, he saw a great big lion's face looking right back up at him!

"Who is that?" roared the lion.

And from deep inside the well, a voice roared back — "ROAR!"

It was only the lion's own echo, of course. But the lion did not know that. He thought the monster was copying him!

"How dare you copy me!" roared the lion, louder than before.

"ROAR!" echoed the well, louder than ever.

The lion's mane stood on end. "Oh no!" he cried. "This monster is bigger and louder than me!" And he turned around and ran, ran, ran — all the way back to the forest, without once looking back.

Bunty waited until the lion was far, far away. Then he trotted back to the well, took a long, cool drink, and laughed a little goat laugh.

"Ha, ha, ha!" he chuckled. "A quick, clever thought is better than fast legs!"

That evening, Bunty told all his goat friends about the monster in the well. They laughed and laughed, and they drank the cool water together, safe and happy.

And from that day on, whenever any animal in the valley was scared, they remembered Bunty and said, "Stop. Think. A clever thought can save the day!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A quick, clever thought can save the day — you do not need to be the biggest or the strongest to be brave.*
