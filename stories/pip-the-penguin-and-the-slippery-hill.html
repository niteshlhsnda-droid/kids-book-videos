Pip the Penguin and the Slippery Hill

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Pip was a small fluffy penguin who lived where the snow sparkled. Every day, his friends slid down Snowberry Hill. Whee! They zoomed on their tummies. Swoosh! They spun in circles.

Pip watched from the top. He wanted to slide too — but the hill looked very tall, and very steep, and very slippery.

Pip felt scared. His flippers trembled.

"What if I go too fast?" he whispered. So Pip stayed at the top, watching everyone else have fun.

His friend Waddle the walrus pup waddled up.

"Why so glum, Pip?" asked Waddle.

"I am scared to slide," said Pip, his voice small. "I might tumble. I might bump."

Waddle smiled a whiskery smile. "Everyone feels scared sometimes," he said. "But I will stay right beside you. Hold my flipper, we will glide."

"Hold my flipper, we will glide."

Pip repeated it softly. "Hold my flipper, we will glide."

It became their brave words. "Hold my flipper, we will glide."

Waddle held out his flipper, and Pip held on tight. They stood at the top together.

"Ready?" asked Waddle. "One, two, three, glide!"

They pushed off gently. Whoosh! The snow rushed past. Pip felt the wind on his face. It was not scary at all — it was fun!

"Hold my flipper, we will glide!" they shouted.

They slid all the way down. Soft landing in fluffy snow. PLOP!

Pip laughed and laughed. He felt brave. He felt proud. "I did it!" he cheered.

Can you glide like Pip? Spread your arms like flippers. Now whoosh down, very slowly. Whee! That was fun!

Pip climbed back up the hill. This time he was not scared. He slid down again. And again. Waddle cheered every time.

Soon Pip was sliding on his own — flippers out, heart happy. The other penguins clapped. "Pip the glider!" they cried.

When the sky turned purple, it was time for bed. Pip snuggled into his snow nest while Waddle curled up beside him.

"Thank you for holding my flipper," whispered Pip.

"Anytime, brave glider," said Waddle.

Pip closed his eyes. He dreamed of gliding down soft snowy hills. Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh.

And he slept warm and cozy.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Holding a friend's flipper makes scary things feel safe.
