# Lola the Ladybird and the Little Lost Leaf

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a sunny garden full of flowers, there lived a little ladybird named Lola.

Lola had a round red back with seven shiny black spots, and two tiny wings that went whirr, whirr, whirr. She loved to fly from flower to flower, saying hello to the bees and the butterflies.

One breezy morning, Lola was flying home when she heard a tiny voice.

"Help! Oh, please help me!"

Lola fluttered down and landed softly on a smooth grey stone. And there, trembling on the stone, was a little green leaf.

The little leaf had blown away from her tree in the night wind, and now she did not know the way home.

"Hello, little leaf," said Lola kindly. "Do not worry. My name is Lola, and I will help you find your tree."

"Oh, thank you!" cried the little leaf. "I live on a tall tree, but I do not know which one. The wind spun me round and round, and now everything looks the same."

"Then we will look together," said Lola. "Climb onto my back, and hold on tight."

The little leaf climbed onto Lola's red back, and Lola spread her wings. Whirr, whirr, whirr! Up they flew into the soft blue sky.

First they flew to the apple tree, where pink blossoms floated down like snow.

"Is this your tree?" asked Lola.

The little leaf looked around. "No," she said. "My tree is much taller, and it does not smell of apples."

So they flew on to the willow tree, where long green branches swayed in the breeze.

"Is this your tree?" asked Lola.

The little leaf shook her head. "No," she said. "My tree stands very still and strong, and its branches reach right up to the sky."

The little leaf began to feel sad. "Oh, Lola," she whispered, "what if we never find my tree?"

"Do not give up, little friend," said Lola. "We will keep looking. A friend never gives up."

So they flew on, over the flower beds and past the pond.

And then, far across the garden, Lola saw it — a tall, tall oak tree, rustling softly in the breeze.

"Look!" cried Lola. "That must be your tree! It is the tallest tree in the whole garden!"

They flew closer and closer. The little leaf leaned forward and gasped. "Yes! Yes! That is my tree! I know those branches! I know that rustle!"

Lola landed on a low branch, and the little leaf hopped off and snuggled into her very own place. The leaves rustled with joy and welcomed her home.

"Thank you, Lola," whispered the little leaf. "You flew me all the way home. You are the kindest ladybird in the world."

Lola smiled. "You are home now, little leaf. Sleep well."

The sun was setting, and the sky turned soft pink and gold. Lola curled up in a cosy hollow beneath the great oak tree and closed her eyes.

Above her, the leaves rustled a soft lullaby, and Lola dreamed of flying through gardens full of flowers.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Helping a friend find home is the warmest journey.*
