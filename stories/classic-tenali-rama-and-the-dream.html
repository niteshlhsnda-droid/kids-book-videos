Tenali Rama and the Dream

A witty Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in the golden city of Vijayanagara, ruled the great King Krishnadevaraya.

In his court lived the cleverest, funniest poet of all — Tenali Rama. His quick wit could turn tears into laughter in a single breath.

One morning, the king came to court looking pale and worried.

"Last night I had a terrible dream," said the king. "I dreamed that all my teeth fell out — every single one! What does it mean? Tell me, my wise courtiers."

The courtiers looked at one another nervously. One old minister stepped forward, trembling.

"Your Majesty," he whispered, "it is a bad, bad sign. It means... it means all your loved ones will... will leave you."

The king's face turned even paler. "Oh no!" he cried. "Guards! Take this minister away! I do not want to hear such gloomy words!"

The guards led the poor minister out. The whole court went silent with fear.

Then Tenali Rama stepped forward, bowed low, and smiled.

"Your Majesty," said Tenali Rama cheerfully, "may I tell you what your dream truly means?"

The king nodded slowly. "Speak, Tenali. But speak only good news!"

Tenali Rama spread his arms wide. "Your Majesty, your dream is the happiest dream of all! It means you will live so long, and be so strong and healthy, that you will outlive everyone else in your family — you will be the last one smiling, with all your loved ones safe around you for many, many years!"

The king blinked. Then his worried face broke into the biggest smile.

"Ha! That is wonderful news!" laughed the king. "Tenali, you have turned my fear into joy! You are truly the cleverest in my court."

The king gave Tenali Rama a bag of gold coins and a garland of fresh jasmine flowers.

That evening, the old minister was brought back, and the king apologized with a kind heart. Everyone in the court laughed and clapped.

And little children in Vijayanagara still say: when you are scared of a bad dream, find the Tenali Rama in your heart — the clever voice that turns fear into hope.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Clever, kind words can turn fear into hope — look for the bright side.
