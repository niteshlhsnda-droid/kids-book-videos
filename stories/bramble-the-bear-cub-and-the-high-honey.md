# Bramble the Bear Cub and the High Honey

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Bramble was a small brown bear cub.
He lived in a den under the pine trees.
Bramble loved honey.
He loved it on berries.
He loved it on toast.
He loved it by the spoonful.
Yum yum yum!
One sunny morning, Bramble smelled something sweet.
He followed his nose.
Sniff sniff sniff.
High in an old oak tree hung a honeycomb.
Golden and dripping.
Bramble's tummy rumbled.
He reached up with his paw.
Too high!
He stood on his tiptoes.
Still too high!
He jumped.
Boing!
Missed!
Bramble felt frustrated.
He plopped down in the grass.
"I am too small," he grumbled.
He felt grumpy.
He felt stuck.

His friend Poppy the porcupine waddled by.
She saw Bramble's sad face.
"What is wrong, Bramble?" asked Poppy.
"The honey is too high," said Bramble.
"I cannot reach it."
"Not even with jumping."
Poppy looked at the honeycomb.
Then she looked at Bramble.
Then she smiled.
"We can solve this together," she said.
"Sweet things taste best when shared."
"Sweet things taste best when shared."
Bramble repeated it slowly.
"Sweet things taste best when shared."
It became their teamwork song.
"Sweet things taste best when shared."

Poppy had a clever plan.
"You stand on my back," she said.
"But gently, please."
Bramble climbed up carefully.
He was light as a feather.
Poppy stood tall.
Bramble stretched his paw.
Stretch, stretch, stretch.
Almost there!
He stood a tiny bit taller.
And plip!
His paw touched the honeycomb.
A golden drop landed on his paw.
Bramble licked it.
"Mmm!" he sighed.
He scooped some for Poppy too.
They sat in the grass.
They shared the honey.
Lick, smile, giggle.
It tasted extra sweet.
"Sweet things taste best when shared," they sang.
Bramble felt happy.
He felt thankful.

Can you reach up high like Bramble?
Stretch your arms up, up, up!
Now pretend to taste honey: mmm!
That afternoon, Bramble and Poppy played by the stream.
They splashed.
They laughed.
They told stories.
Bramble shared his berries with Poppy.
Poppy shared her pinecones with Bramble.
Sharing made everything sweeter.
The sun warmed their fur.
The bees buzzed a lazy song.

At dusk, Bramble curled in his den.
His tummy was full.
His heart was full too.
Poppy nestled in the moss nearby.
"Best honey day ever," whispered Bramble.
"Sweet dreams, honey friend," said Poppy.
Bramble closed his eyes.
He dreamed of golden honey and kind friends.
Drip, drop, yum.
And he slept soundly all night.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Teamwork and sharing make sweet things even sweeter.*
