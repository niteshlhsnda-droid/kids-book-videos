# Nasruddin Counts the Donkeys

*A funny Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a sunny little town, there lived a kind and funny man named Nasruddin.

Nasruddin had a big smile, a twinkly beard, and a donkey called Bablu, who was the gentlest donkey in the whole town. Bablu had soft ears, a swishy tail, and a very loud "Hee-haw!" that always made the children giggle.

One morning, Nasruddin had to take ten donkeys to the market. There was Bablu, of course, and nine other donkeys besides — ten donkeys in all!

Nasruddin climbed onto Bablu's back. "Come on, my friends!" he called. "Off to the market we go!"

The donkeys trotted along the dusty road — clip-clop, clip-clop — with their baskets swinging from side to side. Nasruddin sang a little song, and the donkeys twitched their ears in time.

After a while, Nasruddin thought, "I had better count my donkeys, to make sure none of them has wandered off."

So he sat up tall on Bablu's back and counted out loud. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine — "

Nasruddin stopped. His smile disappeared. "Nine?" he said. "But there should be ten! Oh no! One of my donkeys is lost!"

He counted again, very carefully. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine. Only nine!"

"Oh, my poor lost donkey!" cried Nasruddin. "Where can he be? Did he stop to eat grass? Did he wander into the fields?"

Nasruddin climbed down from Bablu's back and looked behind him. He looked in front of him. He looked under a tree. He looked behind a bush. No lost donkey anywhere!

Just then, his friend Salim came walking down the road. "Hello, Nasruddin!" he said. "Why do you look so worried?"

"Oh, Salim!" said Nasruddin. "I started with ten donkeys, and now I can only count nine! One of them is lost!"

Salim looked at the donkeys. He counted them, one by one, pointing with his finger. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten," he said. "Nasruddin, there are ten donkeys right here!"

"Ten?" said Nasruddin, scratching his beard. "But I counted only nine!"

Salim laughed a big, warm laugh. "That is because you were sitting on the tenth one, my friend! You forgot to count Bablu — the donkey you were riding!"

Nasruddin looked down at Bablu. Bablu looked up at Nasruddin and gave a happy little "Hee-haw!"

Nasruddin burst out laughing. "Ha, ha, ha! I was counting all the donkeys except the one under me!"

Salim laughed too, and the donkeys twitched their ears, as if they were laughing as well.

"Thank you, Salim!" said Nasruddin, climbing back onto Bablu. "You have saved the day. Now — one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten! All ten donkeys, safe and sound!"

And off they went to the market — clip-clop, clip-clop — with Nasruddin singing his little song louder than ever.

That night, Nasruddin told the story to all his friends, and they laughed and laughed. And whenever anyone in the town started counting, they always remembered to count the one they were sitting on too!

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sometimes the thing you are looking for is right under you — so count carefully, and laugh at your own mistakes.*
