The Dog and the Bone

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, in a little village beside a green hill, there lived a happy dog named Bruno.

Bruno was a brown dog with floppy ears and a waggy tail. He loved to run in the fields, and most of all, he loved his dinner.

One sunny morning, Bruno's friend, the kind baker, gave him a wonderful gift. It was a big, juicy bone, fresh from the oven, with tasty meat still on it.

"Oh, thank you!" barked Bruno. "What a lovely bone! I will enjoy it slowly, bite by bite."

Bruno picked up the bone and trotted off down the lane. His tail wagged and wagged. He was the happiest dog in the whole village.

Soon, Bruno came to a little wooden bridge over a sparkling stream. The water below was clear and still, like a shiny mirror.

Bruno trotted onto the bridge, clip-clop, clip-clop, with his bone held high. But then he stopped and looked down into the water — and what did he see? He saw another dog!

"Who is that dog?" thought Bruno. "And why does he have a bone in his mouth too?"

Bruno looked again. The dog in the water was just as brown as he was, with floppy ears. And his bone looked even bigger and juicier than Bruno's!

"That bone looks tastier than mine," thought Bruno. "I want that bone too!"

Bruno forgot all about his own lovely bone. All he could think about was the other dog's bigger bone.

"I will bark at him," said Bruno to himself. "I will bark very loudly, and he will drop his bone. Then I will have two bones!"

So Bruno opened his mouth wide to bark — "WOOF!" But his own bone slipped out — and splash! It fell into the stream below!

Bruno watched sadly as his bone sank down, down, down into the clear water. The dog in the water had vanished too, because it had only been Bruno's own reflection all along!

"Oh no!" whimpered Bruno. "My bone is gone! And there was never another dog at all! That was just me, looking back at myself!"

Bruno sat down on the bridge and felt very, very sad. His tummy rumbled, and his tail stopped wagging.

Just then, his friends came along the lane: Tilly the tabby cat and Sam the little squirrel. "Hello, Bruno!" called Tilly. "Why do you look so sad?"

Bruno told them the whole story, and Tilly and Sam listened kindly. Then they smiled.

"Do not be sad, Bruno," said Sam. "We have brought our supper to share with you. Come and eat with us!"

So Bruno went home with his friends and sat under the big oak tree. Tilly shared her fish, Sam shared his nuts and berries, and Bruno shared his smiles.

Bruno ate his supper slowly and felt warm and happy inside. "I had a lovely bone," he said, "and I should have been happy with it. From now on, I will be thankful for what I have."

And from that day on, whenever Bruno found a tasty treat, he shared it with Tilly and Sam, and they shared with him too.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Be happy with what you have, and share it with your friends.
