# The Sparrow and the Monkey

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One rainy morning, dark clouds rolled over the green forest, and cool rain began to patter on the leaves. A little sparrow named Chutki sat snug in her round nest, tucked under a broad leaf that kept every drop away.

Below her, on a low branch, sat Manku the monkey. He had no nest at all. The rain ran down his fur, and he shivered in the cold wind.

"Br-r-r!" said Manku, hugging his knees. "I wish I had a cosy house like yours, little sparrow."

Chutki peeped out of her nest. "You can build one, Manku!" she chirped. "I will show you how. First, gather soft leaves and thin twigs."

Manku had never built anything before. He gathered leaves in a great heap, but when he tried to pile them up, they slid right off the branch and fluttered to the ground.

"This is too hard!" grumbled Manku. "I will just sit here and get wet."

"Do not give up," said Chutki kindly. "Watch me. Weave one leaf over, and one leaf under, like this — over, under, over, under."

Manku watched her little beak weave the leaves together. Slowly, carefully, he tried again. Over, under, over, under. This time the leaves held together like a little roof!

"Look!" laughed Manku. "My roof is holding! No rain is coming through!"

Chutki clapped her wings. "You did it! Now add a few twigs around the sides, and your house will be snug even in the wind."

Manku worked all afternoon, weaving and tucking, until he had a small leafy shelter of his very own. When the evening rain came pattering down again, he sat dry and happy under his new roof.

"Thank you, Chutki," said Manku. "You taught me something wonderful. Will you share my berries for supper?"

"I would love to," chirped Chutki, and she hopped down to share the sweetest berries in the whole forest.

From that day on, whenever the rain clouds gathered, Manku waved to Chutki from his leafy house, and Chutki sang a happy song from her nest. And both of them stayed warm and dry, side by side.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A kind teacher and a little patience can build a warm home for anyone.*
