- **Original title:** "The Star Money" (German: "Die Sterntaler")
- **Author:** Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm — *Children's and Household Tales*, first published 1812
- **Illustrator:** none (all illustrations generated fresh for this adaptation)

- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (~420 words). The unnamed orphan child of the original is named **Mira**; the gifts of bread, cap, bodice, skirt, and shirt, the dark forest journey, and the falling stars that turn to golden coins follow the original tale's beats, rewritten fully in original wording with a gentle goodnight closing ("Sleep now, little Mira.").
