# Nasruddin and the Cat

*A timeless Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a sunny town by the sea lived Mullah Nasruddin, a funny, kind man with a big turban and an even bigger laugh. Everyone loved Nasruddin, because he always had a joke — and a wise word tucked inside the joke.

One morning, Nasruddin's wife, Fatima, said, "Nasruddin, today I will cook a delicious dinner. Please go to the market and buy a nice piece of meat for our pot."

"At once!" said Nasruddin, and off he went, humming a happy tune.

At the market, he bought a fine, fresh piece of meat — three whole kilos of it! He carried it home carefully and gave it to Fatima.

"What beautiful meat!" said Fatima. "I will cook it slowly all afternoon, and tonight we will have a feast!"

But while Fatima was busy in the kitchen, their plump little cat, Mimi, crept in through the window. Mimi sniffed the meat… and nibbled… and nibbled… and nibbled — until, giggle by giggle, she had eaten every last bit of it!

When Fatima came back, the plate was empty, and Mimi sat beside it, washing her whiskers with a very pleased look on her face.

"Oh, Mimi!" cried Fatima. "You ate all our dinner!"

Just then, Nasruddin came home. "Where is our feast?" he asked.

Fatima pointed at Mimi. "The cat ate it all!"

Nasruddin stroked his beard and thought. Then he picked up Mimi — gently, for he loved her — and put her on the weighing scale.

The scale showed exactly three kilos.

"Aha!" said Nasruddin, with a twinkle in his eye. "Now, my friends, here is a puzzle. If this is the cat, where is the meat? And if this is the meat, where is the cat?"

Fatima stared at the scale. Then she burst out laughing. "Oh, Nasruddin! You and your puzzles!"

Mimi meowed, as if to say, "I am the cat, and the meat is… well, inside the cat!"

Nasruddin laughed too, and he hugged Fatima. "Never mind, my dear. The meat is gone, but we still have each other — and a very full, very happy cat!"

So instead of a meat feast, they had a bread-and-cheese feast, with sweet dates and cool lemonade. Mimi curled up beside them, purring loudly, and Nasruddin told jokes until the stars came out.

And from that day on, whenever anyone in town lost something in a funny way, they would smile and say, "If this is the cat, where is the meat?"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When a little mishap happens, a laugh and a kind heart turn it into a happy memory.*
