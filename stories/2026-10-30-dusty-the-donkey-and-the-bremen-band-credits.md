# Credits — "Dusty the Donkey and the Bremen Band"

- **Original title:** "The Town Musicians of Bremen" ("Die Bremer Stadtmusikanten")
- **Author:** Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm (Brothers Grimm), first published 1819
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations for this version were freshly generated in watercolor style for the kids-book-videos library.
- **Source:** Grimms' Fairy Tales (transl. Edgar Taylor and Marian Edwardes) — Project Gutenberg eBook #2591, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2591 — contains "The Bremen Town-Musicians"
- **License:** Public domain
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a gentle bedtime story: the four animals are given names (Dusty, Bruno, Cleo, Rory), the robbers' fright is kept playful rather than scary, and the story closes with the friends finding a home together instead of travelling on to Bremen.
