# Credits — "Teju and the Friendly Shadow"

- **Original title:** "Teju's Shadow"
- **Author:** Sope Martins
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated in watercolor style for this project.
- **Source URL:** https://bookdash.org/books/ (Book Dash library; "Teju's Shadow", Book Dash Johannesburg, 26 October 2019, ISBN 978-1-928497-89-9)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 (all Book Dash books are published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (~445 words, bedtime tone). Teju's worry about the "super secret spy" following him, Mama's explanation that it is his shadow, the night-time fright when the shadow vanishes in the dark, the shadow-puppet comfort, and the next day's dancing shadow are kept from the original; the ending is softened into a cozy goodnight with a night light glowing beside his bed — the lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
