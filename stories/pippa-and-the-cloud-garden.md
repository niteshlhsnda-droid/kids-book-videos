# Pippa and the Cloud Garden

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On a warm, sleepy night, when the moon was big and round, a little grey mouse named Pippa could not fall asleep.

She tossed and turned in her leaf-blanket. Then she sighed a tiny mouse sigh.

"I am too warm to sleep," she whispered, "and too awake."

So she tiptoed out of her burrow — and stopped.

Behind her burrow, where there had never been anything before, stood a ladder made of moonbeams. It shimmered like silver ribbon, and it went up, up, up into the dark night sky.

Pippa's whiskers wiggled. "A ladder? For me?"

She put one small paw on the bottom rung. It felt cool and tingly, like stepping into a puddle of moonlight.

Up she climbed. Up, up, past the sleepy rooftops, past the dozing birds. The stars winked at her as she climbed. The air grew soft and pillowy, until at last — poof! — her nose bumped into a cloud. It was softer than any pillow she had ever touched.

It was not an ordinary cloud. Rows and rows of flowers grew there, their petals glowing like tiny lanterns. Dew sparkled on the silver leaves. And the whole garden smelled of rain and honey.

"Welcome," yawned a voice. "Careful of the moonflowers."

There sat an old cloud-gardener among the flowers, with a long white beard that trailed behind him like a soft cloud tail. His name was Nimbus, and he was very, very sleepy. He snored a little, then woke himself up with a snort.

"Are you a gardener in the sky?" Pippa whispered.

"Mmm," said Nimbus, his eyes half-closed. "Someone has to tuck the stars to bed."

He reached into his pocket and pulled out something small and bright. "Here, little mouse. A star-seed. Plant it somewhere soft, and it will grow."

Pippa held the star-seed in her paws. It was warm, and it twinkled.

She found a puff of cloud and pressed the seed into it, the way you tuck someone under a blanket. Then she caught one dewdrop on a leaf — just one — and let it drip onto the seed. Plip.

Then, in her tiniest voice, she sang the littlest song she knew:

"Hush now, little seed, sleep deep,
Moon is watching, soft and low,
Grow a little dream for me…"

Slowly, slowly, a green shoot curled up. Then a bud. Then — pop! — a star-flower bloomed, all gold and pink. And then the most wonderful thing happened — it hummed Pippa's song right back to her. A soft, sleepy hum, like a lullaby with no words.

Pippa's eyelids grew heavy. She curled up beside the flower, and the humming wrapped around her like a warm blanket. She fell fast asleep.

When she woke, she was back in her own burrow, and dawn was peeking through the door. Nimbus must have carried her gently down the ladder while she slept. In her paw lay one glowing petal — soft and warm, like a tiny night-light.

It was her gift from the cloud garden.

That night, and every night after, Pippa placed the petal beside her burrow door. Its gentle glow lit her way to sleep, and she dreamed of cloud-flowers swaying in the starlight.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Small kindnesses, planted with love, grow into bright things.*
