# Dusty the Donkey and the Bremen Band

In a little valley beside a turning mill wheel lived an old grey donkey named Dusty. His legs were slow and tired from carrying sacks of flour, but every evening, when the mill fell quiet, he stood by the gate and hummed a low, happy tune, for he loved music above all things.

One golden morning, Dusty decided it was time for a new life. "I will go to Bremen," he said, "and sing in the town band." So he shook the flour dust from his ears and set off down the lane, humming as he went.

Soon he met Bruno, a big brown dog whose legs were stiff and whose bark had grown soft. "To Bremen, to sing in the town band," said Dusty. "Come with me — a band needs a fine deep voice like yours." Bruno wagged his tail and trotted along.

Further on, they met Cleo, a grey cat on a garden wall, whose purr had grown quiet because nobody played with her anymore. "Come to Bremen with us," said Dusty. "A band needs a soft, silvery voice like yours." Cleo purred louder and padded after them.

Last of all came Rory, a red rooster. "I sing louder than anyone in the valley," he crowed, "but nobody wants to hear me at dawn anymore." "Then come and sing with us," said Bruno. "A band needs a bright morning voice like yours." Rory flapped his wings and joined them.

They walked all day, over hills and through meadows, until the sun went down and the forest grew dark. Deep among the trees, a warm light glowed in a cottage window. "Let us rest there," whispered Dusty.

Dusty peeped through the window — and inside sat four gruff robbers, eating supper. "We cannot stay here while they are inside," whispered Cleo. But Dusty had an idea. "We will sing," he said, "all together, as loud and as grand as we can."

So Dusty stood at the bottom, Bruno climbed onto his back, Cleo onto Bruno, and Rory fluttered to the very top. At Dusty's nod, they all sang at once — Dusty brayed, Bruno barked, Cleo yowled, and Rory crowed, the loudest, wildest song the forest had ever heard!

The robbers leapt up in fright. "A monster is singing at the door!" they cried, and ran off into the night, dropping their supper as they went. Laughing, the four friends tiptoed inside, where the fire was warm.

They ate a cosy supper by the fire and sang one soft song together. In the morning they smiled: Bremen was still far away, but they had already found what they sought — a home, and friends to sing with.

Sleep now, little Dusty.
