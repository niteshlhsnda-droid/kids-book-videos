The Farmer and His Sons

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a village with green fields and a giggling little stream, there lived a farmer named Arjun.

Arjun had a big vineyard, full of grapevines. And he had three sons — Aarav, Vihaan, and Ayaan.

But oh dear! The three brothers argued all day long.

"I should carry the basket!" said Aarav.

"No, I should!" said Vihaan.

"I am the strongest!" said Ayaan. "I should carry everything!"

Their father Arjun watched them and shook his head. "My sons," he said, "if you argue all the time, how will you ever work together?"

One evening, Arjun called his three sons to sit with him under the big mango tree.

"My sons," said Arjun, "I am growing old, and my back is tired. I cannot dig the vineyard anymore. But I have a secret for you. A long time ago, I buried a treasure in the vineyard. A treasure of gold! If you dig, you will find it."

The brothers' eyes went wide. "A treasure!" they cried. "Of gold!"

"But you must dig together," said Arjun, "or you will never find it."

The very next morning, Aarav, Vihaan, and Ayaan picked up their spades and went to the vineyard.

"I will dig here!" said Aarav.

"I will dig there!" said Vihaan.

"I will dig everywhere!" said Ayaan.

And they dug. And they dug. They dug in the morning. They dug in the afternoon. They dug until the sun went down and the stars came out.

They dug the whole vineyard, from one end to the other. But they found no gold. Not a single shiny coin!

"There is no treasure!" grumbled Vihaan, wiping his forehead.

"We dug everywhere!" said Ayaan, sitting down with a bump.

"Wait," said Aarav, looking around. "Look at our vineyard!"

The brothers looked. And oh, what a sight! The soft, dug-up earth was perfect for the grapevines. The vines grew strong and green. And when autumn came, the vines were heavy with the biggest, juiciest grapes anyone had ever seen!

The brothers picked the grapes together. They carried the baskets together. They sold the grapes at the market together. And they came home with pockets full of silver coins — and big smiles on their faces.

Father Arjun was waiting under the mango tree. "Well, my sons?" he asked. "Did you find the treasure?"

Aarav laughed. "Father, we found no gold in the ground. But the vineyard gave us more grapes than ever before!"

Vihaan grinned. "And we worked together — all three of us — without a single argument!"

Ayaan nodded. "The treasure was not gold. The treasure was the digging, and the working together!"

Arjun smiled his warmest smile. "Now you understand," he said. "Gold in the ground is found once. But a family that works together finds its treasure again and again, every single year."

And from that day on, the three brothers never argued about who should carry the basket. They carried it together — laughing, singing, and sharing the sweet grapes of their vineyard.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The real treasure is working together — it grows again every year.
