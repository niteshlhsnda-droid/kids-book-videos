# Maya and the Starry Umbrella

*A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One rainy afternoon, Maya was playing in her grandmother's attic. Grandma June's attic was full of wonderful things: old trunks, soft blankets, and boxes tied with string.

In the very back corner, behind a big trunk, Maya found something special. It was an umbrella — but not an ordinary umbrella. It was deep blue, like the night sky, and it was covered in tiny golden stars.

"Grandma! Look what I found!" cried Maya, carrying the umbrella downstairs.

Grandma June smiled. "Ah, the starry umbrella," she said. "It belonged to my grandmother, long ago. They say it only opens for someone with a story to tell."

That night, when the rain pattered on the windows, Maya opened the umbrella inside her bedroom. Pop!

As the umbrella opened, the golden stars on it began to twinkle. One by one, they glowed brighter and brighter, until the whole bedroom ceiling looked like the night sky.

"Wow!" whispered Maya. Her little brother Arjun ran in, rubbing his eyes. "Maya, why is your room full of stars?"

"It is the starry umbrella!" said Maya. "Come and sit under it with me."

Arjun sat beside her under the umbrella. The stars twinkled above them, and Maya began to tell a story.

"See that bright star there?" she said, pointing. "That is the Brave Little Star, who was afraid of the dark — until she learned that her light could help other stars find their way home."

Arjun pointed to another star. "And that one?"

"That is the Sleepy Star," said Maya, "who yawns so wide that all the other stars yawn too, and then they all fall asleep, cosy and happy."

Maya told star story after star story, and with every story, the stars twinkled a little brighter. Arjun's eyes grew heavy. He yawned a big, sleepy yawn.

"One more story?" he mumbled.

"Just one more," said Maya softly. "This one is about a little girl who found a starry umbrella, and shared its light with her little brother, so he would never be afraid of the dark."

Arjun smiled in his sleep. Maya gently closed the umbrella. The stars faded, one by one, like fireflies saying goodnight.

Maya tucked Arjun into bed, put the starry umbrella beside her own bed, and whispered, "Goodnight, stars. Thank you for the stories."

And that night, Maya dreamed of umbrellas full of stars, and of all the stories still waiting to be told.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Stories shared under one umbrella light up the whole night — and the best stories are the ones you tell together.*
