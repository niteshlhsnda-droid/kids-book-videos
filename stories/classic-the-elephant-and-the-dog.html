The Elephant and the Dog

A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in a palace of golden domes, there lived a gentle elephant named Golu. He was the king's own elephant, and his stable was grand and wide, with hay as soft as a cloud. Every morning, the stable boys polished his great grey sides until they shone.

But Golu often felt lonely in the big stable. "Have you ever felt lonely in a big, empty room?" The tall pillars echoed back his footsteps, and his trumpet sounded too loud in all that quiet. A grand stable is no fun when there is no one to share it with.

One sunny morning, a tiny stray dog trotted in — snip-snip went his little paws on the stone floor. His name was Biscuit, and his tail wagged wickety-wackety. He looked up, up, up at the great elephant and licked his own nose. Slurp!

Golu lowered his long trunk, soft as a slide, and Biscuit climbed right up! They shared Golu's breakfast — crunchy apples and soft warm bread. Crunch, munch! From that very day, the two of them were the best of friends.

They did everything together. Biscuit rode on Golu's head in the morning parade — trumpety-toot! At nap time, Biscuit curled up on Golu's big ear, warm as a blanket. "Wouldn't you like a friend to nap with?"

In the afternoons, they played in the palace garden. Golu sprayed cool water from his trunk — splish-splash! — and Biscuit chased the dancing drops, barking with glee. Woof! Woof! The flowers nodded their heads, as if they were laughing too.

Then one day, a man from a far, far village came and took Biscuit away with him. Golu stood by the stable door and waited. He waited till the sun went down. But Biscuit did not come.

Golu grew sad. He would not eat his apples. He would not drink his water. His big ears drooped like two sleepy leaves, and he would not lift his trunk at all.

The worried king came to see him. "Why is my elephant so sad?" he asked. A stable boy bowed low and said, "Your Majesty, his little dog friend has been taken to a far village." At once, the king sent his fastest rider — clip-clop, clip-clop went the horse's hooves down the palace road!

The rider found Biscuit and brought him home. Biscuit ran and ran — pitter-patter, pitter-patter — and jumped straight into Golu's waiting trunk! Golu lifted him high in the air and trumpeted with joy — PAAN! It was the happiest sound in the whole wide palace.

That evening, they shared the biggest meal ever — apples, bread, and one extra bowl of warm milk. Slurp, slurp! And when the round moon rose over the golden domes, they curled up together — Biscuit on Golu's soft ear, warm and snug. Never again would Golu feel lonely in his grand stable.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: True friends make every place feel like home.
