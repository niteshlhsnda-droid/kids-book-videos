# Birbal and the King's Dream

*A witty Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long ago, in a grand palace in India, lived a wise king named Akbar and his clever friend Birbal.

One morning, King Akbar came to his court looking very worried.

"Birbal," he said, "last night I had a strange dream. I dreamed that all my teeth fell out — every single one — except one last tooth!"

The courtiers gasped. "Oh! Oh!" they said. "What a terrible dream!"

The king called his court astrologer, a man who read the stars.

"Tell me, what does my dream mean?" asked the king.

The astrologer bowed low and said, "Your Majesty, I am sorry to say it, but this dream means sad news. It means you will lose everyone you love, one by one, until only you are left."

The king's face fell. The whole court went quiet.

But Birbal stepped forward and smiled.

"Your Majesty," said Birbal, "may I tell you what your dream means?"

"Please do, Birbal," said the king.

Birbal said, "Your dream means something wonderful! It means you will live a very, very long life — longer than all of us! You will be the last tooth standing, strong and smiling, for many, many years!"

The king stared at Birbal. Then he burst out laughing!

"Ha! Ha! Ha!" laughed the king. "Birbal, you have told me the very same thing as the astrologer — but you said it in a way that makes my heart sing instead of sink!"

The whole court laughed too. Even the astrologer smiled and nodded.

"Birbal," said the king, "you are right. The same news can be said in two ways — one that frightens, and one that comforts. From today, I want my court to speak the comforting way!"

And to celebrate, the king gave everyone star-shaped cookies, sweet and crunchy.

Birbal took a cookie and said, "Your Majesty, may your smile last as long as your last tooth!"

And the king laughed again — and his laugh was the happiest sound in the whole palace.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The same truth can be said two ways — choose the kind way.*
