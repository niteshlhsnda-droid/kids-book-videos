The Bear and the Two Travellers

A gentle Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Milo and Tia were the best of friends.

One sunny morning, they set off through the whispering forest to sell sweet berries at the village market. Milo carried the basket. Tia skipped beside him, singing a berry-picking song.

"We will share everything we earn," said Milo. "Half for you, half for me."

"Best friends share," Tia sang back.

Deeper and deeper into the forest they walked. The trees grew tall. The path grew quiet.

Then — crunch, crunch, CRUNCH — from behind the bushes came a great big bear!

Tia gasped. Quick as a squirrel, she scrambled up the nearest tree and hid among the leaves.

Poor Milo had no time to climb. So he did the one thing he could think of — he lay down very still on the soft moss, closed his eyes, and pretended to be fast asleep.

The bear lumbered closer. Sniff, sniff, sniff went her big nose, all around Milo's face.

Milo did not move. He did not even breathe loudly. He just lay there, still as a sleeping stone.

The bear tilted her head. "Hmm," she seemed to say. "Just a little boy taking a nap. I must not wake him."

And with a soft huff, the great big bear turned around and ambled back into the forest, her paws going pitter-pat on the pine needles.

Tia slid down from the tree, her cheeks pink. "Milo! Are you all right?"

Milo sat up and grinned. "The bear thought I was napping!"

Tia hugged him tight. "I am sorry I climbed up without you," she whispered. "That was not very best-friend-ish of me."

Milo hugged her back. "You were scared. Being scared is okay. And look — the bear has gone, the berries are safe, and we are still best friends."

Then Tia laughed. "Tell me — when the bear sniffed your nose, did she whisper a secret to you?"

Milo tapped his chin and smiled. "She whispered this: stay close to friends who stay close to you."

And hand in hand, Milo and Tia walked on to the market, shared their berries, shared their coins — half for you, half for me — and shared the story of the bear all the way home.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Best friends stay close — and forgive quickly when fear gets in the way.
