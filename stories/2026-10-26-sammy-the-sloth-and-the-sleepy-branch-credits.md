# Credits — "Sammy the Sloth and the Sleepy Branch"

- **Original title:** Sleepy Mr Sloth
- **Author:** Paul Kennedy
- **Illustrator:** Graham Paterson (original book art NOT reused — fresh watercolor illustrations were generated for this adaptation)
- **Designer:** Nick Mulgrew
- **Source:** Book Dash (https://bookdash.org) — the first book ever published by Book Dash, 2014
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) — all Book Dash books are published under CC BY 4.0
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a 10-beat bedtime story: Sammy the sloth tries several jungle branches (a giraffe's dinner branch, a noisy parrot branch, a splashy waterfall branch, a shaky elephant-path branch) before a tiny hummingbird leads him to a quiet, leaf-sheltered branch of his own. Fresh illustrations, narration, and original lullaby music were created for this version; no original artwork was reused.
