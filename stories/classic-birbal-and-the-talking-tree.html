Birbal and the Talking Tree

A witty Akbar–Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in the city of Agra, there lived two friends, Ramesh and Suresh. One day they dug a hole under a big banyan tree and buried a pot full of gold coins there, to keep it safe.

"We will share it when we need it," they promised each other.

But one night, Suresh crept back alone, dug up the pot, and took all the gold coins home. The next morning, he went to Ramesh with a sad face and said, "Oh no! Somebody has stolen our gold!"

Ramesh was very upset. "Let us go to Emperor Akbar," he said. "Birbal will help us find the truth."

So they went to the palace, and Birbal listened carefully to both friends. Then he said, "Let us go to the banyan tree itself. The tree saw everything. Perhaps the tree will tell us what happened."

Suresh turned pale. "A tree cannot talk!" he squeaked.

"Let us see," said Birbal with a smile.

That evening, Birbal hid inside the hollow trunk of the banyan tree before the friends arrived. When Ramesh and Suresh stood beneath its branches, Birbal called out in a deep, creaky voice, "I am the banyan tree! I saw who dug up the gold!"

Suresh trembled. "Please, great tree, do not tell!" he cried. "It was me! I took the coins! I am sorry!"

Then Birbal stepped out from behind the trunk, smiling. "The tree did not speak, Suresh. I did. But your own words have told the truth."

Suresh hung his head. "I was greedy, and I broke my promise to my friend. Ramesh, I am truly sorry. Here are all your coins, every single one."

Ramesh hugged his friend. "Thank you for telling the truth. Friends are worth much more than gold."

Emperor Akbar laughed and clapped. "Well done, Birbal! Your clever trick found the truth without hurting anyone."

And Suresh kept his promise from then on. He and Ramesh shared everything fairly, and whenever they passed the big banyan tree, they waved at it and said, "Thank you, talking tree!"

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Telling the truth takes courage — and a true friend will always forgive you.
