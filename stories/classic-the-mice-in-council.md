# The Mice in Council

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cozy farmhouse kitchen lived a great family of mice. There was Grandma Molly Mouse, Papa Max Mouse, and a whole crowd of little mice with twitchy whiskers and bright eyes.

Every night, when the house was quiet, the mice tiptoed out of their little hole in the wall to nibble crumbs of cheese and crusts of bread.

But there was one problem: the farmhouse cat, Cleo.

Cleo was soft and grey, and she walked on the quietest paws in the whole house. Whenever the mice came out for supper, they had to listen very, very carefully — paws, paws, paws — in case Cleo was creeping near.

"We need a plan," said Grandma Molly one night, tapping her walking stick. "Let us hold a council, and every mouse shall have a say."

So all the mice gathered in a circle behind the flour sack, and one by one they shared their ideas.

"We could wear soft slippers," said little Timmy Mouse, "so Cleo cannot hear us either!"

"We could take turns keeping watch," said Tina Mouse. "One mouse watches, while the others nibble."

Then young Milo Mouse stood up on a thimble and squeaked, "I have it! Let us hang a tiny bell around Cleo's neck. Then, wherever she goes, we will hear ding-ding-ding, and we will know she is coming!"

All the mice cheered and clapped their tiny paws. "What a clever plan!" they squeaked. "Ding-ding-ding!"

Then old Grandpa Gus Mouse cleared his throat. "A very fine plan indeed," he said with a smile. "But tell me, my dears — who will hang the bell on the cat?"

Silence. Every mouse looked at every other mouse. Timmy hid behind the flour sack. Tina wiggled her whiskers. Milo slid quietly down from his thimble.

Grandma Molly chuckled warmly. "A plan is only good if someone can do it kindly and safely," she said. "So let us choose a plan we can all do together."

And that is what they did. From that night on, the mice took turns keeping watch, one brave little watcher at the kitchen door, while the others nibbled their supper in peace. And whenever Cleo padded softly by, the watcher would whisper, "Cat coming!" — and all the mice would scamper home, giggling, to their cozy hole in the wall.

No bells were needed after all. Just good friends, good listening, and a plan everyone could share.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A good plan is one that everyone can carry out together.*
