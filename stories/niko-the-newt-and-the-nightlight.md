# Niko the Newt and the Nightlight

*A sweet original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet little pond, under a big green lily leaf, there lived a tiny newt named Niko.

Niko was small and brown, with a soft orange tummy. He loved to swim. He loved to munch tiny water plants. He loved to play hide-and-seek with the tadpoles.

But there was one thing Niko did not love: the dark.

Every night, when the sun went down and the pond grew dark, Niko hid under his lily leaf and shivered.

"It is so dark," he whispered. "So very, very dark."

One night, a little firefly fluttered down and landed on Niko's lily leaf. Her light blinked on and off — blink, blink, blink — soft and golden.

"Hello, little newt," said the firefly. "Why are you hiding?"

"I am afraid of the dark," said Niko, very quietly.

The firefly smiled. "The dark is not scary," she said. "Listen!"

Niko listened. The frogs were singing, "Croak, croak, croak!" The crickets were humming, "Chirp, chirp, chirp!" The water lapped softly against the lily pads — lap, lap, lap.

"It sounds like a lullaby," whispered Niko.

"It is a lullaby," said the firefly. "And I have something for you." She gave Niko a tiny, smooth pebble. It glowed with a soft, golden light.

"This is a nightlight pebble," she said. "Keep it beside you, and its glow will keep you brave."

Niko held the pebble close. Its light was soft and warm, like a tiny moon. And do you know what? Niko did not feel afraid anymore.

That night, Niko swam around the pond with his glowing pebble. He counted the fireflies' blinks — one blink, two blinks, three blinks — and each blink was like a little star saying hello.

Then he saw a tiny tadpole, hiding under a leaf and shivering.

"Are you afraid of the dark too?" asked Niko.

"Yes," whispered the tadpole.

"Then let us share my nightlight," said Niko. He set the glowing pebble between them, and its soft light wrapped around them both like a warm blanket.

The tadpole smiled. "It is not scary now," he said. "Not with your light."

From that night on, Niko and the tadpole slept side by side under the lily leaf, the little pebble glowing softly beside them. And whenever a new friend was afraid of the dark, Niko shared his light.

That night, Niko curled up under his lily leaf, the pebble glowing like a tiny moon. He listened to the frogs sing and the water lap, and he counted the fireflies — one, two, three — until his eyes grew heavy.

And under the twinkling stars, he fell fast asleep, brave and warm.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little light chases away a big fear — and brave hearts share their glow.*
