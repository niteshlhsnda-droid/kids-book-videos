# The Dog and His Reflection

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On a soft, sunny morning, a happy little dog named Bruno trotted along a quiet path beside a sparkling stream. The birds were singing sweet little songs in the trees, and the water went glip-glop, glip-glop over the smooth round stones.

In his mouth, Bruno carried a big, crunchy bone — his favourite treat in all the world.

"What a lovely day," thought Bruno. "I will take my bone home and enjoy every single bite."

Soon Bruno came to a little wooden bridge over the stream. He stopped in the middle of the bridge and looked down into the clear, quiet water.

And oh! Down in the water, he saw another dog! That dog had a bone too — and it looked even bigger than Bruno's own bone! Can you guess who that other dog was?

Bruno did not know that the other dog was only his own reflection — his own picture in the water — smiling back at him.

"That dog has a bigger bone than mine!" said Bruno to himself. "I want that bone too!"

"Hey, you!" barked Bruno. "Give me your bone!"

But the dog in the water only opened his mouth and barked right back — without a single sound.

Bruno's ears pricked up. "Hmm," he thought. "That dog is copying me!"

He gave a little growl. "That bigger bone should be mine!" And SNAP! He opened his mouth wide to grab the other dog's bone.

Plop! Bruno's own bone slipped out of his mouth and fell into the stream with a tiny splash.

Down, down it sank, and Bruno watched it disappear into the soft green water. His ears drooped, and his tail drooped too.

"Oh no," he whispered. "My bone is gone. And there never was another dog at all. It was only me, all along."

Bruno felt small and sorry deep inside. He sat down beside the stream and looked at his own sad face in the water. "I already had a perfectly good bone," he said softly. "I just wanted more and more, and then I lost what I had."

Just then, a kind little fish swam up to the surface and peeked out of the water. "Hello, Bruno," said the fish. "I saw your bone sink. Wait right here. I will bring it back for you."

Down swam the fish, deep into the cool, clear water. Up she came again, pushing the bone gently in front of her, until it bumped softly against the bank.

"Here you are!" said the fish with a happy wiggle. "It is yours."

Bruno picked up his bone with great care. "Thank you, little fish," he said. "You are very kind. I will be happy with what I have."

On the way home, Bruno met his best friend, Bella the little brown puppy. She was sitting in the soft grass, watching a butterfly float by.

"Would you like to share my bone?" asked Bruno. "Sharing makes it taste even better."

"Oh, yes, please!" said Bella, wagging her tail.

And so the two friends sat together in the soft grass, sharing the bone, watching the clouds drift slowly across the sky, feeling happy and glad.

That night, Bruno curled up in his cosy bed with a warm, sleepy smile. He already had everything he needed — a good bone, a kind friend, and a heart that felt full.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Being happy with what you have makes your heart feel full.*
