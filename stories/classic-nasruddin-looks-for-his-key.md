# Nasruddin Looks for His Key

*A funny Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One night, in a little village, Mullah Nasruddin came home late.

He reached his front door — and stopped. "Oh no!" he said, patting his pockets. "Where is my key?"

He patted this pocket. He patted that pocket. No key!

"Never mind," said Nasruddin. "I will look for it under the streetlamp. The light is much better there!"

So Nasruddin walked to the streetlamp at the corner and began to search on the ground.

He looked to the left. He looked to the right. He crawled around on his hands and knees, peering into every crack.

Soon his neighbours came out to see what was happening.

"Nasruddin, what are you looking for?" asked old Uncle Rahim.

"My key," said Nasruddin. "I have lost my key."

"Oh, we will help you!" said the neighbours.

So everyone began to search under the streetlamp. They looked under the bench. They looked behind the lamp post. They looked in the flowerpot.

But no key anywhere!

At last, little Sara, the smallest neighbour, tugged Nasruddin's sleeve.

"Uncle Nasruddin," she asked, "where did you lose your key?"

"At home, of course," said Nasruddin. "Inside my house."

Everyone stopped. Everyone stared.

"Then why," asked Sara, giggling, "are you looking for it here, under the streetlamp?"

Nasruddin scratched his beard and grinned.

"Because, my dear," he said, "here there is light! At home it is dark. How can I search in the dark?"

Everyone burst out laughing — even Nasruddin laughed at himself!

"Come," said Uncle Rahim, chuckling. "Let us take a lamp to your house and look where you actually lost it."

So they all walked to Nasruddin's house with a bright lamp.

And there — right under the doormat — lay the key, winking in the lamplight!

"Found it!" cried Sara.

Nasruddin picked up his key, unlocked his door, and said, "Thank you, my friends. Tonight you taught me something wise."

"What is that?" asked Sara.

"That we should look for things where they are," said Nasruddin, smiling, "not just where it is easy to look!"

And everyone went home laughing, and Nasruddin slept very well that night — with his key safe in his pocket.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Look for things where they really are, not just where it is easy to look.*
