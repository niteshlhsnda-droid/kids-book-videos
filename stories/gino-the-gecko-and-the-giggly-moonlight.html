Gino the Gecko and the Giggly Moonlight

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a warm little house with a garden full of jasmine lived a gecko named Gino.

Gino was small and green, with sticky toes and big, bright eyes. Every night, when the stars came out, Gino climbed up the garden wall to watch the moon rise.

Gino loved the moon. He loved how it glowed like a night-light. And most of all, he loved to talk to it.

"Good evening, Moon!" Gino would call. "You look lovely tonight!"

But the Moon never answered. It just sailed quietly across the sky.

One night, Gino noticed something strange. The Moon looked dim and droopy, like a balloon that had lost a little air.

"Oh dear," said Gino. "The Moon looks sad tonight. I wonder what is wrong?"

Gino climbed higher and higher, right to the top of the tallest mango tree, and called, "Moon, are you all right?"

The Moon spoke in a voice like a sleepy bell. "I am tired, little gecko," said the Moon. "I shine all night, every night, and tonight I have run out of giggles. Without giggles, my light grows dim."

"Giggles?" said Gino. "I can help with giggles! I know the funniest joke in the whole garden!"

Gino puffed out his chest and said, "Why did the firefly bring a tiny lantern to bed?"

"Why?" asked the Moon.

"Because he was afraid of the dark!" giggled Gino.

The Moon chuckled — a small, silver chuckle. And a little sparkle returned to its glow!

"More!" said the Moon. "Tell me more!"

So Gino told his jokes, one after another. He told the joke about the snail who took a taxi because he was in a hurry. He told the joke about the frog who wore rain boots to his bath. And he told the joke about the owl who read bedtime stories to the stars.

With every giggle, the Moon glowed a little brighter. Soon it was shining so brightly that the whole garden sparkled — the jasmine flowers glowed, the mango leaves shimmered, and even Gino's little green toes twinkled!

"Thank you, Gino!" laughed the Moon, now bright and round and happy. "Your giggles have filled me right up! Now I can shine all night long."

"You are welcome, Moon!" said Gino, yawning a big gecko yawn. "But now I think it is my turn to sleep."

"Then sleep well, little friend," said the Moon, shining its softest, gentlest light down on the mango tree. "I will keep watch over your dreams."

Gino curled up on a warm leaf, tucked his tail around his toes, and fell fast asleep under the giggly moonlight.

And if you look out of your window tonight, you might see the Moon shining extra bright — because somewhere, a little gecko is telling it jokes.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A little laughter can brighten even the darkest night — for others, and for you.
