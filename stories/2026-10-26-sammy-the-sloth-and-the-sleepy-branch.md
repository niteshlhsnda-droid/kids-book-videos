# Sammy the Sloth and the Sleepy Branch

In a green and leafy jungle lived a little sloth named Sammy. He had soft grey-brown fur, long gentle arms, and big round eyes that blinked very slowly. Sammy was good at climbing. He was good at swinging. But sleeping was his favourite, favourite thing of all.

One evening, as the sun turned the leaves gold, Sammy gave a great big yawn.

"Yawn! Yawn! Yawn!" he said. "I am sleepy. I need a good branch for a bed."

So Sammy climbed slowly up the tallest tree he could find. Near the top was a wide, leafy branch that looked just right.

But oh no! A tall giraffe was munching leaves there, nibbling crunch, crunch, crunch.

"Excuse me," Sammy said politely. "May I sleep here?"

The giraffe smiled a tall, kind smile. "This branch is my dinner table, little friend," she said. "You would wobble every time I take a bite."

So Sammy climbed down again, slow and steady, and tried another branch. This one was full of bright parrots, squawking and chattering about their day.

"Squawk! Did you hear? Squawk! What a day!"

Sammy covered his ears with his long arms. "Too noisy for sleeping," he whispered, and climbed on.

Just then, Tula the toucan swooped down, her big yellow beak shining in the sunset. "Come with me, Sammy!" she called. "I know a branch by the waterfall. It is lovely and cool!"

Sammy followed her slowly. But the waterfall went splash, splash, SPLASH all night long, and the spray tickled Sammy's nose.

"Too splashy," Sammy said with a sniffle, and climbed on.

Next he tried a low branch near the elephant path. But the elephants came trundling by, trumpeting toots and shaking the whole tree with their heavy, happy feet.

"Too shaky," Sammy said, wobbling. "I will never find a sleepy branch."

Then a tiny hummingbird named Hana zipped up beside him, her wings humming like a soft little song. "Do not worry, Sammy," she hummed. "I know a quiet branch. Follow me."

Hana led him up, up, up to a curved branch tucked under big, soft leaves, far from the giraffe's dinner, the parrots' chatter, the waterfall's splash, and the elephants' path. The branch rocked gently in the evening breeze, like a cradle in the trees.

Sammy climbed on, and curled himself around it. The leaves closed over him like a green blanket. The moon peeped through the branches, and the stars began to twinkle, one by one.

Down below, the giraffe, the parrots, Tula, the elephants, and little Hana all whispered together, "Goodnight, Sammy. Sleep tight."

Sammy smiled his slow, sleepy smile, and his big round eyes closed.

Sleep now, little Sammy.

The end. 🌙
