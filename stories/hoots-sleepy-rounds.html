Hoot's Sleepy Rounds

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Hoot the owl had an important job. Every night he flew his sleepy rounds, checking that every animal was tucked in — only then could he go to sleep himself.

First he flew to the rabbit burrow, where the baby bunnies were snuggled in a fluffy pile, tucked in tight by their mother. Hoot nodded. All good. "All tucked in, hoot, hoot," he called softly.

Next he flew to the beaver lodge. The beaver kits were curled in their bark beds, though one little kit was still wiggling. Hoot sang a soft hoot, and the kit settled down. "All tucked in, hoot, hoot," he called again.

Then he flew to the mouse hole, where the mouse family was cozy in their thimble beds. The littlest mouse waved a sleepy paw, and Hoot waved back with his wing.

Hoot felt proud — his rounds were going well. But he also felt a little sleepy himself. His wings were getting heavy, and his eyes blinked slowly.

He flew to the fox den, where the fox kits were already dreaming — one was chasing dream-butterflies, its paws twitching happily. Hoot smiled. "All tucked in, hoot, hoot," he called one last time.

Every animal was asleep. The meadow was quiet, the stars twinkled down, and Hoot's job was done. Now it was his turn.

He flew home to his hollow tree. His nest was soft with feathers, and he fluffed it up just right before tucking his head under his wing. But wait — who would check that Hoot was tucked in? Just then, Mother Moon peeked through the leaves and shone her gentle light on him. "All tucked in, hoot, hoot," whispered the moon.

Hoot felt warm and loved. Even the checker gets checked. He closed his big round eyes, and his breathing grew slow and deep.

The night was peaceful. Every animal dreamed softly, and Hoot dreamed the happiest dream of all — he was flying through clouds made of pillows.

Sleep now, little owl. Your rounds are done. Everyone is safe, including you.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Those who take care of others deserve to be taken care of too.
