# The Six Swans

*A Grimm's fairy tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, there lived a king with seven children: six brave sons and one kind little daughter named Elise.

The king loved his children very much. But one day, a wicked stepmother came to the castle, and she did not love them at all.

One morning, Elise woke up and found her brothers were gone! In their place, six beautiful white swans flew in circles above the castle, crying softly.

"Oh!" said Elise. "My brothers have been turned into swans!"

That night, as the moon rose, the swans flew back and became her brothers again for a little while.

"Dear sister," said the eldest brother, "the spell can only be broken if you sew six shirts from stinging nettles, and all the while, you must not speak a single word — not for six whole years."

"Six years without speaking?" whispered Elise. "That is a very long time. But I will do it, for you."

So Elise went into the forest and gathered nettles. Her fingers stung, but she did not stop. She spun the nettles into soft thread and began to sew, stitch by stitch, shirt by shirt.

Year after year, Elise sewed and stayed silent. She sewed in summer and in winter, in sunshine and in snow. She sewed five shirts, and then she began the sixth.

One day, a young king from a neighbouring land found Elise in the forest, sewing quietly under the trees. He saw how kind her eyes were, and he took her to his castle, where she kept on sewing, silent and brave.

At last, the six years were nearly over. Elise had finished five shirts, and the sixth was almost done — only one sleeve was missing.

Then the swans came flying over the castle, crying softly. Elise ran out to the courtyard with the shirts in her arms.

"My brothers!" she cried — for the six years were up, and she could speak again!

She threw a shirt over each swan. Five of her brothers stood up as fine young men once more. The youngest brother kept one swan's wing where the last sleeve was missing — but he only laughed and flapped it playfully.

"Dear sister," said the eldest brother, hugging her tight, "you saved us with your patience and your love."

And the six brothers and their sister lived happily ever after, with the youngest brother's white wing reminding them all of Elise's brave, quiet love.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Patient love can undo even the hardest spell.*
