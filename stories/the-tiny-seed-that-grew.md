# The Tiny Seed That Grew

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in the dark soil slept a tiny seed. Her name was Sprout, and she was no bigger than a freckle.

One day, warm rain seeped down. Drip, drip, drip. Sprout woke up, curiosity fluttering in her like a moth. "What is happening?" she asked.

"Spring is here," said the rain. "Time to grow."

Sprout's insides trembled. Growing sounded so big — and she was so, so small. But she pushed. She pushed a tiny root down and a tiny shoot up. Push, push, push. Determination burned in her like a tiny lantern.

The shoot poked through the soil. "Hello, world!" Sprout said, and the sun beamed down. "Hello, little one," said the sun.

Happiness poured through Sprout like warm honey. She drank the sunshine and drank the rain, and day by day she grew: first two leaves, then four leaves, then six!

"Grow, grow, grow," the wind whispered — three times, once for every pair of leaves. Pride bloomed inside Sprout.

Then one morning, something new appeared: a bud! Small and green and tight. "What is that?" Sprout asked, her tiny brow furrowed.

"Wait and see," said the sun.

So Sprout waited. She waited one day. She waited two days. And on the third day, the bud opened — it was a flower! Pink petals, soft as silk, with a golden heart in the middle. Amazement swept through her. She had grown a flower!

A bee came to visit. Bzzz, went the bee, sipping sweet nectar. "Thank you," said the bee.

Joy bubbled up in Sprout. She was not just a seed anymore — she was a flower. She swayed in the breeze and glowed in the sun.

As evening came, she folded her petals. Just a little. Time to rest.

Sleep now, little sprout. Dream of sunshine and rain and growing tall.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The biggest things begin very, very small.*
