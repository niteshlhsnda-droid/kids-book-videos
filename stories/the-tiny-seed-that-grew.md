# The Tiny Seed That Grew

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in the dark soil slept a tiny seed.

Her name was Sprout.

She was no bigger than a freckle.

One day, warm rain seeped down.

Drip, drip, drip.

Sprout woke up.

She felt curious.

"What is happening?" she asked.

"Spring is here," said the rain.

"Time to grow."

Sprout felt nervous.

Growing sounded big.

She was so small.

But she pushed.

She pushed a tiny root down.

She pushed a tiny shoot up.

Push, push, push.

She felt determined.

The shoot poked through the soil.

"Hello, world!" said Sprout.

The sun beamed down.

"Hello, little one," said the sun.

Sprout felt happy.

She drank the sunshine.

She drank the rain.

Day by day, she grew.

First two leaves.

Then four leaves.

Then six!

"Grow, grow, grow," whispered the wind.

That was three times it whispered it.

Sprout felt proud.

Then one morning, something new appeared.

A bud!

Small and green and tight.

"What is that?" asked Sprout.

She felt puzzled.

"Wait and see," said the sun.

Sprout waited.

She waited one day.

She waited two days.

On the third day, the bud opened.

It was a flower!

Pink petals, soft as silk.

A golden heart in the middle.

Sprout felt amazed.

She had grown a flower.

A bee came to visit.

Bzzz, said the bee, sipping sweet nectar.

"Thank you," said the bee.

Sprout felt joyful.

She was not just a seed anymore.

She was a flower.

She swayed in the breeze.

She glowed in the sun.

As evening came, she folded her petals.

Just a little.

Time to rest.

Sleep now, little sprout.

Dream of sunshine and rain and growing tall.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Big things start very, very small.*
