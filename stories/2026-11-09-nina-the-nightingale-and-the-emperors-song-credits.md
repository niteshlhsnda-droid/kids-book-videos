# Credits — Nina the Nightingale and the Emperor's Song

- **Original title:** "The Nightingale" (Danish: "Nattergalen")
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen (1805–1875)
- **Original publication:** 1843
- **Source:** *Andersen's Fairy Tales*, Project Gutenberg eBook #1597 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1597
- **Illustrator:** None — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated watercolour-style art; no source artwork was reused.
- **License:** Public domain (author died 1875; work published 1843)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a ~450-word bedtime story. The plot is shortened and softened for bedtime: the Emperor falls ill with sadness and sleeplessness rather than facing Death, the nightingale returns of her own free will, and the story closes with a gentle goodnight. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
