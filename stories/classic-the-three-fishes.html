The Three Fishes

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, in a clear blue pond fringed with reeds, lived three little fishes who were the best of friends.

The first was Willa, the wise fish. She thought before she swam, and she always had a plan.

The second was Cora, the clever fish. She could wriggle out of any tight spot with a quick wiggle and a wink.

The third was Pip, the happy-go-lucky fish. Pip loved to splash and play, and he never worried about tomorrow.

One sunny morning, Willa heard two fishermen talking by the bank.

"Tomorrow we will come back with our nets," said the first fisherman. "This pond is full of fine fishes!"

"Oh no," whispered Willa to her friends. "We must swim away tonight, while the water is dark and quiet. There is a deep river beyond the reeds, where no net can reach us."

Cora nodded at once. "I will come with you," she said.

But Pip laughed and splashed his tail. "Tomorrow is far away!" he said. "Let us play a little longer. The sun is warm, and the water is lovely."

That night, Willa and Cora swam softly through the reeds and out into the deep, safe river. Pip stayed behind, still playing among the lily pads.

In the morning, the fishermen came with their nets. Pip darted this way and that — and suddenly he remembered Willa's warning!

"Oh!" cried Pip. "I should have listened! Now what shall I do?"

Quick as a wink, Pip thought of Cora's clever trick. He floated up to the top of the water, turned belly-up, and lay very, very still, like a sleepy fish taking a nap.

The fishermen lifted their net and looked at him.

"This one looks poorly," said the first fisherman. "Let us put him back and catch the lively ones instead."

They tipped Pip gently back into the pond — and splash! Pip wriggled free and swam as fast as his little fins could carry him, straight through the reeds to the deep river.

There were Willa and Cora, waiting for him with happy smiles.

"You came!" cried Cora.

"And just in time," said Willa, giving him a friendly fin-hug. "Next time, let us all listen and swim together, right away."

"I will," promised Pip, laughing. "From now on, when a wise friend warns me, I will not wait one more splash!"

And the three fishes lived happily in the deep river, where they played together every day — and always listened to one another first.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Listen to wise advice in good time, and friends will always help you find the way.
