# Credits — "Fern the Fox Cub and the Twinkling Star"

- **Original title:** "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star"
- **Author:** Jane Taylor (1783–1824), first published in *Rhymes for the Nursery* (1806)
- **Illustrator:** Fresh illustrations were generated for this adaptation; no source art was reused
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/files/10607/10607-h/10607-h.htm (The Real Mother Goose, 1916 — the rhyme appears in Mother Goose collections)
- **License:** Public domain ("for the use of anyone anywhere at no cost and with almost no restrictions whatsoever," per Project Gutenberg)
- **Adaptation note:** The nursery rhyme was expanded into a ~450-word original bedtime story about Fern, a little fox cub who discovers the night sky is full of friendly lights, keeping one verse of the rhyme sung by her mother; the lesson (finding comfort and company in the dark) is shown through the story, never stated.
