# Ollie the Owl and the Lost Lullaby

*An original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a tall oak tree in the middle of a quiet forest lived a little owl named Ollie.

Every night, when the stars came out, Ollie sang a soft, sweet lullaby.

"La-la, loo-loo, sleep now, sleep now, under the moon," he sang, and all the forest animals yawned, snuggled down, and drifted off to sleep.

But one evening, something strange happened. Ollie opened his beak to sing — and nothing came out!

He tried again. "La-la... loo..." and then — nothing. The tune had flown right out of his head!

"Oh no!" whispered Ollie. "I have lost my lullaby! How will anyone fall asleep tonight?"

Ollie fluttered down to the meadow to look for his song.

First he met Molly the Mouse. "Have you seen my lullaby?" he asked.

Molly twitched her whiskers. "I heard a little hum near the daisies this morning," she said. "It went... hmm, hmm, hmm!"

Ollie listened. "Hmm, hmm, hmm!" That was part of his tune! He tucked it carefully into his memory.

Next he met Freddie the Firefly, blinking by the pond.

"Have you seen my lullaby?" asked Ollie.

Freddie blinked three times — blink, blink, blink! — in a slow, sleepy rhythm.

"Blink, blink, blink!" said Ollie. "That is the beat of my song!" He tucked that into his memory too.

Last of all, he met Bella the Bunny, sitting under the moon.

"Have you seen my lullaby?" asked Ollie.

Bella yawned a big, sleepy yawn. "Yaaawn," she said. "That is how your song ends — with a soft, sleepy yawn!"

Ollie put it all together: the hum, the beat, and the yawn.

He flew back to his oak tree, took a deep breath, and sang:

"La-la, loo-loo, sleep now, sleep now, under the moon... yaaawn!"

It was his lullaby — found again!

All over the forest, animals yawned, snuggled down, and drifted off to sleep, smiling in their dreams.

And Ollie smiled too. "My lullaby was never really lost," he whispered. "It was just hiding in my friends!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When you lose something precious, good friends help you find it again.*
