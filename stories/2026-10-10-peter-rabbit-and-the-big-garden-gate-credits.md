# Credits — "Peter Rabbit and the Big Garden Gate"

- **Original title:** *The Tale of Peter Rabbit*
- **Author:** Beatrix Potter
- **Illustrator:** Beatrix Potter (original illustrations NOT reused — all
  illustrations for this story were newly generated fresh, watercolor style)
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org (edition basis; also freely
  available via Project Gutenberg's children's bookshelf)
- **License:** Public domain (first published 1902; US and UK public domain)
- **Adaptation note:** A gentle bedtime retelling for ages 4–7. Simplified and
  shortened to ~450 words; the lesson (listening to a mother's gentle warnings
  keeps us safe) is shown through the story rather than stated. Ends with a
  warm goodnight closing line addressed to Peter, per the brand format.
