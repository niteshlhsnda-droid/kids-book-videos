# Felix the Fox and the Firefly Parade

*A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cosy den under a big oak tree, there lived a little red fox named Felix.

Felix had bright orange fur, a white-tipped tail that was big and bushy, and a shy little smile. He loved to watch the fireflies from his den, but he was too shy to join their games.

Every night, the fireflies danced in the meadow. They zoomed and twirled and made golden swirls in the dark. And every year, on the night of the full moon, they held the Great Firefly Parade — the biggest, brightest night of the whole year!

One morning, Spark, the leader of the fireflies, flew to Felix's den. His little light blinked on and off, on and off.

"Felix!" he buzzed. "This year, we want YOU to lead the Great Firefly Parade!"

Felix's eyes went wide. "Me?" he squeaked. "Oh, no, Spark. I cannot lead a parade. I am too shy. And everyone will laugh at my big, bushy tail!"

"Your tail is wonderful!" said Spark. "It waves like a flag! Please, Felix. Say yes!"

Felix thought about it all day. He thought about it while he ate his lunch. He thought about it while he chased a butterfly. He thought about it while the sun went down.

"I want to say yes," he whispered to himself. "But what if I trip? What if everyone laughs?"

That evening, Felix went to the meadow to practise. He walked in a little circle. He waved his tail — swish, swish — like a flag. He took three brave steps forward, and then three steps back.

Spark and his firefly friends watched from the grass. "Look at him go!" buzzed Spark. "He is practising!"

When the night of the full moon came, the meadow was full of animals. The rabbits were there, and the owls, and the hedgehogs, and even the sleepy old badger. Everyone was waiting for the parade.

Felix stood at the front, his heart going thump, thump, thump. His big, bushy tail was shaking a little.

Then Spark flew up and landed right on the tip of Felix's tail. "Ready?" he buzzed.

"Ready," whispered Felix.

And then — oh, wonder! — all the fireflies flew down and landed on Felix's tail, one by one, until his whole bushy tail was covered in tiny golden lights! It glowed like a lantern, like a sparkler, like a little piece of the moon itself!

Felix lifted his tail high, and he led the parade into the meadow — step, step, step — with his tail waving like a golden flag. The fireflies twirled around him. The animals cheered. The moon shone down, round and bright.

Felix was not shy any more. He was smiling the biggest smile in the whole forest!

When the parade was over, Spark buzzed, "Felix, you were the best parade leader ever!"

Felix looked at his glowing tail and laughed. "My big, bushy tail," he said, "is the very best thing about me!"

And from that night on, whenever Felix felt shy, he remembered the parade — and he held his tail high, like a golden flag.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What makes you different can make you shine — so hold your tail high!*
