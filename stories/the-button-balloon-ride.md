# The Button Balloon Ride

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Kavi found a big red button in his grandpa's sewing box.

"What's this for?" he asked.

"It's a balloon button," said Grandpa with a twinkle. "Press it, and see."

Curiosity bubbling up, Kavi pressed the button.

Pop!

A giant hot-air balloon appeared in the yard. Red and yellow, big as a house.

"Hop in!" said Grandpa.

Kavi felt excited and a little nervous as he climbed into the basket, with Grandpa climbing in right behind him.

Up, up, up they floated. The house grew small, the trees grew small — the whole town grew small.

"Wow, wow, wow," Kavi whispered. That was three times he whispered it, and he felt amazed.

They floated over the river, sparkling like a silver ribbon, and over the fields, which looked like a green patchwork quilt.

"Can we touch a cloud?" asked Kavi.

Grandpa steered the balloon close, and Kavi reached out. The cloud felt soft and cool, like cotton candy without the sugar. He felt giggly.

Then Kavi saw something below — a lost kite tangled in a tree.

"Grandpa, can we help it?" he asked, worried for the kite.

Grandpa lowered the balloon, and Kavi reached out and gently freed the kite. "Fly free," he whispered. The kite soared up, happy again, and Kavi felt proud.

As the sun began to set, Grandpa said, "Time to go home."

The balloon drifted down, down, down. It landed softly in the yard.

Pop! went the balloon, back into a button.

Kavi held the button tight. "Best ride ever," he said, feeling sleepy.

That night, Kavi put the button under his pillow. He dreamed of floating over rivers of silver.

Sleep now, little flyer.

Dream of red and yellow balloons in a pink sky.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The best adventures are the ones you share.*
