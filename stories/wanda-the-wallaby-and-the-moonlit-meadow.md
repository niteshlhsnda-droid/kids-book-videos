# Wanda the Wallaby and the Moonlit Meadow

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a wide green valley in Australia, where the gum trees whispered in the evening breeze, lived a young wallaby named Wanda.

Wanda loved to hop. She hopped in the morning when the dew was fresh. She hopped in the afternoon when the sun was warm. And most of all, she loved to hop at night, when the moon came out and turned the meadow silver.

Every night, as the stars winked on one by one, Wanda would bounce out of her cosy burrow and hop, hop, hop across the moonlit meadow. Her long tail swished behind her, and her ears wiggled with joy.

The meadow animals loved to watch her. The fireflies would glow a little brighter as she passed, and the crickets would chirp a happy hopping song: "Chirp, chirp, hop, hop, hop!"

But one night, something was different.

Wanda hopped out of her burrow and looked up at the sky. The moon was gone! A big, soft, grey cloud had drifted across it, and the meadow was dark and quiet. The fireflies glowed only faintly, and the crickets had stopped singing.

"Oh no," whispered Wanda. "Without the moonlight, how will my friends find their way home?"

Just then, she heard a tiny sniffle from behind a gum tree. It was a little possum, all alone in the dark, too frightened to find his way back to his hollow tree.

"Don't worry," said Wanda softly. "I will help you. Hold on to my tail, and hop with me."

So the little possum held on tight, and Wanda hopped slowly and carefully through the dark meadow, calling out, "This way! Hop, hop, this way!"

Soon they heard another sound — a sleepy koala cub, sitting on a low branch, rubbing his eyes. "I can't see my tree in the dark," he whimpered.

"Come along," said Wanda. "Hold on to my paw, and we will all hop together."

So the possum, the koala cub, and Wanda hopped on through the dark meadow, one hop at a time, until they reached the koala's tall gum tree. The cub climbed up happily and waved goodnight.

Then Wanda helped the possum to his hollow tree, where his mother was waiting with open arms. "Thank you, Wanda," said the possum's mother. "You are a true friend."

Wanda smiled and hopped back toward her burrow. And just as she reached the meadow, the big grey cloud drifted away — and there was the moon, round and bright and smiling!

The moonlight poured down over the meadow like silver honey. The fireflies glowed bright again, the crickets sang their hopping song, and Wanda took one last joyful bounce beneath the stars.

"Thank you, Moon," whispered Wanda. "And thank you, my friends, for hopping with me."

Then she curled up in her cosy burrow, tucked her tail around her paws, and fell fast asleep, dreaming of moonlight and meadows and friends who hop together.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A kind hop in the dark is worth more than a hundred hops in the light — friends help friends find their way home.*
