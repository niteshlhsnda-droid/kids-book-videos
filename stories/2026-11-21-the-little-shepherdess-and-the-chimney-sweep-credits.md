# Credits — The Little Shepherdess and the Chimney Sweep

- **Original title:** "The Shepherdess and the Chimney Sweep" (1845)
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen (1805–1875)
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *Andersen's Fairy Tales* (eBook #1597) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1597
- **License:** Public domain (first published 1845; Andersen died 1875)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, shortened to ten story beats from the two little porcelain figures' midnight climb to the rooftop; the old Mandarin's arranged marriage, the chimney climb, the wide starry world, and the happy return to the mantelpiece are kept, and the ending is softened into a cosy morning goodnight so the tale closes gently at bedtime.
