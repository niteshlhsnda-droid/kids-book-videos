Pip the Penguin and the Painted Pebble

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Far away, where the snow sparkles white and the sea is blue as the sky, there lived a little penguin named Pip.

Pip loved colours. He loved the blue of the sea, the yellow of the sun, and the pink of the clouds at sunset.

But the beach where Pip lived was all grey and white — grey pebbles, white snow, and more grey pebbles.

One morning, Pip was waddling along the icy beach — waddle, waddle, waddle — when he found a smooth grey pebble, round as a moon.

"Oh, what a lovely pebble," said Pip. "But oh, how grey you are!"

Then Pip had a wonderful idea. He ran home and fetched his little pots of berry paint — blue berry paint, yellow berry paint, and pink berry paint.

With his tiny flipper, Pip painted a blue wave on the pebble. Then he painted a yellow sun. Then he painted a pink heart, right in the middle.

"Now you are not grey at all!" laughed Pip. "You are a painted pebble, full of colours!"

Pip held the pebble up to the sun, and it sparkled like a tiny rainbow.

Just then, his friend Waddles came waddling by, looking very sad.

"What is wrong, Waddles?" asked Pip.

"I lost my favourite shell," sniffed Waddles, "and now my day feels grey."

Pip smiled and held out the painted pebble. "Here, Waddles. This pebble is for you. It has a blue wave, a yellow sun, and a pink heart — to make your day bright."

Waddles held the pebble tight and smiled the biggest smile. "Thank you, Pip! It is the prettiest pebble in the whole wide world!"

A little later, Pip met his friend Snow, who was hiding shyly behind a snowdrift.

"Hello, Snow," said Pip. "Would you like a painted pebble too?"

Snow peeped out, her eyes wide. "For me? Really?"

"Really!" said Pip. And he painted another pebble, just for Snow, with a pink heart and a yellow sun.

Snow held the pebble close and whispered, "Thank you, Pip. I will keep it forever."

Soon, all the penguins on the beach wanted painted pebbles. Pip painted and painted — pebbles with waves, pebbles with suns, pebbles with hearts and stars.

By sunset, the whole beach sparkled with painted pebbles — blue and yellow and pink, twinkling in the soft evening light.

That night, Pip snuggled into his cosy nest, tired and happy.

He closed his eyes and dreamed of colours — blue waves dancing, yellow suns smiling, and pink hearts floating over a sparkling beach.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A little kindness and a splash of colour can brighten a whole world.
