# Luma the Firefly and the Lantern Song

*An original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a meadow beside a sleepy pond, there lived a tiny firefly named Luma.

Every evening, when the sun yawned and sank behind the hills, Luma polished her little lantern-light — glow, glow, glow — until it shone like a warm drop of honey.

Then she flew along the pond path, humming her lantern-song: "Hmm-hmm, glow-glow, this way home..."

The frogs croaked along. The reeds swayed. And every creature in the meadow knew: when Luma's light passes by, it is time to snuggle down and sleep.

One evening, a baby owl named Ollie was trying his very first night flight.

Flap, flap — wobble, wobble — WHOOPS! A gust of wind spun him around, and when he landed on a branch, he did not know where he was.

The pond looked strange. The trees looked strange. Even the moon looked like it was in the wrong place.

Ollie's bottom lip trembled. "Mama?" he peeped. "Mama?"

Then, through the dark, he heard it — a soft humming: "Hmm-hmm, glow-glow, this way home..."

And he saw it — a tiny golden light, bobbing toward him like a floating star.

"Hello, little owl," said Luma, landing softly on the branch. "Are you lost?"

Ollie nodded, his big eyes shiny with tears.

"Then hold my song," said Luma. "Follow my light. Hmm-hmm, glow-glow, this way home..."

Step by fluttery step, Luma led Ollie along the pond path. The frogs croaked the song. The reeds swayed the song. Even the stars seemed to twinkle it: hmm-hmm, glow-glow.

And there, in the old oak tree, was Mama Owl — with her wings open wide!

Ollie tumbled into her feathers. "Mama! A star came down and sang me home!"

Mama Owl smiled at Luma. "Thank you, little lantern."

Luma glowed a little brighter — which, for a firefly, is the same as blushing.

That night, Ollie fell asleep to his mama's soft feathers and Luma's faraway song: "Hmm-hmm, glow-glow... this way... to sleep..."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little light and a gentle song can guide anyone home.*
