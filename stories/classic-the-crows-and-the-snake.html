The Crows and the Snake

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a big green tree, there lived a kind crow and his wife. Their nest was soft, made of twigs and leaves.

One day, the mother crow laid three small pale blue eggs in the nest.

"We will have three little babies soon," said the father crow.

"We will keep them safe and warm," said the mother crow.

But at the foot of the tree, there was a dark hole. A big hungry snake lived in that hole. He was long and shiny, and he loved to eat eggs.

One quiet night, the snake slid up, up, up the tree. Slither, slither, slither. He ate the three little eggs.

In the morning, the nest was empty.

"We cannot fight the snake," said the father crow. "He is too big and too strong."

"We must think," said the mother crow. "We must think of a clever plan."

The father crow flew high into the sky. He flew all the way to the king's big palace.

There, in the palace garden, the queen was bathing in a cool pool. On a soft cloth beside her lay her shiny necklace. It sparkled in the sun.

The crow flew down, quick and quiet. He took the necklace gently in his beak and flew back to his tree.

Bead by bead, he dropped the shiny beads. Plip, plip, plip. One fell on the grass, one by the roots, and the last one fell right into the snake's dark hole.

The queen saw her necklace was gone. "Oh no!" she cried. "My necklace is gone!"

The king called his guards. "Find my queen's necklace!" he said.

The guards followed the shiny beads across the garden, over the fields, and right to the big tree.

"Look!" said one guard. "The beads lead into this hole!"

There they saw the necklace. And there they saw the long shiny snake.

"Go away, snake!" they cried. They waved their sticks and made a big noise. "Shoo! Shoo! Go far away!"

The snake was scared. He slid out of the hole and hurried away. Slither, slither, slither. He went far, far away and never came back.

The crows were safe at last, and the queen got her necklace back.

Soon the mother crow laid three new eggs. Day by day, she kept them warm. Then one soft morning, crack, crack, crack! Three little beaks poked out.

"Peep, peep!" said the babies. "Peep, peep!"

The crow family sang their happy song. And they lived happily in the big green tree.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A clever plan beats a strong enemy.
