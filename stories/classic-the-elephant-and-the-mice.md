# The Elephant and the Mice

*A timeless Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a kind elephant named Ellie.

Ellie was very, very big. Her feet were as wide as dinner plates, and her ears flapped gently like soft grey blankets when she walked.

Every morning, Ellie went down to the river to drink cool water. Every evening, she ate sweet leaves and slept under the tallest tree in the forest.

At the foot of that tall tree lived a family of tiny mice: Milo, the brave little mouse, his sister Mina, and baby Guddu, no bigger than a grape.

One sunny morning, Ellie was walking to the river, humming a happy tune. She did not see the little mouse hole beside the path. Oh dear! Her big foot was about to step right on top of it!

"Squeak! Look out!" cried Milo.

Ellie stopped just in time. She looked down with her kind eyes and saw the tiny mice peeping out of their little hole.

"Oh, I am so sorry, little friends," said Ellie gently. "I did not see your home. From now on, I promise to watch where I step."

"Thank you, kind elephant," squeaked Milo. "You are the biggest animal in the forest, but you have the kindest heart."

Then one dark night, a hunter came into the forest. He dug a deep hole, covered it with leaves, and tied a strong rope net to the trees nearby. Then he hid behind a bush to wait.

In the morning, Ellie came walking along the path, just as she always did. She was thinking about sweet leaves and cool river water, and she did not see the hunter's trap.

Suddenly — snap! The ropes flew up around her! The net closed tight around her big body, and Ellie could not move at all.

"Oh no! I am caught! Help!" cried Ellie. She pulled with her trunk and pushed with her feet, but the ropes were too strong.

But far away, under the tall tree, the little mice heard her cry. "That is Ellie!" squeaked Milo. "Our friend needs us! Come on, everyone!"

Milo and Mina and Guddu ran as fast as their tiny legs could go, all the way to the trap. There they saw poor Ellie, tangled up in the thick ropes.

"Do not worry, Ellie," said Milo bravely. "We may be small, but our teeth are sharp! We will set you free!"

And the little mice got to work. Nibble, nibble, nibble! They nibbled the ropes with their tiny teeth. Bit by bit, the ropes grew thinner and thinner.

At last — snap! The last rope broke, and Ellie was free!

"Hurrah!" squeaked the mice. Ellie lifted her trunk high and trumpeted happily. "Thank you, my little friends! You saved me!"

Then Ellie promised, "I will always watch my step near your little home, and I will always be your friend."

The mice cheered, and Ellie carried them home on her back as the sun set over the forest.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even the smallest friend can do the biggest good.*
