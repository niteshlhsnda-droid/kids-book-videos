# The Frog and the Ox

*A gentle Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Beside a wide, winding river lived a little frog named Freddy.

Freddy was small and green, with round eyes and long, springy legs. He loved to hop, hop, hop along the riverbank, and splash, splash, splash in the shallow water.

One morning, Freddy hopped up onto a smooth stone and looked across the meadow.

"Wow!" said Freddy. "Look at that!"

Coming down the path was Oscar the Ox — the biggest, strongest animal Freddy had ever seen.

Oscar's legs were as thick as tree trunks. Oscar's back was as wide as a hill. Oscar's gentle eyes looked out over the whole meadow.

"Oh, how I wish I could be as big as Oscar!" sighed Freddy. "Everyone would look at me and say, 'Wow!'"

So Freddy took a deep breath — and puffed himself up.

He puffed his cheeks. He puffed his tummy. He puffed until he was as round as a little green ball.

"Am I as big as Oscar now?" he asked his friend, Polly the frog.

Polly looked at Freddy. "Not quite," she said, giggling. "You are a little bit bigger — but not ox-big."

"Hmm!" said Freddy. He took another deep breath — and puffed himself up even more.

He puffed his cheeks. He puffed his tummy. He puffed until he was as round as a ripe green melon.

"Am I as big as Oscar now?" he asked.

Polly shook her head. "Still not ox-big," she said. "But you are turning a funny shade of green!"

"Hmm!" said Freddy. He took the BIGGEST deep breath of all — and puffed himself up more than ever.

He puffed his cheeks. He puffed his tummy. He puffed until —

"HIC!"

A great big hiccup popped out of Freddy's mouth!

"HIC! HIC! HIC!"

Freddy hiccupped and hiccupped. His whole little body bounced with every hiccup. "Hic! Hic! Hic!"

Polly could not help it — she began to laugh. "Hic!" laughed Polly. "You look like a wobbly jelly!"

And Freddy, hiccupping and all, began to laugh too. "Hic! Ha-ha-ha! Hic!"

Just then, Oscar the Ox came ambling over. "Hello, little friends," said Oscar in his deep, gentle voice. "What is all this laughing about?"

Freddy took a breath — a small, normal breath — and his hiccups stopped. His tummy went back to its usual froggy size.

"I was trying to be as big as you," said Freddy, feeling a little shy. "But I only got hiccups."

Oscar smiled a big, kind ox smile. "Little Freddy," he said, "you do not need to be big like me. You are perfect just as you are."

"Am I?" asked Freddy.

"Of course!" said Oscar. "I cannot hop. I cannot splash in the shallow water. I cannot catch flies with a tongue as quick as yours. You can do all the things a frog can do — and I cannot do a single one!"

Freddy thought about that. He hopped — hop! — high into the air. He splashed — splash! — into the shallow water. He flicked his long tongue — zip! — and caught a fly for his lunch.

"You are right!" said Freddy, beaming. "Being a frog is wonderful!"

That evening, Freddy sat on his smooth stone and watched the sunset. Oscar the Ox grazed nearby, big and calm. Polly the frog sat beside Freddy, small and happy.

And Freddy sang a little froggy song: "Small is fine, big is fine, each of us is just right — ribbit, ribbit, goodnight!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: You do not need to be big like someone else — you are just right as you are.*
