The Two Frogs

A cheerful Japanese folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in a land far away, there lived two frogs. One frog, Midori, lived in a quiet pond near the city of Kyoto. The other frog, Aoi, lived in a sunny pond near the city of Osaka.

Midori often wondered, "What does Osaka look like? I would love to see it one day!"

And Aoi often wondered, "What does Kyoto look like? I would love to see it one day!"

One fine spring morning, both frogs set out on a journey at exactly the same time. Midori hopped along the road towards Osaka. Aoi hopped along the road towards Kyoto. Hop, hop, hop! Their little legs went pitter-pat on the stones.

Halfway along the road stood a tall mountain. Midori hopped up one side, puffing and panting. Aoi hopped up the other side, puffing and panting. And right at the very top, they met!

"Hello!" said Midori. "Where are you going?"

"To Kyoto, to see the city!" said Aoi. "And where are you going?"

"To Osaka, to see the city!" said Midori.

"Let us stand on our hind legs," said Aoi, "and look over the mountain. Then we can each see the city we are travelling to!"

So both frogs stood up tall on their hind legs and stretched their necks. But frogs' eyes are on the tops of their heads, and when a frog stands up, its eyes look backwards!

So Midori, standing tall, looked back towards Kyoto — her own home — and thought it was Osaka. "Oh, how beautiful!" she cried. "Osaka looks just like my home!"

And Aoi, standing tall, looked back towards Osaka — his own home — and thought it was Kyoto. "Oh, how lovely!" he cried. "Kyoto looks just like my home!"

They laughed and laughed at the wonderful sight. Then they sat down together on the mountaintop and shared a picnic of sweet berries and soft green leaves, and told each other all about their ponds.

"You know what?" said Midori, munching a berry. "If Osaka looks just like my home, then my home must be a wonderful place after all. I think I will hop back home."

"And I think so too!" said Aoi. "If Kyoto looks just like my home, then my home is wonderful already!"

So the two frogs waved goodbye, hopped back down the mountain, and went home — hop, hop, hop — happier than ever.

That night, Midori floated on her pond under the stars and whispered, "There is no place like home." And far away in Osaka, Aoi whispered exactly the same thing.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Home is a wonderful place — sometimes you travel far just to find that out.
