# The Quail and the Fowler

*A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a wide golden meadow lived a flock of little brown quails, led by a wise old quail named Queenie.

Every morning, the quails flew together to a field of soft, sweet grain. They pecked and chirped and played, and every evening they flew home together, safe and sound.

But one day, a fowler came to the meadow. He was a man who caught birds in a big net, and he spread his net over the grain field and hid behind a bush to wait.

The next morning, Queenie saw something strange. "Stop!" she called. "Look carefully, little ones. Do you see the net lying flat on the grain?"

The young quails peered down. "Oh! We almost flew right into it!" they chirped.

"Listen well," said Queenie. "If we ever land in that net, we must not flutter and panic, each on our own. We must all lift together, at the very same moment, and carry the net away with us. Together, we are stronger than any net."

The quails practised in the air: "One, two, three — LIFT!" they chirped, flapping their wings in time.

But one greedy little quail named Pipsqueak did not listen. "More grain for me!" he thought, and while the others were practising, he swooped down alone into the field.

Whump! The net fell over Pipsqueak. "Help! Help!" he squeaked.

In a flash, Queenie and all the quails flew down. Each one took hold of the net with beak and claw. "One, two, three — LIFT!" cried Queenie.

Up rose the net, with Pipsqueak safe inside it, and away flew the whole flock, carrying the net like a soft brown blanket through the sky!

The fowler ran after them, puffing and panting, but the quails flew on and on until they reached a tall thorn bush. There they gently set the net down, and Pipsqueak wriggled free.

"Thank you!" chirped Pipsqueak, his cheeks pink with shame. "I should have listened. From now on, I fly with the flock, always."

The fowler looked up at the sky, scratched his head, and said, "Well, I have never seen birds work together like that!" And he folded up his empty net and went home, deciding to grow grain in his own garden instead.

And the quails? They flew home together, singing their evening song, closer than ever before.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When we work together, at the same moment, we are stronger than any trouble.*
