The Straw, the Coal, and the Bean

A timeless Grimm fairy tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a cozy little kitchen with a warm, crackling stove, there lived three tiny friends.

There was Straw — long, golden, and a little bit bendy. There was Coal — small, round, and warm from sitting near the fire. And there was Bean — plump, shiny, and always giggling.

"We're the best of friends," Bean would giggle. "The three of us — forever!"

But one day, the cook came into the kitchen with a big pot. "Time to make soup!" she said cheerfully.

Straw gasped. Coal shivered. Bean stopped giggling.

"We have to get out of here!" whispered Straw. "Quick — out the window!"

So the three little friends squeezed through the open kitchen window — plip! plop! plup! — and tumbled onto the soft grass outside.

"We're free!" cheered Bean. "Now let's go on an adventure!"

They skipped and hopped down the lane until they came to a little stream. The water babbled and sparkled — too wide to jump across.

"How will we get over?" asked Coal.

"I know!" said Straw. "I'll lie across the water like a bridge. You two can walk over me!"

So brave Straw stretched himself across the stream — from one bank to the other — like a golden bridge.

Coal started across first. Tip-tap, tip-tap went his little feet. But halfway across, he looked down at the sparkling water below and got scared.

"Oh no, oh no," he whimpered, wobbling.

Then — whoosh! — a little splash of water touched Straw. And Straw, being straw, caught a tiny spark of warmth from Coal's feet... and POOF! A little flame danced along Straw's end!

"Too hot! Too hot!" cried Straw.

Coal panicked and jumped — SPLASH! — right into the stream. The cool water put out the little flame, but it also put out Coal's warmth. "Brrr!" he shivered, floating like a tiny black boat. "I'm all cold and soggy now!"

And Bean, watching from the bank — oh, Bean couldn't help it. The whole thing was so silly and surprising that she started to laugh.

"Hee-hee! Ha-ha! HOO-HOO-HOO!" Bean laughed and laughed, holding her round tummy, until — POP! — she laughed so hard that her little bean-skin burst open with a seam!

"Oh no!" giggled Bean, clapping her hands over her mouth. "Are you both okay? I'm sorry — I just couldn't stop laughing!"

Just then, a kind tailor happened to walk by with his needle and thread. "Well, well," he chuckled. "What have we here?"

He fished Coal out of the stream and set him in the warm sun to dry. He patted out Straw's little singed end — "There, there, good as new." And then, with the tiniest stitches you ever saw, he sewed Bean's seam back together — stitch, stitch, stitch.

"Thank you!" said the three friends together.

And do you know what? To this very day, every bean in the world has a little seam down its side — right where the tailor sewed Bean back together.

The three friends learned to be a little more careful after that. But they stayed the best of friends — forever and ever.

And Bean? She still giggles. But now she holds her tummy very gently when she does.

"Hee-hee," she giggles softly. "Careful, careful... don't burst the seam!"

Good night, little giggler. Hold your tummy, smile softly, and drift away.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Take care of your friends — and laugh gently, so nobody gets hurt.
