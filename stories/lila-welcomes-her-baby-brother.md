# Lolo Welcomes Her Baby Brother

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Lolo was a little lamb, and she had always been the baby of the family. Until one spring morning, when Mama Sheep had a new baby — a tiny lamb brother. He was very small. He was very squeaky. Everyone said, "Oh, how cute!"

Lolo felt jealous. Her heart felt crowded, and her smile felt thin. "Will Mama still love me?" she wondered, sitting alone in the corner of Firefly Meadow.

Mama Sheep came to her. "I see you feel jealous," she said. Lolo nodded, her eyes watery. "It is okay to feel jealous," Mama told her. "Love grows bigger. It never runs out."

"Love grows bigger. It never runs out," she said again, nuzzling Lolo. "Love grows bigger. It never runs out," she said one more time.

Lolo breathed slowly and looked at her baby brother. He was sleeping, his ears twitching. "He is kind of cute," she whispered.

Mama smiled. "Would you like to be his big sister?" she asked. "Big sisters are very important."

Lolo stood up tall and walked to the baby. "Hello, little one," she said softly. "I am Lolo. I am your big sister." The baby opened his eyes and looked right at her. Lolo's heart melted like warm honey, and big, warm, glowy love bubbled up inside her.

That afternoon, Lolo showed him the meadow — the softest grass, the sunny spot. "I will teach you everything," she said. The baby bleated happily.

That night, Lolo snuggled close to Mama with the baby sleeping on Mama's other side. She had felt jealous earlier — now she felt proud. She was a big sister. Mama kissed them both. "My two precious lambs," she whispered. The stars twinkled over Firefly Meadow as Lolo closed her eyes. She was loved. So, so loved.

Sleep now, little Lolo.

The end. 🌙
