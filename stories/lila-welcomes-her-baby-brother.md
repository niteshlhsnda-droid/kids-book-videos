# Lila Welcomes Her Baby Brother

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Lila was a little lamb.

She was the baby of the family.

Until one spring morning.

Mama Sheep had a new baby.

A tiny lamb brother.

He was very small.

He was very squeaky.

Everyone said, "Oh, how cute!"

Lila felt jealous.

Her heart felt crowded.

Her smile felt thin.

"Will Mama still love me?" she wondered.

She sat alone in the corner of the meadow.

Mama Sheep came to her.

She said, "I see you feel jealous."

Lila nodded.

Her eyes were watery.

"It is okay to feel jealous," Mama said.

"Love grows bigger. It never runs out."

"Love grows bigger. It never runs out," she said again, nuzzling Lila.

"Love grows bigger. It never runs out," she said one more time.

Lila breathed slowly.

She looked at her baby brother.

He was sleeping.

His ears twitched.

"He is kind of cute," Lila whispered.

Mama smiled.

"Would you like to be his big sister?" she asked.

"Big sisters are very important."

Lila stood up tall.

She walked to the baby.

"Hello, little one," she said softly.

"I am Lila. I am your big sister."

The baby opened his eyes.

He looked right at Lila.

Lila's heart melted like warm honey.

She felt love bubble up.

Big, warm, glowy love.

That afternoon, Lila showed him the meadow.

She showed him the softest grass.

She showed him the sunny spot.

"I will teach you everything," she said.

The baby bleated happily.

That night, Lila snuggled close to Mama.

The baby slept on Mama's other side.

Lila felt jealous earlier.

Now she felt proud.

She was a big sister.

Mama kissed them both.

"My two precious lambs," she whispered.

The stars twinkled over the meadow.

Lila closed her eyes.

She was loved.

So, so loved.

Goodnight, Lila.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A new sibling does not shrink love — love grows to fit everyone.*
