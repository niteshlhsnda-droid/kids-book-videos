# Uma the Sea Urchin and the Little Starfish

*A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

At the bottom of the blue sea, in a garden of soft pink coral, there lived a little sea urchin named Uma.

Uma was small and round, and she was covered all over in tiny spikes. She was not a scary sea urchin — her spikes were soft and gentle. But they were still spikes, and Uma worried about them every day.

"I wish I could hug my friends," said Uma, sighing a bubbly sigh. "But I am too prickly. No one can hug me!"

One morning, a little starfish named Stella came floating by. Stella had five soft arms and a smile as bright as the sun.

"Hello, Uma!" said Stella. "Would you like to play with me?"

"I would love to," said Uma. "But I cannot hug you. My spikes are too prickly."

Stella laughed a twinkly laugh. "Who says a hug has to be a squeeze? Watch this!"

Stella reached out one soft arm and gave Uma the gentlest pat on her spikes. Pat, pat, pat.

"See?" said Stella. "That is a starfish hug! Soft and gentle, just right for you."

Uma's eyes went wide. "Oh! That feels lovely!"

Just then, Oscar the Octopus swam over, waving all eight of his arms. "Hello, Uma! Hello, Stella! What are we playing?"

"We are giving gentle hugs!" said Stella.

Oscar smiled. "I know a good one!" He reached out one curly arm and gave Uma a soft, wavy wave — right beside her, without a single poke.

"That is an octopus wave-hug!" said Oscar. "It means, 'I am so glad to see you!'"

Then Coco the Crab scuttled over, clicking his claws. "And I know one too!" he said. He tapped his claw gently against Uma's spikes — TAP, TAP! — and said, "That is a crab tap-hug! It means, 'You are my friend!'"

Uma felt warm all over, from her spikes to her toes. "Oh, thank you!" she said. "I thought I could never be hugged. But you have shown me that love does not always need a squeeze!"

Uma had an idea. She swam to her favourite shell — a small, shiny shell, pink and white like a sunrise — and gave it to Stella.

"Here," said Uma. "This is my softest hug. It means, 'Thank you for being my friend!'"

Stella held the shell close. "It is beautiful," she said. "And I will keep it forever."

From that day on, Uma never worried about her spikes again. Every morning, Stella gave her a starfish pat. Oscar gave her a wave-hug. And Coco gave her a tap-hug. And Uma gave them all her softest smiles and her prettiest shells.

And whenever a little fish asked, "Uma, why are you smiling?" Uma would say, "Because I have learned something wonderful. Love does not always need a hug. A kind word, a gentle pat, and a friend who understands — those are hugs too!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Love does not always need a hug — a kind word and a gentle touch are hugs too.*
