# Poppy the Panda's Bamboo Lullaby

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green bamboo grove, where the tall stalks swayed and whispered in the evening breeze, lived a young panda named Poppy, with round black ears and a tummy as soft as a pillow.

Poppy loved two things best: crunchy bamboo shoots for breakfast — and music.

She hummed while she munched, and while she napped — she even hummed in her dreams.

One quiet evening, as Poppy was munching her bamboo, she tapped a tall stalk with her paw. "Tok!"

It was an accident. But what an accident!

The stalk made a soft, hollow note — like the sound of tapping an empty wooden bowl.

Poppy's round ears stood straight up. She tapped another stalk. "Tuk!" — a little lower. Another. "Tek!" — a little higher.

Each stalk sang a different note!

Poppy's eyes grew big and round. "A bamboo piano!" she whispered.

She tapped them in a row — tok, tuk, tek, tok! — and giggled at the funny little song. Then she tried them backwards — tek, tuk, tok! It sounded like the bamboo was giggling back.

Poppy hugged the tallest stalk. "Thank you for the music," she whispered.

Poppy tapped slowly — tok... tuk... tek... — and a gentle tune began to float through the grove. The tune curled around the tall stalks and drifted over the grass, soft as a lullaby, sweet as honey. Fireflies drifted out to listen, lighting up like tiny lanterns.

Two rabbits, hopping home for the night, stopped mid-hop to listen. Their long ears twitched. Their pink noses stopped their sniff-sniff-sniffing. And they sat down in the grass, side by side.

Three little birds, settling into their nest, tucked their heads a little lower and listened. One by one, their eyes closed into sleepy slits.

Four baby monkeys, swinging through the branches, yawned big, sleepy yawns — and curled up in a cozy pile. The littlest monkey sucked his thumb.

A sleepy sigh went through the grove.

Poppy kept playing — slow and soft, slow and soft — her tune winding through the bamboo like a sleepy ribbon.

The evening breeze joined in, swaying the tall stalks left... and right... in time with her song.

One by one, her listeners drifted off. The rabbits' ears drooped. The birds' feathers fluffed. The baby monkeys breathed slow and deep.

A butterfly landed on a leaf and folded its wings. Even the busy ants slowed down.

Poppy played until the very last listener was asleep.

Her paws were getting heavy. Her eyelids drooped. She played slower... and slower... and slower...

Then she set down her paws, curled up in the soft grass of the bamboo grove, and listened to the stalks whispering above her — shhh... shhh... shhh...

The moon peeked through the bamboo leaves and smiled down at her.

And to the music still humming in her heart, Poppy the panda drifted off to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Music made with love brings rest.*
