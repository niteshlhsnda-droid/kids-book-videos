# Tenali Rama and the Well

*A clever Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a village in South India, there lived two neighbours, Ramu and Somu.

Between their houses stood a deep, cool well. Its water was sweet and clear, and everyone in the village loved it.

But one day, Ramu and Somu began to quarrel.

"The well is mine!" said Ramu. "It stands closer to my house!"

"No, it is mine!" said Somu. "My grandfather dug it with his own hands!"

They argued all morning. They argued all afternoon. Soon the whole village was tired of their shouting.

At last, the villagers went to the king. "Your Majesty," they said, "please help us. Ramu and Somu quarrel over the well all day long."

The king smiled. "Send for Tenali Rama," he said. "He is the cleverest man in my court. He will settle this."

Tenali Rama came to the village. He listened to Ramu. He listened to Somu. Both men talked and talked, each saying the well was his.

Tenali Rama stroked his chin and thought for a moment. Then he smiled a clever little smile.

"Bring me two buckets," he said. "Ramu, draw one bucket of water. Somu, draw one bucket too."

Ramu drew a bucket. Somu drew a bucket. Both buckets were full of cool, clear water.

"Now," said Tenali Rama, "Ramu, taste your water. Somu, taste yours."

Ramu took a sip. "Mmm! Sweet!" he said.

Somu took a sip. "Mmm! Sweet!" he said too.

Tenali Rama clapped his hands. "There you are! Both buckets taste the same — sweet and cool. The water does not know Ramu's name or Somu's name. It is the same water for all!"

Then he turned to the villagers and said, "This well does not belong to one man. It belongs to the whole village. Everyone may drink from it, and everyone must care for it."

Ramu and Somu looked at each other. They felt a little silly — and a little glad.

"You are right, Tenali Rama," said Ramu.

"Let us share the well," said Somu. "Friends are better than quarrels."

From that day on, Ramu and Somu drew water side by side. They even planted flowers around the well, and the whole village came to drink its sweet, cool water.

And Tenali Rama? He went back to the king's court, smiling all the way home.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What is shared with love grows sweeter for everyone.*
