# Piku Is Brave at the Doctor

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Piku was a little penguin chick, and she had a cough. Cough, cough, cough.

Mama said, "We will visit Dr. Hoot today."

Piku felt scared. Her flippers trembled. Her tummy felt wobbly.

"Will it hurt?" she whispered.

Mama held her flipper. "I see you feel scared," she said.

Piku nodded.

"It is okay to feel scared," Mama said. "Doctors help us feel better."

She told Piku a secret. "Brave wings flutter, but they still fly."

"Brave wings flutter, but they still fly," she said again.

"Brave wings flutter, but they still fly," she said one more time.

Piku repeated it softly. "Brave wings flutter, but they still fly."

At the clinic, Dr. Hoot was kind. He had a warm smile.

"Hello, Piku," he said. "Let me listen to your chest."

He put the cold stethoscope on her. Brrr!

Piku giggled. It tickled.

He looked in her ears. He looked in her throat. "Say ahh," he said.

"Ahh," said Piku.

"You are doing great," said Dr. Hoot.

Then came the medicine. It tasted like strawberries. Yum!

Piku felt brave. Her flippers stopped trembling. Her tummy felt calm.

"I did it, Mama!" she said.

"You were so brave," Mama said, giving Piku a big hug.

On the way home, it started to snow — soft, fluffy snowflakes. Piku caught one on her beak. She laughed.

That night, Piku snuggled in her nest. Her cough was already better. She had felt scared earlier. Now she felt brave and cozy.

Mama sang a soft lullaby. The snow fell quietly outside.

Piku closed her eyes. She was brave. She was loved.

Goodnight, Piku.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Doctors are helpers — and being brave means feeling scared and going anyway.*
