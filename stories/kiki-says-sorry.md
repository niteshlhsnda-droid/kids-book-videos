# Kiki Says Sorry

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Kiki was a playful kitten who loved to run and pounce. One afternoon, her friend Beni the bunny built a sandcastle — tall and beautiful, with three towers and a tiny shell flag. Kiki ran past too fast. CRASH! Her tail knocked the castle down, and sand flew everywhere.

Beni's ears drooped. He felt upset. Kiki's tummy felt twisty and her cheeks felt warm with sorriness. "I did not mean to," she whispered. Beni turned away.

Kiki sat very still. She wanted to run away — but Mama Cat had taught her something. She remembered: "Sorry heals when we say it true."

"Sorry heals when we say it true," she said to herself again. "Sorry heals when we say it true," she said one more time.

Kiki walked slowly to Beni and looked at him gently. "Beni, I am sorry I broke your castle," she said. "I ran too fast. I will be careful."

Beni looked at her. His ears lifted a little. "Thank you for saying sorry," he said. "It made me feel better." Then he smiled. "Want to build a new one with me?"

"Yes!" said Kiki. They built it together this time — Kiki carried the sand slowly while Beni shaped the towers. They made four towers now, even taller than before, and put the shell flag on top. It was the best castle ever.

That evening, Kiki told Mama Cat everything. "I said sorry," she said proudly. Mama Cat hugged her close. "Saying sorry takes courage," she said. Kiki purred softly. She had felt sorry earlier — now she felt light and happy. The moon rose over the garden. Kiki curled into her warm basket and closed her eyes. She was forgiven. She was loved. Goodnight, Kiki.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A sorry said with a true heart can mend more than the mistake.*
