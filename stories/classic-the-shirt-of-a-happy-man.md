# The Shirt of a Happy Man

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once there was a king named Arjun who had everything.

He had a palace with a hundred windows. He had chests full of gold, and the softest bed in the whole kingdom. Close your eyes — what do YOU think the softest bed in the whole kingdom felt like?

But King Arjun was sad — his honey-cakes tasted like dust, his crown felt too heavy for his head, and at night he tossed and turned in his big, soft bed.

"Why am I sad?" he asked his wise old minister. "I have everything!"

The minister tugged on his long white beard. "My king," said the old minister, "I know one way to chase your sadness away. Find a truly happy man. Bring me his shirt. Who do YOU think is the happiest person in the whole kingdom? Sleep just one night in the shirt of a truly happy man. Then your sadness will melt away, like snow melting in the warm sunshine."

So the king sent his messengers across the kingdom.

First, they visited a rich merchant with ten tall ships — striped sails bobbing up and down in the salty-smelling harbor. "Are you happy?" they asked.

"Happy?" sighed the merchant. "My ships might sink! My gold might be stolen! I am worried all the time!"

They visited a famous general. "Are you happy?" they asked.

"Happy?" said the general. "I am tired of fighting. I dream of battles every night!"

They visited a great singer, whose voice poured out sweet and warm, like honey on toast. "Are you happy?" they asked.

"Happy?" said the singer. "Everyone wants me to sing, sing, sing. I never get to rest!"

The messengers were losing hope. Then, on a green hill, they heard a sound — la-la-la!

A young shepherd boy was sitting under a tree, singing to his sheep, while the cool grass tickled his bare feet. His smile was so big it nearly touched his ears, and his laughter rang like little bells across the hill.

"Are you happy?" asked the messengers.

"The happiest!" said the boy, spinning round and round in the grass. "I have my sheep, I have my songs, and I have the whole blue sky!"

The messengers were so happy. "Quick! Give us your shirt! The king needs it!"

The boy stopped spinning. He looked down at himself.

And do you know what? The boy looked down at his old clothes and laughed. "I don't even own a shirt!"

What do you think the king did when he heard the happy boy had no shirt at all?

The messengers rode back to the palace and told the king everything.

King Arjun listened. A smile spread across his face. Then he laughed — a real, happy laugh, his first in a very long time.

"I understand now!" he said. "Happiness does not come from gold or palaces or shirts. It comes from loving the life you already have!"

From that day on, King Arjun sang while he worked, smelled the sweet roses in the garden, and smiled at everyone he met.

That night, King Arjun fell asleep in his own soft bed, with a little smile on his face.

The end. 🌙
