# The Four Friends

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a soft green forest, beside a sparkling stream, lived four very best friends.

Dara the deer had long, gentle legs and soft brown eyes. Kuhu the crow had shiny black feathers and a bright, clever mind. Tia the tortoise carried a round green shell on her back and walked slowly, slowly. And Mini the mouse was the smallest of all, with round ears and a quick pink nose.

Every morning, the four friends met under the big peepal tree.

Dara munched the sweet green grass. Kuhu told them all the news from the sky. Tia smiled her slow, sleepy smile. And Mini scampered around their feet, telling tiny jokes that made everyone giggle.

One sunny morning, Dara went to nibble fresh green grass near the edge of the forest. She did not see the soft rope net hidden among the leaves.

SNAP! The net closed around her legs, and Dara could not move at all.

"Oh no!" cried Dara. "I am caught in a net! Help! Help!"

High in the sky, Kuhu the crow heard her friend's cry. Down she flew, fast as the wind. "Hold on, Dara!" she called. "I will fetch Mini and Tia. We will help you!"

Kuhu flew to the peepal tree. "Mini! Tia! Dara is caught in a net! Come quickly!"

Mini scampered as fast as her tiny legs could go. Tia walked as fast as her slow legs could go — which was not very fast at all, but she never gave up.

Soon all three friends reached Dara. What do you think each friend will do to help? "Do not worry," said Mini. "My teeth are small, but very sharp. I will nibble the net, bit by bit."

Nibble, nibble, nibble went Mini, working away at the ropes.

"I will keep watch from the sky," said Kuhu, flying up high. "I will call out if anyone comes."

Tia had a clever plan too. She lay very still on the path, tucked her head inside her shell, and looked just like a smooth, round stone. "If the hunter comes," she said softly, "I will be a surprise that makes him stop and look."

Just then, the young hunter came walking down the path — the one who had set the net that morning. He stopped and stared.

There was the deer, caught in the net. There was the tiny mouse, nibbling the ropes to set her free. There was the crow, circling above, keeping watch. And there was the tortoise, lying so still and brave.

The hunter's eyes went wide. "Oh!" he said. "Look at these friends! They are all helping each other!"

His heart felt soft and warm — he had never seen such friendship.

"You know what?" said the hunter with a smile. "I do not want to catch anyone. Friendship like this is too precious to spoil."

He knelt down and gently untied the net himself. "There you go, little deer. You are free!"

Dara stepped out of the net and shook her soft brown legs. "Thank you!" she said.

Mini stopped nibbling. Kuhu flew down from the sky. Tia peeked her head out of her shell and smiled.

"From today," said the hunter, "I promise to be kind to all the animals. I will only come to the forest to watch you and say hello."

And he did. Every morning after that, the hunter walked past the peepal tree and waved.

And the four friends played together under the big peepal tree, happy and safe and free.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: True friends help each other, and kindness is stronger than any net.*
