# Ollie the Otter's Lost Pebble

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Ollie was a playful river otter who lived by the sparkling stream in Willowmere Wood. He had a favorite pebble — smooth and gray, with a white stripe. Ollie juggled it every day. Toss, catch, toss, catch! He kept it in a secret nook. It was his treasure.

One morning, Ollie went to his nook. The pebble was gone!

He looked under the leaves. No pebble. He looked behind the rocks. No pebble.

Ollie felt worried. His whiskers drooped. "My lucky pebble is lost!" he cried. He felt sad, and a tear plopped into the stream.

His friend Tammy the terrapin swam over — terrapins are excellent divers.

"Why the long face, Ollie?" asked Tammy.

"I lost my pebble," said Ollie. "It had a white stripe. It was my favorite."

Tammy smiled kindly. "Then we will search together," she said. "Treasure is sweeter when found together."

"Treasure is sweeter when found together."

Ollie repeated it softly. "Treasure is sweeter when found together."

It became their searching song. "Treasure is sweeter when found together."

First, they searched the shallow pool of Bluebell Pond. Ollie dove down. Tammy dove deeper. They peeked under every stone. No pebble.

Next, they searched the sandy bank. Ollie dug with his paws. Dig dig dig. Tammy sifted the sand. No pebble.

Ollie felt like crying. But Tammy said, "Treasure is sweeter when found together." So they searched one more place — the little waterfall pool. Ollie dove under the bubbles.

And there it was! The smooth gray pebble with the white stripe. It had washed downstream.

"FOUND IT!" shouted Ollie. He felt overjoyed. He felt relieved. He hugged Tammy tight.

Can you dive like Ollie? Take a deep breath. Now dive! Look left, look right. Found it!

Ollie juggled his pebble all afternoon. Toss, catch, toss, catch! Tammy clapped her flippers. Ollie gave Tammy a turn, and she juggled it too. They laughed and splashed. The pebble was even more special now.

"Treasure is sweeter when found together," they sang.

Ollie felt thankful. He felt happy.

When the sun set, the stream turned golden. Ollie placed his pebble in the nook while Tammy curled on a warm rock.

"Thank you for searching with me," whispered Ollie.

"Anytime, treasure friend," said Tammy.

Ollie closed his eyes. He heard the stream babbling softly. Bubble, splash, sleep. The moon shimmered on the water.

Sleep now, little Ollie.

The end. 🌙
