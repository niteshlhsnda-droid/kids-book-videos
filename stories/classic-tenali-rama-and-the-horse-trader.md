# Tenali Rama and the Horse Trader

*A witty Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

King Krishnadevaraya loved horses — fast horses, strong horses, handsome horses.

One day a trader arrived at the palace gates, leading a plain brown horse. "Your Majesty!" he cried. "This is no ordinary horse! This horse runs like the WIND itself! Worth a hundred gold coins — but for you, only eighty!"

The king's eyes shone. He paid the eighty gold coins at once.

Tenali Rama, the king's cleverest courtier, watched the horse amble slowly around the courtyard. It stopped to sniff a flower. It stopped to swish its tail. It did not look like the wind at all.

"Your Majesty," said Tenali with a bow, "may I test this wonderful wind-horse?"

"Of course!" said the king.

Tenali arranged a race around the palace pond: the trader's "wind-horse" against Moti — the palace's slowest, sleepiest old pony, who was mostly interested in lunch.

The trader puffed out his chest. "My horse will win before your pony has finished yawning!"

"Ready? Go!" Tenali waved his scarf.

The wind-horse trotted. It ambled. It paused to look at a butterfly. It stopped to sniff a marigold. Moti the pony, dreaming of carrots, plodded steadily past it — plod, plod, plod — and won!

The court burst into laughter. The king laughed till his crown wobbled. The trader's face turned the color of a ripe tomato.

"But — but — I said it runs like the wind!" stammered the trader.

Tenali smiled. "My friend, the wind is free — it blows for everyone, asking nothing. You charged the king eighty gold coins for it! A boast is only worth what it can prove."

The king laughed till his crown wobbled. "Tenali is right! Big boasts need big proof." He took back his gold coins and gave the trader one silver coin instead — "for a fine, honest, ordinary horse, honestly sold."

And Moti the pony got three extra carrots for winning the funniest race in the kingdom's history.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Big boasts need big proof — let your actions do the talking.*
