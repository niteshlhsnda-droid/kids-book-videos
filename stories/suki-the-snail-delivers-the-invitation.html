Suki the Snail Delivers the Invitation

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Suki was a tiny garden snail who lived under a cabbage leaf. She had a shiny spiral shell, and she carried it everywhere — slowly. Very, very slowly.

One morning, Suki found a letter. It was an invitation to the Summer Picnic! "Dear friends, come to the meadow at noon!"

Suki wanted to deliver it herself. Her friend Rosie the robin lived all the way across the garden. "I will take it to her!" Suki declared, and set off right away. Slide, glide, slide.

But snails are very, very slow, and the sun kept climbing higher. By the time Suki was only halfway there, worry had crept in.

"I am too slow," she sighed. "The picnic will start without me." Her eyestalks drooped with sadness.

Just then, Buster the bumblebee buzzed by and saw Suki's sad face. "Why so glum, Suki?" he asked.

"I cannot deliver this in time," said Suki. "I am too slow."

Buster landed gently on her shell. "Slow is not bad," he said kindly. "Slow and steady carries love."

Suki repeated it softly. "Slow and steady carries love." It became her walking song, and she sang it all the way: "Slow and steady carries love."

Buster had a kind idea. "I'll carry the letter for you," he offered. "But you come too — slow and steady."

Buzz! Buster zoomed the invitation straight to Rosie, who read it and chirped with joy. Then Buster flew back to Suki. "Hop on!" he called.

Suki climbed onto Buster's fuzzy back. Whee! They flew over the garden, and Suki had never flown before. Below her, the flowers looked like a rainbow quilt. Amazement and happiness bubbled up inside her. Slow and steady had carried love after all.

Can you fly like Suki and Buster? Spread your arms wide and buzz — bzzzz! Now land softly… slow and steady.

They arrived at the meadow just in time, where Rosie was already waiting. "You made it!" she chirped, hugging Suki gently.

The picnic was wonderful — strawberry sandwiches, dandelion tea, and Suki's own crisp lettuce leaf. Everyone shared everything. They all sang together: "Slow and steady carries love!" Suki felt proud — and so wonderfully included.

When the sun set, Suki rode home on Buster's fuzzy back and curled up under her cabbage leaf, her shell still warm from the day.

"Thank you for flying with me," whispered Suki.

"Thank you for teaching me slow," buzzed Buster.

Suki closed her eyes and dreamed of flying over rainbow flowers. Buzz, glide, sleep. The garden grew quiet, and Suki slept — slow and cozy.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Going slowly is just fine — especially when friends help you along the way.
