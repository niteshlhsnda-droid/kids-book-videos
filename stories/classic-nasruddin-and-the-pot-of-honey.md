# Nasruddin and the Pot of Honey

*A gentle Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One sunny morning, Nasruddin walked to the market with his donkey, clip-clop, clip-clop.

He had a few silver coins in his pocket and a big dream in his heart.

"I will buy a pot of golden honey," he said to his donkey, "and bake the sweetest honey cakes in the whole village!"

At the market, the honey seller showed him a big clay pot, full to the brim with golden honey. It smelled of sunshine and flowers. Nasruddin dipped one finger in and tasted it. Oh! It was sweet as sunshine and flowers all at once.

Nasruddin paid his coins, tied the pot carefully to the donkey, and set off for home, smiling all the way. All the way home he hummed a little song about honey cakes, warm from the oven, dripping with gold.

On the road, he met the baker, who was carrying a tray of fresh, warm bread.

"Good morning, Nasruddin! What is in your pot?" asked the baker.

"Golden honey!" said Nasruddin. "Here, taste a spoonful."

The baker tasted it and smiled. "Mmm! Sweet as sunshine!"

A little further on, Nasruddin met a little girl with a red ribbon in her hair.

"Nasruddin, what is that golden pot?" she asked.

"Honey, little one," said Nasruddin. "Here, taste a spoonful."

The little girl tasted it and giggled. "Yum! It tastes like flowers!"

Then Nasruddin met the old gardener, watering his roses.

"Nasruddin, is that honey?" asked the gardener, sniffing the sweet smell.

"It is," said Nasruddin. "Here, old friend, taste a spoonful."

The gardener tasted it and closed his eyes. "Ah! Sweet as summer itself!"

Nasruddin walked on, happy as a bird. But when he reached his little house, he lifted the lid of the pot and peeped inside.

Empty! Not a single drop of honey was left! He peeped again, just to be sure. Still empty!

Nasruddin looked at the empty pot. Then he looked at the sky. Then he laughed — a big, warm laugh.

"The honey is gone," he said to his donkey, "but my heart is full of sweet smiles. The baker smiled, the little girl giggled, and the gardener closed his eyes with joy. That is better than any honey cake!"

The next morning, the baker passed Nasruddin's house and called out, "Thank you for the honey, Nasruddin!" The little girl waved her red ribbon, and the old gardener sent him a rose, sweet as honey itself.

And that night, Nasruddin fell asleep with a smile on his face, dreaming of golden honey and happy friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing what you have makes the sweetness grow.*
