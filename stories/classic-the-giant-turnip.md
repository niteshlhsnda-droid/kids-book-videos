# The Giant Turnip

*A beloved Russian folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a little cottage with a red roof, there lived an old grandfather with a round, happy belly.

One spring morning, Grandfather planted a tiny turnip seed in his garden. He patted the soil and whispered, "Grow, little turnip, grow big and sweet!"

And oh — did it grow! It grew and grew and GREW, until it was the biggest turnip anyone had ever seen — as big as a pumpkin, as big as a barrel, as big as a baby elephant!

When autumn came, Grandfather rubbed his hands. "Time to pull up my giant turnip!"

He took hold of the green leaves. He pulled — heave! He pulled — ho!

But the turnip would not budge. Not one bit.

Grandfather called, "Grandmother! Come and help me!"

Grandmother came running in her flowered apron. She held Grandfather around the waist. They pulled together — heave! They pulled — ho!

But the turnip would not budge. Not one bit.

Grandmother called, "Granddaughter! Come and help us!"

Little Misha came skipping out with her ribbons flying. She held Grandmother around the waist. They pulled together — heave! They pulled — ho!

But the turnip would not budge. Not one bit.

Misha called, "Buddy! Come and help us!" — and Buddy the dog came bounding, bark, bark, bark! He held Misha's skirt in his teeth. They pulled together — heave! They pulled — ho!

But the turnip would not budge. Not one bit.

Buddy barked, "Mittens! Come and help us!" — and Mittens the cat came padding, soft as a whisper. She held Buddy's tail. They pulled together — heave! They pulled — ho!

But the turnip would not budge. Not one bit.

Mittens mewed, "Squeaky! Come and help us!" — and Squeaky the tiny mouse came hurrying, quick as a wink. She held Mittens' tail with her tiny paws.

And they ALL pulled together — Grandfather, Grandmother, Misha, Buddy, Mittens, and little Squeaky —

HEAVE! ... HO! ... HEAVE! ... HO! ...

POP!

Out came the giant turnip! It tumbled over and over in the garden — bump, bump, bump!

Everyone cheered! Everyone laughed! Even Squeaky the tiny mouse did a happy dance on top of the giant turnip.

That evening, Grandmother cooked the biggest turnip soup anyone had ever tasted. They all sat around the table — Grandfather, Grandmother, Misha, Buddy, Mittens, and Squeaky — and ate and ate until their tummies were round and happy.

And Grandfather raised his spoon and said, "Here's to Squeaky — for it was the tiniest pull that did it in the end!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When everyone pulls together, even the tiniest friend can make the biggest difference.*
