# Teju and the Friendly Shadow

On a bright sunny morning, little Teju ran out into the garden. His curly hair bounced as he ran, and his yellow T-shirt glowed in the sunshine. But something was following him.

Teju stopped. The something stopped too. He tiptoed behind the big mango tree and whispered, "It won't find me here!" But when he peeked out, there it was — a dark shape, just his size, standing right beside him.

Teju ran to the tap and splashed water on his face. The shape ran too. He jumped over the low garden wall. The shape jumped too. "It follows me everywhere!" he cried. "It's like a super secret spy!"

"What is all this running?" laughed Mama, coming out with the washing. Teju pointed down. "That thing! It copies everything I do!" Mama smiled. "That is your shadow, my love. It is not a spy. It is a friend who only wants to play."

Mama showed him. She waved, and her shadow waved. She twirled, and her shadow twirled. Teju waved slowly… and his shadow waved right back. He giggled. "Hello, shadow!"

In the low evening sun, his shadow stretched long across the garden like a sleepy giant, and Teju marched home for supper, his shadow marching beside him.

But that night, in his bedroom, something felt wrong. Mama switched the light off, and the room went dark — and his shadow was gone! "Mama!" Teju called. "My shadow has run away!" Mama switched the lamp on, and there it was again, beside his bed. Off — gone. On — back. "It only comes back when the light is on," said Teju, "but I'm scared of the dark!"

Mama sat on his bed and hugged him tight. "Then let us make the dark friendly," she said. She switched on his little night light, soft and golden, and held her hands up in its glow. On the wall, a rabbit hopped. A bird flapped its wings. Teju laughed and made a dog with his own hands, and his shadow-dog barked without a sound.

The next morning, Teju waited for Mama after school. "I'm bored," he said, kicking a stone. Then he remembered. He stood up tall, he jumped, he danced — and his shadow jumped and danced right along with him. He laughed all the way home.

That night, Teju left his little night light glowing, soft and golden. His shadow lay quietly beside his bed, ready for tomorrow's games. Teju pulled his blanket up to his chin and smiled. His shadow was not a spy at all. It was his very own friend, there whenever the sun or a little light shone.

Sleep now, little Teju.

The end. 🌙
