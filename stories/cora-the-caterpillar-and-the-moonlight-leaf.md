# Cora the Caterpillar and the Moonlight Leaf

*A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet garden, under a big, leafy bush, there lived a little caterpillar named Cora.

Cora was green and soft, with sixteen tiny feet that went pitter-patter as she crawled along the leaves. She loved munching fresh leaves. Crunch, crunch, crunch.

One night, when the moon was big and round, Cora crawled to the very top of her bush — and there she found something wonderful.

It was a leaf unlike any other leaf. It glowed softly, silver and gold, as if it had caught a piece of moonlight and kept it.

"Oh!" whispered Cora. "A moonlight leaf!"

She nibbled one tiny corner of the glowing leaf. It tasted like honey and cool night air. And as she nibbled, a soft glow spread over Cora herself, like a little lantern.

Cora was delighted. But then she heard a tiny voice. "Who is shining up there?"

It was a little ant, hurrying home in the dark, who had lost his way.

"Come up and see!" called Cora. "My leaf can light your path."

The ant climbed up, and by the glow of the moonlight leaf, he found his way home. "Thank you, Cora!" he said. "Your light is lovely."

Soon, a sleepy beetle came crawling by. "I cannot find my bed in the dark," he grumbled.

"Come and rest here," said Cora. "My leaf will keep you cosy."

The beetle curled up under the glowing leaf and fell fast asleep, snoring a tiny beetle snore.

Then a little moth fluttered down. "I am afraid of the dark," she whispered.

"Do not be afraid," said Cora. "Sit beside me, and we will watch the moon together."

So the moth sat beside Cora, and the ant came back with his friends, and the beetle snored softly, and the whole bush glowed like a little night-light in the garden.

Cora smiled. She had thought the moonlight leaf was a treat just for her. But sharing its light made it shine even brighter.

When morning came, the glow gently faded, and Cora curled up under an ordinary green leaf for a nap. She dreamed of moonlight, and of friends, and of the little light that grows bigger when you share it.

And every night after that, when the moon rose, Cora's leaf glowed again — a small, warm light for anyone in the garden who needed it.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A light shared shines brighter — kindness makes small gifts grow big.*
