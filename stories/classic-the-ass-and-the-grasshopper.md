# The Ass and the Grasshopper

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One warm summer morning, a little donkey named Dholu was munching grass in a sunny meadow.

Suddenly, he heard the sweetest music: chirp-chirp-chirrup! A band of green grasshoppers was singing in the tall grass, and their song was so lovely that Dholu stopped munching and listened with his long ears wiggling.

"Oh, how beautiful!" cried Dholu. "I wish I could sing like you! What do you eat to make your voices so sweet?"

The grasshoppers giggled. "We eat nothing but dew, dear Dholu — the tiny drops of water that sparkle on the leaves at dawn."

"Nothing but dew!" said Dholu. "Then I shall eat nothing but dew too, and I shall sing just like you!"

So the next morning, Dholu did not eat his hay. He did not eat his carrots. He only licked the dew off the leaves — lick, lick, lick — and waited for a beautiful song to come out of his mouth.

But when he opened his mouth, all that came out was a very hungry, very donkey-ish "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

By lunchtime, Dholu's tummy was rumbling louder than his song. "Rumble, grumble, rumble!" it said.

The grasshoppers hopped over, looking worried. "Dear Dholu, you look pale! When did you last eat properly?"

"Not since yesterday," admitted Dholu, his ears drooping. "But I wanted to sing like you."

The kindest grasshopper, a little one named Chirpy, laughed softly. "Oh, Dholu! Dew suits us because we are tiny grasshoppers. You are a big, strong donkey. You need hay and carrots and crunchy apples to be your best self!"

Dholu thought about that. Then he took a big, happy bite of a carrot — crunch! — and another of an apple — munch! — and he felt strong and cheerful again.

"Thank you, Chirpy," he said. "I do not need to sing like a grasshopper. I can bray like a donkey, and that is just right for me!"

And do you know what happened next? The grasshoppers sang their sweet song, and Dholu brayed along in his own deep, happy voice — hee-haw, chirp-chirp, hee-haw! — and together they made the funniest, friendliest meadow band anyone had ever heard.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What suits one creature may not suit another. Be happy being yourself.*
