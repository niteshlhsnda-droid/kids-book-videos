# Stone Soup

*A beloved world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, a kind traveller named Tomas walked down a long, dusty road.

He had walked all day, and his tummy was rumbling. At last he came to a little village, with neat little houses and a big old tree in the middle.

"Good evening!" said Tomas to the villagers. "I am travelling far, and I am very hungry. Might I have a little supper?"

But the villagers were shy, and each one thought, "I have only a little food. I must keep it for myself."

"I am sorry," said the baker. "I have no bread to spare."

"I am sorry," said the farmer. "I have no vegetables to spare."

"I am sorry," said everyone, and they hurried into their houses and shut their doors.

Tomas did not feel angry. He just smiled, took a smooth round stone from his pocket, and set a big pot over a fire under the old tree.

"What are you doing?" asked a little girl named Sara, peeking out from behind the tree.

"I am making stone soup," said Tomas. "It is the most delicious soup in the whole wide world. But it tastes even better with a little something extra."

Sara's eyes grew wide. "Stone soup?" she said. "I have never tasted stone soup!"

Soon, one villager peeked out of a window. Then another opened a door. The pot bubbled and sang over the fire.

"A pinch of salt would make it perfect," said Tomas, stirring thoughtfully.

"I have salt!" said the baker, hurrying out with a little shaker. "Here — a good pinch!"

"A carrot would make it sing," said Tomas, smiling.

"I have carrots!" said the farmer, hurrying out with three bright carrots. "Here — take them all!"

"Potatoes would make it hearty," said Tomas.

"I have potatoes!" cried another villager, running out with a basket.

Soon everyone was bringing something — onions, beans, herbs, and a loaf of warm bread. Everyone stirred, everyone sniffed, and everyone laughed.

At last, Tomas lifted the lid. A wonderful smell rose up like a warm hug.

"Soup is ready!" he called.

The villagers sat together under the old tree, sharing bowls of hot, tasty soup and warm bread. They talked and laughed long after the stars came out.

"This is the best soup I have ever tasted!" said the baker.

"And all it needed," said Tomas with a smile, "was everyone sharing just a little."

That night, the villagers gave Tomas the softest bed in the village. And the smooth round stone? Tomas washed it clean and gave it to Sara, to remember the night everyone shared.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When everyone shares a little, there is plenty for all.*
