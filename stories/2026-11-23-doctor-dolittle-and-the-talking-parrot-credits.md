# Credits — Doctor Dolittle and the Talking Parrot

- **Original title:** "The Story of Doctor Dolittle" (1920)
- **Author:** Hugh Lofting (1886–1947)
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *The Story of Doctor Dolittle* (eBook #501) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/501
- **License:** Public domain (first published 1920; Lofting died 1947)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, shortened to ten story beats from the opening chapters — the doctor's animal household in Puddleby, Polynesia teaching him the talk of animals, the midnight call about Old Prince's sore tooth, and the animals' thank-you gifts; the ending is softened into a cosy moonlit goodnight so the tale closes gently at bedtime.
