Urashima Taro

A beloved Japanese folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, beside the blue sea in Japan, there lived a kind young fisherman named Taro.

Every morning, Taro walked to the sandy beach with his fishing rod. He sang softly as he fished, and he always threw the smallest fish back into the waves.

One sunny morning, Taro heard a tiny cry from the edge of the water. "Help! Help!"

He ran over and saw a little turtle caught in the waves, tumbling round and round.

"Do not worry, little one," said Taro gently. He picked up the turtle and carried it to the deep, calm water.

"There you go," he said. "Swim safely home."

"Thank you, kind Taro," said the turtle in a soft voice. "One day, I will thank you properly."

Taro laughed and waved goodbye, then went back to his fishing.

The next morning, the little turtle was waiting on the beach. "Taro, please climb on my shell," it said. "I will take you to a wonderful place under the sea."

Taro was surprised, but he was not afraid. He climbed gently onto the turtle's big, smooth shell, and off they went, gliding down through the sparkling blue water.

Down they went, past pink coral and golden fish, past dancing seaweed and shining shells, until they came to a beautiful palace made of coral and pearls.

"This is the palace of the sea princess," said the turtle. "She has been waiting for you."

Inside, a kind princess with long dark hair and a dress like flowing waves smiled at Taro. Her name was Princess Coral.

"Welcome, kind fisherman," she said. "You saved my little turtle friend, and I am so happy to meet you."

She showed Taro her garden, where sea horses danced and starfish twinkled like stars, and they danced together to soft, gentle music.

It was the loveliest day he had ever known, but as the music played, he began to think of home.

"I love your palace," he said, "but I miss my village and my friends. May I go home now?"

"Of course," said Princess Coral, smiling kindly. "A kind heart always loves home best."

She gave him a shiny box, tied with a golden ribbon. "Take this gift," she said. "Keep it closed, and this happy day will stay with you always."

"Thank you," said Taro. "I will keep it closed, just as you say."

The little turtle carried him back up through the blue water to the sandy beach, where the sun was still shining, just as it had been that morning.

Taro walked home with the shiny box tucked safely under his arm, keeping it closed, just as Princess Coral had asked.

In his village, everyone gathered round to hear about the palace under the sea, the dancing sea horses, and the kind princess. He shared his happy day with his neighbours, and they all smiled together.

That night, Taro placed the shiny box on a shelf, where he could see it every day and remember the turtle, the princess, and the palace under the sea.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness is always remembered, and a kind heart always finds its way home.
