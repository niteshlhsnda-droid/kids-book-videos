# Wally the Walrus and the Wobbly Iceberg

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In the cold, blue sea near the top of the world, there lived a big, whiskery walrus named Wally.

Wally loved three things: crunchy fish, sunny naps, and his favourite iceberg.

Every afternoon, Wally would climb onto his iceberg, wiggle into a comfy spot, and take a long, happy nap. "Ahhh," he would sigh. "This is the best bed in the whole sea!"

But one morning, something was wrong. Wally climbed onto his iceberg — and it wobbled!

Wobble, wobble, wobble! The iceberg tipped to the left. Wally slid to the left. The iceberg tipped to the right. Wally slid to the right!

"Splash!" Down went Wally into the sea, with a big, surprised giggle.

His friend Gertie the gull, who was sitting on a nearby rock, laughed and laughed. "Oh, Wally! Your bed has the wobbles today!"

"One more try!" cried Wally. He climbed back onto the iceberg, took a big run-up, and flopped down right in the middle.

He tried the top. Too wobbly! He tried the side. Too slippery! He tried the very tippy-top. Too small!

Wobble, wobble, splash! Down he went again.

All the little penguins on the shore clapped their flippers and giggled. "Again, Wally! Again!"

"My iceberg is broken," sighed Wally, shaking the water from his whiskers. "Where will I nap now?"

Gertie flapped down beside him. "Your iceberg is not broken, Wally," she said. "It is just a wobbly one. But you know what never wobbles?"

"What?" asked Wally.

"The sea itself!" said Gertie. "Watch this!"

Gertie was right. Wally took a deep breath, lay back in the water, and floated on his back — his big tummy up, his whiskers twinkling in the sun, his flippers folded like a blanket.

The waves rocked him gently. Rock… rock… rock…

"One," counted Wally sleepily. "Two… three…"

A tiny silver fish swam by and tickled his whiskers. Wally giggled in his sleep.

"Ohhh," sighed Wally, with the biggest smile. "This is the softest bed in the whole world!"

The sun warmed his tummy. The waves sang a soft, sleepy song. The little penguins on the shore clapped their flippers. And Wally the walrus floated and floated, happy as can be.

From that day on, Wally still loved his iceberg — but when it wobbled, he did not mind one bit. He simply floated on his back, and let the sea rock him to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sometimes the best bed is the one you already have.*
