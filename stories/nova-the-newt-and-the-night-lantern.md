# Nova the Newt and the Night Lantern

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a mossy corner of the forest pond lived a newt named Nova — no bigger than a thumb, her skin shimmering green and gold like sunlight on water.

Every evening, when the other animals padded off to bed, Nova would sit on her favorite lily pad and watch.

She watched the old toad hop home in the dark, bumping into roots. Bonk!

Then the field mouse came hurrying home, tripping over twigs. Trip-trap!

"The night path is too dark for my friends," said Nova. "Someone should light the way."

So Nova thought and thought — until a bright little plan popped into her head.

She waited until the moon rose high. Then she climbed up the tall reeds and gathered the biggest, roundest dewdrops — plip, plip, plip — one by one.

Each one sat cool and round in her little hands, like a marble made of moonlight.

She hung each dewdrop on the tip of a leaf along the path. And when the moonlight touched them, they glowed!

Tiny lanterns. Dozens of them. All along the winding path through the ferns.

The night air smelled of wet leaves, and far away a frog sang low — rribbit… rribbit…

Shut your eyes — can you see them twinkling?

The next evening, the old toad came hopping along. "Well, well!" he said, stopping to look. "What is this lovely light?"

"Your lanterns, Mr. Toad," said Nova, bowing. "So you will not bonk into the roots."

The toad blinked and blinked, and his eyes shone happy. "No one has ever lit my way home before," he said softly.

Then came the field mouse, hurrying. "Oh!" she squeaked. "It is like walking through stars!"

One by one, all the night animals came — the hedgehog, the shrew, the sleepy dormouse — and every single one smiled at the glowing dewdrop lanterns.

From that night on, Nova hung her lanterns every evening. It became the best part of her whole day.

And when the last friend had gone safely home, Nova curled up on her lily pad. She pulled a soft leaf over herself like a blanket, and drifted off to sleep — warm and sleepy with happiness.

The pond was quiet. The lanterns swayed gently in the night breeze.

And every light seemed to whisper, "Goodnight… goodnight…"

Maybe one of them is whispering goodnight to you, too.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little light, shared with friends, makes the whole night cozy.*
