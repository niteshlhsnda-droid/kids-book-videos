Bramble the Bear's Bedtime Parade

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Bramble was a big brown bear, soft as a warm blanket.

He loved parades. Loud parades with drums and trumpets!

But most of all, Bramble loved the quiet parade. The bedtime parade.

Every evening, when the sun tucked behind the hills, Bramble picked up his little lantern.

Tap, tap, tap… his soft paws padded on the soft green moss.

"Tap, tap, tap… sleep tight, sleep tight," he whispered.

First he came to Pippa the rabbit's burrow.

Pippa was already in her pajamas, rubbing her eyes.

"Is it time?" she yawned.

"It's time." Bramble smiled.

She hopped in line. Tap, tap, tap… they tiptoed past the whispering ferns.

Shhh… said the ferns and the trees.

"Tap, tap, tap… sleep tight, sleep tight," they whispered together.

Next was Milo the mouse, peeking out of his tiny door in the oak tree.

He climbed onto Bramble's warm shoulder and held on tight.

"Can I ride?" he squeaked.

"You may," said Bramble.

Tap, tap, tap… tap, tap, tap…

Then came Hoot the baby owl, nodding on his branch.

Hoot was the sleepiest owl in the whole forest. His eyelids were droopy-droopy.

"I'm too tired to fly," he hooted softly.

Bramble tucked him gently under one big paw.

"Tap, tap, tap… sleep tight, sleep tight," they all whispered.

Last was Tilly the turtle, slow and smiley, waiting by the pond.

She joined the end of the line. Tap… tap… tap…

Now the parade wandered through the woods, softer with every step.

First they stopped at Pippa's burrow.

"Sleep tight," everyone whispered.

Hug. Yawn. "Sleep tight," said Pippa.

Tap, tap… a little tap now.

At the oak tree, Milo curled into his acorn bed.

"Sleep tight," everyone whispered.

Hug. Yawn. "Sleep tight," squeaked Milo.

Tap… even softer.

Bramble set Hoot down in his nest of feathers.

Hoot was already dreaming. "Sleep tight," breathed Bramble.

Then only Bramble and Tilly were left, marching on tiptoe.

At the pond, Tilly sank slowly into the soft mud.

"Sleep tight," whispered Bramble.

"Sleep… tight…" sighed Tilly, closing her eyes.

Only Bramble and his lantern were left now.

What do YOU think Bramble dreams about?

He climbed into his cozy den and pulled his blanket of leaves up to his chin.

He leaned toward the lantern and blew — pffff!

The light went out like a sleepy sigh.

"Tap, tap, tap… sleep tight, sleep tight," Bramble whispered one last time.

He curled up, round and warm, and drifted into dreams of tomorrow's parade.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Saying goodnight to the ones we love is the happiest part of the day.
