# The Quail and the Fire

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green forest full of tall trees, there lived a tiny quail named Choti. She was no bigger than a mango, but she had a brave little heart and a soft, speckled coat.

One hot afternoon, Choti smelled smoke. She looked up and saw orange flames flickering at the edge of the forest, where dry leaves had caught the sun's heat. The flames crackled and crept closer to the trees where all the birds lived.

"Fire! Fire!" chirped Choti. All the birds fluttered up into the sky. The big birds flew far away, flapping their strong wings. "We cannot stop a fire," they cried. "It is far too big for us!"

But Choti did not fly away. She looked at the little stream that bubbled through the forest. She dipped her tiny wings in the cool water, flew back to the flames, and shook the drops over them. Pssst! The drops hissed and vanished.

Again and again Choti flew to the stream and back. Dip, fly, shake. Dip, fly, shake. Her wings grew tired, and her little legs ached, but she kept going.

"Little quail, why do you try?" called a big eagle from high above. "Your drops are so small, and the fire is so big!"

"Because this is my home," said Choti, panting, "and my friends live here. If I do nothing, the fire will take it all. If I try, at least I am helping."

The eagle watched her for a long time. Her drops were small, but her heart was huge. Slowly, one by one, the other birds came back. The sparrows dipped their wings. The pigeons dipped their tails. Even the big eagle dipped his great wings and shook the water down.

Hundreds of drops pattered onto the flames like a soft rain. Pssst, pssst, pssst! The flames grew smaller and smaller, until only a thin ribbon of smoke curled up into the sky.

And just then, soft grey clouds gathered overhead. Plip, plop, plip! Real rain began to fall — gentle, cool rain that washed the forest clean and put out the very last ember.

All the birds cheered. "Hurrah for Choti!" they sang. "The littlest bird had the biggest heart!"

That evening, the forest smelled fresh and green again. Choti snuggled into her nest under a soft fern, tired and happy, while the rain sang its lullaby on the leaves above her.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even the smallest helper can make a big difference when they keep trying.*
