# Oscar the Owl and the Sleepy Lighthouse

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High on a rocky hill, beside the soft blue sea, there stood a tall white lighthouse.

And inside the lighthouse lived a kind little owl named Oscar.

Oscar had big, round eyes, soft brown feathers, and a gentle voice.

Every evening, Oscar sat by the window and watched the sun sink slowly into the sea.

Then, when the stars came out, the lighthouse lamp would glow, bright and warm, guiding the little boats safely home.

"Twinkle, twinkle, little lamp," Oscar would hoot, softly. "Guide the boats home, all safe and sound."

The fishermen waved to Oscar from their boats, for the lighthouse was their friend.

One foggy night, thick grey fog rolled in from the sea, soft as a blanket.

Oscar blinked his big round eyes. He could not see the boats. He could not see the shore.

Then, flicker, flicker — the lighthouse lamp went out!

"Oh no!" whispered Oscar. "The lamp has gone to sleep, and the boats are still out at sea."

Oscar flapped his soft wings and flew to the top of the lighthouse. He looked out at the dark, foggy sea.

Far away, he heard a little boat calling, "Toot, toot! Where is the light? We cannot find our way home."

Oscar took a deep breath and thought hard.

"I cannot make the lamp shine," he said, "but I can sing. My song can guide the boats home."

So Oscar opened his beak and sang a gentle guiding song.

"Hoo, hoo, hoo," he hooted, soft and slow. "Follow my song, little boats. Follow my song home."

His song floated out over the sea, soft as a feather, sweet as a lullaby.

The little boats listened. "There!" said the captain, with a smile. "I hear Oscar! Follow his song!"

Slowly, the boats turned toward the sound, gliding gently through the fog.

Then the moon peeked out from behind a cloud. She saw Oscar singing, all alone in the dark.

"What a kind little owl," whispered the moon. "I will help him."

The moon poured her soft silver light over the sea, making a glowing path across the waves.

"Follow the moonlight, little boats," hooted Oscar, happily. "Follow the light, and follow my song."

The boats sailed along the silver path, one by one, safe and steady.

Soon, the first boat reached the shore. Then the second. Then the third.

"Thank you, Oscar!" called the fishermen, waving from their boats. "Your song brought us home!"

Oscar smiled, and his big round eyes sparkled with joy.

Then, with a soft flicker, the lighthouse lamp woke up again. It glowed bright and warm, like a sleepy friend rubbing its eyes.

"Welcome back, little lamp," hooted Oscar, softly. "You had a good rest."

The fog lifted, and the stars came out, twinkling above the sea.

Oscar tucked his wings around himself and settled down in his cozy window nook.

The moon sang him a soft goodnight song, and the sea hummed a gentle lullaby.

One by one, the boats rocked to sleep by the shore, safe and sound, all the way home.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even a small, gentle song can guide friends safely home.*
