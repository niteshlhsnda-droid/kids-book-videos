# Chami the Little Chameleon and the Garden of Colours

In a sunny garden full of soft grass and tall flowers, there lived a little chameleon named Chami.

Chami was small, with big round eyes, a tiny curled tail, and skin that changed colour with her feelings. When she felt calm, she was leafy green. When she felt happy, she turned soft pink. And when something wonderful happened, she glowed warm gold.

Today was Garden Singing Day. At sunset, all the garden friends would gather under the big rose bush and sing together. Chami loved listening to them sing. But today, her tummy felt fluttery, and her skin had turned a worried grey.

"My voice is too small," she whispered to a daisy. "And squeaky. What if everyone laughs?"

So when the sun began to sink, Chami hid behind a big green leaf and peeked out with one round eye.

Just then, Bella the butterfly fluttered down beside her. Bella's wings were soft blue, like the morning sky. "There you are," she said, settling gently on the leaf. "I saved you a dewdrop. It is the sweetest one."

Chami sipped the dewdrop. It was cool and sweet. Bella did not ask her to hurry. She just sat beside her and hummed a slow, gentle tune, and after a while, Chami's worried grey began to soften into green.

Under the rose bush, the singing began. A bumblebee buzzed a low, buzzy note. A ladybird tapped a tiny beat on a leaf. Then a little sparrow opened his beak and sang a wobbly note that wobbled all the way up and all the way down again.

Everyone smiled. The sparrow smiled too, and sang another wobbly note, and this time the bee buzzed along with him.

Chami felt her tummy flutter again, but something else fluttered too — a small, brave feeling, right in the middle. She took a deep breath, stepped out from behind her leaf, and sang.

Her voice was small, and a little bit squeaky, and it was hers. And as she sang, her colours bloomed — pink for happy, gold for brave, green for calm — all at once, like a tiny rainbow sitting on a leaf.

The garden friends sang with her, and the evening filled with music and colour. The sparrow's wobbly notes, the bee's buzzy hum, Bella's soft fluttering wings — it all fit together, like the colours of a rainbow.

When the last song faded, the moon rose over the garden. Chami curled up on her favourite leaf, and her bright colours softened, one by one, into sleepy blue.

Sleep now, little Chami.

The end. 🌙
