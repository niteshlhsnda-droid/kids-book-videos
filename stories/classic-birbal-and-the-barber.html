Birbal and the Barber

A merry Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in the grand city of Agra, there lived the wise Emperor Akbar and his clever minister Birbal.

One morning, the royal barber, Chotu, was trimming the Emperor's beard. Snip, snip went the scissors.

As he worked, Chotu began to boast. "Your Majesty," he said proudly, "I am the greatest man in all of Agra!"

Akbar raised one eyebrow. "Oh? Greater than my brave soldiers? Greater than my wise Birbal?"

"Greater than everyone!" said Chotu, puffing out his chest. "For every single morning, even YOU bow your head before ME!"

The courtiers gasped. Birbal smiled his clever smile.

"Is that true, Birbal?" asked the Emperor, stroking his half-trimmed beard.

"Let us find out," said Birbal calmly. "Chotu, please show us. Ask His Majesty to bow his head before you, exactly as you say he does every morning."

Chotu gulped. He turned to the Emperor and said in a tiny voice, "Your... Your Majesty... would you please bow your head... so I can finish your beard?"

The Emperor bent his head — and Chotu quickly snipped the last hairs. Snip, snip.

"There!" said Birbal, clapping his hands. "You see, Your Majesty? Chotu is right! You DO bow your head before him every morning!"

The whole court burst out laughing — even the Emperor chuckled into his beard.

"But," said Birbal, turning to Chotu with a twinkle in his eye, "tell me, Chotu — when the Emperor bows his head, is it because you are greater? Or is it because he is KIND enough to let you do your work?"

Chotu's face turned as red as a tomato. "It... it is his kindness," he mumbled. "I was only boasting. Forgive me, Your Majesty."

Akbar laughed heartily. "No harm done, Chotu! You have given us all a good laugh this morning. But remember — a clever tongue is a gift. Use it to make people smile, not to make yourself big."

From that day on, Chotu the barber still trimmed the Emperor's beard every morning — snip, snip. But he never boasted again. Instead, he told funny stories that made the whole court giggle.

And whenever anyone started to boast, Birbal would smile and say, "Careful — or the Emperor may have to bow to YOU tomorrow morning!"

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Clever words are for making people smile, not for making yourself seem big.
