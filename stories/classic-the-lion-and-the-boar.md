# The Lion and the Boar

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On a hot summer day, the forest spring was the coolest, loveliest place in all the woods. Its water sparkled like glass, and soft grass grew all around it.

Leo the lion came padding down the path, his tongue out and his throat dry. "At last, a drink!" he roared happily.

But at the very same moment, Balu the boar came trotting down the other path, just as thirsty. "At last, a drink!" he grunted happily.

They reached the spring together, nose to nose.

"I was here first!" said Leo, stamping a big paw.

"No, I was here first!" said Balu, stamping a little hoof.

"I am the king of the forest," said Leo, "so I should drink first!"

"And I am the strongest boar in the woods," said Balu, "so I should drink first!"

They glared at each other. Leo raised a paw. Balu lowered his head. They were just about to have a great big quarrel when a shadow passed overhead.

Circling high above them were two big vultures, waiting and watching, hoping the lion and the boar would fight and tire each other out.

Leo looked up. Balu looked up. They looked at each other again, and both of them felt a little silly.

"Wait a moment," said Leo slowly. "If we fight, those vultures will be the only ones who are happy. And we will both still be thirsty!"

"You are right," laughed Balu. "A drink tastes much better when it is shared. Let us drink together, side by side."

So Leo knelt on one side of the spring, and Balu knelt on the other side, and they drank the cool, clear water together. Ahh! It was the best drink in the whole forest.

When they had finished, they splashed their paws in the water and laughed. The vultures, seeing no quarrel at all, flapped away to find their supper somewhere else.

"Come back tomorrow," said Leo, "and we will drink together again."

"I will," said Balu, "and I will bring sweet roots to share."

And from that day on, the lion and the boar were the best of friends. Every hot afternoon they met at the spring, drank side by side, and told each other stories until the sun went down.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing a drink is much better than fighting over it — friends always win together.*
