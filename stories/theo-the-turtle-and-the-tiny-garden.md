# Theo the Turtle and the Tiny Garden

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet corner of the meadow, behind the old stone wall, there lived a little turtle named Theo.

Theo was slow. Very slow. When his friends raced to the pond, Theo was still tying his tiny shoes. When they raced back, Theo was only just arriving!

"Hurry up, Theo!" his friends would call. "You are always so SLOW!"

Theo did not mind too much. He liked going slow. Slow meant he could see the tiny ants marching. Slow meant he could smell the sweet clover flowers. Slow meant he could hear the bees humming their sleepy songs.

One spring morning, Theo found a little patch of empty earth behind the stone wall. It was quiet. It was sunny. It was just right.

"I am going to grow a garden," said Theo.

His friend Ravi the rabbit laughed. "A garden? That will take forever, Theo! You are far too slow!"

"Maybe," said Theo with a smile. "But slow is just fine for a garden."

So Theo began. He dug the earth with his little claws — dig, dig, dig. He planted tiny seeds — pat, pat, pat. He watered them with drops of water from a little leaf cup — drip, drip, drip.

Every day, Theo visited his garden. He pulled out the weeds, one by one. He talked to his little plants. "Grow, little ones," he would say. "Take your time. There is no hurry at all."

Ravi the rabbit raced past every day. "Is your garden ready yet, Theo?" he would call, without even stopping.

"Not yet," Theo would say, smiling. "Soon."

Lulu the lark flew past every day. "Any flowers yet, Theo?" she would chirp.

"Not yet," Theo would say, smiling. "Soon."

And then, one morning, Theo woke up and peeped out of his shell — and there they were!

Tiny green shoots! All over his little garden! They had pushed up through the earth while he was sleeping!

Day by day, the shoots grew taller. Leaves opened up like little green umbrellas. And then — oh! — the flowers began to open!

First one pink flower. Then two yellow ones. Then a whole row of purple ones, nodding in the breeze!

Theo's garden was the prettiest garden in the whole meadow!

Ravi the rabbit came hopping over. "Oh, Theo!" he cried. "Your garden is BEAUTIFUL! Can I sit in it?"

"Of course," said Theo. "Would you like some clover tea?"

Lulu the lark came fluttering down. "Oh, Theo! May I sing in your garden?"

"Of course," said Theo. "Your songs make the flowers grow even taller!"

Soon all the animals came to Theo's garden. They sat among the flowers, drank clover tea, and listened to Lulu's songs. And nobody was in a hurry at all.

"Theo," said Ravi the rabbit, "I am sorry I laughed at you. Your slow garden is the very best garden!"

Theo smiled his slow, happy smile. "You see," he said, "slow and steady grows the sweetest garden of all."

And from that day on, whenever anyone in the meadow was in a hurry, they would stop by Theo's garden, sit among the flowers, and remember — some of the very best things take their time.

The end. 🌙
