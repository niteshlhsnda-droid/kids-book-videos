# Gus the Goose and the Giggly Pond

*A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a little pond behind a green hill, there lived a big white goose named Gus.

Gus had a long, curvy neck, orange feet that went slap, slap, slap on the grass, and a laugh that went "Honk, honk, honk!" whenever anything made him happy.

And Gus was happy a lot — because his pond was the giggliest pond in the whole world. The water giggled when the wind tickled it. The reeds giggled when the dragonflies landed on them. Even the little fish giggled, blowing bubbles that went pop, pop, pop!

But one morning, Gus woke up and something was wrong. The pond was quiet. Too quiet.

The water did not giggle. The reeds did not giggle. The little fish did not blow a single bubble.

"Oh no!" honked Gus. "Where has all the giggling gone?"

He waddled around the pond — slap, slap, slap — looking for the giggles. He looked under the lily pads. He looked behind the reeds. He looked in the mud. No giggles anywhere!

Gus sat down on the bank and sighed a big, sad sigh. "Hooooonk."

Just then, his friend Piku the little brown duck came waddling over. "Gus, why are you so sad?" she quacked.

"The pond has lost its giggles," said Gus. "And I do not know how to bring them back."

Piku thought hard. "Maybe the giggles are hiding," she said. "Let us try to make the pond laugh!"

So Gus and Piku tried everything. Gus did a silly dance on the bank — waddle, waddle, spin! Piku did a funny quack — quack, quack, quack-quack! They told each other their silliest jokes.

"What do you call a fish without eyes?" quacked Piku.

"I do not know!" honked Gus. "What?"

"A fsh!" said Piku, and they both laughed so hard that Gus nearly fell into the pond.

And do you know what? The pond heard them laughing. The water rippled — giggle, giggle. The reeds swayed — giggle, giggle. The little fish blew a bubble — pop! — and then another, and another!

"The giggles are coming back!" honked Gus, and he laughed even harder. "Honk, honk, honk!"

Soon the whole pond was giggling again. The water giggled, the reeds giggled, and the little fish blew bubbles that went pop, pop, pop, all the way up to the sky.

That evening, as the sun went down and the stars came out, Gus floated on the pond and listened to the giggles all around him.

"You know what, Piku?" he honked softly. "I think the giggles were never really lost. They were just waiting for someone to laugh first."

"And you laughed first, Gus," quacked Piku. "That is the very best kind of brave."

Gus smiled, tucked his beak under his wing, and fell asleep to the sound of his giggly pond — giggle, giggle, giggle — all night long.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Laughter is catching — when you laugh first, the whole world giggles with you.*
