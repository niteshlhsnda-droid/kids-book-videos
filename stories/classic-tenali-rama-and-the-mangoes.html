Tenali Rama and the Mangoes

A timeless Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in the kingdom of Vijayanagara, there lived a wise and funny man named Tenali Rama.

Tenali Rama was the king's favourite friend, because he could solve any puzzle with a smile.

Now, King Krishna Deva Raya loved mangoes more than anything in the world. In his royal garden grew the sweetest mango trees, and every summer their branches bent low with golden fruit.

But one summer, something strange began to happen.

Every morning, when the gardener counted the mangoes, some were missing!

"Three mangoes gone!" he cried on Monday.

"Five mangoes gone!" he cried on Tuesday.

"Seven mangoes gone!" he cried on Wednesday.

The king was most upset. He set guards all around the garden, day and night. But still, every morning, mangoes were missing!

"Tenali Rama!" said the king. "Find my mango thief, or there will be no mangoes left for anyone!"

"Do not worry, my king," said Tenali Rama, with a twinkle in his eye. "I will keep watch tonight myself."

That night, when the moon was round and bright, Tenali Rama wrapped a soft blanket around his shoulders and settled down behind the biggest mango tree.

He counted the stars — one, two, three… all the way to a hundred. He watched a little firefly blink on and off, on and off, like a tiny lantern. He listened to the crickets singing their sleepy song.

He waited… and waited… and waited…

Just after midnight, he heard a soft rustle. A dark figure crept into the garden, looked left, looked right, and reached up to pick a ripe, golden mango.

"Aha!" cried Tenali Rama, jumping out. "Got you, mango thief!"

The figure turned around — and Tenali Rama's eyes went wide with surprise.

It was the king himself!

"Your Majesty!" laughed Tenali Rama. "You are the mango thief!"

The king laughed too, so hard that his crown wobbled. "Do not tell anyone, Tenali! I love my mangoes so much that I cannot wait for morning!"

Tenali Rama laughed and laughed. "My king, if you love mangoes so much, why not share them? A mango eaten alone is sweet — but a mango shared is sweeter still!"

The king thought for a moment. Then he smiled a big, happy smile.

"You are right, Tenali Rama!" he said. "From today, every morning, the garden's mangoes will be shared with everyone in the kingdom!"

And so, the next morning, baskets of golden mangoes went to every home in Vijayanagara. Children laughed, grandmothers smiled, and the whole kingdom tasted the sweetest mangoes of all. And the king himself handed the very first mango to Tenali Rama, with a wink and a smile.

And the king? He still sneaked into the garden sometimes — but now he brought Tenali Rama with him, and they shared the mangoes together, laughing under the moon.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A mango eaten alone is sweet — but a mango shared is sweeter still.
