The Brahmin, the Thief and the Ghost

A wise Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a little village with neem trees and mud-brick walls, there lived a kind, wise man — a Brahmin named Mohan.

Mohan was gentle and wise. He fed the sparrows every morning, and the village children said the sparrows sang his name. Little round-bellied sparrows would hop onto his windowsill, and Mohan would scatter grains for them, humming back in his soft, low voice.

One evening, the villagers gave Mohan a gift: a gentle little cow, soft-eyed and sweet, with a little bell that went ting-a-ling.

Mohan was walking home through the forest at dusk, while crickets sang their evening song, when two shadows began to follow him.

The first shadow was Monty, a sneaky thief. "That cow would make me rich!" whispered Monty.

The second shadow was Bhootu, a grumbly ghost. Bhootu did not want the cow at all — he only wanted to give Mohan a good scare. (But Bhootu was a very silly ghost: he kept tripping over his own see-through feet, and his scare-face always came out looking like a hiccup.)

Monty whispered to Bhootu, "Let me steal the cow first!"

Bhootu whispered back, "No! Let me scare him first!"

Soon the two shadows were arguing — loudly!

"Let ME go first!" said Monty.

"No, let ME go first!" said Bhootu.

Mohan heard them, of course. But Mohan did not run. He kept walking, calm as moonlight, and a small clever smile grew on his face.

What was Mohan planning? Let's listen…

At last he stopped and turned around. "Welcome, friends!" he called out sweetly. "I can hear you both. Will you walk with me a little way?"

Monty and Bhootu froze. Nobody had ever called them "friends" before!

Mohan went on, "I am going to fetch you both warm milk from my cow — fresh and frothy. But first, tell me one thing: which of you is the stronger and the cleverer?"

Monty puffed up his chest. "ME! I can sneak past any door!"

Bhootu wobbled proudly. "ME! I can scare anyone with one look!" — then he tripped over his own foot and fell with a soft, see-through thud.

"I am stronger!" cried Monty.

"I am cleverer!" cried Bhootu.

"I can creep without making a sound!" said Monty.

"I can float without any feet!" said Bhootu — and to prove it, he tried to float, wobbled, and sat down hard on thin air.

"Stronger!" "Cleverer!" "Stronger!" "Cleverer!"

While they bickered and boasted, Mohan quietly turned down a narrow side path, lined with fireflies blinking like tiny lamps. He walked slowly so his little cow could keep up, and her bell went ting-a-ling, ting-a-ling, all the way home.

When the moon rose high and round, the two were still there, arguing in the middle of the forest.

"Stronger!" "Cleverer!" "Stronger!" "Cleverer!"

They are probably still arguing. But Mohan and his little cow were already home, drinking warm milk under the stars — safe, snug, and smiling.

And the sparrows sang Mohan's name that night, soft and sweet, like a lullaby.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A calm, clever mind outwits even two quarrelsome foes.
