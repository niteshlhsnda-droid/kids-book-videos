# Credits — "Thumbelina and the Swallow's Song"

- **Original title:** "Thumbelina" (Danish: "Tommelise", 1835)
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated in watercolor style for this project.
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1597/1597-h/1597-h.htm (Project Gutenberg, *Andersen's Fairy Tales*, eBook #1597, containing "Thumbelina")
- **License:** Public domain (Andersen died 1875; the tale was first published in 1835)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (449 words, bedtime tone). The tulip birth, walnut-shell bed, toad and lily pad, fishes freeing the leaf boat, field mouse's winter shelter, the mole's dark tunnel, Thumbelina nursing the hurt swallow in secret, the spring flight over the sea, and the flower prince's welcome are kept from the original; frightening and grown-up passages are softened, and the ending closes with a cozy goodnight — the lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
