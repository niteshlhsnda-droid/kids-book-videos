The Talking Eggs

A warm world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, in a little village at the edge of a green wood, there lived two sisters.

Maya was kind and gentle, always ready with a smile. Riya was grumpy and cross, and she hardly ever smiled at all.

One sunny morning, the two sisters went into the woods to gather firewood.

Deep among the trees, they met a kind old woman, sitting beside a little basket of eggs.

"Good morning, little ones," said the old woman, with a warm smile. "Would you help an old woman carry her basket home?"

"Of course!" said Maya at once. "I would love to help."

"Hmph," said Riya. "Why should we carry your basket? We have our own work to do."

But Maya took one handle of the basket, and the old woman took the other, and Riya followed behind, grumbling all the way.

As they walked, Maya heard a tiny voice, soft as a whisper. "Take us home and listen, take us home and listen."

Maya looked down. The eggs in the basket were talking! The little brown eggs and the little white eggs were whispering, "Take us home and listen, take us home and listen."

Maya smiled. "Did you hear that?" she asked. "The eggs are talking!"

"Hmph," said Riya. "Eggs do not talk. You are imagining things."

But Maya listened carefully, and she believed what she heard.

When they reached the old woman's little cottage, the old woman smiled at Maya. "Thank you, kind child," she said. "Now take the eggs home with you, and listen to them."

So Maya carried the basket of talking eggs home, and she listened, just as they had asked.

"Open us gently," whispered the eggs, "and look inside."

Maya opened the eggs, one by one, very gently. And inside each egg was a sweet surprise! One held a little honey cake. One held a juicy plum. One held a shiny red toffee. Another held a tiny ginger biscuit.

The eggs filled Maya's basket with sweet treats, one after another, until the basket was full to the brim!

"Oh!" cried Maya, clapping her hands. "How wonderful! Thank you, little eggs!"

Riya stared at the basket full of treats. "I want treats too!" she said. "Why did the eggs not talk to me?"

"Because you did not listen," said Maya kindly. "The eggs were talking all along. You only have to stop and listen."

Riya thought about this. She took a deep breath, and she closed her eyes, and she listened. And this time, she heard it too — the soft little whisper, "Take us home and listen."

"Oh!" said Riya. "I hear them! I hear them now!"

From that day on, Riya learned to listen — to the eggs, to the birds, to her sister, and to her own kind heart.

And the two sisters shared the sweet treats together, bite by bite, and they laughed and laughed under the old oak tree, until the sun went down and the stars came out.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: If you listen with a kind heart, you will hear wonderful things.
