Sable the Squirrel Shares One Acorn

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Sable was a red squirrel who lived in a tall oak tree. She loved acorns — she collected them every day, hid them in hollows, and counted them every night. One, two, three, all the way to twenty. Her acorns were her treasure.

One chilly morning, her friend Chip the chipmunk knocked. Knock knock knock.

"Hello, Sable!" said Chip. "I am so hungry. My store is empty. May I have just one acorn?"

Sable felt nervous. Her tummy twisted. What if she ran out? What if winter was long?

"I need them all," she whispered.

Chip's smile faded. He turned to go.

Sable felt sad. She did not like seeing Chip hungry.

Sable's grandmother had told her something. "Sharing makes the happy grow," Grandma used to say.

"Sharing makes the happy grow." Sable said it out loud.

"Sharing makes the happy grow." It became her brave thought.

"Sharing makes the happy grow."

Sable looked at her pile of acorns — twenty acorns. She picked the biggest, shiniest one.

"Wait, Chip!" she called. "Here is an acorn for you."

Chip's eyes went wide. "Really? For me?"

Sable nodded. She felt scared, but she smiled.

Chip took a big crunchy bite. "Mmm! Delicious!" he said.

Sable felt warm. She felt happy. Sharing felt better than keeping.

Can you count acorns with Sable? One shiny acorn for Chip. One for you, one for me. Count them: one, two, three!

Chip shared his blackberry with Sable. They ate together on the branch. Crunch, munch, giggle.

Sable gave Chip one more acorn. She still had eighteen left — plenty for winter.

"Sharing makes the happy grow," they sang. And it was true. Their happy grew and grew.

That evening, the sky turned pink and gold. Sable curled in her leafy nest while Chip slept in the hollow below, tummy full.

"Thank you for sharing," he whispered up.

"You are welcome," yawned Sable.

She counted her acorns one last time. Eighteen, safe and sound. Her heart felt fuller than her store.

And Sable drifted into a cozy, happy sleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Sharing a little can make everyone feel full.
