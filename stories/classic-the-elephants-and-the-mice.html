The Elephants and the Mice

A wise Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Every morning at sunrise, a herd of elephants marched down to the blue lake to drink.

Stomp-stomp-stomp went their great feet, and the ground trembled like a drum.

But right in their path lay Mouse Village — a cozy tumble of tiny burrows with round doors and windows no bigger than buttons.

Poor little mice! Each morning they woke to CRACK and CRUNCH as the elephant feet squished their roofs flat.

"We must do something!" squeaked Miku, the tiny mouse king, twitching his pink nose. "I will speak to the elephant king myself!"

Up he scampered — up a tall grass stem, up onto a stone — until he stood face to face with Gaj, the great elephant king, whose ears were wide as banana leaves.

"Great King Gaj!" squeaked Miku in his bravest voice. "Please, please walk around our village! Your feet are kind, but our homes are small!"

Gaj the elephant king bent his huge head down, down, down, until one gentle eye looked straight at the tiny mouse.

"Little king," rumbled Gaj softly, "I did not know. From today, we march around Mouse Village — I give you my word."

And he did. Every morning the elephants curved around the tiny burrows, and the mice waved their tails in thanks.

Many moons later, hunters came to the forest with long, strong ropes. In the night they tied the elephants' legs tight and trapped the whole herd in nets.

"Help! Help!" trumpeted Gaj.

Who heard? The mice! Miku gathered every mouse in the village — hundreds of them — and they scampered through the dark, quick as whispers.

Nibble-nibble-nibble went ten thousand tiny teeth. Snip went the ropes. Snap went the nets.

One by one, the elephants stepped free!

Gaj lifted Miku gently onto his trunk and held him high, like a tiny star. "Little king," he rumbled, "you saved us all."

Miku giggled. "Even the smallest friend can do the biggest help!"

And from that night on, whenever the elephants passed Mouse Village, they stopped to share sweet sugarcane — and the mice shared stories, and everyone was glad.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Even the smallest friend can give the biggest help — kindness always comes back around.
