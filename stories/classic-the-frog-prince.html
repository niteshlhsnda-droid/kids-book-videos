The Frog Prince

A timeless Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, in a kingdom by a deep green forest, there lived a young princess named Elsie.

Elsie had a favourite toy: a golden ball that sparkled in the sun. Every afternoon, she played with it beside the old stone well in the palace garden.

One warm afternoon, Elsie threw her ball high into the air — up, up, up! But this time the ball did not come back down into her hands. Plop! It bounced once, twice — and fell splash into the well!

"Oh no!" cried Elsie. "My golden ball! It is gone!"

Elsie sat beside the well and cried. Just then, she heard a small, splashy voice.

"Why are you crying, Princess?"

Elsie looked down into the water. There sat a little green frog, blinking up at her with big, round eyes.

"My golden ball fell into the well," sniffled Elsie. "And I cannot reach it."

"I can get it for you," said the frog, with a polite little bow. "But first, will you promise me something?"

"What?" asked Elsie.

"Promise that you will be my friend," said the frog. "Let me eat from your plate, drink from your cup, and sit beside you at story time."

Elsie thought about it. "That sounds easy," she said. "Yes, I promise!"

The frog dived down, down, down into the cool water, and came back up holding the golden ball in his mouth. He dropped it at Elsie's feet.

"Thank you!" cried Elsie, hugging her ball. And — oh dear — she ran back to the palace so fast that she quite forgot her promise!

But that evening, as Elsie sat down to supper, there came a knock at the door. Knock, knock, knock!

"Who is there?" called the king.

"It is I, the frog from the well," said a small voice. "Princess Elsie promised to be my friend!"

Elsie's cheeks went very pink. "Father," she whispered, "I did promise. But I forgot!"

"A promise is a promise, my dear," said the king kindly. "Even a promise to a frog."

So Elsie opened the door, and in hopped the little frog. She set a plate for him, and he ate a few crumbs. She gave him a cup, and he took a tiny sip. And at story time, the frog sat beside her chair and listened to every word.

At first Elsie felt shy. But the frog was funny! He made silly faces, he hummed along to the songs, and he knew a wonderful joke about a dragon who was afraid of ducks.

Soon Elsie was laughing so hard that her tummy hurt. "You are a very good friend," she said to the frog.

The frog smiled his widest froggy smile. And then — POP! — something magical happened!

In a swirl of sparkles, the little frog turned into a young prince, wearing a green coat and a golden crown!

"I was under a spell," said the prince, "and only a true promise kept with a kind heart could break it. Thank you, Elsie, for keeping your word."

Elsie clapped her hands with delight. "You were the frog all along!"

From that day on, Prince Freddie — for that was his name — visited the palace every day. He and Elsie played with the golden ball, told each other stories, and laughed until the stars came out.

And they were the very best of friends, for ever and ever after.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Keep your promises, and treat every friend — big or small — with kindness.
