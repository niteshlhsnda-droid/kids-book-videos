# Credits — Prince Milo and the Little White Cat

- **Original title:** "The White Cat" ("La Chatte Blanche" in French, from *Contes de Fées* by Marie-Catherine d'Aulnoy)
- **Retold from:** *The Blue Fairy Book*, edited by Andrew Lang (London: Longmans, Green, & Co., 1889), included via the Project Gutenberg edition
- **Editor of collection:** Andrew Lang; original tale author: Marie-Catherine d'Aulnoy
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/503
- **License:** Public domain (published 1889; the 1889 edition's copyright has long expired)
- **Illustrations:** All three illustrations are freshly generated originals (not reused source art).
- **How it was adapted:** The tale was completely retold in original wording for ages 4–7, compressed to ~440 words in a gentle bedtime register. The three quest-tasks of the original were simplified to one (the tiniest puppy); the White Cat was given the name Snowdrop; the young prince was named Milo; the dark enchantment-breaking scene was replaced with a soft magical transformation; and the ending was recast as a warm friendship with a clear moral about kindness.
