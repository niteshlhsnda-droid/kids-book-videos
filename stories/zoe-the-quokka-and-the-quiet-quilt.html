Zoe the Quokka and the Quiet Quilt

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On a sunny island far across the sea lived a little quokka named Zoe. Quokkas are famous for their smiles, and Zoe had the biggest smile of all — a smile so warm that even sleepy lizards woke up smiling too.

Zoe lived in a cozy burrow under a big gum tree, and in her burrow she had a very special quilt. It was soft and blue, with yellow stars stitched all over it, and her grandmother had made it just for her. Every night, Zoe wrapped herself in the quiet quilt, and it whispered, "Shhh… shhh…" like a gentle lullaby, until she fell fast asleep.

One evening, as the sky turned pink and gold, Zoe heard a tiny sound outside her burrow. Sniff… sniff… sniff.

It was Pip, the baby possum from next door, and he was crying softly.

"What is wrong, Pip?" asked Zoe, hopping out with her quilt wrapped around her shoulders.

"I cannot sleep," sniffed Pip. "My bed is too scratchy, and the night sounds are too loud. Hoo-hoo goes the owl, and rustle-rustle go the leaves!"

Zoe thought for a moment. Then she smiled her biggest smile. "Would you like to borrow my quiet quilt, just for tonight?" she asked. "It whispers 'shhh… shhh…' and helps you drift off to sleep."

Pip's eyes went wide. "Really? Just for tonight?"

"Just for tonight," said Zoe, wrapping the soft blue quilt around little Pip. "And I will sit right here beside you until you fall asleep."

So Pip snuggled into the quilt, and Zoe sat beside him, humming a soft, sleepy hum. Hoo-hoo went the owl — but under the quilt, it sounded far away and friendly. Rustle-rustle went the leaves — but under the quilt, it sounded like a lullaby.

Soon, Pip's eyes grew heavy… and heavier… and he fell fast asleep, smiling in his dreams.

Zoe smiled too. But now she had no quilt of her own! She curled up in the grass under the gum tree, and she felt a little bit cold, and a little bit wide awake.

Just then, she heard a soft flutter. It was Wanda, the wise old wombat, carrying a warm blanket made of soft, grey wool.

"I heard what you did, Zoe," said Wanda kindly. "You shared your quilt, so now I will share my blanket with you."

Wanda wrapped the warm blanket around Zoe, and Zoe snuggled down, cozy and happy. "Thank you, Wanda," she whispered, and she fell asleep under the stars, dreaming of quilts and kind friends.

In the morning, Pip woke up bright and rested. "Thank you, Zoe!" he chirped, handing back the quiet quilt. "I slept all night long!"

"And I slept well too," said Zoe, smiling her biggest smile. "Because kindness always comes back, like a warm blanket on a cool night."

From that night on, Zoe's burrow had two cozy covers — her quiet quilt, and Wanda's warm blanket — and whenever a friend could not sleep, Zoe shared one of them, and they all slept happily ever after.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When you share your comfort with a friend, comfort finds its way back to you.
