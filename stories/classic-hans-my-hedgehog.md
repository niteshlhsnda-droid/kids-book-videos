# Hans My Hedgehog

*A timeless Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, in a little cottage at the edge of a great forest, there lived a boy named Hans. Hans was not like other boys. From his head to his toes, he was covered in soft, prickly quills, just like a hedgehog.

That is why everyone called him Hans My Hedgehog.

Hans did not mind his prickles much, because he had a wonderful gift. He could play the bagpipes, and when he played, the music was so sweet that birds stopped to listen and rabbits danced in the grass.

Every day, Hans rode through the forest on his big, proud rooster, playing his bagpipes. The trees seemed to sway, and the flowers seemed to nod along.

One day, Hans heard a sad voice in the forest. "I am lost! I am lost!"

It was a king, wandering between the tall trees, quite lost and quite tired.

"Do not worry," said Hans, hopping down from his rooster. "I know every path in this forest. I will lead you home."

Hans played a cheerful tune on his bagpipes, and the king followed the music, step by step, all the way out of the forest to his palace.

"Thank you, Hans!" cried the king. "You have saved my day. I would like to give you a gift. When you are grown, come back to my palace, and you shall marry my daughter, Princess Lily, who loves music more than anything in the world."

Years passed, and Hans grew tall. One fine morning, he rode his rooster all the way to the palace, playing his bagpipes.

Princess Lily heard the music from her window. "What beautiful music!" she said, and she ran down the palace steps to meet him.

She was not afraid of his prickles at all. "You play like the wind through the trees," she said. "Will you play for me every day?"

"I would love to," said Hans, smiling a prickly, happy smile.

Hans and Lily became the best of friends. They walked in the palace gardens, and Hans played while Lily sang, and the whole palace felt full of music.

And then, on their wedding day, something wonderful happened. As Hans played his happiest tune of all, his prickles began to soften and shine. They turned into the softest, curliest hair, and Hans stood there smiling, a handsome young man with music still dancing in his eyes.

Everyone cheered. The king cheered. The rooster crowed. And Hans and Lily danced together in the garden, while the birds sang along.

From that day on, Hans played his bagpipes every evening in the palace garden, and anyone who felt lonely or lost only had to follow the music to find a friend.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What makes you different can also be your gift — and kind hearts see past prickles to the music inside.*
