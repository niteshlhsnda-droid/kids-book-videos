# Credits — Chiku the Monkey and the Jamun Tree (2026-10-18)

- **Adapted from:** "The Monkey and the Crocodile" (Book IV, *Labdhapranasam* — "Loss of Gains")
- **Original title:** *The Panchatantra*
- **Author:** Traditionally attributed to Vishnu Sharma (Gupta era, c. 3rd century CE)
- **Illustrator:** none reused — all illustrations generated fresh for this story
- **Source URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Panchatantra.djvu/414 (Arthur Ryder's 1925 English translation, on Wikisource)
- **License:** Public domain (ancient Sanskrit fable collection; Ryder's 1925 translation is public domain in the USA)
- **Adaptation note:** Fully retold in original, warm, child-friendly language for ages 4–7; the classic plot (crocodile befriends monkey, wife demands the monkey's heart, monkey's quick thinking saves him) is kept, all dialogue is original, the ending is softened to forgiveness and continued friendship, and the tale is given a gentle bedtime closing. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
