# Kiki the Koala and the Sleepy Eucalyptus

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High up in the tallest eucalyptus tree in the forest lived a little koala named Kiki.

Kiki loved her tree. She loved its soft grey-green leaves, its smooth trunk, and the way it swayed gently in the breeze.

Every evening, as the sun sank low and painted the sky pink and gold, Kiki would curl up in her favourite fork of the tree and listen.

One evening, the leaves began to whisper, "Sleepy, sleepy... sleepy, sleepy..."

Kiki's ears twitched. "Did you hear that, tree?" she whispered. "Are you talking to me?"

The wind blew softly through the branches, and the whole tree began to hum — a soft, slow lullaby, like a mother singing her baby to sleep.

Hmm... hmm... hmm... went the tree.

"Oh!" said Kiki, her eyes growing wide. "You hum a lullaby when the wind blows! What a lovely, sleepy song!"

Kiki closed her eyes and swayed, just a little, to the soft, slow song. Her eyelids grew heavy, and a warm, sleepy feeling wrapped around her like a soft blanket. But she did not want to keep the beautiful song all to herself.

The next evening, Kiki climbed down and called to her friends.

"Wombat! Platypus! Little Possum! Come quickly! My tree sings a lullaby, and you must hear it!"

Wombat came waddling through the grass. Platypus came splashing up from the creek. Little Possum came tumbling down from her branch, her tail curled tight.

"What is it, Kiki?" they asked.

"Shhh," whispered Kiki. "Listen..."

The evening breeze blew through the tall eucalyptus, and the leaves whispered, "Sleepy, sleepy..."

Then the tree began to hum its soft lullaby — hmm... hmm... hmm...

Wombat yawned a big, slow yawn. Platypus closed his sleepy eyes. Little Possum snuggled close to Kiki, warm and safe.

"This is the sleepiest song in the whole forest," murmured Wombat.

"And the softest," murmured Platypus.

Kiki smiled and wrapped her furry arms around her friends. "Then stay," she whispered. "Stay and listen, and we can all fall asleep together, under the humming tree."

So Wombat curled up at the foot of the tree. Platypus nestled in the soft grass. Little Possum tucked herself under Kiki's warm arm. And Kiki leaned back against the smooth trunk, listening to the tree hum.

Hmm... hmm... hmm...

One by one, their eyes closed. One by one, their breathing grew slow and soft.

And high above them, the stars came out, twinkling down on the sleepy eucalyptus and its four sleepy friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A soft song shared with friends makes the sweetest sleep.*
