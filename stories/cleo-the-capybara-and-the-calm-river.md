# Cleo the Capybara and the Calm River

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Beside a slow, green river in a land of tall grasses, there lived a capybara named Cleo. Capybaras are the calmest animals in the whole world, and Cleo was the calmest capybara of them all.

Every afternoon, Cleo floated down the river on her back, her ears just above the water, watching the clouds drift by. "Ahh," she would say softly. "This is the perfect way to spend a day."

One afternoon, a little duck named Didi came paddling frantically down the river, splashing and quacking. "Hurry! Hurry!" cried Didi. "I must reach the reeds before sunset, and I am so tired!"

"Come float with me, Didi," said Cleo calmly. "The river will carry us both, and we do not need to hurry at all."

Didi was not sure. She had always paddled as fast as she could. But she was very tired, so she turned onto her back beside Cleo and let the gentle water hold her up.

"Oh!" said Didi, surprised. "The water is holding me! I am floating!"

"Yes," smiled Cleo. "When you stop struggling, the river does the work. Just breathe in slowly... and breathe out slowly... like this."

Didi breathed in slowly. She breathed out slowly. Her little wings relaxed, and her feet stopped splashing. The river carried them both around a bend, past pink flowers and sleepy dragonflies.

Soon, a tired frog, a busy otter, and a little turtle all joined the floating parade. Cleo taught each of them to breathe slowly and float calmly, and soon the whole river was full of happy, relaxed friends drifting under the golden sky.

When they reached the reeds at sunset, Didi quacked happily. "Cleo, you were right! Floating got us here just as fast — and it felt wonderful!"

"That is the secret of the calm river," said Cleo, smiling. "Slow down, breathe, and let the water help you."

And every evening after that, Cleo's friends gathered for the calm river float, breathing slowly and watching the stars come out, one by one.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When you slow down and breathe, even a busy day feels calm and kind.*
