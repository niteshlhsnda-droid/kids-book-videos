The Lion and the Dolphin

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Leo the Lion lived on a golden beach, where the sand was warm and the sea sang all day long.

Leo was strong and brave. He could run faster than the wind. But Leo had one wish.

"I wish I had a friend in the sea," said Leo. "A friend who knows the waves and the deep blue water."

One morning, Leo saw something wonderful. A dolphin leaped out of the sea — SPLASH! — and dived back in, laughing.

"Hello!" called Leo. "I am Leo the Lion. Will you be my friend?"

The dolphin popped up again, smiling. "Hello, Leo! I am Dolly the Dolphin. Of course I will be your friend!"

Leo wagged his tail with joy. "Wonderful! Then come onto the beach, and we can play together all day!"

Dolly shook her head gently. "Oh, Leo, I cannot come onto the land. I breathe in the water. On the sand, I cannot breathe at all."

Leo's ears drooped. "Then how can we be friends, if you cannot come to me?"

Dolly laughed a bubbly laugh. "Friends do not have to be the same, Leo. I will come to the edge of the water, and you can come to the edge of the sand. We will meet right there — where the sea kisses the beach!"

So that is what they did.

Every evening, Leo ran along the beach. And Dolly leaped in the waves beside him — up, down, SPLASH!

"Race you to the big rock!" shouted Leo, running as fast as he could.

"Race you back!" laughed Dolly, leaping through the waves.

When Leo was thirsty, Dolly showed him where the fresh water bubbled up near the rocks. When Dolly was tired, Leo told her stories about the forest, and Dolly told him stories about the deep blue sea.

One day, a little crab asked Leo, "Why are you friends with a dolphin? She cannot even walk on the land!"

Leo smiled. "Dolly cannot walk on the land," he said. "And I cannot swim in the deep sea. But we can both laugh. We can both play. And we can both meet where the sea kisses the beach. That is enough for the very best friends."

And Dolly leaped high out of the water — SPLASH! — as if to say, "Yes! Yes! That is just right!"

From that day on, whenever you walk along a beach at sunset and hear a lion laughing and a dolphin splashing, you will know — it is Leo and Dolly, playing their happy game, right where the sea kisses the sand.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: True friends do not have to be the same — they meet each other where they are.
