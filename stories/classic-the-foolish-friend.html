The Foolish Friend

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, there lived a king who had a pet monkey named Bandar. Bandar loved the king with all his heart, and did such silly dances — spinning in circles, wiggling his ears, crossing his eyes — that the king laughed and laughed until his cheeks hurt.

Every afternoon, while the king took his nap, Bandar sat beside the tall carved bed, waving a big leaf-fan to keep him cool. The afternoon sun fell in soft gold squares across the blankets.

One afternoon, the king was sleeping peacefully — "khurrr… khurrr…" — when a naughty little fly came buzzing in — "Bzz! Bzz!" — and landed right on the king's nose.

Bandar waved his fan. Shoo! The fly zipped off… and came right back. "Bzz!"

"That fly is NOT getting away with this!" thought Bandar. "I'll show that fly!" … Now — what do YOU think Bandar picked up?

Bandar looked around, grabbed the heaviest thing he could find — and — WHAM! — he brought it down on the fly! It was a coconut — brown and hairy and heavy as a stone — and where did it land? BONK! — right on the king's nose!

"Ouch! My nose!" said the king, sitting up straight and rubbing his poor red nose. "Bandar! What are you DOING?"

"The… the fly…" mumbled Bandar, his face turning redder than the king's nose.

"Oh, Bandar…" said the king, holding his nose and trying not to smile. "You kind, kind monkey. But — ouch — next time, let's think first, yes?"

"I'm sorry, Your Majesty," said Bandar, hanging his head. "Next time I'll think first. I promise."

And from that day on, Bandar was the most careful friend — and the king smiled, closed his eyes, and drifted back into his afternoon nap. Can you hear him? "Khurrr… khurrr…"

Goodnight, Bandar. The end. 🌙

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kind hearts think before they act.
