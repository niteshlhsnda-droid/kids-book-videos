# Credits — Dorothy and the Scarecrow in the Cornfield

- **Original title:** *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (Chapter IV, "The Road Through the Forest", 1900)
- **Author:** L. Frank Baum (1856–1919)
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (eBook #55) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/55
- **License:** Public domain (first published 1900; Baum died 1919)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, shortened to ten story beats from Dorothy's first meeting with the Scarecrow in the cornfield; the yellow brick road, the talking scarecrow who longs for brains, and the journey toward the Emerald City are kept, and the ending is softened into a cosy haystack goodnight so the tale closes gently at bedtime.
