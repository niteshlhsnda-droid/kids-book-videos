The Sandy Road

A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, a wise leader named Sarthak took a caravan across a hot desert — a winding line of travelers, with five hundred carts. They carried big leather bags full of water.

"You must not waste a single drop!" said Sarthak. "Travel by night, when the sand is cool — and don't pour out your water for any reason!"

The stars were bright overhead, and the cool sand went shush-shush under the cart wheels.

At midnight, a desert trickster came floating by — thin as smoke, with a wide, tricky grin. "Why are you carrying all this heavy water?" he whispered. "Just ahead there is a cool, blue lake! Pour out your water! Then no more heavy bags to carry — and sweet, fresh water waiting at the lake!"

Some of the thirsty drivers in Sarthak's caravan believed him. "WATER!" they cried. "A lake! A LAKE!" And glug-glug-glug — they poured out all their water onto the sand! Can you make the glug-glug-glug sound with me? … But wait — do YOU think there was really a lake?

But Sarthak held firm. "Hold fast, friends!" he called to his own travelers. "Not a single drop — that whisper is a trick!" And his own travelers held on to their water bags.

The trickster laughed and floated away — for there was no lake at all, only more hot sand! Now their bags were light as air — and empty of every single drop!

Soon the sun rose, round and red, and the thirsty drivers had no water left. They wished they had listened.

But Sarthak's travelers shared their water with everyone, and drop by drop, sip by sip, they all crossed the desert safely through the long, hot day. The drivers looked down at the sand. "We were silly," they said softly. "We threw away real water for a dream of water." "Remember, friends," said Sarthak gently, "never throw away real water for a dream of water." And when the stars came out again, they drank all they wanted under the soft, starry sky, and slept.

Goodnight, travelers. The end. 🌙

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Hold on to what is real and true — never throw away real water for a dream of water.
