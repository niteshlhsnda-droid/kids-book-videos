# Mina the Manatee and the Moonlit Lagoon

*A gentle original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a warm, quiet lagoon, where the water shimmered silver under the moon, there lived a gentle manatee named Mina.

Mina was big and slow and kind. She loved to drift through the lagoon at night, humming a soft, sleepy hum, while the moon painted a sparkling path across the water.

One moonlit night, as Mina hummed her way past the mangrove trees, she heard a tiny, frightened squeak.

"Help! Help!" squeaked a little voice. "I am lost!"

It was a baby seahorse named Nilo, no bigger than Mina's nose. He had been playing hide-and-seek with his brothers and sisters, and now he could not find his way home.

"Do not be afraid, little one," said Mina in her softest voice. "Climb onto my back, and I will help you find your family."

Nilo curled his tiny tail around Mina's fin and held on tight. Mina began to glide slowly through the lagoon, following the moon's sparkling path.

"Look," whispered Mina. "Do you see those bubbles near the sea grass?"

Nilo peered through the water. "I see them!" he squeaked. "That is where we play!"

Mina glided closer, and there, among the swaying sea grass, were Nilo's brothers and sisters, waiting and worrying.

"Nilo!" they cried, twirling with joy. "You are home!"

Nilo's mother swam over and hugged him close. "Thank you, Mina," she said. "You are the kindest guide in the whole lagoon."

Mina smiled her slow, happy smile. "It was the moon who showed the way," she said. "I only hummed along."

Then, as the moon climbed high and the lagoon grew quiet, Mina drifted home, humming her sleepy hum, while Nilo and his family waved their tiny fins and called, "Goodnight, Mina! Goodnight!"

And Mina, warm with kindness, drifted off to sleep under the moonlit water.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A gentle guide can turn a scary lost feeling into a safe homecoming.*
