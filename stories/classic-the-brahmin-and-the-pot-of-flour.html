The Brahmin and the Pot of Flour

A wise Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a quiet little village, there lived a poor brahmin named Bhanu. A brahmin is a wise man who reads holy books — and Bhanu was the kindest one of all.

Bhanu didn't have much. He had a small hut, an old mat, and a big empty belly that rumbled when he was hungry. "Grrr-grrr-grr!" Sometimes his tummy rumbled so loudly that the sparrows on the roof looked down to see what the noise was!

One day, a kind villager gave Bhanu a gift — a whole pot of flour! Bhanu's eyes went round and wide. "Oh, thank you!" he said, hugging the pot.

That night, Bhanu sat down with the pot of flour beside him and started to dream. Oh, what a dreamer he was!

"If I sell this flour," he said, "I will get some coins. With the coins, I will buy two little hens. The hens will lay eggs — cluck, cluck, cluck! I will sell the eggs and buy a goat. The goat will give sweet milk. I will sell the milk and buy... a cow! Then another cow! Then I will be RICH!"

Bhanu's eyes sparkled. "I will build a fine big house! I will have a soft bed! And everyone in the village will say, 'There goes rich Bhanu!'" Bhanu grinned from ear to ear. In his dream, he was already rich — richer than the richest man in the village!

He was dreaming so hard that he forgot the pot of flour was real and right beside him.

"What do you think happens when you kick your foot high in the air — right next to a pot of flour?" Well...

"When I am rich," Bhanu said, puffing up his chest, "I will wave my foot like THIS — " and he lifted his foot high in the air and gave it a big, show-off kick.

SMACK! His foot hit the real pot of flour!

Crash! Over went the pot! The flour spilled everywhere — on the mat, on the floor, in a big white puff, soft as snow!

POOF! Bhanu sat in the middle of a cloud of flour. His hair was white. His nose was white. Even his eyebrows were white! A little flour landed right on the tip of his nose. It tickled! "A... a... ACHOO!"

And you know what Bhanu did? He looked at the spilled flour... and he burst out laughing! "Ha-ha-ha! Ho-ho-ho!"

"Oh, Bhanu!" he chuckled. "You dreamed of a fine big house — and now you are sitting in a pile of flour!"

Then Bhanu did the wisest thing of all. He swept up every bit of flour he could save.

And he made the tastiest, warmest little rotis you ever saw! They puffed up, round and golden. The smell filled the little hut — warm, toasty, delicious! And he ate them with a happy heart.

For Bhanu had learned something important: daydreams are fun, but they don't fill your pot. What fills your pot is caring for what you already have.

That night, with his tummy full of warm rotis, Bhanu lay down on his mat. He smiled a soft, sleepy smile... and drifted off to dream — small, sweet dreams this time.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Daydreams don't fill pots — care for what you already have.
