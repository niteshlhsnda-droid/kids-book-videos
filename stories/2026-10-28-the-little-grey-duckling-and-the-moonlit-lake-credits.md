# Credits — "The Little Grey Duckling and the Moonlit Lake"

- **Original title:** "The Ugly Duckling" ("Den grimme Ælling")
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen (1805–1875)
- **First published:** 1843
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *Andersen's Fairy Tales* (eBook #1597) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1597
- **License:** Public domain (author died 1875; work published 1843)
- **Illustrator:** None — no source art reused; all illustrations for this adaptation were freshly generated in watercolor style for the kids-book-videos library.
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a gentle bedtime story: the teasing is softened, the winter hardship is shortened, and the ending lands on a moonlit lake at bedtime, with the duckling's growth shown through the story rather than stated.
