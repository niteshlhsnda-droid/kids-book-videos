# Runa the Red Panda and the Lantern Promise

In a green bamboo forest, where the leaves whispered in the evening breeze, there lived a little red panda named Runa.

Runa had soft rust-red fur, a bushy striped tail, and round ears that twitched. She lived in a cozy hollow tree above a silver pond.

Every evening, Runa lit the big lantern on the hill above the pond. Its warm golden light helped all the forest animals find their way home in the dark.

One evening, her friend Tobo the tiny toad hopped up the hill. "Runa," he said, "tonight I must cross the pond to sing my little sister to sleep. Will you keep the lantern burning until I come back?"

Runa nodded and held out her paw. "I promise," she said. "The lantern will burn until you are home again."

Tobo hopped away, splash-splash, across the pond, and Runa sat beside the lantern.

But then the wind began to blow. The bamboo swayed, dark clouds rolled over the moon, and pitter-pat, pitter-pat, the rain began to fall.

Whoosh! A gust of wind blew the lantern out. The hill went dark.

Runa's heart thumped. "Oh no," she whispered. "Tobo is out there, and my promise is in the dark."

She tried again. Strike, fizzle. Strike, fizzle. The matches were wet, and the wind kept blowing.

Runa wanted to run inside and hide from the rain. But she thought of Tobo, hopping alone in the dark, looking for her light. She took a deep breath.

She remembered the dry hollow log at the bottom of the hill, where she kept an old ember box. She scampered down and carried it back up under a roof of big leaves.

In the shelter of the log, she cupped her paws around the ember and blew softly until a tiny flame danced up. She lit a dry candle and lifted it to the lantern.

Glow, glow, glow! The lantern burst back to life, and its golden light spilled over the hill, over the pond, all the way to the far shore.

On the far shore, Tobo looked up and saw the light. "Runa kept her promise!" he cried, and hopped home, splash-splash, along the golden path.

When Tobo reached the hill, wet and tired but smiling, he said, "Thank you, Runa. Your light showed me the way."

Runa wrapped him in a soft dry blanket, and they sat together under the lantern as the rain grew quiet.

The clouds drifted away, and the stars came out, one by one.

Runa yawned a big red-panda yawn. Tobo curled up beside her, warm and safe.

Sleep now, little Runa, keeper of the lantern light.

The end. 🌙
