Penny the Penguin and the Polar Lullaby

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On a big, white iceberg, far away where the snow sparkles, lived a little penguin named Penny.

Penny loved her home. She loved sliding on her tummy down the snowy slopes. She loved watching the soft pink clouds drift across the sky.

But at bedtime, Penny had one big problem. She could not sleep.

The iceberg was just too noisy! The wind whistled. The waves slapped the ice. The walruses snored like big, rumbly drums. And the seals — oh, the seals barked in their sleep!

"How can anyone sleep with all this noise?" Penny sighed.

One quiet evening, Penny had an idea. She put on her softest scarf, waddled to the top of the iceberg, and said, "If the night is too noisy, I will collect the quiet sounds instead."

First, Penny listened to the snow. Little flakes fell down, down, down, so soft and so slow. Shhh... shhh... shhh. That was a quiet sound.

Then she waddled over to a big, sleepy seal. The seal yawned a long, slow yawn. "Yaaaaawn." That was a quiet sound too.

Then Penny listened to the wind. It did not whistle that night. It hummed, very gently, like a mother humming to her baby. Hmmmm... hmmmm.

Penny collected each quiet sound in her heart, one by one. Snow. Yawn. Wind.

Then she sat on a smooth piece of ice, closed her eyes, and began to sing.

"Shhh, shhh, falling snow... yaaawn, sleepy seal... hmmmm, gentle wind..."

Her little song was soft like a snowflake and sweet like warm milk. It floated across the iceberg, curling around the snowy hills like a warm blanket.

The wind heard the song and grew softer. The waves heard the song and grew calmer. The walruses stopped snoring. The seals stopped barking.

One by one, all the animals on the iceberg closed their eyes. The whole iceberg became quiet and peaceful, tucked under a blanket of stars.

Penny smiled a sleepy smile. Her own song had made her eyes feel heavy too.

She waddled back to her cozy nest, snuggled into her mama's soft feathers, and whispered, "Good night, iceberg. Good night, snow. Good night, wind."

And in her dreams, she was still singing her soft little lullaby.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Quiet little sounds can hush even the noisiest night — and a gentle song can help everyone rest.
