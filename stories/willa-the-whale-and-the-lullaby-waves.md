# Willa the Whale and the Lullaby Waves

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in the blue, blue ocean lived a gentle whale named Willa.

Willa was the biggest animal in the sea, but she had the softest heart. Every evening, as the sun melted into the waves, Willa sang her lullaby.

"Ooo-ooo-oooh," she sang, low and sweet, and her song rolled through the water like warm honey.

All the baby sea creatures loved Willa's song. Sammy the seahorse, Tilly the baby turtle, and the tiny silver fish all gathered close to listen.

But one evening, Willa's throat felt scratchy. "Ahem," she coughed. "Oh no — I have lost my singing voice!"

The baby sea creatures looked at her with wide, worried eyes. "But… who will sing us to sleep?" whispered Sammy.

Willa thought for a moment. Then she smiled her big whale smile.

"I may not be able to sing tonight," she said, "but the OCEAN can sing for us. Listen!"

She lifted her great tail — SWISH! — and sent a soft wave rolling toward the shore.

The wave lapped against the rocks — shhhh, shhhh — like a mother whispering, "Sleep, my baby, sleep."

Willa swished again, and another wave came — shhhh, shhhh — rocking Sammy gently, back and forth, back and forth.

Tilly the turtle closed her eyes. The tiny silver fish stopped wiggling. Sammy yawned the biggest seahorse yawn.

Shhhh, shhhh went the waves. Shhhh, shhhh.

One by one, every baby sea creature drifted off to sleep, rocked by Willa's lullaby waves.

And Willa? She floated quietly, watching over them all, her heart humming the lullaby even though her voice was resting. Then she closed her own big eyes and drifted, rocked by the very waves she had made.

The next morning her voice was back — "Ooo-ooo-oooh!" — but from that night on, Willa always let the waves help her sing, because the sweetest lullabies are the ones we share.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When you cannot do something alone, ask for help — the sweetest songs are the ones we sing together.*
