# The Frogs Asking for a King

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green pond, under a soft blue sky, lived a crowd of happy little frogs.

All day long, they hopped from lily pad to lily pad, singing ribbit, ribbit, ribbit!

But the smallest frog of all sighed a big sigh.

"We have no king," she said. "Every pond should have a king."

The other frogs nodded their little green heads. "Yes, yes!" they croaked. "We want a king!"

So all the frogs looked up at the sky and called out together, "Please, dear sky, send us a king!"

The sky heard them. A soft wind blew, the reeds swayed, and then — SPLASH!

Down fell a big brown log, right into the middle of the pond.

The frogs peeked out from behind the lily pads. "Is that our king?" they whispered.

The log just floated there, quiet and still.

But slowly, the frogs swam closer and climbed onto the log. It was smooth and wide and just right for sitting in the sun.

"This is a very good king," said the smallest frog. "He is quiet, and he lets us rest."

The frogs were happy. They sang and napped on their log and watched the clouds drift by.

But after a while, the smallest frog sighed again.

"Our king never moves," she said. "Our king never sings. I wish we had a hopping, singing king."

The other frogs nodded. "Yes, yes!" they croaked. "We want a hopping, singing king!"

So once more, they looked up at the sky and called out together, "Please, dear sky, send us a hopping, singing king!"

The sky heard them. The reeds swayed, the water rippled, and down came a tall grey stork, with long, long legs and a pointed beak.

The stork landed at the edge of the pond with a soft thump and looked right at the frogs.

"Oh my!" whispered the smallest frog. "This king is very tall. And very, very still."

The frogs sat as still as stones.

"Our log king was quiet too," whispered the smallest frog. "But our log king was kind and soft. This new king makes my tummy feel all wobbly."

Then the smallest frog hopped forward and bowed low.

"Dear tall stork," she said in her smallest, most polite voice, "thank you for coming to our pond. But we have a king already. He is a brown log, and he is just right for us. Please, fly on and find a pond that needs you more."

The stork blinked, gave a little nod, and spread his great grey wings. Up, up, up he flew, into the soft blue sky.

The frogs watched him go, then hopped back to their log, one by one. They wriggled their toes and sang their froggy songs — ribbit, ribbit, ribbit!

"This is our king," said the smallest frog, patting the warm brown log. "He is quiet. He is soft. He is just right."

And as the sun went down and the stars came out, the frogs curled up on their log king, closed their sleepy eyes, and dreamed of the green pond they loved.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What you already have may be just right — wishing for more can spoil a good thing.*
