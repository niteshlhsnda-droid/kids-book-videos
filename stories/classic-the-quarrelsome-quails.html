The Quarrelsome Quails

A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a wide green field, there lived a big flock of quails. Quails are small, round birds with soft brown feathers and quick little feet.

Every morning, the quails came to the field to peck at the seeds. Peck, peck, peck!

But every morning, a hunter came too, with a big net.

"Now!" the hunter would cry, and throw his net over the quails.

But the quails had a clever trick. All together, they would lift the net with their little wings and fly up, up, up into the sky — net and all!

Then they would fly to a thorny bush, drop the net, and hop away, safe and free.

Day after day, the hunter went home empty-handed. "Those quails are too clever for me," he grumbled.

The secret was simple: the quails always flew together. One quail alone could never lift the net. But a hundred quails, all flapping at once, could lift it with ease.

Then one morning, something silly happened.

Two quails began to quarrel over a single shiny seed.

"That seed is mine!" said Pip.

"No, it is mine!" said Pop. "I saw it first!"

Soon more quails joined in. "Mine!" "No, mine!" They pecked and squabbled and flapped their wings at each other.

That morning, when the hunter threw his net, the quails did not fly together. Some flapped left, some flapped right, and some did not flap at all.

The net stayed right where it was — and the quails were caught!

The hunter picked up the net and smiled. "At last! Today I have caught you all!"

The quails were very sorry. They looked at each other with sad little eyes.

"Oh, Pip," said Pop softly. "I am sorry I quarrelled over that seed."

"And I am sorry too," said Pip. "Friends are worth more than one shiny seed."

The hunter heard their soft, sorry voices. He was a kind-hearted man, and his heart melted a little.

"You are sorry, and you have learned your lesson," he said gently. "Go and be good friends again."

And he opened the net and let them all go!

The quails fluttered out, chirping with joy. From that day on, they never quarrelled again. When the hunter's net fell, they flew up together — all as one — just like before.

And every evening, as the sun went down, the little quails huddled close together and sang their soft, happy song.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When friends stay together, no trouble can hold them down.
