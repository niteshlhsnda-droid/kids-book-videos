Ollie the Owl and the Sleepy Stars

A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

High up in a tall old oak tree, in a soft nest of leaves and feathers, lived a little owl named Ollie.

Ollie had big round eyes, soft brown wings, and a tiny lantern that glowed warm and golden. Every night, when the moon rose, Ollie lit his lantern and flew out to say good night to the forest.

But one night, Ollie could not sleep at all.

He tossed and turned in his nest.

"Too bright, too bright!" he hooted softly. "The stars are twinkling so hard tonight that my eyes will not close!"

Above his tree, the stars were shining and sparkling and winking, brighter than ever before. They danced and twinkled all across the dark blue sky.

Ollie blinked his big round eyes. "If only the stars could rest for a little while," he said, "then the whole forest could sleep."

Just then, a tiny star near the top of the tree gave a big sleepy yawn. "Yaaawn," it said in a small, tired voice. "I have twinkled all night long, and now I am so very sleepy."

Ollie looked up and smiled. "You poor sleepy star!" he said. "Wait right there. I will help you."

Ollie took his little lantern and flew up into the sky.

He reached the sleepy little star and held out his wing. "Come with me," he whispered. "I know just the place for you."

The star twinkled softly and floated down onto Ollie's wing. Ollie carried it gently to a big soft cloud, tucked it under a fluffy fold, and whispered, "Sleep tight, little star. I will keep you safe until morning."

The little star closed its eyes and fell fast asleep.

Then Ollie heard another tiny yawn. "Yaaawn," said a second star. "I am sleepy too!"

Ollie flew up again, took the second star by its twinkly hand, and tucked it into the cloud beside its friend.

Then a third star yawned, and a fourth, and a fifth! One by one, Ollie gathered the sleepiest stars and tucked each one into the soft cloud, just like tucking little children into bed.

"Sleep tight," he whispered to each one. "Dream sweet dreams. I will wake you in the morning."

Soon the sky grew calm and dark and quiet. The brightest stars had gone to sleep, and only a few gentle stars twinkled softly, like tiny night-lights in the sky.

Down below, the forest began to settle. The rabbits curled up in their burrows, the little birds tucked their heads under their wings, and even the busy bees stopped buzzing and drifted off to sleep.

Ollie flew back down to his oak tree, feeling very tired himself. He yawned a great big owl yawn. "Yaaawn," he said. "Now the sky is calm, and the forest is sleeping. It is time for me to sleep too."

He blew out his lantern, snuggled into his nest, and pulled his blanket up to his beak.

Above him, the sleepy stars dreamed of tomorrow night, when they would shine all over again.

And Ollie closed his big round eyes and dreamed among the stars he had saved for tomorrow.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A little kindness helps everyone rest.
