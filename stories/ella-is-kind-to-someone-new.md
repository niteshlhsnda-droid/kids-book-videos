# Ella Is Kind to Someone New

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Ella was a young elephant calf.

She lived by the big waterhole.

She had many friends there.

One morning, a new family arrived.

There was a small foal.

His name was Zip.

He stood alone at the edge.

He looked shy.

He did not know anyone.

Ella felt curious.

She walked over slowly.

She said, "Hello! I am Ella."

Zip looked up.

"Hello," he said quietly.

"I am Zip. We just moved here."

Ella saw he felt nervous.

She remembered her first day too.

She wanted to be kind.

Mama Elephant always said, "Kindness is a gift we all can give."

"Kindness is a gift we all can give," Ella said to herself.

"Kindness is a gift we all can give," she said again.

"Kindness is a gift we all can give," she said one more time.

Ella smiled at Zip.

"Want to see the best splashing spot?" she asked.

Zip's eyes lit up.

"Yes, please!"

Ella showed him around.

The shallow end for splashing.

The shady tree for naps.

The sweet grass patch.

She introduced him to her friends.

"This is Zip," she said proudly.

They all said hello.

They played water games together.

Splash, splish, splash!

Zip laughed and laughed.

He did not feel new anymore.

He felt welcome.

At sunset, Zip's mama came over.

"Thank you, Ella," she said.

"You made our first day wonderful."

Ella felt warm inside.

Kindness felt like sunshine.

That night, Ella lay beside her mama.

She thought about Zip's smile.

She felt happy.

She had been kind.

The stars reflected in the waterhole.

Ella closed her eyes.

She was kind.

She was loved.

Goodnight, Ella.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little kindness can make a new friend feel at home.*
