Clover the Caterpillar and the Cozy Cocoon

A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a green garden full of soft leaves, there lived a little green caterpillar named Clover.

Clover loved to munch leaves — munch, munch, munch! — and to wiggle along the branches in the sunshine.

But one day, Clover felt very, very sleepy. Her eyes drooped, and her little legs felt heavy.

"It is time," said a wise old ladybird, "for you to build your cocoon and take a long, cosy sleep."

"A cocoon?" said Clover, feeling a little bit scared. "But it will be dark inside. And I will be all alone."

"Not alone," buzzed a friendly firefly. "I will keep my little light on, just outside your cocoon, all night long."

"And I will sing you a soft song," said a little robin, perching on the branch above.

So Clover began to spin her cocoon — round and round, soft and silky. It wrapped around her like a warm blanket, snug and safe.

Inside, it was dark, but it was also warm and quiet. Clover could hear the firefly's soft hum outside, and the robin's gentle song:

"Sleep, little Clover, sleep tight, Dream of the morning, dream of the light…"

Clover closed her eyes. She dreamed of green leaves and warm sunshine. She dreamed of floating on soft clouds. She dreamed of wings — soft, gentle wings, painted with colours like a rainbow.

Days went by. The firefly kept his light glowing, and every morning he told Clover a tiny story through the wall of the cocoon. The robin sang his song every evening, and once he brought Clover a cool drop of dew on a leaf. And inside the cocoon, Clover slept and slept, growing and changing in the dark.

Then one bright morning, the cocoon began to wiggle — wiggle, wiggle, wiggle!

"She is waking up!" cried the firefly.

"She is coming out!" sang the robin.

Slowly, slowly, the cocoon opened — and out came Clover! But she was not a caterpillar any more. She had soft, beautiful wings, green and gold, like a little leaf touched by the sun.

"Oh!" cried Clover, fluttering her new wings. "I can fly!"

She flew up into the morning sky, round and round, over the flowers and the leaves. The firefly danced beside her, and the robin sang the happiest song of all.

"Thank you for keeping me company," said Clover. "I was scared of the dark, but your light and your song made it cosy."

And from that day on, every evening, Clover the butterfly flew home to her garden, folded her soft wings, and fell asleep under the stars — dreaming of her next sunny day.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A dark, quiet place is not so scary when friends keep their light and songs close by.
