# Credits — Tilly the Tortoise and the Racing Hare

- **Original title:** "The Tortoise and the Hare" (fable from *The Aesop for Children*, a collection of Aesop's Fables)
- **Author:** Aesop (ancient Greek fabulist)
- **Illustrator:** All artwork in this package is freshly generated watercolor illustrations. Source illustrations (Milo Winter's 1919 plates) were *not* reused.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg children's bookshelf — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/bookshelf/215 (Aesop's fables are in the public domain worldwide; Aesop lived c. 620–564 BCE)
- **License:** Public domain (no rights reserved)
- **How it was adapted:** The ancient fable was retold in ~450 warm, simple words for ages 4–7 with a gentle bedtime tone — the tortoise became "Tilly," the meadow friends (Mouse, Squirrel, Robin) and the cozy ending were added, and the pace was slowed for a read-aloud bedtime rhythm. Plot and moral preserved: slow and steady finishes the race.
