# The Two Parrots

*A timeless Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One sunny morning, a kind king strolled through the forest with his young daughter. The birds were singing, and the trees arched together like soft, shady tunnels above their heads.

"Ssh, Papa, listen!" whispered the little girl. "I hear tiny cheeps!"

Under a big mango tree, they found a nest with two baby parrots inside — all alone, with no mother bird in sight. They were round and fluffy, with bright green feathers and soft, sleepy eyes.

"Hello, little ones," said the king softly. "You are too small to be alone."

The king wanted both babies to be safe and loved. So he gave one parrot, Mitu, to the old gardener who lived by the palace gate. The gardener was wise and gentle, and his home was full of flowers and kind words.

The other parrot, Chintu, went to the forest watchmen's hut. All day the watchmen played silly robber games, shouting and laughing with big, booming voices.

A whole year passed. The parrots grew big and strong, and both learned to talk. Can you picture them now — big and green, with long, sweeping tails?

One day, the king and his daughter visited the watchmen's hut first. As soon as they came near, Chintu fluffed up his feathers and squawked, "Stop! Who goes there? Give me your mangoes!"

The king laughed and laughed. "Oh my! What a brave little guard you have become!"

Then they went to the gardener's home. The garden was cool and sweet-smelling. Mitu was sitting on a swing made of flowers. When he saw them, he sang out, "Welcome, welcome! Rest a while, drink some cool water!"

The little girl clapped her hands and giggled. "Papa, why do they talk so differently? They are brothers!"

The king smiled and lifted her up. "Because we start to talk like the friends we spend time with, little one," he said. "Mitu heard gentle voices every day, so he speaks gently. Chintu heard loud shouts, so he shouts."

He thought for a moment. Then he gently brought Chintu to live with the old gardener too.

Day by day, Chintu heard the gardener's kind voice and Mitu's sweet songs, and slowly his squawks turned into songs too — until he was saying the kindest words of all: "Goodnight, sleepy moon!"

At night, both parrots perched side by side on the garden wall. They chirped soft goodnights as the palace lamps were lit one by one. Their little green heads nodded sleepily in the warm, golden light.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: We start to sound like the friends we spend time with — so speak kindly, and kindness comes back to you.*
