# Otto the Otter and the Moonlit River

*A sweet original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On the bank of a wide, winding river, there lived a little otter named Otto.

Otto loved the river. He loved to splash in it all day. He loved to slide down its muddy banks — whee! — and float on his back like a little boat.

But at bedtime, Otto had one big problem. He could not fall asleep!

"Everyone else is yawning," said his mother, "and your eyes are still wide open, little one."

"I try, Mama," said Otto. "But my paws want to splash, and my tail wants to wiggle!"

One night, when the moon was round and bright, Otto slipped out of his burrow and tiptoed down to the river.

The moon was painting a long, silver path across the water. It shimmered and sparkled, like a road made of stars.

"Oh!" whispered Otto. "A moonlit road!"

Just then, he heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help!"

It was Lulu, a little firefly. Her glow had gone out, like a tiny lamp with no light left.

"I lost my twinkle," sniffed Lulu. "And now I can't find my way home."

"Don't worry," said Otto. "I will help you. Climb onto my nose, and we will follow the moonlit road together."

So Lulu climbed onto Otto's nose, and Otto slid into the river — splash! — and swam along the silver path.

They swam past three silver fish, jumping one by one over the moonlit road — one, two, three! — plip, plip, plip! Otto counted them softly, and Lulu giggled on his nose.

They swam past the sleeping ducks, who bobbed gently on the water. "Shhh," whispered Otto. "Don't wake them."

They swam past the old turtle, who was snoring softly on a log. "Shhh," whispered Lulu. "Don't wake him."

At last, they came to a tall, whispering reed bed. And there, glowing softly, was Lulu's home — a little lantern made of leaves!

"Home!" cried Lulu. And as she landed on her doorstep, her glow came back — twinkle, twinkle, twinkle! — brighter than ever before.

"Thank you, Otto!" she said. "You are the kindest otter on the whole river."

Otto smiled. All that swimming had made his paws tired and his tail sleepy. He floated on his back and looked up at the moon.

"Goodnight, moon," he yawned. "Goodnight, river. Goodnight, little firefly."

And the moon rocked him gently, all the way home, where his mother tucked him into his soft, warm nest.

"See, little one?" she whispered. "Helping a friend is the best way to feel sleepy."

And Otto slept — deep, deep asleep — dreaming of silver rivers and twinkling lights.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Helping a friend is the best way to feel sleepy — and kindness always lights the way home.*
