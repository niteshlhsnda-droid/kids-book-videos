# Chira the Chick and the Way Home

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Chira was a tiny yellow chick who lived in a nest under the rose bush. One morning, she chased a butterfly. Flap flap flutter! She chased it past the fence, past the pond, and into the tall grass.

Then the butterfly flew away. Chira looked around. Nothing looked familiar. Oh no! She was lost.

Chira felt frightened, her little heart pounding. "I do not know the way home," she peeped, a tear sliding down her beak.

Her friend Tansy the tortoise ambled by — tortoises walk slowly, but they know every path.

"Why are you crying, little chick?" asked Tansy.

"I am lost," sobbed Chira. "I chased a butterfly too far."

Tansy nodded kindly. "Then we will follow the friendly sounds home," she said.

"Follow the friendly sound home."

"Follow the friendly sound home," Chira repeated softly.

"Follow the friendly sound home." It became their guiding words.

"Follow the friendly sound home."

Tansy lifted her wrinkled head. "Listen," she whispered.

Far away, a hen was clucking. Cluck cluck cluck.

"That is my mama!" cried Chira.

"Follow the friendly sound home," said Tansy.

They walked toward the clucking, step by step. Tansy walked slowly, and Chira walked slowly beside her. Cluck cluck cluck — louder now. Chira felt hopeful and braver. The sound was leading them home.

Can you listen like Chira?
Close your eyes. What do you hear?
Pretend you hear: cluck cluck cluck!

They passed the pond. They passed the fence. The rose bush came into view.

"Mama!" peeped Chira. She ran the last few steps, and her mama wrapped her in warm wings.

"My lost chick is found!" clucked Mama.

Chira felt safe and loved. Tansy smiled and waved goodbye.

That night, Chira snuggled in the nest with her mama's wing warm over her.

"Thank you, Tansy," whispered Chira.

"Sleep tight, little traveler," said Tansy from below.

Chira closed her eyes and heard the night sounds, soft and friendly — crickets, breeze, and Mama's breathing. Cluck, breathe, sleep. And Chira drifted into the coziest sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Listening carefully and walking slowly can guide you home.*
