Sage the Seahorse and the Sleepy Sea Garden

A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Deep under the sea, in a quiet, sandy hollow, there lived a little seahorse named Sage.

Sage was small and golden, with a curly tail that wrapped around the seaweed whenever he wanted to rest. And Sage had a very special job: he looked after the sleepy sea garden.

The sleepy sea garden was full of soft, swaying flowers. There were pink fan-flowers, purple bubble-blossoms, and tiny blue star-flowers that opened only at night. When the flowers swayed, they sang a soft, sleepy song, and all the sea creatures nearby felt their eyes grow heavy.

Every evening, Sage swam slowly through his garden, checking every flower. He straightened a bent stem here. He brushed away a grain of sand there. "Sleep well, little flowers," he whispered. "Grow sweet dreams for everyone."

One evening, a little clownfish named Coral came darting into the garden, her fins fluttering fast.

"Sage! Sage!" she cried. "I cannot sleep! I tossed and turned all last night, and now I am so tired that I bumped into a rock!"

"Oh, dear," said Sage. "Come and sit beside the blue star-flowers. They open at night, and their song is the sleepiest song in the sea."

Coral settled softly among the star-flowers and listened. The star-flowers swayed. Hush, hush, hush, sang the garden. Coral's fins slowed. Her eyes blinked. Once. Twice. And then she was fast asleep, rocking gently in the water.

Soon, a tiny turtle paddled in. "I cannot sleep either," he yawned. "The waves are too noisy tonight."

"Come and rest here," said Sage. "The fan-flowers will sing you a lullaby."

The turtle tucked his head under a fan-flower, and hush, hush, hush went the garden. In a minute, he was asleep too, floating like a little boat.

Then came a baby octopus, all eight arms in a tangle. "Too much wiggling," he mumbled. "I cannot stop wiggling!"

Sage smiled. "Wrap your arms around this soft seaweed," he said, "and listen to the bubble-blossoms."

Blub, blub, blub sang the bubble-blossoms. The baby octopus's arms went soft and still, and he drifted off to sleep with a happy little smile.

Sage looked around his garden. Coral slept among the star-flowers. The turtle slept under the fan-flower. The baby octopus slept in the seaweed. The whole garden swayed and sang its sleepy song.

Sage wrapped his curly tail around his favourite stem, closed his eyes, and whispered, "Goodnight, garden. Goodnight, friends. Sleep well, everyone."

And under the deep, dark, gentle sea, they all slept soundly until morning.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A calm place and a kind friend can help anyone rest — even the wiggliest octopus.
