# The Monkey and the Peas

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a sunny forest, there lived a cheerful monkey named Miko.

Miko loved peas — round, green, sweet peas. He ate them for breakfast. He ate them for lunch. Sometimes he even dreamed about peas — round, green peas bouncing through his dreams! He loved them more than mangoes! More than bananas! Peas were his favorite food in the whole wide world.

One morning, a kind farmer gave Miko a whole bag of peas. "Here you go, Miko!" said the farmer. "Peas for the whole day!"

Miko was SO happy. He swung the bag over his shoulder and skipped along the path, humming his little pea song. "La-la-la! Peas, peas, peas!"

Then — plip! One little pea slipped out of a tiny hole in the bag and fell into the tall, tall grass.

Miko stopped. "Oh! My pea!" he said.

He set his big bag of peas down on the path and bent over to look for the lost pea. "Where are you, little pea? Where did you go?"

He searched here. He searched there. He parted the tall grass with his paws — the grass tickled his nose! — peek-peek-peeking all around... A cricket chirped. A butterfly fluttered right past his nose. But no pea!

But while Miko was busy hunting for that one little pea — uh-oh! The bag of peas tipped over on the path!

Out rolled the peas — roll, roll, roll! Down the path they went, bouncing and rolling everywhere! Where do you think all those peas rolled to?

When Miko looked up, the bag was empty. All his peas had rolled away — down the hill, into the bushes, under the big banyan tree with its hanging roots!

All of them. Gone.

Miko sat down on the path. He looked at the empty bag. He looked at the empty grass. And you know what he did?

He laughed! "Ha-ha-ha!" Miko laughed and laughed until his tummy hurt.

"Oh, Miko," he giggled. "You lost a whole bag of peas while chasing just ONE pea!"

Just then, his friends came swinging by — Pippa the parrot and Bunty the squirrel. "Miko, why are you laughing?" they asked.

Miko told them everything. "And then — whoosh! — all my peas ran away!" he said, waving his arms wide. Pippa and Bunty giggled. And then he did the kindest thing.

He searched all around and found a few peas that hadn't rolled far — just a little handful. And he shared them, one by one, with Pippa and Bunty. Pippa crunched her pea — crunch! Bunty nibbled his — nibble, nibble. "Best peas ever!" said Bunty.

They sat together under the banyan tree, munching the peas and laughing at Miko's silly story.

And Miko learned something that day: it is much better to enjoy what you have — and share it with friends — than to lose everything chasing one little thing.

That evening, as the sun went down, soft and gold, Miko curled up in his cozy tree. He felt warm and happy inside. And he drifted off to sleep, dreaming of green peas and good friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Enjoy what you have — don't lose everything chasing one small thing.*
