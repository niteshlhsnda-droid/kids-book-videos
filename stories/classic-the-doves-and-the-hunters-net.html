The Doves and the Hunter's Net

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On a bright, sunny morning, a flock of soft grey doves flew down into a golden field. The sun was warm on their wings, and the air smelled sweet, like fresh hay.

"Look!" cooed little Piku, the smallest dove. "Grains! Hundreds of golden grains, scattered all over the ground!"

The doves fluttered down happily. They pecked and pecked, peck, peck, peck, filling their round little tummies. The grains were crunchy and sweet, and the doves forgot all about the time.

But the grains were not lying there by chance. A hunter had spread them on top of a great big net, hidden under the soft grass. And as the doves pecked away, the hunter tugged his rope.

Whoosh! The net flew up and fell over the whole flock.

"Oh no!" cried Piku. "We are caught!"

The doves flapped and fluttered and bumped into one another. Feathers flew everywhere. But the more they flapped, the more tangled they became.

"Stop, stop," said Grandma Dove, the oldest and wisest of them all. Her voice was calm and soft. "Flapping alone will not help us. But flapping together will."

"What do you mean?" asked Piku, trembling.

"Listen," said Grandma Dove. "When I say one, two, three, we must all flap our wings at the very same time, as hard as we can. Together, we can lift this net right off the ground."

The doves looked at one another. Then they nodded. They were frightened, but they trusted Grandma Dove.

"One," said Grandma Dove.

"Two," said Grandma Dove.

"THREE!" cried all the doves together.

Flap! Flap! Flap! Every wing beat at once. And wonder of wonders, the net began to rise! Up, up, up it went, with all the doves flying underneath it, carrying it like a great soft blanket.

"Keep flapping!" called Grandma Dove. "We are flying! We are free!"

The doves flew over the field, over the river, and over the tall green trees, all the way to a quiet little hollow where their friend, Nini the mouse, lived.

"Nini! Nini!" called Grandma Dove. "Please help us!"

Nini the mouse peeked out of her tiny house. Her eyes grew round when she saw the net full of doves.

"Oh my," she squeaked. "Hold still, my friends. I will nibble the ropes for you."

Nibble, nibble, nibble. Nini's sharp little teeth worked quickly. One rope snapped. Then another. Then another. And soon, pop! The net fell away, and every dove was free.

"Thank you, thank you, dear Nini!" cooed the doves. They brought her the sweetest seeds they could find, and Nini munched them happily.

Then the doves flew home together, their wings beating in the same happy rhythm. The sun was setting, and the first stars were twinkling in the soft blue sky.

That night, as they tucked their heads under their wings, Piku whispered, "Alone, I was small and scared. Together, we could fly."

And all the doves drifted off to sleep, safe and warm, dreaming of golden fields.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Together we are strong.
