# Birbal and the Moon

*A timeless Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One soft, silver night, Emperor Akbar walked in his palace garden.

The moon hung round and bright in the dark sky, like a big pearl lamp.

Akbar looked up at it and sighed a long, slow sigh.

"Oh, Birbal," he said, "I wish I could hold the moon in my hands, just once."

Birbal smiled his kind, clever smile.

"Your Majesty," he said, "wait here. I will bring you the moon."

Akbar laughed. "No one can hold the moon, Birbal. It lives far, far away in the sky."

"Just wait and see," said Birbal, and he hurried away.

Birbal went to the palace kitchen and came back with a wide, shiny silver bowl.

He filled it with cool, clear water, right up to the brim.

Then he set the bowl down on a low stone in the middle of the garden.

"Now, look," Birbal whispered.

Akbar bent down and looked into the bowl.

There it was! The round, glowing moon was floating in the water, right in the silver bowl.

It shimmered and twinkled, soft and bright, like a little lamp made of light.

"Oh!" said Akbar, his eyes wide with wonder. "The moon is here!"

"Hold it gently, Your Majesty," said Birbal.

Akbar reached out with both hands and lifted the silver bowl.

The water stayed still and calm, and the moon stayed right there, floating in the water.

Akbar held the moon in his hands, just as he had wished.

He turned the bowl this way and that way, and the moon turned with him, glowing softly.

"How beautiful," he whispered. "How soft and bright it looks."

Birbal stood beside him, smiling.

"You see, Your Majesty," he said, "some wishes do not need long arms. They need a clever, gentle heart."

Akbar laughed a soft, happy laugh.

"You are right, Birbal," he said. "I held the moon, and I did not even have to leave my garden."

Then Akbar looked up at the big moon in the sky, and down at the small moon in his bowl.

"Thank you, Birbal," he said. "Tonight, I will remember this forever."

The stars twinkled above them, soft and sleepy.

The garden smelled sweet, like jasmine flowers in the cool night air.

Akbar sat down on the low stone, still holding his bowl of moonlight.

He felt calm and happy, like a child with a wonderful secret.

Birbal sat down beside him, and together they watched the moon glow in the water.

Akbar closed his eyes for a moment and breathed in the cool night air. He could hear soft crickets singing in the grass, and far away, a night bird called, sweet and low. The whole garden felt quiet, gentle, and full of wonder.

Soon, Akbar yawned a small, sleepy yawn.

"It is time for bed," said Birbal gently.

Akbar nodded and set the bowl down with great care.

"Good night, little moon," he whispered to the bowl.

"Good night, big moon," he whispered to the sky.

And as Akbar walked back to his palace, the moon followed him with its soft light, all the way home.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever, gentle heart can bring even the faraway moon close.*
