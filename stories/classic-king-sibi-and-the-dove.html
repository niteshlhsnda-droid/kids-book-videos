King Sibi and the Dove

A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, there lived a king named Sibi, and his promise was this: "I will protect anyone who comes to me for help — no matter who, no matter what."

One morning, King Sibi sat in his garden among the cool green leaves and the sweet smell of roses. A little dove came fluttering down from the blue sky and hid, trembling, under the king's soft robe. Can you hear how fast the little dove's heart is beating — pit-pat, pit-pat?

"Save me, oh king!" gasped the dove, all out of breath. "The hawk — he's chasing me!"

And down swooped the hawk. "That dove is my dinner!" he cried. "Give him to me!" He was such a tiny dove — hardly one mouthful!

King Sibi held the little dove close. "This little one has asked for my protection," he said. "I cannot give him up."

The hawk's eyes glittered — he had just thought of a clever plan. "Then give me what you love most, oh king — the same weight as the dove!"

The king brought out a great shining pair of scales — two silver dishes swaying on a golden chain. On one side sat the little dove, his feathers warm and puffed. On the other side, the king placed his treasures — his gold crown, his sparkling jewels, even his royal robes. What would you put on the scale next, little one? But no matter what he added, the dove's side would not rise. It was as if the tiny dove were as heavy as all the gold in the world! The little dove just sat there, fluffing up his feathers, looking very pleased with himself.

"More," said the hawk, softer now.

King Sibi closed his eyes, ready to give something of his very own — and just then, the sky glowed soft and golden.

The hawk and the dove were no ordinary birds — they were two shining gods, glowing soft and warm like candlelight, come to test the king's heart.

"Oh, kind King Sibi," they said softly. "We only came to test your heart. Your kindness is greater than any gold."

And with a smile, the two bright gods drifted softly into the quiet sky.

And that night, the little dove slept safe and warm among the soft garden leaves, just as the king had promised.

Goodnight, little dove. The end. 🌙

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: True kindness protects the weak — a promise to help is the noblest promise.
