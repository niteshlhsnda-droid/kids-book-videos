Nibbles the Rabbit and the Sleepy Moon

A cozy original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Under the green hill was a cozy little burrow. It smelled of warm earth and sweet dry grass. And in it lived a little rabbit named Nibbles.

Nibbles loved bedtime. He loved his warm blanket and the way Mama Rabbit kissed his nose goodnight.

But tonight, Nibbles could not sleep.

He hopped to the burrow door and peeked out. The moon was up in the sky — but half its round face was tucked behind a soft gray cloud, like a cheek hiding under a blanket.

"Oh no," whispered Nibbles. "The moon looks sad tonight. I have to cheer it up."

So Nibbles told Mama Rabbit, "I'll cheer the moon up!" She tied his tiny slippers and said, "Come straight back, little one." Off he bounced into the moonlit meadow.

First, he found a fluffy feather lying in the cool grass. Nibbles held it up to the moonlight. "Now — whose feather do you think this is?"

"This'll be soft and cozy for the moon," he said, tucking it gently behind his ear.

Then he heard the crickets. They were humming a sleepy little lullaby — hum, hum, hum.

"Shhh," Nibbles whispered, listening. "That's the sleepiest song I ever heard." He hummed it softly to himself so he would remember. Can you hum it too? Hum, hum, hum.

Then he hopped home for just a moment. Mama Rabbit poured him milk in his tiny blue cup.

"For the moon?" Mama Rabbit asked, her whiskers twitching. "What a kind little idea, my bunny."

Nibbles carried the feather and his warm milk to the top of the hill, with the lullaby humming softly in his head. There the grass shone silver, and every drop of dew sparkled like a tiny moon.

He looked up... and smiled softly. What do you think he saw?

The moon was not sad at all! The little cloud was drifting away, and underneath it, the moon was yawning — a big, round, sleepy yawn.

"You're not sad," said Nibbles softly. "You're just sleepy — like me."

The moon glowed silver. The cloud at its edge curled up like a smile — and it twinkled once, just for him.

So Nibbles settled into the soft grass. He drank his milk, one tiny sip at a time. He hummed the crickets' lullaby softer and softer. Soon it was only a whisper.

The feather slipped down from behind his ear and lay in the grass beside him. Nibbles laid his head on the cool grass and watched the stars blink, slow and sleepy, one by one.

Down in the meadow, the flowers drooped their sleepy heads. The crickets' humming grew slower... and slower... and slower.

Nibbles gave a long rabbit yawn. His pink nose scrunched up, and his ears flopped back. Then, very sleepily, he padded home to his burrow, curled up under his own warm blanket, and Mama Rabbit kissed his nose.

High above, the moon yawned too, and drifted off behind its soft cloud blanket.

And the whole meadow sighed... and fell asleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Sometimes worries are just shadows — rest comes when we do.
