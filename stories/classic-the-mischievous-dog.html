The Mischievous Dog

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a little village with green fields and warm, dusty lanes lived a small brown dog named Bruno.

Bruno was a happy little dog who loved to run, jump, and chase butterflies down the lane.

But Bruno had one naughty habit. When visitors came, he ran up and nipped at their heels — nip, nip, nip! Then he barked and barked, loud as a drum.

"Oh, Bruno!" his master would say. "That is not a kind way to say hello."

But Bruno only wagged his tail and barked again. He thought it was great fun.

One morning, his master had an idea. He took a small shiny bell and tied it onto Bruno's collar.

"There," he said with a smile. "Now everyone will hear you coming, little Bruno."

Bruno looked down at the bell. It was round and golden, and it went tinkle-tinkle when he moved.

"Oh!" thought Bruno. "What a beautiful prize! My master must think I am the best dog in the village!"

So Bruno pranced down the lane, his head held high, and the bell went tinkle, tinkle, tinkle.

"Look at me!" he barked at the hens. "I have a golden bell! I am a very special dog!"

The hens flapped away, and nobody came close. But Bruno did not mind — he thought they were staring at his bell.

That evening, Bruno trotted past the mango tree, where Mimi, the wise old cat, was washing her paws.

"Hello, Mimi!" barked Bruno. "Look at my golden bell! Is it not the finest bell you have ever seen?"

Mimi blinked her green eyes slowly and smiled.

"It is a very pretty bell, Bruno," she said. "But do you know why your master tied it on?"

"Because I am the best dog, of course!" said Bruno, puffing out his chest.

Mimi shook her head gently. "No, little friend," she said. "Your master tied it on so everyone can hear you coming. It is a gentle warning, not a prize — so nobody gets a fright."

Bruno's ears drooped, and his tail drooped too. The bell went tinkle... tinkle... softly.

"Oh," he said in a small voice. "I thought everyone was looking at me because I was special."

"You are special, Bruno," said Mimi kindly. "But a bell cannot make a dog good. Kindness can. Try a wag and a smile, instead of a nip and a bark."

The next morning, when the postman came up the lane, Bruno did not run or nip. He sat very still and wagged his tail, thump, thump, thump, on the dusty ground.

The postman smiled and patted Bruno's head. "Well, hello, Bruno," he said. "What a good dog you are today."

Bruno's bell still went tinkle-tinkle when he walked. But now nobody ran away, and Bruno did not prance or show off. He just wagged his tail, warm and happy inside.

That night, as the moon rose, Bruno curled up in his basket, and his bell gave one soft little tinkle. The best prize of all, he knew, was not a shiny bell. It was being a kind dog, loved by everyone.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A shiny bell cannot make you good — kindness and calm can.
