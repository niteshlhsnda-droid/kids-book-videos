# Goldie and the Three Bears

In a green wood lived three bears in a cottage with a red door: great big Papa Bear, middle-sized Mama Bear, and tiny Baby Bear. Each morning, Mama Bear cooked honey porridge in three bowls: a great big bowl, a middle-sized bowl, and a tiny wee bowl.

One sunny morning, the porridge was too hot to eat. "Let us walk in the wood while it cools," said Papa Bear. So the three bears ambled off down the path, leaving the cottage quiet.

Down the same path came Goldie, a little girl with golden curls. She had wandered further than she meant to, and her tummy rumbled at the sight of the cottage. She knocked twice, but nobody answered. The door stood ajar, and warm porridge smelled delicious.

Goldie peeped inside and called, "Hello?" Nobody answered. She stepped in, just to look. On the table stood three bowls of porridge. Papa Bear's was too hot, Mama Bear's too cold, but Baby Bear's tiny bowl was just right, and she ate it all up.

Then Goldie wanted a rest. Papa Bear's great big chair was far too high, and Mama Bear's middle-sized chair was far too soft. But Baby Bear's tiny chair was just right — until crack! As she wiggled, the little chair broke into pieces.

Goldie felt sorry, but she was so sleepy. Upstairs were three beds: great big, middle-sized, and tiny wee. The big bed was too hard, the middle bed too lumpy, but the tiny bed was just right. She curled up and fell fast asleep.

Just then, the three bears came home. "Somebody has been at my porridge!" growled Papa Bear. "Somebody has been at my porridge!" said Mama Bear. "Somebody has been at my porridge — and eaten it all up!" squeaked Baby Bear.

"Somebody has been sitting in my chair!" said Papa Bear. "Somebody has been sitting in my chair!" said Mama Bear. "Somebody has been sitting in my chair — and broken it!" squeaked Baby Bear. Upstairs they tiptoed, and there lay Goldie, fast asleep.

Goldie woke with a start. "Oh! I'm so sorry! I was hungry and tired, and forgot to ask. I ate your porridge, broke your chair, and slept in your bed." Baby Bear smiled. "Help me mend my chair," he said, "and stay for porridge — if you ask first." So Goldie helped mend the chair, and they shared fresh porridge.

As evening stars came out, Goldie walked home, and the three bears waved from the red door until she was out of sight. From that day on, Goldie always knocked first when she passed the cottage — and the bears always called, "Come in, Goldie!"

Sleep now, little Goldie.

The end. 🌙
