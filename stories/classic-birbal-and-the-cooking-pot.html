Birbal and the Cooking Pot

A clever Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a grand palace in India, there lived a wise man named Birbal, the best friend of Emperor Akbar.

One day, Birbal wanted to cook sweet rice pudding for his family, but his own pot was too small.

"I need a big pot," said Birbal. "I will borrow one from the emperor's kitchen."

So Birbal walked to the palace kitchen and smiled at the cook, a round, friendly man with a big white hat.

"Dear cook," said Birbal, "may I borrow your biggest cooking pot for one day?"

"Of course, Birbal!" said the cook. "Take it, and bring it back soon."

Birbal carried the big pot home and cooked the sweetest rice pudding you can imagine. His whole family ate it with happy smiles.

A few days later, Birbal walked back to the palace kitchen. He carried the big pot — and inside it, a tiny little pot!

The cook stared. "Birbal, what is this tiny pot?"

Birbal smiled his clever smile. "Your big pot had a baby pot!" he said. "While it was at my house, it had a little baby. So the baby pot is yours too!"

The cook laughed and laughed. "Ha, ha, ha! A baby pot! How funny!" He kept the tiny pot and put it on his shelf.

A few weeks later, Birbal borrowed the big pot again — and this time he did not bring it back. The cook waited three long days.

At last he marched to Birbal's house and knocked on the door. "Birbal! Where is my big cooking pot?"

Birbal opened the door slowly and looked very sad. "Oh, dear cook, I have bad news. Your big pot has died."

"Died?" shouted the cook. "A pot cannot die! Pots are not alive!"

But Birbal just shook his head. "I am sorry. The pot is gone."

The cook was so cross that he ran to Emperor Akbar and told the whole story.

"Your Majesty!" he cried. "Birbal borrowed my big pot and will not give it back! He says the pot has died! Can a pot die?"

Emperor Akbar stroked his beard. He looked at Birbal and smiled a little smile, because he knew Birbal's clever ways.

"Birbal," said the emperor, "tell us the truth. What happened to the pot?"

Birbal bowed and said, "Your Majesty, if a pot can have a baby, then a pot can also die. That is only fair!"

For a moment, no one spoke. Then Emperor Akbar laughed — a big, warm laugh that filled the whole hall. "Ha, ha, ha! Birbal is right!"

The cook thought for a moment. Then he laughed too. "Ha, ha, ha! I kept the baby pot, so I must believe the pot can die!"

Birbal laughed as well. He brought back the big pot, shiny and clean, and shared sweet rice pudding with everyone in the palace.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Be fair — if you accept the funny part, you must accept the whole story.
