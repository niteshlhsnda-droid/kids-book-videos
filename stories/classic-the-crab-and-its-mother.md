# The Crab and Its Mother

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, on a soft, sandy beach, there lived a mother crab named Moti and her little baby crab named Bittu.

Moti was a big, round crab with strong claws and eight busy legs. Bittu was a tiny crab with a shiny shell and eight tiny legs that went scuttle-scuttle-scuttle across the sand.

Like all crabs, Moti and Bittu walked sideways. Scuttle-scuttle, to the left. Scuttle-scuttle, to the right. That is simply how crabs walk!

But one morning, Moti looked at Bittu and said, "Bittu, my dear, you must learn to walk straight. Walking sideways looks so untidy! Walk forward, like the seagulls do. One foot in front of the other. Straight, straight, straight!"

Bittu looked up at his mother with his big, round eyes. "But Mama," he said, "crabs do not walk straight. We walk sideways! Watch — scuttle-scuttle!"

"That is the problem!" said Moti. "From today, you must walk straight. It is much better. Now, show me — walk straight, please."

Bittu tried. He put one tiny leg forward. Then another. Then — scuttle! — he slid sideways into a little shell.

"Oh, Bittu!" sighed Moti. "Why can you not walk straight?"

Bittu tilted his little head. "Mama," he said in his small, sweet voice, "can you show me how? Can you walk straight, just once, so I can copy you?"

Moti smiled. "Of course I can! Watch me, Bittu. Straight, straight, straight!"

Moti lifted one big leg. She put it forward. Then she lifted another leg — and scuttle! — she slid sideways, right into a puddle!

SPLASH!

Bittu giggled. "Mama! You walked sideways too!"

Moti looked at her wet claws. Then she looked at Bittu's laughing face. And she started to laugh too.

"Oh, my little Bittu," she said, "you are right! I asked you to do something I cannot do myself. That was not fair of me."

Bittu scuttled over and gave his mother a little hug with his tiny claw. "It is all right, Mama," he said. "We are crabs. Sideways is our way!"

Moti nodded. "Yes," she said. "Sideways is our way. And it is a very good way! We can scuttle to the left, scuttle to the right, and never bump into anything!"

So Moti and Bittu spent the whole afternoon practising their sideways walk together. Scuttle-scuttle to the left, scuttle-scuttle to the right. They had a sideways race. They played sideways hide-and-seek. And they laughed all the way home as the sun went down over the sea.

And from that day on, whenever Moti wanted Bittu to learn something new, she showed him first — step by step, side by side.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Show, don't just tell — and love the way you walk.*
