# Gigi the Giraffe and the Gentle Goodnight

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On the wide golden savanna lived a young giraffe named Gigi.

Gigi was the tallest animal for miles around. Her legs were long, her neck was longer, and her head — with its two soft little horns and big fluttery eyelashes — reached higher than the acacia trees.

Being tall was wonderful in the daytime. Gigi could nibble the sweetest top leaves. She could spot her friends coming from far, far away. She could even tickle clouds when they drifted low.

But at bedtime, being tall was a problem.

You see, when Gigi curled up to sleep, her body was warm down in the soft grass — but her head was way, way up in the cool night air. And up there, the stars were SO close and SO twinkly that Gigi could not close her eyes.

"Twinkle, twinkle," went the stars. "Look at us! Look at us!"

Gigi tried counting clouds. "One cloud… two clouds…" But the clouds had all gone to bed.

She tried humming a sleepy song. "Hmm-hmm-hmm…" But her own humming tickled her ears and made her giggle.

"Oh," sighed Gigi, "how will I EVER fall asleep?"

Just then, the moon rose — round and silver, with a soft smile spreading across its glowing face. It looked down at Gigi and smiled a moon-smile.

"Little one," said the moon in its soft moon-voice, "why are your eyes still open?"

"Because the stars won't stop twinkling," said Gigi. "They are too close and too bright."

The moon thought for a moment. Then it said, "Little one, that tall neck is a gift. Only YOU can reach the stars. Go on — wish them goodnight, and watch them sleep."

Gigi's eyes went wide. She had never thought of it that way!

Slowly, she stretched her long neck up into the glittering sky. And one by one, she whispered to the stars:

"Goodnight, little star."

"Goodnight, bright star."

"Goodnight, sleepy star."

Now it's your turn — which star gets YOUR goodnight?

And as she said goodnight to each one, the stars seemed to yawn — tiny, sleepy yawns — and their twinkling grew softer… and softer… and softer.

At last, only the moon was left. Gigi whispered, "Goodnight, moon."

"Goodnight, Gigi," said the moon. And it blew her a silver kiss that drifted down, down, down — and settled over her like the softest blanket in the world.

Gigi curled her long neck down, rested her chin on her knees, and closed her fluttery eyelashes.

And she slept — tall, cozy, and happy — all night long.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What makes you different is often what makes you wonderful.*
