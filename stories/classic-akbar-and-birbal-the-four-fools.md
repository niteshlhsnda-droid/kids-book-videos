# Akbar and Birbal: The Four Fools

*A timeless Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Emperor Akbar loved asking tricky questions. And his clever friend Birbal loved answering them.

One morning, Akbar leaned back on his velvet cushion and said, "Birbal! By sunset today, bring me the four biggest fools in my kingdom!"

Birbal bowed. "As you wish, Your Majesty."

He walked out into the noisy, sunny streets of the city, smiling a secret smile.

First, he saw a farmer in a field, pouring water on a big grey stone.

"Why are you watering a stone?" asked Birbal.

"So that flowers will grow from it!" said the farmer happily.

Birbal smiled. "Fool number one," he said, and led the farmer to the palace.

Next, in the marketplace, he saw a little boy holding an empty jar up to the sky.

The air smelled of sweet mangoes, and sellers called out in sing-song voices.

"What are you doing?" asked Birbal.

"Trying to catch a moonbeam in my jar, so I can keep it in my room!" said the boy.

Birbal smiled kindly. "Fool number two," he said, and walked the boy gently to the palace to meet the Emperor.

Then, near the palace gates, he saw a rich merchant dropping gold coins into a wooden parrot's beak.

"Why are you feeding gold to a wooden parrot?" asked Birbal.

"So it will lay a diamond egg!" said the merchant, very seriously.

Birbal laughed softly. "Fool number three," he said, and led the merchant to the palace.

At sunset, Birbal stood before Akbar with only three fools.

Akbar frowned. "Birbal! I asked for FOUR fools. Where is the fourth?"

Hmm… who do YOU think the fourth fool was?

Birbal bowed very low. "Your Majesty, the fourth fool is… you! You asked your friend to do what no one could do in a single day!"

For a moment, the court was silent.

Then Akbar threw back his head and laughed and laughed, with happy tears on his cheeks.

"Birbal!" he said, wiping his eyes. "You are right! Asking for what no one can do makes a fool of the asker too!"

The whole court smiled and nodded softly. And the three fools smiled too, for they were only gentle dreamers.

That night, under the soft silver moon, Akbar smiled and whispered the story one last time — then closed his eyes and drifted to sleep.

The end. 🌙
