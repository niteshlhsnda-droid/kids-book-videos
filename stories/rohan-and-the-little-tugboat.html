Rohan and the Little Tugboat

A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Rohan lived beside a harbour where big ships came and went. Every evening, he watched the boats from his bedroom window and waved to the sailors.

But Rohan's favourite boat was the smallest one of all — a little red tugboat named Tilly, who puffed up and down the harbour all day long, pushing big ships gently into place.

"Tilly is the smallest boat," said Rohan's father, "but she does the most helpful work in the whole harbour."

One night, as Rohan was falling asleep, he heard a soft, friendly sound — toot! toot! — from the harbour. He rubbed his eyes. There was Tilly, bobbing beside his window, with a tiny captain's hat on her funnel!

"All aboard, Captain Rohan!" tooted Tilly. "I need your help tonight. The harbour boats are all sleepy, and someone must tuck them into their berths."

Rohan climbed aboard — in his dreams, everything is possible — and put on the tiny captain's hat. "All aboard!" he cried. "Let us tuck in the boats!"

First came Big Bertha, the great cargo ship. "I am too big to sleep," grumbled Bertha. But Tilly nudged her gently, Rohan sang a soft song, and Bertha yawned a huge yawn and settled into her berth.

Next came Sally the sailing boat, whose sails flapped and fluttered. "I cannot stop wiggling," giggled Sally. But Rohan folded her sails down like a blanket, Tilly rocked her gently, and Sally soon rocked herself to sleep.

Then came the fishing boats, one by one — Finn, Fifi, and little Freddy. Rohan counted them as they settled: "One… two… three… good night, little boats."

At last, the whole harbour was quiet. The water was smooth as glass. The moon shone down on the sleepy boats, and Tilly puffed a soft, sleepy puff of steam.

"Thank you, Captain Rohan," tooted Tilly quietly. "You tucked in every boat. Now it is your turn to sleep."

Rohan yawned. He climbed off Tilly and into his bed. His father tucked his blanket in, just like Rohan had tucked in the boats.

"Good night, Tilly," whispered Rohan.

And from the harbour came the softest, sleepiest sound — toot… toot… good night.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Helping others settle down for the night is the cosiest way to end your own day.
