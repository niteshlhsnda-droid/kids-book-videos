# Pippa the Penguin's Pillow Parade

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cozy ice-nest at the bottom of the world, where the snow was soft as whipped cream, lived a little penguin named Pippa.

Pippa had a round fluffy tummy, tiny flippers, and feet like two orange mittens. She lived with Mama and Papa in the warmest, snuggliest nest in all of Antarctica.

One cold night, the wind whispered *whooo... whooo...* outside. Mama tucked Pippa into the nest. Papa sang a sleepy penguin song. *Laa-loo, laa-loo.*

But Pippa could not fall asleep.

She wiggled. She squirmed. She flapped her tiny flippers.

"I am not sleepy," she announced. "Not one little bit."

Mama smiled. "Then let us make you sleepy," she said. "Let us start a pillow parade."

"A pillow parade?" said Pippa.

"Yes," said Mama. "We will gather the softest things in the whole world, one by one, and line them up like a parade into your nest. Each soft thing will get a goodnight."

Pippa liked the sound of that. So off she waddled — *waddle, waddle* — to collect soft things.

First, Pippa found a downy feather that Mama had dropped. It was white and fluffy as a dandelion puff.

She laid it in the nest. She patted it gently.

"Goodnight, feather," she whispered. "Soft and light."

The feather settled. *Poof.*

Then Pippa scooped up a puff of fresh snow. It was cold and powdery and squeaked a little. *Squeak, squeak.*

She laid it in the nest, next to the feather.

"Goodnight, snow," she whispered. "Cool and quiet."

The snow settled. *Sshhh.*

Then Pippa found another soft thing — a piece of soft sea-moss, green and springy, that the waves had washed up. *Squish, squish.*

She laid it in the nest, next to the snow.

"Goodnight, sea-moss," she whispered. "Bouncy and bright."

The sea-moss settled. *Squish.*

Then Pippa found a warm, smooth pebble by the shore, where the waves went *lap, lap, lap*. The pebble was round and comforting, like a held hand.

She laid it in the nest, next to the sea-moss.

"Goodnight, pebble," she whispered. "Warm and smooth."

The pebble settled. *Plop.*

Then Pippa collected another soft thing — a curl of soft Arctic wool from Papa's old scarf. It smelled of home and felt like a cloud.

She laid it in the nest, next to the pebble, and patted it flat.

"Goodnight, wool," she whispered. "Cozy and kind."

The wool settled. *Fluff.*

Then Pippa collected one more soft thing — the softest thing of all. She waddled back to Mama and snuggled right into Mama's fluffy belly.

"Goodnight, Mama," she whispered. "Softest of all."

Mama wrapped a flipper around her. *Snuggle.*

Now the pillow parade was complete. Feather, snow, sea-moss, pebble, wool, Mama — all lined up, soft and cozy, in the warm ice-nest.

Pippa lay back on her pillow parade. She felt the feather's fluff. She felt the snow's hush. She felt the sea-moss's bounce. She felt the pebble's warmth. She felt the wool's softness. And she felt Mama's heartbeat — *lub-dub, lub-dub* — slow and steady.

She yawned a great big penguin yawn. *Yaaawn.*

Her eyes blinked. Blink... blink... blink...

"I am sleepy," she whispered, surprised. "Just a little bit sleepy."

"Shhh," said Mama. "The parade is over. It is time to sleep."

Outside, the wind whispered *whooo... whooo...* — softer now, like a lullaby.

Inside, little Pippa closed her eyes, snug in her nest, surrounded by all the soft things in the world.

Feather. Snow. Sea-moss. Pebble. Wool. Mama. Goodnight.

And Pippa drifted off into the sleepiest, dreamiest sleep, while the aurora danced green and gold above the ice.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When sleep won't come, gather the soft and cozy things — and let them carry you gently to dreamland.*
