# Tara the Turtle and the Twinkling Tide Pool

*A sweet original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

On a sandy beach where the sea sang softly, there lived a little green turtle named Tara.

Tara loved the tide pools. When the sea went out, it left behind little pools of water full of wonders — pink shells, wiggly seaweed, and tiny crabs going sideways, sideways, sideways.

One evening, as the sun was setting, Tara paddled over to her favourite tide pool. And oh! What did she see?

The pool was twinkling! Hundreds of tiny lights sparkled in the water, like stars that had fallen from the sky.

"How beautiful!" whispered Tara. "It looks like the night sky came down to swim!"

But then she heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help!"

It was a baby fish, no bigger than Tara's toe. He had been left behind when the sea went out, and now he was flopping in the shallow water.

"I'm too far from the sea!" cried the baby fish. "I will never get home!"

"Don't worry," said Tara. "I will help you. But first, we must be gentle — the twinkling lights are sleeping."

Tara looked closely. The twinkling lights were not stars at all. They were tiny glowing sea creatures, curled up asleep in the pool, dreaming their sparkly dreams. Some glowed soft blue, some glowed gentle green, and some glowed the prettiest pink Tara had ever seen.

"Shhh," whispered Tara. "We must not wake them."

So Tara did something very clever. She dipped her shell into the pool and made a little wave — splish! — and the wave carried the baby fish, soft and slow, over the rocks and back toward the sea.

"Almost there!" whispered Tara.

One more gentle wave — splish! — and the baby fish was back in the big, blue sea!

"Thank you, Tara!" sang the baby fish, wagging his tail. "You are the kindest turtle on the whole beach!"

And just then, the tiny glowing creatures woke up — twinkle, twinkle, twinkle! — and they sparkled even brighter, as if to say thank you too.

Tara smiled. The sun had set, and the stars were coming out, one by one, in the dark blue sky. She counted them softly — one, two, three — until her eyes grew heavy and her shell felt warm and cosy.

"Goodnight, little lights," yawned Tara. "Goodnight, sea. Goodnight, baby fish."

She tucked herself under a warm rock, and the waves sang her a soft, slow lullaby — shhh, shhh, shhh.

And Tara slept — deep, deep asleep — dreaming of tide pools full of twinkling stars.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A gentle helping hand — or a gentle wave — can carry a friend all the way home.*
