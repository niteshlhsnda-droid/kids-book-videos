Perry the Pika and the Cosy Mountain Nests

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

High up in the mountains, where the air was crisp and the clouds drifted past like soft sheep, lived a tiny pika named Perry.

Pikas are small, round, furry animals with big ears and squeaky voices, and Perry was the smallest pika on the whole mountainside. But what Perry lacked in size, he made up for in kindness.

All summer long, while the sun warmed the rocks, Perry scampered about the mountainside, collecting things. He gathered soft moss, dry grass, and round, smooth stones that had been warmed by the sun all day.

"What are you collecting, Perry?" squeaked his friend, a little mountain mouse.

"I am building cosy nests," said Perry proudly. "Winter is coming, and I want every animal on the mountain to have a warm place to sleep."

So Perry worked and worked. On a sunny ledge, he built a nest of soft moss for the mountain mouse. In a sheltered hollow, he built a nest of dry grass for the little brown hare. And in the cosiest corner of all, beneath a big warm rock, he built a nest of sun-warmed stones and soft feathers for a tiny baby marmot who was the smallest of all the mountain babies.

"There," said Perry, patting the last nest smooth. "Now everyone will be warm."

But as the days grew shorter and the wind grew colder, Perry realised something. He had been so busy building nests for everyone else that he had forgotten to build one for himself!

The first snowflake fell. Then another, and another. Soon the whole mountainside was white and silent, and poor Perry was shivering on a cold, bare rock.

"Brr," squeaked Perry, wrapping his little paws around himself. "I am so cold."

Just then, he heard a chorus of tiny voices calling his name. "Perry! Perry! Come here!"

It was the mountain mouse, the brown hare, and the baby marmot, all peeping out of their cosy nests.

"You built nests for all of us," said the mouse. "Now we have built one for you!"

And sure enough, in the warmest, most sheltered hollow on the mountainside, his friends had built the cosiest nest of all — soft moss, dry grass, sun-warmed stones, and even a tiny feather blanket.

Perry hopped inside and snuggled down. It was warm. It was soft. It was perfect.

"Thank you, my friends," whispered Perry, his eyes growing heavy. "This is the cosiest nest in the whole wide world."

Outside, the snow fell softly over the mountain, covering the rocks in a white blanket. Inside their nests, the mouse, the hare, the marmot, and little Perry slept safe and warm, dreaming of spring sunshine and summer days.

And whenever the winter wind howled outside, Perry would smile in his sleep, because he knew that kindness always comes back to keep you warm.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness always comes back to keep you warm — when you care for others, others will care for you.
