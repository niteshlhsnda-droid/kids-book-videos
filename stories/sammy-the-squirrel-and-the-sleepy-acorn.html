Sammy the Squirrel and the Sleepy Acorn

A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Sammy the squirrel lived in a big old oak tree in the middle of the wood. He had a round, fluffy tail and bright eyes. He loved to hop and scamper all day long. But most of all, Sammy loved acorns.

One golden autumn morning, Sammy was crunching through the fallen leaves when he saw something shiny. There, under a soft pile of leaves, was the biggest acorn he had ever seen. It was smooth and brown and shiny. "Oh!" said Sammy. "What a wonderful acorn! It is the best acorn in the whole wood!"

Sammy picked it up with both paws and hugged it tight. "I will keep you safe," he whispered. "I will guard you all day and all night. Nobody will take you away."

So Sammy sat on a mossy log and held the acorn close. He watched the leaves float down and the birds fly home. He watched the sun sink low, all pink and gold. But he would not put the acorn down. "I must stay awake," he said. "I must, I must."

Then the moon came up, round and silver. The stars began to twinkle, one by one. The wood grew quiet. Hush, hush, hush. Sammy's eyes felt heavy. His little head nodded down. Then up again. Then down again. "I am not sleepy," he said with a big yawn. But his eyes kept closing, all by themselves.

Just then, his mother came hopping softly through the leaves. "Sammy, my love," she said in a gentle voice. "It is time for bed. Why are you still awake?"

"I cannot sleep, Mama," said Sammy. "I found the best acorn in the whole wood, and I must guard it all night."

Mother Squirrel smiled. She took Sammy's paw and led him to the old oak tree. There, in the trunk, was a small, snug hollow lined with soft leaves. "Look," she said. "This is a safe, cosy home for your acorn. It will wait here all night, safe and sound, till morning."

Sammy tucked the acorn into the hollow and patted the leaves around it, like a little blanket. "Good night, acorn," he whispered. "Sleep tight. I will see you in the morning."

Then Sammy curled up in his own warm nest, high in the oak tree. His mother tucked a soft leaf over him like a blanket. "Now close your eyes," she said. "Dream sweet dreams."

Sammy closed his eyes. He dreamed of his acorn growing roots and reaching up, up, up into the sky, till it became the tallest tree in the wood. And as he dreamed, the moon shone softly through the leaves, and Sammy fell fast asleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Good things wait safely till morning.
