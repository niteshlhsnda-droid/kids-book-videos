Nasruddin Looks for His Donkey

A merry Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a little village with white houses and a crooked old well lived Nasruddin, the silliest wise man in the world.

Nasruddin had a grey donkey named Dapple, with long ears and a swishy tail. Every morning Nasruddin rode Dapple to the market, and every evening they trotted home together — clip-clop, clip-clop.

One morning, Nasruddin went to the stable and — oh no! Dapple was gone! The stable door was open, and there was not a single clip-clop to be heard.

"My donkey! My donkey!" cried Nasruddin, running into the village square. "Has anyone seen my Dapple?"

"I will help you look!" said the baker. "I will help!" said the tailor. "Me too!" said the little children.

So they all searched — behind the haystacks, beside the pond, under the big mulberry tree.

And all the while, Nasruddin kept chuckling to himself. "Hee, hee! Thank goodness! Thank goodness!"

The baker scratched his head. "Nasruddin, your donkey is LOST. What are you thanking goodness for?"

"Thank goodness," said Nasruddin with a very serious face, "that I was not RIDING Dapple when he wandered off — or I would be lost too, and you would have to search for ME as well!"

Everyone burst out laughing — ha, ha, ha! Even the grumpy old goat by the well gave a little bleat of laughter.

Just then, the little children shouted, "THERE HE IS!" — for Dapple was standing in the clover field, munching the sweetest green clover, his swishy tail wagging happily.

Nasruddin hugged Dapple's long neck. "You gave us all a merry morning, you naughty donkey!" he said.

And that evening, Nasruddin shared his sweet dates with everyone who had helped search — because laughter shared is happiness doubled. And whenever the villagers felt worried after that day, they remembered Nasruddin's laugh and looked for the bright side too.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Even in a worry, look for the bright side — and a laugh shared with friends makes everything better.
