The Dream Garden

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Suri the squirrel brushed her teeth, put on her pajamas, and climbed into her leafy bed. But her mind would not be quiet — she felt worried about tomorrow.

Tomorrow was the big nut-gathering day. What if she was too slow? What if she found no nuts at all? The worries buzzed around like busy bees.

Her grandmother had told her a secret. "When worries visit at bedtime," Grandma said, "plant them in the Dream Garden."

Suri tiptoed to the garden behind her tree, where moonlight made the flowers glow silver. In the middle stood a small empty pot with a wooden sign that read: PLANT WORRIES HERE.

Suri closed her eyes, gathered up her buzzy worries, and dropped them into the pot like tiny seeds. Plip. Plop. Plip.

"Now water it," whispered the night breeze.

Suri did not have a watering can, so she watered it the special way — she gave the pot a great big yawn. Yawns are the very best water for worry seeds.

"Grow soft, grow slow, let your worries go," she sang.

A tiny green sprout poked up and stretched toward the moon. It grew two round leaves, and then a bud appeared — blue as bedtime.

"Grow soft, grow slow, let your worries go," Suri sang again.

The bud opened into a beautiful flower with petals soft as pajamas, smelling of warm milk and honey. It swayed gently in the breeze.

"Grow soft, grow slow, let your worries go," Suri sang one last time.

The flower began to hum a lullaby, low and sweet. Suri's buzzy worries melted away, and she felt calm and peaceful. Tomorrow would take care of itself.

She carried the flower back to her bed and set it on her windowsill, where its petals glowed softly in the dark like a tiny nightlight.

Suri snuggled under her blanket, her eyes feeling heavy, her tail curling around her like a scarf, while the flower hummed its sleepy song.

Sleep now, little squirrel. Your worries have bloomed into something beautiful. Rest soft until the morning sun.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Worries grow smaller when you set them down before sleep.
