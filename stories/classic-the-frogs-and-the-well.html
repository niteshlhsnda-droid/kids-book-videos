The Frogs and the Well

A gentle Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, beside a cool green pond, there lived two little frogs.

They were the very best of friends. Every day, they hopped and splashed together. They caught tiny flies. They floated on lily pads like little green boats. And they sang, "Croak! Croak! Croak!" all day long.

One hot summer day, the sun shone down, down, down. The pond grew smaller and smaller. The mud at the edge of the pond grew dry and crackly.

"Oh dear," said the first little frog, wiping his brow. "The pond is drying up! Where shall we go to keep cool?"

Just then, the second little frog spotted something. "Look!" he cried. "There is a deep, deep well by the old stone wall! The water in there will be cool and fresh. Let us jump in!"

"Wait," said the first little frog. "Let us think first. The well is very deep. How will we climb out again?"

But the second little frog was too hot to wait. "Splash!" he cried, and he jumped — plop! — right into the well.

The water was cool and lovely. "Ahh!" he sighed. "This is wonderful! Come on in!"

But when he tried to climb out, the walls of the well were smooth and slippery. He slipped back down. "Slip!" He tried again. "Slop!" He could not climb out at all.

"Oh no!" he cried. "I am stuck! The walls are too smooth, and I am too small!"

The first little frog did not laugh. He did not say, "I told you so." He was a true friend.

"Do not worry," he called down. "I will fetch help. Hold on tight!"

And off he hopped — hop, hop, hop — all the way to the garden. There he found the ants, marching in a neat little line. He found the beetles, shiny and strong. And he found a kind old spider, spinning her web in the corner.

"Please, please help us!" said the little frog. "My friend is stuck in the well!"

The ants, the beetles, and the spider hurried to the well. The spider spun a long, strong rope out of grass and silk. The ants and the beetles held one end tight. The little frog called down, "Hold on to the rope!"

The frog in the well grabbed the rope with both hands. "Ready?" cried the ants.

"One! Two! Three! Pull!" cried the beetles.

They pulled and pulled. The rope went up, up, up — and out popped the little frog, wet and happy!

"Thank you! Thank you!" he cried, hugging his friend. "I jumped without thinking, and you saved me!"

The two little frogs hopped home to their pond, side by side. That evening, the rain came — pitter-patter, pitter-patter — and filled the pond up again, cool and fresh.

The two little frogs floated on their lily pads and counted the raindrops as they plopped into the pond — one, two, three, four, five — until their eyes grew heavy.

And under the twinkling stars, they both fell fast asleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Look before you leap — and good friends will always lend a rope.
