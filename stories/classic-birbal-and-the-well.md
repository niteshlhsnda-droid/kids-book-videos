# Birbal and the Well

*A timeless Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In the days of Emperor Akbar, there lived a clever minister named Birbal, who could solve any puzzle with a smile.

One day, a farmer named Ramlal came to the court, looking very worried. "Your Majesty," he said, "I bought a well from my neighbour, Gopal. But now Gopal says I may have the well, but not the water in it! Every time I draw water, he shouts, 'That water is mine!'"

The whole court gasped. "That is not fair!" whispered the courtiers.

Akbar turned to Birbal. "Birbal, what do you say to this?"

Birbal smiled and called for Gopal. "Gopal," he said kindly, "you sold Ramlal the well, but you kept the water. Is that right?"

"Yes!" said Gopal proudly. "The well is his. The water is mine."

"Very well," said Birbal. "Then you must take your water out of his well at once. A man cannot keep his things in someone else's well without paying rent!"

Gopal's proud smile vanished. "Take my water out? But... but the water fills the whole well! How can I take it out?"

"Exactly," said Birbal, still smiling. "Either your water leaves the well today, or you must pay Ramlal rent for keeping it there — one gold coin every single day!"

Gopal thought very hard. Taking out all the water was impossible. Paying rent every day would cost him a fortune!

At last, Gopal laughed and scratched his head. "Birbal, you have caught me! The water goes with the well, of course. Ramlal, my friend, draw all the water you wish. I was only being greedy."

Ramlal beamed. "And you are welcome to draw water from it too, Gopal — as my guest!"

Akbar clapped his hands with delight. "Birbal, once again, your wisdom has turned a quarrel into a friendship!"

And from that day on, Ramlal and Gopal shared the well happily, and whenever anyone in the village tried a silly trick, the villagers would grin and say, "Careful — or Birbal will make you pay rent for your own water!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever answer can turn a quarrel into a friendship.*
