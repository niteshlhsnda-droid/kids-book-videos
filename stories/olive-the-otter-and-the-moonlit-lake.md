# Olive the Otter and the Moonlit Lake

*A gentle bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cosy burrow beside a wide, quiet lake, lived a little otter named Olive.

Olive had soft brown fur, bright eyes, and a long, swishy tail. She loved the lake in the daytime. She loved to splash and float and chase the ripples with her paws.

But at night, Olive did not love the lake at all.

"The water looks too dark," she whispered, peeking out of the burrow. "What if it is cold and scary and full of shadows?"

So every evening, when the sun went down and the stars came out, Olive stayed inside, curled up tight, while the lake waited outside in the dark.

One evening, Mama Otter sat down beside her and wrapped a warm paw around her.

"Come with me, little one," said Mama Otter softly. "Let us look at the lake together. You do not have to swim. We will only look."

Olive took Mama Otter's paw, and together they padded down to the water's edge.

And oh! The lake at night was not scary at all. It was beautiful. Can you guess what made it beautiful?

The moon was shining round and bright, and it had laid a soft, silvery path across the water, like a road made of light.

"Look," whispered Mama Otter. "The moon has come to say goodnight to the lake."

Under the water, the little fish were sleeping, tucked between the soft green reeds, their fins moving slowly, slowly, in their sleep.

And just then — twinkle, twinkle — a parade of fireflies came floating over the water, their tiny lanterns glowing gold. They danced in a slow, sleepy circle, like a row of little stars that had come down to play.

"Hello, Olive!" twinkled the fireflies. "Come and see! The night lake is gentle!"

Olive dipped one tiny toe into the water. It was cool and soft, not cold and scary at all.

She dipped another toe. Then she slipped in, slow and careful, holding Mama Otter's paw all the way.

The water held her up, soft as a hug. The moonlight sparkled around her like tiny diamonds. The sleepy fish swam by in a lazy line, and the fireflies floated above her like a crown of warm lights.

"This is lovely," said Olive, with a happy little sigh. "The dark is not scary. The dark is gentle and beautiful."

Mama Otter smiled. "The night is just the day, tucked up in its blanket," she said. "And the lake is the same kind lake you love — only sleepier."

Olive floated on her back and watched the stars twinkle high above. The fireflies danced their slow, sleepy dance. The water went lap, lap, lap against the shore, like a soft lullaby.

Her eyes grew heavy. Her tail went still. She yawned a big, sleepy yawn.

Mama Otter scooped her up and carried her back to their cosy burrow, wrapped her in a soft, warm blanket, and tucked her in tight.

"Goodnight, lake," whispered Olive, with her eyes already closing. "Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, fireflies. See you tomorrow."

And Olive the otter drifted off to sleep, dreaming of moonlight and fireflies and a lake that sparkled softly, all night long.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The dark can be gentle and beautiful when someone kind is beside you.*
