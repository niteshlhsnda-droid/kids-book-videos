# The Brahmin and the Pot

*A timeless Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a small village lived a kind, dreamy brahmin named Madhav.

One day, a neighbour gave him a gift: a big clay pot full of fine, white flour.

"Thank you, thank you!" said Madhav, hugging the pot. "I shall make the softest rotis in the whole village!"

That night, Madhav lay on his cot with the pot beside him, and he began to dream — not asleep, but wide awake, with his eyes open!

"If I sell this flour," he thought, "I can buy a little hen. The hen will lay eggs. The eggs will hatch into chicks. I will sell the chicks and buy a goat!"

He smiled up at the ceiling.

"The goat will give milk. I will sell the milk and buy a cow. The cow will give lots of milk. I will sell the milk and become rich!"

His smile grew bigger and bigger.

"Then I will buy a fine house with a garden! And a horse! And I will be the happiest man in the village!"

Madhav was so excited by his wonderful dream that he kicked his legs in the air — whack!

His foot nearly knocked the pot over. It wobbled... it wobbled... and then it settled back, safe and sound.

Madhav sat up with a laugh. "Oh my! My dream nearly broke my pot!"

He looked at the pot of flour and thought again, more quietly this time.

"Why dream of a hen and a goat and a cow I do not have," he said, "when I have this lovely flour right here, right now?"

So the next morning, Madhav rolled up his sleeves and made soft, round rotis — pile upon pile of them.

He buttered them, stacked them high on a plate, and called all his neighbours.

"Come, come!" he cried. "Fresh rotis for everyone!"

The neighbours came running. The children munched happily. The old grandmother smiled and said, "Madhav, these are the best rotis in the whole village!"

And Madhav smiled too, because his pot of flour had not made him rich — but it had made a whole village happy, and that was even better.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Do not dream away what you have — share it now, and joy grows bigger than any dream.*
