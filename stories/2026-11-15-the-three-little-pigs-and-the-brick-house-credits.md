# Credits — The Three Little Pigs and the Brick House

- **Original title:** "The Story of the Three Little Pigs"

- **Author:** Traditional English folktale; collected and retold by Joseph Jacobs (1854–1916)

- **Original publication:** 1890, in *English Fairy Tales*

- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #7439 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/7439

- **Illustrator:** None — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated watercolour-style art; no source artwork was reused.

- **License:** Public domain (published 1890; listed as public domain by Project Gutenberg)

- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a ~485-word bedtime story. The three pigs are named Pip, Posy and Plum; the first two pigs run to their brother's brick house instead of being eaten, and the wolf gets a sooty splash in a pot of water and runs away. The turnip-field and fair episodes are left out; the apple-tree trick is kept. The story closes with a warm goodnight. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
