The Three Feathers

A timeless Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, there lived a kind old king with three sons. The two elder sons were clever and proud, but the youngest son, Dummling, was gentle and quiet, and everyone called him simple because he smiled more than he spoke.

One day the old king said, "My sons, I must choose who will look after our kingdom. Whoever brings me the finest woven blanket shall be my heir."

The king climbed to the top of the palace tower and blew three feathers into the air. "Follow your feather, and it will lead you to your fortune!"

The first feather flew east, and the eldest son followed it to a grand city. The second feather flew west, and the second son followed it to a rich town. But the third feather fluttered straight down and landed beside a quiet pond at the edge of the forest.

Dummling sat down beside the pond, wondering what to do. Just then, a big green frog hopped out of the water and croaked, "What are you looking for, young man?"

"A fine blanket for my father, the king," said Dummling politely.

The frog smiled a wide froggy smile. "You are the first visitor who ever spoke kindly to me," she said. "Wait here, and I will bring you something special."

The frog dived deep into the pond and came back carrying a beautiful blanket, woven with flowers of every colour, soft as a cloud. Dummling thanked her warmly and carried it home.

When the king saw the blanket, he clapped his hands. "This is the finest blanket I have ever seen! But let us have one more test. Whoever brings me the most beautiful ring shall be my heir."

Once again the feathers flew, and once again Dummling's feather landed by the pond. The kind frog dived down and brought him a golden ring that sparkled like a tiny sun.

The king was delighted. "One last test!" he said. "Whoever brings home the kindest friend shall be my heir."

Dummling went back to the pond and told the frog everything. The frog laughed her happy froggy laugh. "Then take me with you!" she said. "I am a kind friend, and I know all the songs of the pond."

So Dummling carried the frog gently home in his hands. At the palace, the frog sang the sweetest song anyone had ever heard, and all the courtiers clapped and cheered.

"You have brought home kindness itself," said the king, smiling. "Dummling, you shall look after our kingdom."

And Dummling ruled with a kind heart, the frog lived happily in the palace garden pond, and the two elder brothers — who had laughed at first — soon became Dummling's dearest friends and helpers.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness, not cleverness, is the finest treasure a ruler can own.
