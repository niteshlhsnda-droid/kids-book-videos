# Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, in a kingdom far away, there lived a little princess named Snow White. Her skin was white as fresh snow, her cheeks were pink as roses, and she had the kindest heart in the whole kingdom. She loved to sing to the birds.

But in the castle lived a queen who was cross and jealous. Every morning, she asked her mirror, "Who is the fairest of them all?" And every morning, the mirror answered, "Snow White."

The queen's face turned red. "I must send Snow White away!" she said. So one morning, Snow White was sent away into a big, green forest. The trees were tall, the birds were singing, and the forest was full of flowers and butterflies.

Snow White walked and walked until she came to a tiny little cottage. She knocked softly on the door. "Hello? Is anyone home?" Nobody answered, so she peeked inside. There were seven tiny chairs, seven tiny beds, and seven tiny bowls of soup.

"How sweet!" said Snow White. She swept the floor and made the little beds soft and cosy. Then she sat down on a tiny chair and fell fast asleep.

When the sun went down, seven kind dwarfs came home from their work in the hills. They were small and cheerful. "Who is this little girl?" whispered one. "She has made our cottage so clean and cosy! Let her stay with us," said another.

When she woke up, seven kind faces smiled at her. "Please stay with us," they said together. "This is your home now." Snow White was so happy. Every morning, the dwarfs went off to work, and Snow White kept the cottage tidy.

But one day, the cross queen came to the cottage in disguise with a shiny red apple. "Dear child," she said in a sweet voice, "would you like a bite of my apple?" Snow White did not know the old woman was really the cross queen. The apple looked so shiny and sweet that she took one little bite — and oh! The apple was a sleepy apple, and she sank into a deep, deep sleep.

The dwarfs came home and found her sleeping so peacefully. "She is only asleep," said one dwarf. "We will watch over her." So they carried her very gently to her little bed, tucked a soft blanket around her, and sang soft songs to keep her safe.

Then, as the dwarfs lifted her up, the little piece of apple slipped out of her mouth — and Snow White's eyes fluttered open! "Good morning!" she said with a happy smile. "I had the loveliest dream!" The seven dwarfs cheered and hugged her.

When the queen heard that Snow White was safe and well, her cross heart melted. She came to the cottage, said sorry, and promised to be kind from that day on. And Snow White forgave her with a smile.

From that day on, Snow White lived happily with her seven dear friends. And every night, as the stars came out, they all tucked themselves into bed, safe and warm, and drifted off to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A kind heart is the fairest thing of all.*
