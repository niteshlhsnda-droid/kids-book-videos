The Hermit and the Mouse

A wise Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a little hut at the edge of a quiet forest lived a kind hermit named Rishi Dev. He spent his days watering his tulsi plant, feeding the birds, and meditating under the banyan tree.

One morning, as he scattered grain for the sparrows, a hawk swooped down from the sky — whoosh! A tiny grey mouse ran squeaking straight into Rishi Dev's lap, trembling like a leaf.

"Do not be afraid, little one," said the hermit softly, covering the mouse with his shawl until the hawk flew away.

But the mouse was so small, and the forest was so full of dangers. Rishi Dev looked at her with kind eyes. "Would you like to be safe, little mouse? Then I shall make you into a little girl, so no hawk or cat can harm you."

He closed his eyes, whispered a gentle blessing — and where the tiny mouse had been, there now stood a little girl with bright eyes and a smile like morning sunshine. Rishi Dev named her Musi, and raised her as his own daughter.

Musi grew up happy. She watered the tulsi plant, sang to the sparrows, and laughed like a temple bell. Rishi Dev loved her more than all the mangoes in the orchard.

When Musi was grown, Rishi Dev said, "My dear, it is time for you to choose a husband — the strongest and best in all the world. Whom shall I ask for you?"

Musi thought for a moment. "The Sun!" she said. "He is the brightest of all."

So Rishi Dev called to the Sun. But the Sun said, "The Cloud is stronger than I — he can cover my face."

So they asked the Cloud. But the Cloud said, "The Wind is stronger than I — he pushes me across the sky."

So they asked the Wind. But the Wind said, "The Mountain is stronger than I — he does not move when I blow."

So they asked the Mountain. But the Mountain said, in his deep, slow voice, "The little mouse is stronger than I — he digs his tunnels right through my feet, and I cannot stop him."

Musi clapped her hands. "Then I choose the mouse! He is small like I once was, and clever, and brave."

Rishi Dev smiled. He whispered his gentle blessing once more — and Musi became a tiny grey mouse again, with bright eyes and a smile like morning sunshine.

She scampered off to the mouse hole, where a handsome young mouse was waiting. And Rishi Dev watched them go, his heart full of quiet joy.

For Musi had learned the loveliest lesson of all: it is good to be exactly who you are.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The happiest heart is the one that is glad to be itself — be proud of who you are.
