# Hugo's Moon Blanket

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Hugo the hedgehog was ready for bed. His burrow was cozy and warm, and his blanket was soft and blue. But Hugo could not fall asleep — he felt worried about something, and he did not know what.

He peeked out of his burrow door. The moon was big and round tonight, but it had no blanket at all. It looked cold up there, all alone.

"Oh dear," whispered Hugo. "The moon must feel so chilly."

Hugo felt sorry for the moon. He wanted to help his friend in the sky, so he tiptoed out into the night grass of Firefly Meadow. The grass felt cool under his little feet. The stars blinked hello to him as he walked.

Soft clouds floated slowly across the sky. They looked fluffy and warm. Hugo had a wonderful idea.

"Clouds," he called softly, "will you help me, please? The moon needs a blanket tonight."

One little cloud drifted down low. Then another cloud joined it. Then one more, soft as cotton candy. They piled up white and fluffy. Hugo pushed them gently toward the moon. The clouds floated higher and higher, settling around the moon like a warm quilt.

"Tucked in tight, all snug and right," sang Hugo.

The moon glowed a little brighter. It already looked warmer and happier, and Hugo felt happy too. But one corner of the moon still peeked out — that spot looked chilly and bare. Hugo patted another cloud into place.

"Tucked in tight, all snug and right," he sang again.

The moon's light turned soft and golden, becoming a nightlight for all of Willowmere Wood. A cricket chirped a sleepy thank-you. Hugo felt proud of his kind work. He scurried back to his burrow and crawled under his blue blanket. It felt extra cozy tonight.

"Tucked in tight, all snug and right," he whispered one last time.

The moon shone gently through his door. Its light was warm and gentle. Hugo's eyes felt heavy now. His little nose stopped wiggling. He felt sleepy and safe. The moon was tucked in tight. And Hugo was tucked in too.

Sleep now, little Hugo.

The end. 🌙
