The Dog, the Rooster and the Fox

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Rocky the dog and Kuku the rooster were the best of friends. One bright morning, they set off together to see the wide, wide world beyond the hills. "What fun we shall have!" crowed Kuku, fluffing his feathers. "Woof!" agreed Rocky, wagging his tail till it thumped the ground.

They walked all day — pad-pad went Rocky's paws on the dusty path, and tap-tap went Kuku's little feet. "Have you ever walked such a long, long way?" At noon they rested by a cool stream. Kuku pecked at shiny pebbles while Rocky lapped the water. Slurp! They shared a crust of bread for lunch — nibble, nibble — and told each other stories of the road.

At nightfall, they came to a tall, shady tree with branches like open arms. Kuku fluttered up, up, up — flup-flup! — and tucked his head under his wing. Rocky curled up among the soft roots below. "Good night, Rocky," whispered Kuku. "Good night, Kuku," yawned Rocky.

At dawn, the first pale light peeked over the hills. The dew sparkled on the grass like tiny diamonds. Kuku stretched his neck, fluffed his red comb, and crowed with all his might — "Cock-a-doodle-doo!" His song rang through the quiet woods.

Down in the bushes, a sly fox pricked up her ears. "Oh, what a beautiful voice!" she called in her sweetest, silkiest voice. "Please come down, dear singer, and sing just for me!"

But Kuku was clever. He bowed politely from his branch and said, "Thank you kindly! But first, please wake my doorkeeper — he is fast asleep among the roots." "A doorkeeper?" said the fox, licking her lips. "How very grand!"

The fox crept to the roots on soft, silent paws and pounced — POUNCE! But out jumped Rocky with big, strong teeth — GRRR! The fox yelped — "Yipe!" — tucked her tail between her legs, and ran off into the bushes as fast as she could.

Kuku fluttered down from his branch, laughing — "Ha-ha-ha!" Rocky wagged his tail — wickety-wackety! "Stay close to me," said Rocky, "and no fox can ever fool you." "And stay close to me," said Kuku, "and I will sing you awake every single morning!"

So the two friends walked on together, side by side — pad-pad, tap-tap. Kuku sang a little walking song — la-la-la! — and Rocky hummed along in his deep, doggy voice. And when the wind whispered through the trees, it seemed to say: friends who stay close stay safe. "Do you have a friend you love to walk with?"

That night, under a blanket of twinkling stars, they curled up close — Kuku on his branch, Rocky at the roots — and dreamed the sleepiest, sweetest dreams of all. The moon watched over them, round and kind, and the whole world grew quiet.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Stay close to loyal friends, and flattery can't fool a clever heart.
