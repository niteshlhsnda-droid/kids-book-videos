Nasruddin and the Thief

A timeless Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a little town lived a funny, clever man named Nasruddin.

Nasruddin was not rich. His house was small, his cupboard was nearly empty, and his only treasure was a soft, warm blanket.

One dark night, while Nasruddin was fast asleep, a thief quietly tiptoed into his house.

The thief opened the cupboard — empty! He looked under the table — nothing! He peeked behind the door — nothing at all!

Just then, Nasruddin woke up. He sat up in bed, rubbed his eyes, and saw the thief standing in the middle of his room.

"Good evening!" said Nasruddin cheerfully, as if the thief were an old friend.

The thief jumped in surprise. "Oh! I... I was just..."

"Looking for something worth taking, I suppose?" said Nasruddin with a smile. "Well, if you find anything, please tell me! I have been living here for years and I have never found anything either!"

The thief could not help laughing. "Your house is truly empty, Nasruddin!"

"Not quite," said Nasruddin. "I have warm bread and milk. Sit down, my friend, and let us share it."

So Nasruddin and the thief sat together and ate warm bread and drank sweet milk.

"Why do you steal?" asked Nasruddin gently.

The thief looked down at his empty hands. "Because I have no work, and my children are hungry."

Nasruddin nodded. "Then come tomorrow morning, and I will take you to the baker. He needs a strong helper, and he pays in bread and coins."

The thief's eyes lit up. "You would do that for me — after I sneaked into your house?"

"Everyone deserves a second chance," said Nasruddin, "and a warm meal."

The next morning, Nasruddin kept his word. The thief became the baker's helper, and he never stole again.

And whenever people in the town asked Nasruddin about that night, he just smiled and said, "A kind word and a warm meal can change a whole life!"

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness and a second chance can turn even a thief into a friend.
