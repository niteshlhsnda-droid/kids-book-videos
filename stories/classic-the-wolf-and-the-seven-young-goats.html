The Wolf and the Seven Young Goats

A timeless Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a cosy little house at the edge of the meadow lived a mother goat and her seven young kids.

They were sweet, bouncy little goats, and they loved to play hide-and-seek all day long.

One morning, Mother Goat had to go to the market to buy fresh greens.

"My little ones," she said, "while I am away, do not open the door for anyone — especially not the big grey wolf! You will know him by his rough voice and his big grey paws."

"We won't, Mother!" promised the seven little goats. "We will stay safe inside!"

Off went Mother Goat, and the little goats played quietly at home.

Soon came a knock at the door — knock, knock, knock!

"Open up, little goats!" said a rough, growly voice. "It is your mother, back from the market!"

"No, no!" cried the little goats. "That is not Mother's voice! Mother's voice is soft and sweet. You are the wolf — go away!"

The wolf went off, grumbling. But soon he came back and knocked again, this time in a soft, sweet voice.

"Open up, little ones! It is your mother!"

"Show us your paws through the window!" said the cleverest little goat.

The wolf put his paw up — and it was big and grey!

"No, no!" cried the little goats. "Mother's paws are white and soft. You are the wolf — go away!"

The wolf tried once more. He rolled in a pile of white flour until his paws looked white!

"Open up!" he called in his sweetest voice. "See, my paws are white!"

But the cleverest little goat peeped through the keyhole and saw the wolf's big grey tail!

"No, no!" she cried. "Your tail gives you away! Go away, wolf!"

The wolf stomped off, hungry and cross, and never came back to that little house.

Just then, Mother Goat came home with her basket of greens.

She hugged her seven clever little goats, one by one, and said, "Well done, my clever ones! You listened, you looked, and you kept each other safe!"

And that night, the seven little goats slept soundly, all snug in their cosy little house.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Listen carefully and look closely — clever thinking keeps you safe.
