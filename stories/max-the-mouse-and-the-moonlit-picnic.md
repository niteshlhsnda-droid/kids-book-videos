# Max the Mouse and the Moonlit Picnic

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a little hole under the roots of an old oak tree lived a small grey mouse named Max.

Max had soft grey fur, round pink ears, and a long tail that curled like a question mark.

Every evening, Max liked to sit at the mouth of his hole and watch the sky turn pink, then purple, then dark blue.

But tonight was special. Tonight, the moon was big and round and bright as a silver coin.

"A perfect night for a picnic," said Max, clapping his tiny paws. "A moonlit picnic!"

So Max packed his picnic with great care: a crumbly piece of cheese, five fat, juicy berries, and warm milk poured into a tiny cup — just the right size for a mouse.

He tied it in a soft red cloth, slung it over his shoulder, and off he tiptoed into the moonlight.

Tip-toe, tip-toe, tip-toe went his tiny feet through the soft grass.

The air smelled sweet, like flowers and dew, and the moon lit the path like a lantern.

"Who is out on such a lovely night?" called a soft voice.

Max looked up. There, floating above the grass, were his friends the fireflies. They twinkled and glowed, like tiny stars that had come down to play.

"It is me, Max!" he squeaked. "I have brought a picnic. Will you share it with me?"

"Oh yes!" twinkled the fireflies. "A moonlit picnic! How lovely!"

So Max spread the red cloth on a soft, flat stone and set out the crumbly cheese, the five fat berries, and the tiny cup of warm milk.

The fireflies danced around him, and in their gentle glow the cheese looked golden and the berries shone like little rubies.

Max took a nibble of cheese. "Mmm!" he said. "Crumbly and creamy."

A firefly sipped the milk. "Mmm! Warm and sweet," she glowed.

They ate, and they laughed, and they told stories.

Max told the story of the time he found a shoe as big as a house. And the fireflies told how they once lit the way home for a lost ladybird.

And all the while, the moon watched over them, round and kind and bright.

Then Max yawned — a very small yawn, but a very big yawn for a mouse.

"Are you sleepy, Max?" twinkled a firefly.

"A little bit," said Max, rubbing his round pink ears. "My eyelids feel heavy, like two tiny curtains closing."

"Then it is time to go home," glowed the fireflies. "We will light your way."

So the fireflies floated ahead of Max, twinkling softly, all the way back to the old oak tree.

Max waved his tiny paw. "Goodnight, fireflies. Thank you for the picnic."

"Goodnight, Max," twinkled the fireflies. "Sweet dreams."

Max curled up in his soft nest of leaves, pulled his little blanket up to his chin, and closed his eyes with a smile.

And as moonlight fell softly through the roots of the old oak tree, Max dreamed of cheese, berries, fireflies, and the loveliest picnic of all.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The best meals are the ones we share with friends — even a tiny picnic tastes wonderful together.*
