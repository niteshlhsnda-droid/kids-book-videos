Tenali Rama and the Unlucky Man

A witty Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in the sunny kingdom of Vijayanagara, there ruled a wise king named Krishnadevaraya. The palace gardens smelled of jasmine, and parrots chattered in the mango trees.

In his court lived the cleverest poet of all — Tenali Rama, who could make the whole court laugh with a single sentence.

Now, in the town there lived a gentle man named Bholu. Bholu was kind to everyone. But the townspeople whispered a silly thing about him. "Don't look at Bholu's face first thing in the morning," they said, "or your whole day will go badly!"

Can you imagine? Poor Bholu had done nothing wrong at all.

One day, even King Krishnadevaraya heard the whisper. He believed it! "Keep that man far away from me in the mornings," the king ordered.

Tenali Rama heard this, and his heart felt heavy. "This is not fair," he thought. "No person can make another person's day go bad. That is just a silly, silly idea."

So Tenali Rama made a plan.

Very early one morning, before the sun was fully up, Tenali Rama led Bholu to the palace garden. And when King Krishnadevaraya stepped out for his morning walk — guess whose face he saw first?

Bholu's!

The king frowned. "Oh dear," he muttered. "Today will be a bad day."

But do you know what happened? The king had a perfectly lovely day. His breakfast was tasty. His meetings went well. He even sang a little song in his bath! Splish, splash, tra-la-la!

And Bholu? Oh, poor Bholu had the clumsiest day. He spilled his milk at breakfast. He tore his dhoti — his long white cloth — on a fence. And he banged his toe on a stone — "Ouch!"

That evening, Tenali Rama bowed before the king and said, "Your Majesty, this morning you saw Bholu's face first — and your day was wonderful. But Bholu saw YOUR face first! And look at HIS day: spilled milk, a torn dhoti, and a banged toe!"

Tenali Rama smiled. "So tell me, Your Majesty — whose face is unlucky now?"

The king thought for a moment. Then he laughed — a big, warm, rolling laugh. "Ha, ha, ha! You are right, Tenali Rama! No face is unlucky. It was a silly idea all along."

And from that day on, the silly order was lifted. In fact, the king invited Bholu to share breakfast with him the very next morning — and they both laughed about the banged toe.

That night, Bholu walked home under the soft, twinkling stars, his heart feeling light and happy. And the whole town learned that no person is unlucky — only ideas can be silly.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: No person is unlucky — superstition is a silly idea.
