# The Deer and the Hunter

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a deep green forest lived a gentle deer named Chandni.

Her coat was the color of warm sand, and her eyes were soft and kind.

All the forest animals loved Chandni. She never pushed. She never hurried. She shared the sweetest leaves with everyone.

One evening, as the sun went down, a hunter named Arjun walked into the forest.

He had come a long way, and now the path had disappeared among the tall trees.

"This way? That way?" he said, turning this way and that. "Oh dear, I am lost!"

The forest grew dark. An owl hooted. A twig snapped.

Arjun sat down on a stone and put his head in his hands. "I am tired, and hungry, and lost," he whispered.

Just then, he heard soft footsteps — tap, tap, tap — on the fallen leaves.

It was Chandni.

She was not afraid. She looked at the tired man with her kind eyes and said, "You look lost, friend. Would you like me to show you the way home?"

Arjun stared. A deer, talking to him! And offering to help!

"Yes, please," he said. "I have walked so far, and I cannot find the path."

"Then follow me," said Chandni. "Walk slowly. The moon will light our way."

So Chandni walked ahead, step by step, through the tall grass and over the little stream.

The moon came out and silvered the path. Fireflies twinkled like tiny lamps.

At last, they reached the edge of the forest. There were the lights of Arjun's village, warm and yellow.

"You saved me," said Arjun. "I came here to hunt deer. But you — you helped me, even though I am a hunter. Why?"

Chandni smiled. "Because a kind heart does not ask who you are first," she said. "It just helps."

Arjun felt his heart grow warm — and a little bit sorry, too.

"Chandni," he said, "from today, I will never hunt deer again. Instead, I will guard this forest, so that you and all your friends are safe."

And he kept his promise.

Every morning, Arjun walked through the forest — not with a bow, but with a smile.

And whenever he met Chandni at the stream, she nodded at him, and he nodded back, like old friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: One kind act can turn even a hunter's heart into a guardian's heart.*
