# Bella the Bunny and the Bedtime Garden

*A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a soft, green meadow, under a big oak tree, lived a little bunny named Bella.

Bella had brown fur, long floppy ears, and a nose that wiggled when she was happy.

One spring morning, Bella found a tiny packet of seeds on her doorstep. On the packet, in curly letters, it said: "Moonflowers. They open only at bedtime."

"Oh!" said Bella. "A garden that wakes up when I go to sleep! I must plant it right away!"

So Bella dug a tiny patch of soft earth beside her burrow. She planted the seeds one by one, patted the soil gently, and watered them with her little thimble.

Every day, Bella took care of her garden. She pulled out the weeds. She sang to the seeds. When the night turned cold, she tucked a tiny leaf blanket over the soil. She gave them water from her thimble, drop by drop.

But nothing grew.

"Maybe they need more water," said Bella. So she watered them again.

Still nothing grew.

"Maybe they need a song," said her brother Benji.

So Bella sang a soft, sweet song to the seeds every evening. And still, nothing grew.

Bella felt a little sad. But she did not give up. She kept watering and singing and waiting, day after day.

Then one evening, as the sun went down and the first star blinked in the sky, Bella heard a tiny sound. Pop! Pop-pop!

She looked at her garden — and gasped!

The moonflowers were opening! Petal by petal, they unfolded like little white stars, glowing softly in the dark.

"They bloom at bedtime!" cried Bella. "Just like the packet said!"

The flowers glowed so gently that the whole burrow lit up, soft and golden, like a night-light made of flowers.

All the sleepy bunnies came out to see. Baby bunnies yawned. Mama Bunny smiled. Even old Grandpa Bunny hopped over slowly to smell the sweet flowers.

"They waited for the night," said Bella. "They just needed me to be patient."

From that night on, Bella's moonflowers bloomed every evening. Their soft glow lit the burrow, and every bunny slept soundly under their gentle light.

And Bella? She fell asleep with a happy heart, dreaming of gardens that glow.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Good things take time — keep caring, keep waiting, and they will bloom.*
