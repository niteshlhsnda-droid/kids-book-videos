# Credits — "Princess Amara and the Little Pea"

- **Original title:** "The Princess and the Pea" (Danish: "Prinsessen paa Ærten", 1835)
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated in watercolor style for this project.
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1597/1597-h/1597-h.htm (Project Gutenberg, *Andersen's Fairy Tales*, eBook #1597, containing "The Princess and the Pea")
- **License:** Public domain (Andersen died 1875; the tale was first published in 1835)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (452 words, bedtime tone). The prince's search for a true princess, the stormy night and the knock at the door, the queen's pea test under twenty mattresses and twenty quilts, Amara's sleepless night, the morning reveal, the garden walk, and the spring wedding are kept from the original; the pea's museum afterlife is kept as a fond closing image, and the ending closes with a cozy goodnight — the lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
