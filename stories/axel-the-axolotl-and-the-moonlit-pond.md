# Axel the Axolotl and the Moonlit Pond

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a clear, cool pond, under a sky full of twinkling stars, lived a little axolotl named Axel. Axel was pink and smiley, with six soft, feathery frills on his head that waved gently in the water like tiny fans.

Axel loved his pond. He loved the green lily pads, the swaying reeds, and his best friends — Fiona the frog, who could jump higher than anyone, and Shelly the snail, who carried her home on her back wherever she went.

But there was one thing Axel was afraid of: the dark.

Every night, when the sun went down and the pond grew dark, Axel would hide under the biggest lily pad and whisper, "Oh, I wish it were morning already."

One night, Fiona hopped onto Axel's lily pad. "Axel, why are you hiding?" she asked.

"Because the dark is so… dark," said Axel, peeking out with one eye.

Fiona laughed her froggy laugh. "But Axel, the dark is when the best part of the pond wakes up! Come with me, and I will show you."

So Axel swam out, very slowly, holding Fiona's hand — well, her froggy foot. And what did he see?

First, he saw the fireflies, dancing over the water like tiny flying lanterns. Blink… blink… blink! They spelled out a twinkling hello.

Then he saw the moon, round and silver, smiling down at the pond. The moonlight made a shimmering silver path across the water, and Axel swam right along it, like a little boat on a moonbeam road.

Then he saw Shelly the snail, gliding along a lily pad, leaving a silvery trail that sparkled in the moonlight. "Good evening, Axel!" called Shelly. "The pond at night is my favourite time. Everything is quiet, and everything glows."

Axel looked around in wonder. The reeds swayed softly. The water lilies had closed their petals for the night, like little pink cups. A sleepy fish drifted by, blowing tiny bubbles that rose up, pop… pop… pop.

"You know what, Fiona?" whispered Axel. "The dark is not scary at all. It is soft, and quiet, and full of lights!"

"That is right," said Fiona, smiling. "The dark is just the pond's bedtime blanket."

From that night on, Axel never hid under the lily pad again. Instead, every evening, he swam out to the middle of the pond, waved his feathery frills, and watched the fireflies dance and the moon smile down.

And when it was time to sleep, Axel curled up in his cozy corner of the pond, tucked under a lily leaf, and dreamed of moonlight paths and twinkling fireflies — until the morning sun woke him with a warm, golden yawn.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Things that seem scary in the dark often turn out to be beautiful, once you look with a friend beside you.*
