# The Loyal Parrot

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green valley beside a giggling stream, there stood a very old mango tree.

Its branches were wide, its leaves were soft, and in a cozy hollow near the top lived a bright green parrot named Pip.

Pip loved his tree. In spring, he sang among the pink mango blossoms. In summer, he shared the sweet, juicy mangoes with every bird in the valley. In autumn, he lined his hollow with the softest fallen leaves.

"You are my home," Pip would chirp each night, snuggling into his leaves. "Good night, old tree."

Then one year, the rains did not come.

The stream grew quiet. The leaves turned brown and crispy. The mangoes never came.

One by one, the birds of the valley flew away to find food in far-off lands.

"Come with us, Pip!" called the sparrows. "There is nothing left here!"

But Pip looked at his tired old tree and shook his bright green head.

"This tree gave me blossoms and mangoes and a home," he said. "I will not leave it now."

So Pip stayed.

Each morning, he flew to the far river — flap, flap, flap — and filled his beak with cool water. Back he flew — flap, flap, flap — and poured it, drop by drop, onto the tree's thirsty roots.

Each evening, he sang to the tree — his sweetest, softest song.

"Grow strong, old tree," he sang. "I am here. I am here."

The tree could not speak, but its last few leaves seemed to lean toward Pip's song, listening.

Then one night, as Pip slept in his hollow, he heard it — pit-pat, pit-pat, pit-pat on the leaves!

Rain! Soft, silver rain, dancing down from the clouds!

Pip woke up laughing and twirled in the rain — splish, splash, splish!

By morning, the stream was giggling again. The roots drank and drank. And in a few weeks, tiny green buds opened all over the old tree's branches.

The birds came flying back. "Pip! The tree is alive!" they cheered.

And from that day on, whenever the valley birds sang their evening song, they sang one extra verse — about Pip, the little parrot who stayed, and the tree that loved him back.

"You are my home," Pip still chirps each night, snuggling into his leaves. "Good night, old tree."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: True friends stay close in hard times — loyalty makes love grow.*
