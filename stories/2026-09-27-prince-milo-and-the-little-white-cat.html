Prince Milo and the Little White Cat

Once upon a time, in a kingdom tucked between green hills, there lived a king with three sons. The two older princes were proud and quick to boast. The youngest, Prince Milo, was quiet and kind — he shared his bread with sparrows and always remembered to say thank you.

One autumn day, the old king called his sons to the throne room. "I am growing tired," he said, "and I need a new crown prince. Bring me the tiniest, most beautiful little puppy in all the land. Whoever brings the littlest one shall wear my crown."

The older brothers raced off to the biggest cities in grand carriages. Milo packed a small bag, kissed his father goodbye, and walked into the world alone. He searched villages and valleys for weeks, but every puppy he found was just the usual size.

One evening, rain began to pour. Milo stumbled through a dark forest until a golden light twinkled between the trees. He followed it to a palace with glowing walls and a tall golden door. Inside, on a soft blue cushion, sat the tiniest white cat he had ever seen.

"Welcome, Prince Milo," she said in a soft, tinkly voice. "I am Snowdrop. Please stay — it is far too wet to travel tonight."

Snowdrop gave him warm soup and a cozy blanket, and invisible hands tidied his room while he slept. "A tiny dog is a rare treasure," Snowdrop said. "But I will help you — because you thanked everyone here, even me."

The days passed like minutes. Milo was so happy playing with Snowdrop that he nearly forgot the king's task. Just in time, she gave him a tiny acorn. "Open this only before your father," she said with a secret smile.

At the palace, his brothers showed off their small, sweet puppies. Then Milo opened the acorn. Inside was a hazelnut. Inside the hazelnut was a cherry stone. Inside the cherry stone was a single tiny seed. And inside the seed, no bigger than a thimble, sat the tiniest, most beautiful puppy anyone had ever seen.

The king clapped his hands with joy. "The crown goes to the kindest prince of all!"

Milo hurried back to Snowdrop's palace to thank her. But when he arrived, the little white cat was gone — and in her place stood a queen in a moon-white gown. Milo's kindness had broken an old spell that had turned her into a cat. From that day on, the queen and Milo were the very best of friends.

So remember: be kind to everyone you meet — even the smallest friend can work the biggest magic.

Sleep now, little listener.
