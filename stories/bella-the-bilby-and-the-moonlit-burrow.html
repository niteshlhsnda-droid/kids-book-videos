Bella the Bilby and the Moonlit Burrow

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In the red desert of Australia, under a sky full of stars, there lived a little bilby named Bella.

Bella had soft grey fur, a long pink nose, and the longest, floppiest ears you ever saw. She lived in a deep, cosy burrow under a spinifex bush, with her friend Bindi the bandicoot next door.

Bilbies love the night. They hop out when the moon comes up, sniffing the cool air for seeds and berries. But Bella did not love the night — not inside her burrow, anyway.

"I love the moonlight outside," said Bella. "But inside my burrow, it is so dark. The dark corners are scary. What if there is something hiding in there?"

So every night, Bella stayed near the entrance of her burrow, where a little moonlight came in. She never went deep inside. She never slept in her soft, warm sleeping corner.

One night, Bindi the bandicoot came hopping by. "Bella!" she called. "Why are you sleeping at the door again? Your sleeping corner is so soft and warm!"

"I am scared of the dark corners," whispered Bella. "They are too dark."

Bindi smiled. "Then let us look at the dark corners together," she said. "Bring your brave ears, and I will bring my brave nose. We will see what is really there."

Bella took a deep breath. She held Bindi's paw. And together, they hopped — hop, hop, hop — deeper into the burrow.

It was dark. Very dark. Bella's heart went thump-thump-thump.

But then Bindi pointed her little nose up. "Look!" she whispered.

A thin beam of moonlight came down through a tiny hole in the burrow roof. It fell on the sandy floor like a silver ribbon.

"Oh!" said Bella. "It is beautiful!"

"And look here," said Bindi, wiggling her long ears. The moonbeam made two soft shadows on the wall — and the shadows looked just like two little bilbies dancing!

Bella laughed. She held up her paws, and her shadow made a funny shape on the wall. Bindi held up her paws too, and her shadow made a little star!

They played shadow puppets for a long, long time. A bilby. A star. A flower. A funny little kangaroo!

"The dark is not empty," said Bella, smiling. "It is full of moonlight and shadows and shapes!"

"Exactly," said Bindi. "When we look closely, the dark is full of friendly things."

At last, Bella hopped to her soft, warm sleeping corner, right in the deepest part of the burrow. She curled up in her blanket of soft leaves. The moonbeam fell gently across her long ears.

"Goodnight, moonbeam," whispered Bella. "Goodnight, shadows. Goodnight, Bindi."

"Goodnight, Bella," whispered Bindi from next door. "Sweet dreams."

And Bella fell asleep, safe and warm, dreaming of silver moonbeams and dancing shadows — right in the deepest, darkest, friendliest corner of her burrow.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When we look closely, the dark is full of friendly shapes.
