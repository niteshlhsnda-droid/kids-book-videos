The Dream Boat

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Marin the mouse had a paper boat that he sailed in his bathtub every night. It was white with a blue sail. But tonight, something felt different — the boat glowed faintly.

Curious, Marin touched the sail. It felt warm, like sunshine. "Are you magic?" he whispered.

The little boat bobbed, as if nodding, and a tiny ladder dropped from its side — just Marin's size.

"Climb aboard, sailor," squeaked a tiny voice. It was a cricket wearing a captain's hat! "Tonight we sail to Dreamland."

Marin felt excited and a little nervous, but the cricket smiled kindly, so Marin climbed the tiny ladder. The boat was bigger inside than outside, with a cozy blanket and a pillow.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket captain.

The boat sailed out of the bathtub and floated into the air! Marin gasped. Below him, his bedroom grew small; above him, the stars grew close.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket again.

They sailed past the moon, and the moon waved hello. They sailed past silver clouds that felt soft as cotton. Marin felt drowsy — the gentle rocking was lovely.

"Row, row, into dreams, past the moon and silver streams," sang the cricket one last time.

They arrived at Dreamland — a shore made of pillows, where trees grew candy stars. Marin stepped off the boat, so sleepy he could barely stand.

The cricket tucked him into the pillow sand. It pulled a cloud over him like a blanket. "Sweet dreams, sailor," it whispered. "I will sail you home by morning."

Marin closed his eyes as the boat rocked gently beside him. He dreamed of sailing silver seas — the bravest little sailor in the world.

Sleep now, little sailor. Your dream boat will carry you home by morning. Dream of moonlit waves and starry skies.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Imagination can turn an ordinary bath into an extraordinary voyage.
