# Sana the Snail and the Starlight Trail

In a quiet garden at the edge of a wood, where the evening sky turned soft and purple, a little snail named Sana woke up in her cozy house under a big green leaf. Sana had a round shell swirled like a cinnamon roll, curious eyes on little stalks, and the slowest, steadiest feet in the garden.

Tonight was the Pond Party, when the fireflies lit the pond like fallen stars, and every garden friend gathered to sing and share sweet berry juice. Sana polished her shell until it shone and set off down the garden path.

Soon, Tiko the frog came hopping past, boing, boing, boing. "Come on, Sana! The party is starting!"

And Ria the rabbit came bouncing past, hop, hop, hop. "Hurry, Sana! The fireflies are already twinkling!"

Sana waved her little stalks. "I'm coming as fast as I can." Her friends hopped and bounced away, and soon Sana was all alone on the path.

As she glided beneath a tall blade of grass, Sana heard a tiny, frightened sound. "Help! Oh, help!"

There, tangled in a soft silver cobweb, was Lumi, a little firefly. Her light flickered, and one wing was caught.

"Don't worry," whispered Sana. "I'll be very, very careful."

Sana could not rush. Slowly and gently, one tiny bit at a time, she loosened every sticky thread. At last, Lumi was free! She spun in the air, glowing bright and golden. "Thank you, Sana! You were so slow and careful, and that's exactly what I needed."

Together they went on down the path, Lumi's warm light dancing beside her.

But then, big grey clouds slid across the moon, and the garden turned dark. Ahead, Tiko and Ria stood at the muddy crossing, unable to see the safe stepping stones.

"Don't be afraid," called Sana softly. "Follow Lumi's light, and follow me. I know the slow way across."

Step by careful step, Sana glided over the stones, Lumi lighting each one, and her friends following close behind, until they were all safely across.

"Hurrah for Sana!" cheered Tiko. "Hurrah for Lumi!" cheered Ria. "Your slow and steady feet showed us the way!"

At the pond, everyone made room for Sana right at the front, where she could see the lights shimmering on the water. Sana sipped her berry juice and smiled. She had not been the fastest, but her slow, careful way had helped her friends, and that felt even better than hurrying.

When the party was over, Sana glided home beneath the stars, Lumi twinkling beside her. She curled up in her cozy house and closed her sleepy eyes.

Sleep now, little Sana.

The end. 🌙
