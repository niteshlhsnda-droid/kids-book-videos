Akbar and Birbal: The Longer Line

A clever Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in the grand city of Agra, there lived a great emperor named Akbar.

Emperor Akbar loved clever questions. He asked them at breakfast. He asked them at dinner. And his cleverest friend was his minister, Birbal, who always had a twinkle in his eye.

"Birbal," said Akbar one morning, rubbing his hands with glee, "today I have a puzzle for ALL my courtiers — a puzzle so tricky that not even YOU will solve it!"

All the courtiers hurried to the marble hall. They straightened their turbans. They cleared their throats. What could the emperor's puzzle be?

Akbar bent down and drew a long, straight line on the floor with a piece of chalk.

"Listen carefully," said Akbar. "Here is my challenge. Make this line SHORTER — but you may not touch it, you may not erase it, and you may not cover any part of it!"

The courtiers scratched their heads. "Not touch it? Not erase it? Not cover it?" they murmured. "But... how?"

A wise old courtier stroked his long white beard. A young courtier bit his lip. Still, no one spoke a word.

One courtier closed one eye and looked at it from far away. Another walked all the way around it, peering from every side. A third one stared so hard that his turban slipped sideways!

But nobody could think of a way. It seemed quite, quite impossible.

"Ha!" said Akbar, folding his arms. "Nobody? Not even my clever Birbal?"

Then Birbal stepped forward with a smile. He picked up the chalk, and — sreeek! — he drew another line, right beside Akbar's line. But his line was even LONGER!

Akbar looked at his own line. Then he looked at Birbal's line. Then he burst out laughing! "Ha-ha-ha!" Clap-clap-clap went his hands.

"Now, Jahanpanah," said Birbal, bowing low to his king, "your line looks shorter — and I never touched it!"

The courtiers leaned in close. And do you know what? Akbar's line really did look shorter next to Birbal's long, long line!

All the courtiers clapped too. "Wah, Birbal, wah!" they cheered. Akbar hugged Birbal and said, "You are the cleverest of them all!"

Birbal's chalk line stayed on the marble floor for many days, and every courtier who passed it smiled.

That evening, the palace lamps glowed soft and golden. Akbar looked at the two lines on the floor and smiled. He had learned something wonderful.

Have you ever wanted to be the very best at something, little one? Here is a little secret: you don't have to make anyone else smaller. Just make your own line a little longer — and you will shine!

And under the soft golden lamps, the clever emperor and his clever friend laughed and laughed, and the palace fell asleep with a smile.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: you don't need to pull others down to shine — just make your own line longer.
