The Mouse and the Saint

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a green forest, beside a slow river, there lived a tiny grey mouse named Milo.

Milo was small, quick, and kind, with bright black eyes.

Every morning, he scampered out of his little hole under a big mango tree and said good morning to every ant and beetle he met.

But Milo had one big wish.

"I wish I were bigger," he squeaked to the morning sun. "Then I could run fast and far, and nothing would ever scare me."

One soft evening, Milo scampered to the hut of Sage Arjun, the kindest saint in the forest.

"Hello, little Milo," said the sage, softly. "What brings you here tonight?"

"Dear Sage Arjun," said Milo, "please make me bigger. I want to be brave and strong."

The sage smiled and waved his hand gently. Puff! Milo grew and grew, until he was a soft orange cat, with a long, swishy tail.

"Oh!" said Milo the cat, in a happy meow. "I am big now! I can jump so high!"

But soon, a big brown dog came bounding down the path, barking a loud, playful bark.

Milo the cat scampered back to the sage. "Dear Sage Arjun," he meowed, "that dog is even bigger than me! Please make me bigger still."

The sage smiled and waved his hand gently. Puff! Milo grew and grew, until he was a big brown dog, with floppy ears and a waggy tail.

"Oh!" said Milo the dog, in a happy woof. "I am big now! I can run so fast!"

But soon, a great golden tiger padded softly through the tall grass.

Milo the dog scampered back to the sage. "Dear Sage Arjun," he woofed, "that tiger is even bigger than me! Please make me the biggest of all."

The sage smiled and waved his hand gently. Puff! Milo grew and grew, until he was a great golden tiger, with soft stripes and big, round paws.

"Oh!" said Milo the tiger, in a great roar. "I am the biggest of all! I am strong! I am brave!"

But then Milo the tiger forgot to say thank you. He forgot his little hole, and his friends, the ants and the beetles.

"I am a tiger now," he said, proudly. "I do not need to be a little mouse anymore."

"Dear Milo," said the sage, softly, "being big is fun. But being kind is better. And being yourself is best of all."

Milo the tiger blinked, and his big ears drooped.

"I forgot to say thank you," he whispered. "And I miss my little hole. I miss my friends."

The sage smiled and waved his hand gently. Puff! Milo grew small again, until he was a tiny grey mouse, with bright black eyes.

"Oh!" said Milo, happily. "I am me again! Thank you, dear Sage Arjun!"

He scampered home, said good night to every ant and beetle, and curled up in his little hole, safe and happy.

The moon rose, soft and silver, and Milo closed his bright black eyes, dreaming of cats and dogs and tigers, all playing gently together.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Being yourself is best of all — and a kind thank you makes any wish sweeter.
