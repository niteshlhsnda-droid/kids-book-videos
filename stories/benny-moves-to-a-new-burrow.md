# Benny Moves to a New Burrow

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Benny was a little badger.

He loved his old burrow.

He knew every tunnel.

He knew every corner.

Then Mama said, "We are moving to a new burrow."

Benny felt scared.

His tummy felt twisty.

His paws felt cold.

"What if I do not like it?" he asked.

"What if I miss our home?"

Mama hugged him tight.

She said, "I see you feel scared."

Benny nodded.

"It is okay to feel scared," Mama said.

"New places feel strange at first."

She told him the badger saying.

"Home is where love lives, not walls."

"Home is where love lives, not walls," she said again.

"Home is where love lives, not walls," she said one more time.

Benny packed his favorite things.

His soft blanket.

His wooden pinecone.

A picture of his old burrow.

They walked to the new burrow.

It smelled different.

It looked different.

Benny felt shy.

He peeked inside.

It was cozy.

There was a warm sleeping nook.

There was a window to the sky.

Mama put his blanket in the nook.

"Papa and I are here," she said.

"Our love moved with us."

Benny placed his pinecone by the bed.

He hung the picture on the wall.

Slowly, it felt a little like home.

That afternoon, Benny explored.

He found a tunnel to the meadow.

He found a neighbor mole.

"Hello!" said the mole.

"I am new too."

They played together.

Benny felt happy.

He felt brave.

At bedtime, Benny snuggled in his nook.

His blanket smelled like home.

Mama sang their same old song.

Benny felt scared earlier.

Now he felt safe.

Home was here.

Because love was here.

The earth hummed softly around them.

Benny closed his eyes.

He was brave.

He was loved.

Goodnight, Benny.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A new place becomes home when love moves with us.*
