Ruby the Robin and the Rainbow Seeds

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a tall oak tree at the edge of a sunny meadow, there lived a little robin named Ruby.

Ruby had a bright red tummy, a sweet singing voice, and a very big dream. "One day," she said, "I want to grow a garden that makes everyone smile."

One spring morning, as Ruby was hopping along the garden path, she found something shiny in the grass — a tiny paper packet, tied with a golden string.

"What is this?" chirped Ruby. She pecked the packet open with her beak, and out spilled seven little seeds. But these were no ordinary seeds! Each one sparkled with a different colour — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and pink!

"Rainbow seeds!" sang Ruby. "Oh, how wonderful!"

Ruby carried the seeds home, one by one, in her beak. Then she dug seven little holes in the soft earth under her oak tree, dropped a seed into each hole, and patted the earth down gently.

"Grow, little seeds, grow," she whispered. "Grow big and bright and beautiful."

Every morning, Ruby watered her seeds with drops of morning dew. Every evening, she sang them her sweetest song.

One day, a tiny green shoot peeped out of the earth. Then another, and another! Soon there were seven little plants, growing taller and taller.

And then, one sunny morning — POP! POP! POP! — the plants burst into flowers! A red flower! An orange flower! A yellow flower! A green flower! A blue flower! A purple flower! And a pink flower!

"Oh!" gasped Ruby. "It is a rainbow garden!"

All the animals in the meadow came to see. Freddy the frog hopped over. Bella the butterfly fluttered down. Even old Mr. Hedgehog came shuffling along, sniffing the sweet-smelling air.

"What a beautiful garden!" said Freddy the frog.

"Can I smell the pink flower?" asked Bella the butterfly.

"Of course you can!" said Ruby. "A garden is for sharing!"

So Ruby shared her garden with everyone. Freddy the frog sat by the blue flower. Bella the butterfly danced around the yellow one. Mr. Hedgehog had a little nap under the green one.

But Ruby had one more idea. When the flowers made new seeds, she gathered them into seven tiny packets, one for each of her friends.

"Here," she said, giving a packet to each one. "Plant these near your home, and you will have a rainbow garden too!"

Soon there were rainbow gardens all over the meadow — by the pond, by the hedge, and under every tree. And wherever the animals went, they saw flowers of red and orange and yellow and green and blue and purple and pink, smiling up at the sun.

And every evening, as the sun went down, Ruby would sit on her branch and sing her sweetest song to her rainbow garden.

Because the very best gardens are the ones you grow — and share.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The sweetest things grow even sweeter when you share them.
