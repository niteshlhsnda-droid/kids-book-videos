# The Dove and the Bee

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a sunny meadow, beside a stream that giggled over smooth grey stones, there lived a gentle dove named Diya.

Every morning, Diya flew down to the stream for a cool drink of water. She loved the sparkle of the water and the soft sound it made.

One hot morning, as Diya was drinking, she heard a tiny voice crying, "Help! Help!"

Diya looked down. There, in the stream, was a little bee named Mimi. She was buzzing and splashing, and the water was carrying her round and round.

"Oh no!" said Diya. "The little bee is in trouble!"

Mimi flapped her wings as hard as she could. "I came to drink, and I slipped in!" she cried. "The water is too strong for me!"

Diya thought quickly. She flew to a nearby tree and plucked a big green leaf with her beak. Then she flew back and dropped the leaf gently onto the water, right beside Mimi.

"Climb on, little bee!" called Diya. "Climb onto the leaf!"

Mimi climbed onto the leaf with a happy buzz. The leaf floated like a tiny green boat, all the way to the edge of the stream. Mimi hopped onto a dry stone and shook the water off her wings.

"Thank you, thank you!" buzzed Mimi. "You saved me, Diya. One day, I will help you too!"

Diya laughed softly. "You are very small, little bee. But I will remember your kind words."

A few days later, Diya was sitting in a tree, singing a sweet song. She did not see the hunter creeping through the grass below. The hunter had a net, and he was reaching up, up, up — ready to throw it over Diya!

But Mimi the bee saw him from her flower. "Oh no!" buzzed Mimi. "That is my friend Diya! I must help her!"

Mimi flew as fast as her little wings could carry her. She flew right up to the hunter — and gave him one tiny sting on the tip of his nose!

"OW! ATCHOO!" cried the hunter. He sneezed a great big sneeze. The net flew out of his hands and landed in a bush. The hunter rubbed his nose, sneezed again, and ran all the way home.

Diya fluttered down from the tree. "Mimi!" she said. "You saved me!"

Mimi buzzed proudly. "You saved me first, Diya. A kindness is like a little seed. You plant it, and one day, it grows into a big, beautiful flower."

And from that day on, Diya and Mimi were the very best of friends. Every morning, Diya brought Mimi a cool drink on a leaf. And every evening, Mimi sang Diya a tiny buzzing lullaby.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A small kindness grows into a big kindness — help given is help returned.*
