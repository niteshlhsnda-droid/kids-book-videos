Nori the Narwhal's Night Light

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Under the thick white ice of the far North, in water deep and blue, lived a little narwhal named Nori. Narwhals are sea unicorns with one twisty tusk — and Nori's tusk glowed, just a little, like a night light. He was not afraid of the dark.

But the other sea babies were a little afraid of the deep dark water at bedtime. Lulu the seal pup curled under the ice. Finn the tiny fish hid behind a rock. And Pippa the penguin chick bobbed alone on her floating piece of ice, under the starry sky.

So every night, Nori swam slowly from friend to friend, shining his gentle tusk-light so each one could snuggle down to sleep. First he floated beside Lulu and shone his gentle light over her silver fur. "Goodnight, Lulu," he whispered. Glow. Lulu tucked her nose under her flipper and sighed. She was asleep.

Then he shone his light by Finn's rock, and Finn's silver scales sparkled like tiny stars. "Goodnight, Finn," Nori whispered. Glow. Finn blew one sleepy bubble and fell asleep.

Then he swam up, up to the surface and lit warm ripples around Pippa's little floating ice. "Goodnight, Pippa," he whispered. Glow. Pippa nestled her beak into her fluffy feathers and yawned the biggest little yawn.

"Glow, glow, little light — goodnight, goodnight."

Just as Nori was about to swim home, he heard the tiniest whimper. Down in the dark drifted Jelly, the littlest jellyfish in the sea — so small Nori could barely see her. "It's too dark," Jelly squeaked. "I can't find my dreams."

Nori floated closer than close and shone his light just for her. The glow wrapped around her like a warm blanket. Like a hug made of light. There it is — Nori's glow, soft and golden through the waves. Jelly smiled the sleepiest smile and fell asleep in the glow, safe and snug.

"Glow, glow, little light — goodnight, goodnight."

At last, Nori tucked into his cozy ice hollow, with the softest sand for a bed. He curled his tail around himself and tucked his chin in. His tusk glowed on… then a little less. And a little less. Lower… lower… until it was only a tiny sleepy glow, shining softly in his dreams.

All through the dark water, everything was still and warm. Lulu dreaming of fish. Finn dreaming of bubbles. Pippa dreaming of snow. And tiny Jelly, floating in her hug of light. Goodnight, goodnight.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Even the smallest light can chase away someone else's dark.
