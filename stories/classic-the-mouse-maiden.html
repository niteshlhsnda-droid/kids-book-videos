The Mouse Maiden

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, there lived a kind old sage named Dev. He had a little hut beside a sparkling river, and every morning he sat on the riverbank and sang a soft, sleepy song to the water.

One morning, as the sun peeked over the hills, Dev saw something small floating down the river. It was a tiny grey mouse, holding on to a leaf with all her might!

"Oh, little one!" cried Dev. He reached out his hand and scooped the mouse gently out of the water. "You are safe now."

The little mouse was cold and shivering. Dev wrapped her in a soft cloth and gave her a warm drop of milk. As he dried her tiny ears, he smiled.

"You are so brave," he said. "I wish I had a little daughter just like you."

And would you believe it? In that very moment, with a soft poof of golden light, the little mouse turned into a baby girl! She had round cheeks, big dark eyes, and a tiny tuft of hair that stuck up like a feather.

Dev laughed with joy. "Welcome, my little Sara!" he said, and he carried her into his hut.

Sara grew up in the little hut beside the river. She learned to sing like the birds and to share her food with every hungry animal that came by. She was kind and clever and brave, just like the little mouse she had once been.

When Sara was all grown up, Dev said to her, "My dear, it is time to find you a kind husband. I will ask the Sun, for the Sun is the strongest of all."

So Dev called to the Sun. "Dear Sun, will you marry my daughter Sara?"

The Sun smiled warmly. "I am strong," he said, "but the Cloud is stronger than me. The Cloud can cover my face and hide my light."

So Dev asked the Cloud. "Dear Cloud, will you marry my Sara?"

The Cloud puffed up proudly. "I am strong," he said, "but the Wind is stronger than me. The Wind can push me across the whole sky!"

So Dev asked the Wind. "Dear Wind, will you marry my Sara?"

The Wind whistled and whooshed. "I am strong," he said, "but the Mountain is stronger than me. I cannot move the Mountain, not even a little bit!"

So Dev asked the Mountain. "Dear Mountain, will you marry my Sara?"

The Mountain rumbled in a deep, slow voice. "I am strong," he said, "but the little Mouse is stronger than me. The Mouse digs little holes in me, all day long, and I cannot stop him!"

Sara clapped her hands. "Then I know who I should marry!" she said. "The little Mouse! He is brave and clever and strong — just like me."

With a soft poof of golden light, Sara turned back into a little grey mouse. She scampered to the meadow, where a kind little mouse with bright eyes was waiting for her.

"Hello," he squeaked. "Would you like to share my burrow? It is warm, and I have saved the very best seeds for you."

"Yes, please!" squeaked Sara, and they shared the seeds together, nibble by nibble.

That night, the moon shone on the little hut, and Dev sang his soft, sleepy song — happy that his daughter had found a home where she could be herself.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Be proud of who you are — the very thing that makes you different may be your greatest strength.
