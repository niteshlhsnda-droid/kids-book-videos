# Issun-boshi, the One-Inch Boy

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in Japan, there lived an old farmer and his wife who wished and wished for a little child.

At last, their wish came true — but their baby boy was so very, very small! He was only one inch tall — no bigger than your thumb!

They named him Issun-boshi, which means "One-Inch Boy."

Issun-boshi grew up to be brave and kind and clever. He could climb a blade of grass like a tall tree. He could ride on a dragonfly. He could drink from a single drop of dew.

But Issun-boshi dreamed of great adventures. "Mother, Father," he said one morning, "I am going to the city to seek my fortune!"

His mother gave him a little bowl to sail in, a chopstick for an oar, and a sewing needle for a sword.

"Be brave, little one," said his father. "And be kind."

So Issun-boshi sailed down the river in his bowl — row, row, row! — until he came to the great city.

In the city lived a kind princess named Hana. When she saw the tiny boy with his needle sword, she clapped her hands with delight.

"What a brave little samurai you are!" she said. "Will you be my friend and helper?"

Issun-boshi bowed low. "I would be honoured, Princess!"

Every day, Issun-boshi helped the princess. He kept her rooms tidy. He told her funny stories. He made her laugh with his brave little sword dances.

One day, the princess and Issun-boshi went for a walk in the garden — when suddenly, a great big ogre came crashing through the trees!

"ROAR!" went the ogre. "I have come to steal the princess!"

The princess was frightened. But little Issun-boshi was not afraid at all. He climbed up the ogre's big shoe, up his leg, up his coat — and with his needle sword, he gave the ogre's nose the tiniest little tickle!

"Ha… ha… ha… ACHOO!" sneezed the ogre — the biggest sneeze you ever heard!

The sneeze blew the ogre right off his feet! He tumbled over and over, and as he rolled away, something shiny fell out of his pocket — a little golden mallet!

The ogre ran away into the forest, and he never, ever came back.

"Oh, Issun-boshi!" cried the princess. "You saved me! And look — the ogre dropped his magic mallet!"

She picked up the little golden mallet and gave it a gentle shake. "Little mallet, little mallet," she said, "please make my brave friend Issun-boshi grow big and tall!"

POOF! A puff of golden sparkles floated around Issun-boshi — and he grew! He grew and he grew, until he was as tall as any fine young man in the city!

"Look at me!" laughed Issun-boshi. "I am big at last!"

The princess laughed too. "You were always big, Issun-boshi," she said. "Big in bravery, and big in kindness. Now you are big on the outside too!"

And Issun-boshi lived happily in the city with his friend the princess, and he never forgot his mother and father, who sent him off in a little bowl with a needle for a sword.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Being brave and kind makes you big, no matter how small you are.*
