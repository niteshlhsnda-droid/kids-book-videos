# Birbal and the Talkative Barber

*A clever Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Emperor Akbar loved to look his best, so every morning the royal barber came to trim his beard. Snip, snip went the scissors.

But this new barber, whose name was Babloo, had a very waggy tongue. While he snipped, he chattered — about the weather, about the price of onions, about his cousin's goat, about everything under the sun.

"And then I told my wife," chattered Babloo, snip-snip, "and my wife told the neighbor, and the neighbor told—"

"Enough!" said Akbar one morning, holding up his hand. "Babloo, when you are cutting my beard, you must not talk. A barber's mouth should rest while his hands work."

"Yes, Your Majesty," said Babloo, bowing low. "I shall be silent as a stone."

The next morning, Babloo began to trim the Emperor's beard. Snip... snip... For one whole minute, he was silent. Then his cheeks puffed out, his eyes went round, and the words burst out of him like water from a pump.

"Your Majesty, did you hear about the new mangoes in the market, they are so sweet, my cousin ate four and—"

"BABLOO!" roared Akbar.

All the courtiers covered their smiles with their hands. Birbal, the cleverest minister, stroked his beard and said, "Your Majesty, may I try something?"

"Please do," sighed Akbar.

The next morning, Birbal stood beside the barber's chair. As Babloo lifted his scissors, Birbal began to ask him questions — quick, interesting questions, one after another.

"Babloo, what did you have for breakfast? Is your cousin's goat well? What color is your new turban? Do you like rainy days or sunny days?"

Babloo's mouth opened and closed like a fish. He wanted to answer — oh, how he wanted to answer! But the Emperor had ordered him not to talk while cutting. So he shook his head, and nodded, and shrugged, and his scissors went snip-snip-snip, neat and quick and perfect.

In ten minutes, the Emperor's beard was the finest it had ever looked.

Akbar laughed out loud. "Birbal! You are a genius! You gave his tongue a puzzle it could not solve, and his hands did their best work."

Birbal bowed. "Your Majesty, when the mouth is busy, the hands get lazy. When the mouth rests, the hands do their finest work."

And from that day on, whenever Babloo felt words bubbling up while he worked, he would hum a little tune instead — hmm-hmm-hmm — and his scissors went snip-snip-snip, neat as ever.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Do one thing well at a time — a quiet mind makes the hands clever.*
