Tia the Toucan and the Rainbow Fruit

A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a green jungle where the trees touched the sky, there lived a toucan named Tia.

Tia had a beak as bright as a rainbow — red and orange and yellow and green and blue, all in one beak! She loved her beak very much. But most of all, she loved to find new fruits to taste.

One sunny morning, Tia flew high, high up into the tallest tree in the jungle. And there, hiding among the leaves, she saw something wonderful.

A tree full of rainbow fruit!

There were red fruits, round like little rubies. There were orange fruits, soft like the sunset. There were yellow fruits, bright like the sun. There were green fruits, fresh like spring leaves. And there were purple fruits, sweet like grape juice.

"Oh, my!" said Tia. "I have never seen so many colours! I must taste them all!"

Tia picked one red fruit. CRUNCH! It was sweet and juicy. She picked one yellow fruit. CRUNCH! It was soft and sunny. She picked one purple fruit. CRUNCH! It was the sweetest of all!

Just then, her friends came flying and hopping through the trees. Jojo the Jaguar padded over, and Lulu the Lemur swung down from a branch.

"Tia! Tia!" called Jojo. "What are you eating? It smells delicious!"

"Rainbow fruit!" said Tia. "From the tallest tree!"

"May I have one?" asked Lulu, her eyes shining.

Tia looked at her basket. It was full of rainbow fruit — red and orange and yellow and green and purple. For a moment, she thought, "All this fruit is mine. I found it!"

But then she looked at her friends' smiling faces. And she remembered something her mother always said: "A fruit shared tastes twice as sweet."

Tia smiled her brightest toucan smile. "Of course!" she said. "Let us have a rainbow picnic!"

So Tia, Jojo, and Lulu sat under the tall tree. Tia gave Jojo a red fruit. She gave Lulu a yellow fruit. She kept a purple fruit for herself.

"Try the red one!" said Jojo. "It tastes like strawberries!"

"Try the yellow one!" said Lulu. "It tastes like sunshine!"

They shared and shared, tasting every colour, until the sun began to go down and the sky turned pink and gold.

"Thank you, Tia," said Jojo, licking his lips. "This was the best picnic ever!"

"Yes," said Lulu. "The fruit was sweet. But sharing it with friends made it sweeter!"

Tia looked at her bright rainbow beak and laughed. "You know," she said, "my beak has all the colours of the rainbow. And now my heart does too!"

And as the stars came out, one by one, Tia flew home to her nest, dreaming of red fruits and yellow fruits and purple fruits — and of friends who share.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A fruit shared tastes twice as sweet — sharing makes everything better.
