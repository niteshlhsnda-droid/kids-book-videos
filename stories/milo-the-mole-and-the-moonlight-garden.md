# Milo the Mole and the Moonlight Garden

*A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Under a soft green hill lived a little mole named Milo.

Milo loved to dig. He dug tunnels here. He dug tunnels there. Dig, dig, dig — all day long!

But there was one thing Milo had never seen: the stars.

"You see," said Milo to his friend, the field mouse, "I live underground. When I come out, it is always daytime. I have never seen the night sky."

"Then come out tonight," said the field mouse. "Tonight the moon is round and bright. You will love it!"

That evening, Milo climbed out of his tunnel and sat on top of the hill.

But oh! The night was dark, and Milo's little mole eyes could not see very far.

"I cannot see the stars," said Milo sadly. "It is too dark for me."

Just then, a wise old owl hooted from a tree. "Hoo! Hoo! Little mole, if your eyes cannot find the light, plant a garden that brings the light to you!"

"A garden?" said Milo. "What kind of garden glows in the dark?"

"A moonlight garden," said the owl. "Plant moonflowers. They open only at night, and they shine like little moons!"

So the very next day, Milo planted moonflower seeds all over his hill.

He watered them. He sang to them. He waited, and waited, and waited.

And one evening — pop! pop! pop! — the moonflowers opened!

They were white and soft, and they glowed in the dark like a hundred little moons.

Milo gasped. "Oh! Oh! How beautiful!"

The field mouse came. The fireflies came. The owl came. Even the sleepy hedgehog came, yawning.

They all sat in Milo's moonlight garden, glowing softly in the dark.

And Milo looked up. There, above his glowing garden, was the real moon — round, and bright, and smiling down at him.

"I can see it!" cried Milo. "I can see the moon! And my garden is shining right back at it!"

From that night on, Milo's hill was the brightest, coziest spot in the whole meadow.

And every night, Milo sat among his moonflowers, looked up at the moon, and whispered, "Good night, moon. Thank you for shining."

And the moon seemed to whisper back, "Good night, Milo."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: If you cannot find the light, plant a garden that glows — and the light will find you.*
