The Donkey in the Lion's Skin

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, in a green valley, there lived a little grey donkey named Bobby.

Bobby carried heavy baskets for the farmer every day, up the hill and down again.

He was a kind donkey, and he worked hard, but he often wished he looked grand.

"I wish I were big and strong, like the lion," Bobby sighed one day.

"I wish all the animals bowed to me, just as they bow to Leo the lion."

One morning, Bobby was walking past the edge of the forest when he saw something amazing.

There, lying on the soft grass, was a big, golden lion's skin!

Leo the lion had left it there after a bath in the river, to dry in the warm sun.

Bobby looked left. He looked right. Nobody was watching.

"Oh, what a fine coat!" said Bobby. "Just my size, I think!"

He put the lion's skin over his back, and he pulled it up over his ears.

He looked at himself in the river. "ROAR!" he tried to say, in his deepest voice.

"Why, I look just like a lion!" laughed Bobby. "A big, brave, golden lion!"

Bobby walked into the forest, swishing his new tail.

The rabbit saw him and gasped. "A lion! A lion!"

The rabbit bowed low, and hopped away as fast as his little legs could hop.

The deer saw him and gasped. "A lion! A lion!"

Even the big grey elephant saw him and bowed his great trunk.

"A lion! A lion!" trumpeted the elephant.

"This is wonderful!" said Bobby. "I am the king of the forest now!"

Just then, he saw his old friends, the little monkeys, swinging in the trees.

"Hello, friends!" Bobby wanted to shout. "Look at me! Look at me!"

He was so excited that he forgot all about being a lion.

He threw back his head and brayed with all his might, "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

The monkeys stopped swinging. The deer stopped running. The rabbit stopped hopping.

And then — oh, what a sound! The whole forest burst out laughing!

"Ha, ha, ha!" laughed the monkeys, holding their tummies.

"It is not a lion at all!" laughed the deer. "It is Bobby the donkey!"

"It is our own dear Bobby!" laughed the rabbit. "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

Bobby's ears turned pink, and then he began to laugh too.

"Ha, ha, ha!" laughed Bobby. "You are right! I am Bobby the donkey, and I am happy to be me!"

Just then, Leo the lion came padding softly out of the trees.

"There is my skin!" laughed Leo. "Thank you for keeping it warm, Bobby."

Bobby gave the skin back with a smile, and Leo put it on again.

"Being a lion is fine," said Leo kindly, "but being Bobby is best of all."

And from that day on, Bobby never wished to be anyone else.

He carried his baskets with a happy heart, and he brayed his happy song all the way home.

"Hee-haw! Hee-haw! I am Bobby, and I love being me!"

And all the animals smiled, for they loved Bobby just the way he was.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: You are happiest when you are simply yourself.
