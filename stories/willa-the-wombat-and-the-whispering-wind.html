Willa the Wombat and the Whispering Wind

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Willa the Wombat lived in a snug burrow under the whispering grasses.

Every night, Willa curled into a round, warm ball, closed her eyes tight... and waited for sleep.

But sleep did not come. Willa tossed. Willa turned. Willa wiggled her round tummy.

"Hmmph," she grumbled. "Everyone else is asleep. Why can't I sleep?"

Just then, she heard it — a soft sound outside her burrow door.

"Shhh... shhh... shhh..."

It was the night wind, whispering through the tall grasses.

"Hello, Wind," whispered Willa. "What are you doing?"

"I am singing the grasses to sleep," whispered the wind. "Listen."

Willa listened. The wind moved through the grass — sway... sway... sway — humming the softest song in the world.

"Shhh... shhh... close your eyes, little leaves... shhh... shhh... dream your green dreams..."

Willa felt her eyelids grow heavy. Her wiggles slowed. Her round tummy rose and fell... rose and fell.

"Wind," she yawned, "will you sing me to sleep too?"

"Of course, little wombat," whispered the wind. And it wrapped around her burrow like the softest blanket in the world.

"Shhh... shhh... close your eyes, Willa... shhh... shhh... dream your wombat dreams..."

And Willa slept — deep and warm and safe — while the wind kept whispering its lullaby over the grasses, all night long.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Listen to the quiet — rest is waiting there for you.
