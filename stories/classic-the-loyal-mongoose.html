The Loyal Mongoose

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a small village with mud houses and mango trees, there lived a kind farmer. His wife was called Mira. And they had a baby boy named Babloo.

They also had a pet mongoose named Mangu — a small, quick, furry animal with a long, thick tail. And Mangu loved baby Babloo with all his heart.

One sunny morning, Mira said, "I'm off to the well for water. Mangu — you watch Babloo, okay?"

"Chik-chik!" said Mangu. That meant, "Don't you worry!" And when Mira patted his head, he stood up tall and said, "Chik-CHIK!" — "I'm the best guard in the whole wide village!"

Mangu sat up very, very straight beside the cradle — the stillest, most serious guard you ever saw… until his tummy went grrr-grr, and he looked down at it in surprise.

Mira had just stepped out when… shhh. Listen. Sss-sss-sss. Something quiet is sliding into the house. What do YOU think it could be? … A long black snake — sliding straight toward baby Babloo's cradle!

Mangu spotted it right away. Now — what do you think brave Mangu does next? … WHOOSH! He leapt at the snake! They wrestled and rolled across the floor — dust puffing up everywhere — until at last the snake gave up and slithered away, out of the house!

Baby Babloo giggled in his cradle, safe and snug.

Just then, Mira came back with her water pot on her shoulder. At the door she saw Mangu — fur all ruffled, mouth open, breathing fast.

"Oh no," whispered Mira, her voice all shaky. "Look at him — he must have hurt my Babloo!"

She never even stopped. She never even looked inside. In her hurry and her fright, she pushed Mangu aside — and he tumbled into the dust — small and sore — watching her with big round eyes.

Then Mira hurried inside — and peeked into the cradle. There was Babloo, cooing softly.

And on the floor, in the dust, she saw Mangu's paw-prints — and the long, wavy trail where the big black snake had slid away. Mangu had fought the snake — and chased it right out of the house!

Mira sank to her knees in the doorway, tears spilling down her cheeks, and whispered, "Oh, Mangu! My brave Mangu! You saved my baby — and I didn't even look. I'm so, so sorry."

She held Mangu close and stroked his fur. Slowly, Mangu blinked. He gave a tiny "chik-chik" — which meant, "Still the best guard."

And that night, the little house grew very, very quiet. Mangu curled up warm beside Babloo's cradle, his tiny chest going up… and down… and up… and down. And everyone in the little house drifted off to sleep — safe, and warm, and loved.

From that day on, Mira always stopped — and looked — before she acted.

Goodnight, brave Mangu. Goodnight, little Babloo. The end. 🌙

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Never act in a hurry — stop, look, and learn the truth first.
