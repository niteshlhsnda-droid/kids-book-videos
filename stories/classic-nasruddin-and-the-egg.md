# Nasruddin and the Egg

*A timeless Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One sunny morning, Nasruddin found a small white egg in his garden.

It was smooth and warm, and it sat all alone under the big fig tree.

"Oh my!" said Nasruddin, picking it up gently. "A little egg! Where is your mother, little one?"

He looked all around the garden, but there was no hen, no duck, and no bird to be seen.

"Well then," said Nasruddin, patting the egg softly, "I will look after you myself."

Nasruddin carried the egg into his house and made a soft nest in a basket.

He lined the basket with soft hay and a warm, fluffy cloth.

"There," he said, tucking the egg in like a baby. "Snug and warm, little one."

Then Nasruddin sat down beside the basket and sang a soft, silly song.

"Hush-a-bye, little egg, warm and white, sleep in your nest all through the night."

The egg did not move. It just sat there, quiet and warm.

Nasruddin leaned close and whispered, "Do not worry, little egg. I am here."

All afternoon, Nasruddin kept the egg warm.

He wrapped it in his soft shawl when the wind blew cool.

He held it in his hands and told it funny stories about his garden.

"Once, a goat ate my hat!" he chuckled. "And once, I chased my own shadow!"

The egg still did not move, but Nasruddin did not mind at all.

"Good things take time," he said with a smile.

That evening, Nasruddin set the basket by his bed.

"Good night, little egg," he whispered. "Dream of sunshine and soft grass."

Then he blew out his lamp and drifted off to sleep, dreaming of tiny wings.

In the morning, the sun peeped through the window, warm and golden.

Nasruddin stretched, yawned, and peeked into the basket.

Crack! Crack! Crack!

The little egg was wobbling! A tiny crack was growing across its shell!

"Oh!" cried Nasruddin, his eyes wide. "It is happening!"

Crack! And out popped a tiny yellow chick, soft as a dandelion puff!

"Peep! Peep!" said the chick, blinking its bright little eyes.

Nasruddin laughed and clapped his hands with joy.

"You are here! You are here, little one!" he cried.

The chick shook its tiny wings and peeped again, happy as can be.

Nasruddin gave it a crumb of bread and a sip of cool water.

"Eat up, little chick," he said. "You will grow big and strong."

The chick pecked the crumb and peeped a cheerful thank-you song.

Nasruddin smiled and watched it waddle about the garden, soft and sunny.

"You see," he said to the fig tree, "a little patience and a lot of love can hatch the sweetest surprise."

That night, the chick slept in its soft nest, warm and safe.

Nasruddin sang his soft, silly song once more.

"Hush-a-bye, little chick, warm and bright, sleep in your nest all through the night."

And under the twinkling stars, Nasruddin and his little chick drifted off to sleep, happy and snug.

The end. 🌙
