# How the Leopard Got His Spots

*A playful Just So story by Rudyard Kipling, retold for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in the days when the world was new, there lived a leopard named Leo.

Leo was sandy-yellow all over, from the tip of his nose to the tip of his tail. He was as plain as a sand dune and as smooth as a pebble.

Leo lived on the wide, open grassland with his friend Kito, a man with skin as dark as night.

Every day, Leo and Kito went hunting together. They chased the zebra and the giraffe and the little brown antelope.

But the animals were clever. "Hide in the forest!" said Zuri the Zebra. "Hide in the trees!" said Gigi the Giraffe.

So Zuri the Zebra ran into the speckly, spotty shadows of the forest, and — pop! — her black and white stripes melted into the light and shade. You could hardly see her at all!

And Gigi the Giraffe ran into the tall trees, and — pop! — her patchy, blotchy coat melted into the leaves. You could hardly see her at all!

And the little brown antelope ran into the bushes, and — pop! — his sandy coat melted into the dry grass. You could hardly see him at all!

Leo and Kito came home hungry, night after night.

"We can see everything on the open grassland," grumbled Kito. "But in the forest, the animals vanish!"

"We must change our coats," said Leo, twitching his plain sandy tail. "We must learn to hide, just as they do."

So Kito painted himself with dark stripes and swirls, all over his arms and legs and face, until he looked like the shadows of the forest.

"Now you, Leo," said Kito. "Hold very still."

Kito dipped his fingers in his pot of black paint. And with the very tips of his fingers, he dabbed Leo — dab, dab, dab — all over his sandy coat.

He dabbed five little spots on Leo's forehead. He dabbed five little spots on Leo's ears. He dabbed spots on Leo's back, spots on Leo's tummy, spots on Leo's long, twitchy tail.

"There!" said Kito, stepping back. "Now you look like the speckles of sunlight through the leaves!"

Leo looked at himself in a still, clear pool. Oh my! He was no longer plain sandy-yellow. He was covered in lovely dark spots, like a handful of stars scattered over the sand.

"Thank you, Kito!" laughed Leo, and he jumped and rolled in the soft grass.

Then Leo crept into the forest, quiet as a whisper. The sunlight fell through the leaves in little spots — and Leo's spots melted into the light and shade, just like Zuri's stripes and Gigi's patches.

The zebra walked right past him. The giraffe walked right past him. The little brown antelope walked right past him. None of them saw Leo at all!

Leo caught his dinner at last, and Kito caught his dinner too. And they sat together under the tall trees, full and happy, watching the fireflies blink in the dark.

"From now on," said Leo, licking his spotted paws, "I will always wear my spots. They are the prettiest coat in all the forest."

And that is why, to this very day, every leopard wears a coat of lovely dark spots — dabbed on by a friend, long, long ago.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A good friend helps you shine — and sometimes a little change is just what you need.*
