# Otis the Okapi and the Hidden Rainbow

*A gentle original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in a green forest, where rain pattered softly on the big leaves, there lived a shy okapi named Otis.

Otis had velvety brown fur, stripy legs like a zebra, and ears that wiggled when he was curious. And Otis was very curious about rainbows.

"Where does a rainbow go when the rain stops?" he asked his mother one rainy morning.

"That," said his mother, smiling, "is a secret only a patient watcher can learn."

After the rain, Otis tiptoed through the dripping forest. In every puddle, he saw little flashes of colour — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple — winking up at him.

"Hello, colours!" whispered Otis. "Where do you hide?"

He followed the puddle colours, hop by hop, splash by splash, until he reached a quiet valley he had never seen before. And there, resting on a soft bed of moss, was the rainbow itself, taking a nap!

Its colours glowed softly, like a sleepy smile.

"Shhh," whispered the rainbow, opening one sleepy eye. "I rest here after every rain, until the sun calls me back to the sky."

Otis's ears wiggled with delight. "You are so beautiful," he whispered. "May I visit you again?"

"Of course," said the rainbow, "but you must keep my resting place a secret, so I can sleep in peace."

"I promise," said Otis, placing a hoof on his heart.

From that day on, after every rain, Otis would tiptoe to the valley, sit quietly beside the sleeping rainbow, and hum a soft little tune until it was time for the rainbow to rise into the sky again, bright and beautiful for everyone to see.

And Otis never told a soul where the rainbow slept. Some secrets, he learned, are loveliest when they are kept gently — and shared only with the sky.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Keep a friend's secret gently — and beauty will return again and again.*
