# Rosie the Rabbit and the Rainbow Umbrella

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One rainy morning, in a green meadow full of soft grass and tiny white flowers, lived a rabbit named Rosie.

Rosie had long soft ears and a bright pink nose that wiggled when she was happy.

That morning, the rain went patter, patter on the leaves.

Rosie hopped out of her burrow. "What a lovely rainy morning!" she said.

Just then, something shiny caught her eye. Beside a big round stone stood a beautiful umbrella!

It was a rainbow umbrella, with stripes of red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple, shining brightly even in the rain.

"Oh, how lovely!" said Rosie. "It must belong to the rainbow. I will keep it safe and give it back at sunset."

Rosie opened the umbrella and held it high above her long ears.

As she hopped along the path, she heard a tiny voice. "Brrr, brrr!"

Under a wet bush sat a little bird named Chiru, cold and shivering.

"Poor Chiru!" said Rosie. "Come under my umbrella."

Rosie held the umbrella over him, and the red stripe glowed warm and bright. Soon Chiru stopped shivering, and his feathers fluffed up soft and dry.

"Thank you, Rosie!" chirped Chiru. "The red stripe feels like a warm hug!"

Just then, Rosie heard a grumpy grumble. "Hmph! Rain, rain, rain. I do not like rainy days at all!"

Under a big leaf sat a little hedgehog named Kito, curled up in a prickly ball.

"Hello, Kito!" said Rosie with a smile. "Come under my umbrella."

Rosie held the umbrella over him, and the yellow stripe glowed bright and sunny. Kito gave a happy giggle.

"Why, it feels like sunshine!" said Kito. "I love rainy days now!"

Further along the path, Rosie heard a sad little croak. "Croak, croak. I am all wet, and my lily pad is floating away!"

In a puddle sat a little frog named Neel, shivering and sad.

"Do not worry, Neel!" said Rosie. "Come under my umbrella."

Rosie held the umbrella over him, and the blue stripe glowed soft and calm. Neel smiled a big froggy smile.

"Thank you, Rosie!" croaked Neel. "The blue stripe feels like a warm, cosy pond!"

All morning, Rosie shared the umbrella with her friends. The orange stripe cheered a cold butterfly, the green stripe helped a wet caterpillar, and the purple stripe made a sleepy snail smile.

By sunset, the rain had stopped. The sky turned soft pink and gold, and high above the meadow shone the great rainbow.

"Thank you, little rabbit," said the rainbow. "You shared my umbrella with all your friends, and made a rainy day bright."

Rosie smiled and held the umbrella up high. "It was never mine to keep," she said. "It belongs to you, dear rainbow."

The rainbow lifted the umbrella back into the sky, and painted one soft stripe of colour over Rosie's burrow, as a thank-you.

That night, Rosie snuggled into her burrow, warm and happy, with her friends tucked close beside her. Above her home, the soft stripe of rainbow light glowed gently.

And Rosie smiled in her sleep, dreaming of rainy days and rainbow umbrellas.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing a little colour brightens everyone's day.*
