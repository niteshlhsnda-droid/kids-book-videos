Petra the Puffin and the Polar Lights

A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Petra the puffin lived by the snowy sea. Her home was a little burrow in a soft, white cliff, warm and cosy and smelling of sea salt.

One cold night, Petra peeked out of her burrow. The night sky was dark and quiet. The sea whispered softly below. Shhh, shhh, shhh. The snow sparkled like tiny diamonds.

And then — oh! — the sky began to dance!

Green ribbons of light swayed across the stars. Pink ribbons twirled and curled. They moved slow and soft, like a song you could see. They shimmered and glowed and flowed across the whole wide sky.

"Wow!" whispered Petra. "The sky is dancing!"

She had never seen anything so beautiful. Her heart went thump, thump, thump. She had to share it. She waddled out of her burrow, pitter-pat, pitter-pat, across the soft snow.

"Friends! Friends!" she called. "Come out! Come and see! The sky is dancing!"

One by one, her puffin friends popped out of their burrows. Pip came first, rubbing his sleepy eyes. Then Poppy. Then little Puff, the smallest puffin of all.

"What is it, Petra?" yawned Pip.

"Look up!" said Petra.

They all looked up. And they all gasped.

"Oh!" said Pip.

"Oh!" said Poppy.

"Oh!" said little Puff.

The puffins waddled across the snow and climbed up onto the smooth, flat ice. They sat down together, shoulder to shoulder, in a cosy row. Above them, the green and pink lights swayed. They moved like slow waves in the sky. They shimmered and glowed, soft as silk.

"It looks like a slow song," said Petra.

"It is the best show in the whole world," said Pip.

They watched and watched. The lights danced on and on. The stars twinkled. The sea whispered below. The puffins' eyes grew heavy. Their little heads began to nod, nod, nod.

Pip snuggled close to Petra. Poppy snuggled close to Pip. Little Puff snuggled right in the middle, warm as toast.

One by one, their eyes closed. The cold night was far away. They were warm together, under the dancing sky.

Petra closed her eyes too. The lights swayed softly above her, green and pink, like a blanket of stars.

"Goodnight, dancing sky," she whispered. "Thank you for the best show in the whole world."

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Some nights are made for watching together.
