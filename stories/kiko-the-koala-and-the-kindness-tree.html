Kiko the Koala and the Kindness Tree

A gentle bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Kiko the koala lived high in a gum tree, where the leaves were soft and green and the breeze smelled of rain. Every morning he climbed down, stretched his grey arms, and set off to see what the day would bring.

One morning, on a round green hill, Kiko found a little tree he had never noticed before. It stood all alone. It had no leaves. It had no blossoms. No birds sang in its branches, and nobody ever stopped to visit it.

Kiko tilted his round head. "A tree with no visitors must feel lonely," he said. "I think I will be its friend."

So the next day, Kiko came back with his lunch — a bunch of soft green leaves tied up in a leaf. He sat down beside the little tree, ate his lunch slowly, and left the softest leaves at its roots.

"For you," he said. "Everyone likes lunch with a friend."

The day after that, Kiko came back and sang the tree a song. It was a soft, humming song about rain pattering on leaves and sunshine warming the bark. Kiko's voice wobbled a little on the high notes, but he sang it all the way through, and the little tree's twigs seemed to sway along.

"For you," he said. "Everyone likes a song."

The day after that, Kiko folded a star out of yellow paper — fold, fold, fold — and hung it on the tree's lowest twig, where it turned slowly in the breeze and caught the light.

"For you," he said. "Everyone likes something shiny to look at."

Now, when Kiko came to the hill, he was not alone. Willa the wren had heard the song, and she came to sing too. Toby the tortoise had seen the paper star, and he came to look at it. Soon a whole line of friends was walking up the hill each day — bringing water for the roots, singing songs, and hanging up stars of their own.

The little tree was not lonely any more. Its branches filled with birdsong. Its roots drank the water. Its twigs held a whole sky of paper stars.

Then one golden morning, Kiko climbed the hill and stopped. He rubbed his eyes. He looked again.

The little tree was covered in blossoms — pink and white, soft as clouds, sweet as honey in the air. Bees hummed happily among the flowers. A bird was already building a nest in the highest branch.

"It bloomed!" cried Willa. "It bloomed because we were kind to it!"

From that day on, everyone called it the Kindness Tree. And every evening, as the sun went down and the stars came out one by one, Kiko and his friends sat under its blossoms, quiet and happy, listening to the leaves whisper in the breeze.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: One small kindness can grow into something beautiful.
