# Dana the Deer and the Dewdrop Garden

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet green forest lived a gentle deer named Dana.

Dana had soft brown fur, long, lovely legs, and the kindest eyes in the whole forest.

Every morning, Dana walked the forest paths, saying good morning to the birds, the rabbits, and the tall, whispering trees.

One morning, Dana noticed something she had never noticed before.

Behind a curtain of hanging leaves, there was a little gap — a gap just wide enough for a deer to step through.

"What is behind those leaves?" wondered Dana.

She pushed the leaves aside with her soft nose and stepped through.

Oh! What a lovely place — a hidden garden!

Soft moss covered the ground like a green carpet, tiny white flowers nodded in the morning light, and on every leaf sat a shining dewdrop, round and clear as a little jewel.

Dana tiptoed from leaf to leaf.

Then Dana heard a very small, thirsty voice.

"Please… water… please…"

Dana looked down. In a patch of dry earth stood a tiny little seedling, a baby plant, its two small leaves drooping and its thin stem bending over.

"Oh, you poor little thing," said Dana softly. "You are so thirsty. But I have no bucket, and I have no cup."

Dana looked around the garden — and then she had an idea!

She picked a big, strong leaf, curled it into a little cup, and very carefully tipped the dewdrops into it, one by one.

Plip! Plop! Plip! went the dewdrops into the cup.

Dana carried the cup to the seedling and tipped it gently. The cool water trickled down onto the dry earth.

"There you are, little one," she whispered. "Drink up."

The seedling drank and drank, its little leaves lifting up and its thin stem standing straight and tall.

"Thank you, Dana," whispered the seedling. "You are very kind."

Dana filled her leaf cup again and again, until the earth around the seedling was soft and dark and damp.

Then Dana lay down beside the seedling in the soft moss.

"Sleep now, little one," she said. "Grow strong while you dream."

And Dana closed her kind eyes and drifted off to sleep, with the dewdrops twinkling softly all around her.

When morning came, the sun rose warm and golden.

Dana opened her eyes — and gasped.

Where the little seedling had been, there now stood the prettiest flower in the whole forest!

Its petals were soft and pink, with a golden heart, its stem was strong and green, and it smelled sweet, like honey and morning rain.

"Oh!" said Dana. "You bloomed! You are beautiful!"

The flower swayed gently. "Thank you, Dana," it whispered. "You gave me water when I was thirsty, and now I will share my sweetness with the whole forest."

The bees came softly to visit, and the butterflies danced slowly around the flower in the golden light.

And Dana lay down in the soft moss beside her flower, closed her kind eyes, and dreamed of dewdrops and blooms.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little kindness, given at the right moment, can help something small grow into something beautiful.*
