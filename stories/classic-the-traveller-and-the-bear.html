The Traveller and the Bear

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, two friends named Vikram and Kabir set off on a journey together.

Vikram was tall and strong. Kabir was quick and clever. And they promised each other, "Whatever happens on this road, we will face it together, as true friends do."

They walked through green fields and over little hills, singing songs and telling stories. The sun was warm, the birds were singing, and everything was lovely.

But then they came to a deep, dark wood. The trees were tall and thick, and the path was narrow and quiet. The leaves whispered overhead. A little bird called, "Cheep! Cheep!" somewhere far away. Vikram and Kabir walked a little closer together, and they stopped singing.

Suddenly — SNAP! — a twig broke behind them. And out of the bushes came a great big brown bear!

"GRRR!" growled the bear, rising up on his hind legs. He was as tall as a tree!

Vikram did not wait. He did not think. He did not remember his promise. Up, up, up he scrambled into the nearest tree — quick as a squirrel! — and hid among the leaves.

Poor Kabir was left all alone on the path, face to face with the great big bear.

Kabir's heart thumped — thump, thump, THUMP! But then he remembered something his grandmother had once told him: "A bear will not touch a person who lies very still, like a sleeping log."

So Kabir lay down flat on the ground, closed his eyes tight, and did not move — not even a little bit. He held his breath. He kept as still as a stone. A leaf fell on his nose. An ant tickled his toe. But Kabir did not move — not even a little bit!

The bear came closer. Sniff, sniff, sniff! He sniffed Kabir's ear. He sniffed Kabir's nose. He sniffed Kabir's toes!

Kabir did not move. Not even a wriggle!

At last, the bear grunted — "Hmph!" — and lumbered away into the bushes, looking for his lunch somewhere else.

When the bear was gone, Vikram climbed down from the tree, laughing with relief.

"Well, my friend!" he said. "That was lucky! Tell me — what did the bear whisper in your ear?"

Kabir stood up, dusted off his clothes, and looked Vikram straight in the eye.

"The bear whispered something very important," said Kabir. "He said: never travel with a friend who leaves you alone in danger."

And with that, Kabir picked up his bag and walked on down the road — a little wiser, and a little sadder, but safe.

Vikram stood there, feeling very small and very sorry. He had been quick to climb the tree — but he had been quicker to forget his promise.

From that day on, Vikram never forgot: a true friend stays beside you, even when the bear comes.
