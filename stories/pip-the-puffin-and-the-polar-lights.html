Pip the Puffin and the Polar Lights

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Far, far away, on a snowy island near the top of the world, there lived a little puffin named Pip.

Pip had a bright orange beak, soft black-and-white feathers, and a little sister named Poppy, who was afraid of the dark.

Every night, when the sun went down and the island turned dark and quiet, Poppy would whisper, "Pip, I do not like the dark. It is too big and too black."

Pip would pat her wing and say, "Do not worry, Poppy. I will stay right beside you."

One cold, clear night, something magical happened.

Pip woke up and peeped out of their little rock home — and the whole sky was dancing!

Green ribbons of light swirled and twirled above the sea. Purple waves rippled like silk. Pink stars twinkled and blinked. It was the polar lights, painting the night sky!

"Poppy! Poppy! Wake up!" cried Pip. "You must see this!"

Poppy rubbed her sleepy eyes and peeped out. "Oh!" she gasped. "The sky is dancing!"

Together, the two little puffins waddled to their favourite rock and sat down to watch the lights.

"Look!" said Pip. "The green light is making the shape of a fish!"

"And now it is a star!" laughed Poppy.

"And now — oh, look! — it is a big, wobbly boat, sailing across the sky!"

Then Pip had a game. "Let us count the colours!" he said. "One — green, like the sea in summer! Two — pink, like a rose! Three — purple, like a plum! Four — gold, like the sun!"

"My favourite is green," said Poppy, "because it dances the fastest!"

"My favourite is gold," said Pip, "because it twinkles like a hundred tiny candles."

The lights danced and danced, slow and gentle, like a lullaby made of colour. The sea below sparkled. The snow twinkled. And the dark night did not feel dark at all — it felt full of light and wonder.

Slowly, very slowly, the lights began to fade — soft as a candle being blown out, one colour at a time. Green… pink… purple… gold… goodnight.

Poppy yawned a big, sleepy yawn. "Pip," she whispered, "the dark is not scary anymore. It is beautiful."

"That is because the lights are here," said Pip, tucking his wing around his sister. "And because we are together."

Poppy's eyes closed. Pip's eyes closed too.

And the two little puffins fell fast asleep under the quiet sky, dreaming in green and pink and gold.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Some wonders are best watched together — and the dark is never scary when you are not alone.
