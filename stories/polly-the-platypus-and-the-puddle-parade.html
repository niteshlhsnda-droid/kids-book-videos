Polly the Platypus and the Puddle Parade

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a clear, winding creek in Australia lived a platypus named Polly.

Polly had soft brown fur, a flat duck-like bill, and a wide, flat tail. She loved to swim, she loved to dive, and most of all, she loved rainy days.

Why? Because on rainy days, the creek banks were full of puddles! Big puddles, little puddles, round puddles, wobbly puddles — puddles everywhere!

One rainy morning, Polly had a wonderful idea. "I am going to have a Puddle Parade!" she announced to her friends. "Everyone can march, splash, and stomp through the puddles, and we will sing a puddle song!"

Her friends loved the idea. Kooka the kookaburra laughed, "Koo-koo-koo!" Wombat Wally waddled over. And little Echidna Edna curled and uncurled with excitement.

"But wait," said Polly, looking at the sky. "The rain is stopping! If the sun comes out, the puddles will dry up!"

Sure enough, the clouds were drifting away, and the sun was peeking through.

"Oh no!" cried Polly. "My parade will be ruined!"

But then Polly remembered something her grandmother had taught her: when a problem seems too big, look for a little solution.

"Friends," said Polly, "the puddles near the creek will stay wet the longest, because the creek keeps them full! Let us have our parade along the creek bank!"

So they did! Polly led the way, splashing through the first puddle — SPLASH! Kooka followed, laughing his kookaburra laugh. Wally stomped — STOMP! Edna toddled — PLOD!

And they sang the puddle song Polly made up:

"Splash and stomp and splish and splosh, Through the puddles we go! Rainy days are the best days, Splishy-splashy, ho ho ho!"

They marched along the creek, splashing and singing, until they came to the biggest puddle of all — a puddle as round as the moon!

Each friend had their own special splash. Kooka did a giggly hop-splash. Wally did a slow, wobbly stomp-splash that made everyone laugh. Edna did a tiny tippy-toe splash, pitter-patter, pitter-patter. And Polly did a splendid swirl-splash with her wide, flat tail, sending sparkly drops flying like little stars.

"Your splash is the splashiest, Polly!" giggled Kooka.

"And yours is the giggliest, Kooka!" laughed Polly. "Every splash is special in its own way!"

"One last splash, all together!" cried Polly.

"One! Two! Three! SPLASH!"

The whole parade jumped in together, and water sparkled in the sunshine like a thousand tiny diamonds. Everyone laughed until their tummies hurt.

As the sun dried the last puddles, Polly smiled at her friends. "Thank you for marching with me," she said. "The rain may stop, but a good parade never really ends — it lives in our giggles!"

And that night, as Polly floated on her back in the creek, watching the stars, she whispered, "Best. Parade. Ever."

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When a plan gets rained off, look for a little solution — and share the fun with friends.
