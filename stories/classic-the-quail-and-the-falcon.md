# The Quail and the Falcon

*A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cozy plowed field lived a little quail named Chintu.

It was his home ground. He knew every stone, every tuft of soft grass, and every tiny hole where he could hide.

One sunny morning, Chintu felt very brave.

"I will go and see the big wide world!" he said, and flutter-flutter-flutter — off he flew, far beyond his field.

He landed in a bare, rocky place. No soft grass. No friendly bushes. Just hard, gray stones.

"Oh," said Chintu, looking around. "This doesn't feel like home at all."

WHOOSH!

Down from the sky swooped a broad-winged falcon, his brown feathers gleaming in the sun. He swooped down and caught Chintu softly by Chintu's tail feathers.

Chintu was not hurt at all. But oh, he was scared!

"Oh no!" peeped Chintu, his little heart going thump-thump-thump. "Please, sir Falcon — out here you caught me, but in MY field I'd be too quick for you! I know every hiding spot there!"

The proud falcon chuckled, delighted. "Ho-ho! Show me, little one!" he said. "Show me this clever field of yours!"

With Chintu held gently in his strong claws, the falcon spread his wings and flew him all the way back to the plowed field. Chintu held on tight — what a ride!

The moment their feet touched the soft, brown earth — quick-quick-quick!

Chintu slipped free and duck-duck-ducked into a tiny hole between two big lumps of earth.

"Pip-pip!" he chirped, popping his head out safely. "You can't catch me here! This is my home!"

The falcon looked at the little hiding hole, then at the big field — and he smiled.

"You were right, little friend," he chuckled kindly. "Everyone is strongest in their own home."

"Fly safe, Chintu," said the falcon, and he drifted gently away into the soft evening sky.

Chintu hopped back to his warm little nest as the evening breeze rustled through the grass.

One by one, all the birds of the field sang their soft goodnight songs. Pip-pip, pee-pee, twit-twoo.

And Chintu closed his eyes, safe and cozy in his very own home. You can close your eyes now too, little one.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Everyone is strongest and safest in their own home.*
