# Chloe the Chameleon and the Calm Colours

*A gentle original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a leafy garden full of hibiscus flowers and sun-dappled branches, there lived a little chameleon named Chloe.

Chloe was a wonderful colour-changer. When she was happy, she turned sunny yellow. When she was surprised, she turned bright pink. But when she felt worried or cross, she turned a muddly, grumbly brown — and she did not like that colour at all.

One morning, Chloe could not find her favourite leaf to sit on. "Where is my leaf?" she grumbled, turning muddly brown. "This is a terrible day!"

Just then, a slow old tortoise named Mr. Pebble came ambling by, munching a lettuce leaf.

"Good morning, Chloe," said Mr. Pebble in his slow, warm voice. "You look a little brown today."

"I am cross," said Chloe. "I lost my leaf!"

Mr. Pebble nodded slowly. "When I feel cross, I do my slow-breath trick. Would you like to try it with me?"

Chloe nodded, though she was still brown.

"Breathe in slowly," said Mr. Pebble, "one… two… three…"

Chloe breathed in. One… two… three…

"And breathe out slowly," said Mr. Pebble, "one… two… three…"

Chloe breathed out. One… two… three…

To her amazement, a little patch of soft green appeared on her tail. She breathed again, in and out, slow and steady, and the green spread up her back, across her tummy, and right to the tip of her nose!

"Look!" laughed Chloe, now a calm, leafy green all over. "I am green again!"

"Feelings come and go, like clouds," said Mr. Pebble, smiling. "But slow breaths help the sunny colours come back."

From that day on, whenever Chloe felt muddly brown, she remembered Mr. Pebble's trick. She would breathe in, one… two… three…, breathe out, one… two… three…, and watch her calm colours return, like sunshine after a shower.

And at bedtime, Chloe would curl up on her favourite leaf — which she always found in the end — and breathe herself gently, greenly, to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Slow breaths can turn a grumbly feeling into a calm one.*
