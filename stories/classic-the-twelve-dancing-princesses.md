# The Twelve Dancing Princesses

*A gentle Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, in a kingdom far, far away, there lived a king with twelve daughters.

Twelve princesses! Each one kind. Each one curious. Each one with dancing feet that could not keep still.

Every morning, the king would walk past their bedroom door and find — twelve pairs of dancing shoes, all worn out!

"How strange!" said the king. "My daughters dance all night in their dreams? Or do they sneak out to dance? I must find out!"

So the king made a royal announcement: "Whoever can find out where my twelve daughters dance each night will win a great reward!"

Many princes tried. Many soldiers tried. But every night, they all fell fast asleep — and every morning, the shoes were worn out again.

Then one day, a young gardener named Sam came to the palace. Sam was not a prince. Sam was not a soldier. Sam was a quiet young man who loved music, and flowers, and dancing.

"I will try," said Sam.

That evening, Sam sat in a chair outside the princesses' room. He did not fall asleep. Instead, he listened — and he heard the softest sound: twelve pairs of feet, tiptoeing down a secret stair!

Sam followed quietly, down, down, down the secret stair. At the bottom was a door. Sam peeped through —

Oh! What a wonderful sight!

Beyond the door lay a garden made of silver and gold. Trees with silver leaves. Flowers with golden petals. A lake that sparkled like a thousand stars.

And in the middle of the garden, twelve princesses were dancing — twirl, twirl, twirl — in their beautiful dresses, laughing and spinning under the moonlight.

The eldest princess, Princess Lila, saw Sam. She did not run away. She smiled and held out her hand.

"Would you like to dance?" she asked.

Sam bowed. "I would love to," he said.

So Sam danced with the twelve princesses — twirl, twirl, twirl — until the moon began to fade and the stars began to wink out.

"Morning is coming," said Princess Lila. "We must go back. But you are welcome here any night you like."

The next morning, Sam went to the king and told him everything — about the secret stair, and the silver garden, and the dancing under the moon.

The king was amazed! "You solved the mystery!" he cried. "What reward would you like?"

Sam smiled. "I do not need gold," he said. "I only ask one thing — let the princesses dance openly, in the palace garden, every full-moon night, for everyone to enjoy. And let me play the music for them."

The king laughed with joy. "Done!" he said.

And so it was. From that night on, on every full-moon night, the palace garden filled with music. Sam played his flute. The twelve princesses danced — twirl, twirl, twirl — and all the people of the kingdom danced with them.

And the twelve pairs of dancing shoes? They still wore out. But now, nobody wondered why. Everybody knew: dancing is happiest when it is shared.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Joy grows when it is shared with everyone.*
