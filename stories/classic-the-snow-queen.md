# The Snow Queen

*A timeless tale from Hans Christian Andersen for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a little town with red roofs, two best friends lived next door to each other. Their names were Gerda and Kai.

Every summer, Gerda and Kai played in their shared garden. They played among the roses and read stories under the warm sun.

"We will be best friends forever," said Gerda.

"Forever and ever," said Kai.

But one winter, snow fell soft and thick, and the roses slept under their blanket of snow. One cold, sparkling day, a beautiful sleigh glided down the street.

In the sleigh sat the Snow Queen. Her dress shimmered like fresh snow. She looked gentle, but very, very lonely, living all alone in a palace of ice far away in the north.

"Kai," said the Snow Queen kindly, "will you come and see my palace of ice? It is full of shining towers — but it is so quiet there, all alone."

Kai was curious. He had never seen a palace made of ice. So he climbed into the sleigh, and off they flew, over snowy fields and frozen lakes, all the way to the north.

But the Snow Queen's palace was so cold that, little by little, Kai forgot how warm home felt. He forgot the warm sun and the roses. He even forgot about his best friend, Gerda.

When Kai did not come back, Gerda put on her warm red coat and her woolly hat, and set out to find him.

"I will find Kai," she said. "Best friends do not give up."

First, Gerda came to a little house with flowers blooming, even in winter. A kind old woman lived there. She gave Gerda warm soup, a soft bed, and listened to her whole story.

"Follow the river," said the old woman. "It will show you the way."

So Gerda followed the river until it led her to a dark forest. There, a friendly crow hopped down from a branch.

"Where are you going, little traveller?" he asked.

"I am looking for my best friend, Kai," said Gerda. "He went away with the Snow Queen."

"Then follow the North Star," said the crow. "It shines over her palace. And take this — a warm scarf."

Gerda thanked the crow and walked on under the North Star until she met a kind reindeer.

"I know the way to the palace of ice," said the reindeer. "Climb onto my back, and I will carry you there."

So Gerda climbed on, and the reindeer carried her to a palace that glittered like a thousand diamonds.

Inside, Kai sat in a great hall of ice, quiet and cold. He did not remember the roses.

"Kai!" cried Gerda. She ran to him and wrapped her arms around him in the biggest, warmest hug.

And something wonderful happened. Gerda's warm tears fell on Kai's cold hands, and all the cold melted away. Kai blinked, looked up, and remembered the sun, the roses, and his best friend.

"Gerda!" he cried. "You found me!"

The Snow Queen watched them, and her lonely heart felt a little warmer too. She had never had a friend to share her palace with.

"Tell me about your garden and your home," said the Snow Queen softly.

So they told her about summer and friends who never give up, and the Snow Queen smiled — a real, warm smile.

Then the Snow Queen let them go home, with her blessing. As they travelled back, the snow began to melt.

When they reached home, spring had come, and the roses were blooming again. Gerda and Kai sat in their garden and told each other everything.

And far away in the north, the Snow Queen felt a little less lonely, because now she knew that kindness could warm even the coldest palace.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Love and kindness can warm even the coldest heart.*
