Tami the Tapir and the Starlight Trail

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Tami was a little tapir with a velvety nose and ears like soft leaves.

She lived in a cozy hollow under the roots of a big rain tree. A slow, sleepy river flowed right past her door.

Tami loved the daytime. She loved splashing in the shallow water. She loved munching sweet fallen mangoes.

But when the sun went down and the dark came softly in, Tami would curl up tight and whisper, "Oh, the dark is so big. And I am so small."

One evening, Tami was invited to sleep at her grandmother's house, just across the river. But the sky was already turning purple, and the first stars were peeping out.

"I can't go," Tami said in a tiny, trembly voice. "It is too dark."

Just then, she saw a firefly's little lantern going blink. Blink-blink.

A firefly! Then another. And another.

One by one, the fireflies lined up in the air — blink, blink-blink — like a trail of tiny lanterns leading all the way to the river.

"I wonder where they want me to go?" Tami whispered.

"Follow us," the fireflies blinked. "We will light your way."

Tami took one small step… then another, a little braver. The fireflies bobbed ahead of her, glowing like little golden marbles.

As she walked, Tami began to notice things. The moon was painting silver ripples on the river, and the water lapped softly at the banks — lap, lap, lap. The night flowers were opening their petals with the softest pop-pop-pop. A sleepy frog sang a low, lazy song — croak… croak…

Tami tried to croak along — her nose wiggled instead. Croak… wiggle.

"It is not scary at all," Tami whispered. "The night is full of friends!"

She crossed the river on the stepping stones, the fireflies dancing all around her like tiny nightlights.

On the other side stood her grandmother, waiting with open arms and a warm hug.

"My brave little Tami," Grandmother whispered, nuzzling her velvety nose. "You walked through the whole night!"

"Grandma," Tami whispered, snuggling close, "the dark is not big and scary. It is soft and full of lights."

That night, Tami fell asleep in her grandmother's cozy hollow, watching the fireflies blink goodnight through the leaves.

And Tami wondered which firefly was saying goodnight to her…

Blink… blink-blink… goodnight.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The dark is softer than it seems — it is full of tiny lights.
