# The Bear and the Travellers

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, two friends — Ravi and Kavi — set off on a journey through a tall, whispering forest.

Ravi was brave and strong. Kavi was quick and clever. They had promised each other, "Whatever happens on the road, we will face it together!"

They walked all morning, singing songs and sharing roasted peanuts from Ravi's little cloth bag.

But in the afternoon, the forest grew dark and quiet. The birds stopped singing. A twig snapped — CRACK!

Out from behind the big oak trees lumbered a bear! A big, brown, shaggy bear, sniffing the air with her great round nose.

Kavi gasped. Quick as a squirrel, he scrambled up the nearest tree and hid among the leaves. He did not even call out to his friend!

Poor Ravi had no time to climb. So he did the one thing he could think of — he lay down very still on the soft moss, closed his eyes, and held his breath.

The bear padded closer — pad, pad, pad. She sniffed Ravi's head. Sniff, sniff. She sniffed his ear. Sniff, sniff. She nudged his shoulder gently with her nose, as if to say, "Are you all right, little friend?"

Ravi lay as still as a stone. He did not move a whisker.

The bear sniffed once more, gave a soft, curious huff — and ambled away into the trees. She had decided Ravi was just a strange, sleepy log!

When the bear was gone, Kavi climbed down, his face red. "Ravi! Are you all right? What did the bear whisper in your ear?"

Ravi sat up slowly. He looked at his friend for a long moment.

"She whispered," said Ravi quietly, "'Never travel again with a friend who leaves you at the first sign of trouble.'"

Kavi's eyes filled with tears. "Oh, Ravi — you are right. I climbed that tree so fast, I forgot my promise. I forgot you. Please forgive me."

Ravi thought for a moment. Then he smiled and held out the bag of peanuts. "Friends make mistakes," he said. "What matters is what we do after. But next time — we climb the tree together!"

Kavi laughed through his tears. "Together!" he promised.

And from that day on, whenever they walked through the forest, they walked side by side — and they always, always shared the peanuts.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A true friend stays beside you — promises are for the scary moments, not just the easy ones.*
