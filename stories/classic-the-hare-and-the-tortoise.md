# The Hare and the Tortoise

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a meadow full of daisies, there lived a hare named Champa who could run faster than the wind. She zoomed past the pond, zipped around the oak tree, and always won every race in the meadow.

One sunny morning, Champa met Kachhua the tortoise, who was plodding slowly along the path with his round green shell on his back.

"Oh, slow tortoise!" laughed Champa. "You walk so slowly! Shall we race to the big banyan tree?"

"Yes, let us race," said Kachhua with a calm smile. "Slow and steady is my way."

All the meadow animals gathered to watch. The squirrel waved a red flag. "Ready... steady... GO!"

Zoom! Champa shot off like a streak of lightning. In no time at all, she was far ahead. She looked back and could not even see Kachhua.

"I have plenty of time," chuckled Champa. "I will take a little nap under this shady tree, and still win the race easily."

So Champa curled up under a cool, leafy tree and fell fast asleep, dreaming of daisies.

Meanwhile, Kachhua kept plodding on. Step, step, step. He did not hurry, and he did not stop. He passed the pond. He passed the oak tree. He passed the sleeping hare, very quietly, so as not to wake her.

Step, step, step... and at last, Kachhua reached the big banyan tree!

The animals cheered. "Hooray for Kachhua! Hooray!"

Champa woke up with a start. She rubbed her eyes, stretched, and zoomed to the banyan tree — but Kachhua was already there, smiling his calm smile.

"You won!" said Champa, a little out of breath. "You never stopped, and I did. Well done, my friend!"

"Shall we walk home together?" asked Kachhua.

"Yes!" laughed Champa. "And this time, I will walk slowly, so we can chat all the way."

And so the fast hare and the slow tortoise walked home side by side, the very best of friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Slow and steady wins the race — and a kind winner is the best kind of friend.*
