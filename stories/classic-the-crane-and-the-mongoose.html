The Crane and the Mongoose

A timeless Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Beside a wide, gentle river, there was a tall, green tree. In the tree lived a kind white crane named Clara.

Clara had built a soft, cozy nest of twigs and leaves. In the nest lay three smooth, speckled eggs. Clara loved her eggs very much. Every day she sat on them, keeping them warm, and sang them a soft, sweet song.

But Clara had one worry. Sometimes, late at night, she heard a rustling sound in the tall grass. It was a sneaky snake, sliding quietly toward her tree.

"I must keep my eggs safe," said Clara. "But I cannot watch them all day and all night."

One morning, a little mongoose named Milo came scampering along the riverbank. Milo was small and brown, with bright eyes and a very clever mind.

"Hello, Clara!" called Milo. "Why do you look so worried?"

Clara told him about the sneaky snake and her precious eggs.

Milo thought and thought. Then his eyes lit up. "I have a clever plan!" he said. "And the best part is, no one will get hurt."

That evening, Milo gathered a trail of shiny pebbles. He laid them one by one, all the way from the riverbank to a faraway sunny rock, where a big pile of sweet berries grew.

When the sneaky snake came sliding through the grass that night, he saw the shiny pebbles glinting in the moonlight. One by one, he followed them, all the way to the sunny rock.

And there he found the sweet berries! He was so happy that he forgot all about the crane's nest. He ate the berries and curled up for a long, happy nap in the warm sun.

Meanwhile, Milo stood guard under Clara's tree. He stayed awake all night, listening carefully. And when morning came, he woke all the river animals with a cheerful call.

"Wake up, everyone! The snake is far away, and the eggs are safe!"

The ducks quacked with joy. The little fish splashed happily in the river. Clara smiled down from her nest.

"Thank you, Milo," said Clara. "You are the cleverest and kindest friend in the whole river."

From that day on, Milo and Clara were the best of friends. Every morning, Milo brought Clara fresh berries. Every evening, Clara sang Milo her softest song.

And when the three speckled eggs finally hatched, three fluffy baby cranes peeked out at the world. Milo was the first one they saw, and they chirped with joy.

That night, the river was calm and quiet. Clara tucked her babies under her soft wings, Milo curled up in his cozy burrow, and everyone drifted off to sleep, safe and happy, beside their very best friend.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Clever kindness can keep everyone safe.
