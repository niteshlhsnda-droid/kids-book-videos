# The Kind Doves

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a big shady peepal tree lived Kabutar the Dove and his wife Guddi — a soft grey pair with rosy pink feet. Every evening they fluffed their feathers, closed their eyes, and cooed, "Coo-coo! All are welcome in our little nest!" Can you coo it with them? Coo-coo!

One wild, rainy night, rain was drumming on the peepal leaves, and the branches were creaking in the wind. Knock-knock-knock! At the foot of the tree stood a tired, shivering traveler, his wet clothes stuck to him.

"Who will give me shelter tonight?" he called. "I am cold and hungry!"

Oh dear — what do YOU think two kind little doves will do?

"Come up, friend!" cooed Kabutar. "Coo-coo! All are welcome!" The traveler was ever so big, and the nest was ever so small — he had to scrunch up like a sleepy cat just to fit!

He struck a spark with two little sticks. Kabutar dropped dry twigs onto the spark — crackle-crackle! — until a warm fire was glowing gold. Guddi laid down soft green leaves for a bed, and the whole tree smelled of warm woodsmoke.

"Dear friend," said Kabutar, "we have no food to give you — but what is ours is yours. Rest by our little fire, and take the warmest spot in our nest."

"Then I will sit beside the fire all night," said Guddi softly, "and keep it glowing with my wings, so you stay warm till morning."

"Wait!" said the traveler, smiling kindly. A warm golden glow spread softly around him. He was no longer a traveler at all — he was Agni, the gentle spirit of fire, smiling in the firelight. What a surprise — Guddi had promised to keep the spirit of fire himself warm all night, and never even guessed!

"Your hearts are the warmest in all the world," said Agni. "For your kindness, your tree will always have grain — enough to share with every hungry bird who comes."

From that very night, Kabutar and Guddi never went hungry again. Every morning, golden grain lay waiting in a soft heap under the peepal tree. Kabutar and Guddi shared it with every bird in the forest — and the heap never once ran out.

Goodnight, Kabutar. Goodnight, Guddi. The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: True kindness gives from the heart — and kindness always returns.*
