# Hugo the Hedgehog and the Moonlit Mushroom

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green forest, where the trees whispered in the evening breeze, there lived a little hedgehog named Hugo.

Hugo had a round, prickly back, a tiny pink nose, and the kindest eyes in the whole forest. Every evening, just as the sun went down, Hugo loved to walk along the winding forest path and say goodnight to all his friends.

One evening, the moon rose big and round, and the stars came out one by one, twinkling like tiny silver lights.

Hugo was walking home when he saw something wonderful. Beside the path, under a big old oak tree, there was a mushroom. But this was no ordinary mushroom. It glowed with a warm, golden light, like a tiny lamp left on just for him.

"Oh, what a lovely mushroom," whispered Hugo. "You glow like a little piece of the moon."

Hugo sat down beside the mushroom and watched it glow. Its light was warm and gentle, and it made the path shine like a ribbon of gold.

Just then, Hugo heard a small, sleepy voice. "Hugo? Is that you?"

It was Ruby the rabbit, hopping slowly along the path. Her ears were drooping, and her eyes were heavy with sleep.

"I am so sleepy," yawned Ruby, "but I cannot find my way home in the dark."

"Follow the golden light," said Hugo with a smile. "The mushroom will show us the way."

Ruby hopped along beside Hugo, and the mushroom's golden light lit every step of the path.

A little further on, they met Oliver the owl, sitting on a low branch and blinking his big, round eyes.

"Hello, friends," hooted Oliver. "I was looking for my cosy hollow tree, but the fog tonight is so thick I got all turned around."

"Come with us," said Hugo. "The moonlit mushroom will light the way home."

So Oliver flew softly above them, following the golden glow.

Then they met Bella the badger, who was carrying a tiny basket of berries for her breakfast in the morning.

"I am very tired," said Bella, "and my burrow seems so far away."

"Not far at all," said Hugo. "Just follow the light, and we will all be home soon."

One by one, Hugo's friends joined him on the path. The mushroom glowed a little brighter with every friend who came, as if it were happy to help.

Hugo walked slowly so no one would be left behind. He sang a sleepy song as they went, and his friends hummed along.

At last, they came to a little clearing where all their homes stood close together. Ruby's burrow was under the bramble bush. Oliver's hollow tree stood tall and safe. Bella's burrow was warm and snug beneath the hill.

"Goodnight, Ruby," said Hugo. "Goodnight, Oliver. Goodnight, Bella."

"Goodnight, Hugo," they all called back. "Thank you for lighting our way home."

Hugo smiled and walked back along the golden path to his own little home under the oak tree. The mushroom was still glowing softly, like a night light for the whole forest.

Hugo curled up in his cosy nest of leaves, pulled his blanket up to his chin, and closed his eyes.

"Goodnight, little mushroom," he whispered. "Thank you for your light."

And as the moon smiled down on the quiet forest, Hugo drifted off to sleep, warm and happy, knowing all his friends were safe at home.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A little light shared with friends can guide everyone safely home.*
