# The Travellers and the Plane Tree

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, on a hot, dusty road, two travellers walked side by side.

One was named Kabir, and the other was named Dev. They carried small bundles on their backs, and their feet were tired and sore.

The sun shone down, hot and bright. The road stretched on and on, with no house and no shade in sight.

"Oh, my feet!" groaned Kabir, wiping his brow. "And oh, this hot sun!"

"Look!" said Dev, pointing ahead. "A tree! A big, beautiful tree!"

There, beside the road, stood an old plane tree. It was tall and wide, with great green leaves and branches that spread like open arms.

Kabir and Dev ran to the tree and flopped down in its cool, soft shade. Ah! The shade was like a cool drink on a hot day. The leaves whispered in the breeze. A little bird sang above them.

"What a lovely rest," sighed Dev, stretching out his legs. "Thank you, dear tree."

But Kabir yawned and said, "Hmm. This tree is useless, really. It has no fruit to eat. It has no nuts to crack. What good is a tree with nothing to eat?"

The two travellers laughed a little, and closed their eyes for a nap.

Just then, a soft voice rustled through the leaves.

"Excuse me," said the old plane tree, in a slow, gentle voice.

Kabir and Dev sat up straight. Their eyes went wide. A talking tree!

"You are resting in my shade," said the tree, "and while you rest, you call me useless. But tell me, my friends — is my shade not keeping the hot sun off your heads? Are my leaves not whispering you a cool, soft song?"

Kabir's cheeks turned pink. Dev's cheeks turned pink too.

"Oh, dear tree," said Kabir quickly, "you are right. I spoke without thinking. Your shade is the kindest gift on this hot road."

"And your leaves sing the loveliest song," said Dev, patting the tree's rough bark. "Thank you for letting us rest here."

The old plane tree rustled its leaves happily. "That is better," it said. "A kind word is like cool water. It makes everyone feel fresh again."

So Kabir and Dev rested a little longer, listening to the leaves and the little bird. When their feet felt strong again, they stood up and bowed to the tree.

"Goodbye, kind tree," they said together. "We will remember you, and we will never call a helpful friend useless again."

And as they walked down the dusty road, the tree waved its branches after them, slow and gentle, like a grandmother waving goodbye.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even a friend who gives only shade gives something precious — be thankful for every kindness.*
