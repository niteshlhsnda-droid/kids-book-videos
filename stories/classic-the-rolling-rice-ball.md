# The Rolling Rice Ball

*A kind Japanese folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a village in Japan, there lived a kind old man named Jiro.

Every morning, Jiro went to the mountains to cut wood. And every morning, his wife packed him a lunch of warm rice balls, wrapped in green leaves.

One sunny morning, Jiro sat down on a big stone to eat his lunch. He unwrapped a rice ball — plop! — and it slipped from his fingers and rolled away.

"Oh no!" cried Jiro. The rice ball rolled down the hill, round and round, and — plip! — it fell into a little hole in the ground.

Jiro knelt down and peeked into the hole. "Hello?" he called. "Has anyone seen my rice ball?"

To his great surprise, a tiny voice answered, "Hello, kind sir! Please come down and eat with us!"

Jiro was so surprised that he tumbled gently into the hole — bump! — and landed in a cosy little room under the ground.

There, around a tiny table, sat a family of little mice. And on the table sat Jiro's rice ball, round and white and warm.

"Please, sit with us," said the littlest mouse. "We love rice balls!"

Jiro laughed and sat down. He shared his rice ball with the mice, and the mice shared their tiny cakes with him. Then the mice sang a song, and they danced round and round the table, their little tails twirling.

"Thank you for sharing with us," said the littlest mouse. "Kind people should always be thanked."

The mice gave Jiro a little bag of gold coins. "Use it well, kind sir," they said. "And come and visit us again!"

Jiro climbed back up out of the hole, smiling all the way home. He told his wife everything, and they used the gold to make their little house warm and cosy.

Now, in the next village lived a grumpy old man named Goro. When he heard Jiro's story, he said, "Gold! I want gold too!"

The next morning, Goro sat by the same hole and dropped his rice ball in on purpose.

"Hello?" he called. "Give me gold!"

The little mice peeped out. "Please, come down and eat with us," they said.

Goro tumbled down — bump! — and the mice gave him a tiny cake. But Goro pushed it away. "I don't want cake!" he grumbled. "I want the big bag of gold!"

The littlest mouse looked sad. "Kindness is the price of gold," she said softly. "And you have not paid it."

Goro felt his cheeks turn red. "I… I am sorry," he said. "May I share your cake after all?"

"Of course!" squeaked the mice. And they all sang and danced together, round and round the table.

When Goro climbed home that evening, the littlest mouse pressed a small bag of gold into his hand. "For a kind heart," she said, "even a small bag is enough."

And from that day on, Goro was never grumpy again. Every morning, he packed an extra rice ball — just in case a little mouse was hungry.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Share what you have with a kind heart, and kindness will come back to you.*
