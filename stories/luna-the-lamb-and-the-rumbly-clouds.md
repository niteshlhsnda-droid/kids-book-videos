# Luna the Lamb and the Rumbly Clouds

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Luna was a fluffy white lamb.
She lived in a green meadow.
Luna loved sunny days.
She loved munching clover.
She loved skipping with her friends.
Skip skip skip!
But Luna did not love stormy days.
When the clouds turned gray, her heart beat fast.
When thunder rumbled, she trembled.
RUMBLE!
"Oh no," whispered Luna.
She ran under the big oak tree.
She felt scared.
She felt small.
"I do not like the rumbly clouds," she said.

Her friend Gus the goat trotted over.
He was not scared at all.
He was munching grass, calm as could be.
"Why are you hiding, Luna?" asked Gus.
"The clouds are grumbling," said Luna.
"They sound angry."
Gus shook his head and smiled.
"They are not angry," he said.
"They are only talking to each other."
"Clouds are only talking to each other."
"Clouds are only talking to each other."
Luna repeated it softly.
"Clouds are only talking to each other."
It became her calming words.
"Clouds are only talking to each other."

Gus sat beside Luna.
"Listen with me," he said.
RUMBLE!
"That one said hello," said Gus.
RUMBLE RUMBLE!
"That one said, how are you?"
Luna giggled.
"Really?" she asked.
"Really," said Gus.
"Clouds are only talking to each other."
Luna listened again.
Rumble.
This time it sounded friendly.
Like a giant's soft tummy grumble.
Luna felt calmer.
She felt braver.
The clouds were just chatting.

Can you rumble like a friendly cloud?
Take a breath and say: rumble rumble.
Now softer: rumble.
See? Just clouds talking!
Luna and Gus watched the clouds together.
They found shapes in the gray puffs.
"That one looks like a sheep!" said Luna.
"That one looks like a boat!" said Gus.
The rain began to patter.
Pitter patter pitter patter.
It sounded like a lullaby.
Luna did not hide anymore.
She stood beside her friend.

When the storm passed, the sky turned pink.
A rainbow arched over the meadow.
Luna skipped through the wet grass.
Splash splash splash!
Gus skipped beside her.
That night, Luna curled in the warm barn.
Gus slept in the straw nearby.
"Goodnight, cloud listener," whispered Gus.
"Goodnight," yawned Luna.
She heard a soft rumble far away.
Just clouds saying goodnight.
And Luna slept safe and sound.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Understanding a scary sound can turn fear into comfort.*
