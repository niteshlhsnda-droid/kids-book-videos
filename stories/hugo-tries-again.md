# Hugo Tries Again

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Hugo was a little hedgehog who wanted to climb Sunny Hill — all his friends had climbed it. He tried on Monday, but he slipped on the leaves and rolled back down. "I cannot do it," he said, feeling sad and ready to give up.

On Tuesday, he tried again. He got halfway up, then tumbled down. His nose wrinkled and his spikes drooped with frustration. "Maybe I am just too small," he sighed.

Grandma Hedgehog sat with him. "I see you feel frustrated," she said. Hugo nodded. "It is okay to feel frustrated," she told him. "Trying again is how we grow." Then she shared a secret. "Fall down seven times, get up eight."

"Fall down seven times, get up eight," she said again. "Fall down seven times, get up eight," she said one more time. Hugo liked the sound of that. Get up eight.

On Wednesday, Hugo tried again. He went slowly — step by step by step. He slipped once. He got up. He slipped twice. He got up. And then he was at the top!

Hugo could not believe it. He looked down at the whole meadow. The flowers waved at him, and the sun warmed his spikes. "I did it!" he cheered. "I tried again, and I did it!" His friends cheered from below while Grandma Hedgehog clapped her paws. "You never gave up," she said.

That night, Hugo curled into his cozy leaf pile. His legs were tired, but his heart was happy. He had felt frustrated earlier — now he felt proud. Trying again had worked. The night breeze rocked him gently as he closed his eyes. He was brave. He was loved. Goodnight, Hugo.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Every time we try again, we grow a little stronger.*
