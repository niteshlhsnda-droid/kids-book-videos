# Hugo Tries Again

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Hugo was a little hedgehog.

He wanted to climb Sunny Hill.

All his friends had climbed it.

Hugo tried on Monday.

He slipped on the leaves.

He rolled back down.

Hugo felt sad.

He felt like giving up.

"I cannot do it," he said.

On Tuesday, he tried again.

He got halfway up.

Then he tumbled down.

Hugo felt frustrated.

His nose wrinkled.

His spikes drooped.

"Maybe I am just too small," he sighed.

Grandma Hedgehog sat with him.

She said, "I see you feel frustrated."

Hugo nodded.

"It is okay to feel frustrated," she said.

"Trying again is how we grow."

Then she told him a secret.

"Fall down seven times, get up eight."

"Fall down seven times, get up eight," she said again.

"Fall down seven times, get up eight," she said one more time.

Hugo liked the sound of that.

Get up eight.

On Wednesday, Hugo tried again.

He went slowly.

Step by step by step.

He slipped once.

He got up.

He slipped twice.

He got up.

And then he was at the top!

Hugo could not believe it.

He looked down at the whole meadow.

The flowers waved at him.

The sun warmed his spikes.

"I did it!" he cheered.

"I tried again, and I did it!"

His friends cheered from below.

Grandma Hedgehog clapped her paws.

"You never gave up," she said.

That night, Hugo curled into his cozy leaf pile.

His legs were tired.

His heart was happy.

He felt frustrated earlier.

Now he felt proud.

Trying again had worked.

The night breeze rocked him gently.

Hugo closed his eyes.

He was brave.

He was loved.

Goodnight, Hugo.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When we try again, we grow stronger every time.*
