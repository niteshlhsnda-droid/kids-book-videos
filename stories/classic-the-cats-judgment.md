# The Cat's Judgment

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a meadow by the river — where the grass was soft and green, and the water went *shhh* over the smooth stones — lived Polo the partridge — a small, round bird — and Hopper the Hare. They were neighbors — and, oh, they did love to fight!

"This grass is MINE, mine, MINE!" chirped Polo, fluttering his wings.

"Well, that burrow is MINE!" said Hopper, thumping his big back foot.

They stamped their feet and flapped their wings, and bits of grass flew everywhere.

One day their fight grew so big that they decided to find someone to say who was right. Just then, along came Meena the Cat. She walked slowly, her eyes half closed — looking like the sweetest cat in the whole meadow.

"O wise and gentle cat!" said Polo, bowing low.

"Please say who is right!" added Hopper, bowing too.

Meena's mouth curved into a slow, sleepy smile, and her tail swished soft and lazy behind her. "Of course, my dears. But these old ears of mine are weak. Come closer… closer… and whisper your story into my ears."

Hmm… do you think Meena is really as gentle as she looks?

Polo fluttered closer. Hopper hopped closer… closer… closer…

But Hopper's long ears were quick too. As he leaned in, he heard a soft, hungry rumble — grrrr — from Meena's tummy. Then he saw her whiskers twitch… and her smile turn into a hungry grin.

"POLO! RUN!" shouted Hopper. "This cat is TRICKING us!"

And what a scamper that was! Wings flapping, feet thumping — away they went, faster than fast!

Meena was left sitting in the dust, her slow, sleepy smile all gone. Her tummy rumbled one last time — grrrr. What do you think it was saying? She licked her lips and padded home for a warm bowl of milk instead — lap, lap, lap.

That evening, Polo and Hopper sat together under the moon. "We were silly to fight," said Polo.

"And sillier still — we trusted a stranger's sweet words," said Hopper. "From now on, we fix our fights OURSELVES — as friends!"

And they never asked a cat to say who was right again.

That night, Polo tucked his head under his wing, and Hopper curled up in his warm burrow. And the moon kept watch over them both.

Goodnight, Polo. Goodnight, Hopper. Can you whisper goodnight to them, too?… The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Beware of sweet words from strangers — a sweet-talker is not a friend.*
