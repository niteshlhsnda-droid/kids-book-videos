# Ruby the Robin and the Rainy-Day Rainbow

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Ruby the robin loved rainy days. She loved the soft pitter-pat on the leaves. She loved the smell of wet earth. She loved to hop in the puddles, splish, splash, splosh.

One morning, the rain stopped. The last little drops fell from the leaves, plip, plop, plip. Then the garden was quiet.

Ruby sat on her branch and waited.

"Come back, rain," she sang softly. But the rain did not come back.

The sky stayed grey — not blue, not sunny, just grey. The trees dripped, drip, drip. Ruby's little heart felt grey too.

"Why are you sad, Ruby?" asked a little sparrow, hopping onto the branch beside her.

"I love the rain," said Ruby. "But now the rain is gone, and the sky is still grey. Where did all the pretty go?"

"Just wait," said the sparrow. "Wait and watch. The sky is not finished yet."

So Ruby waited. She tucked her little feet under her. She watched the clouds drift slowly across the sky. They drifted this way. They drifted that way. They looked soft, like big grey pillows.

A tiny breeze came along. It pushed one cloud. Then it pushed another. The clouds began to drift apart, slow and gentle, like curtains opening.

A little patch of blue peeped through. Then a ray of sun peeped through too. The sun touched the last drops of rain on the leaves, and they sparkled like tiny stars.

And then — oh! — a rainbow bloomed across the sky.

It was red and orange and yellow and green and blue and purple. It arched high over the trees, all the way across the world. It was so bright and so beautiful that Ruby forgot to be sad.

"Wow!" sang Ruby. She fluttered up from her branch. She flew gently through the rainbow's colours, up and down, round and round. The colours tickled her wings. The sunshine warmed her feathers.

"Friends! Friends!" she called. "Come and see! Come and see!"

Her friends flew out of the trees. The sparrow came. The bluebird came. The little wren came. They all looked up at the rainbow. Their eyes went wide and bright.

"How beautiful!" they sang together.

They all sat in a row on the branch and watched the rainbow fade, soft and slow, like a song ending gently. The colours melted into the evening sky, pink and gold and sleepy blue.

Ruby tucked her head under her wing. Her heart was not grey any more. It was full of colours, all the colours of the rainbow.

"Goodnight, rainbow," she whispered. "Thank you for waiting to say hello."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: After the rain, look up — something beautiful is waiting.*
