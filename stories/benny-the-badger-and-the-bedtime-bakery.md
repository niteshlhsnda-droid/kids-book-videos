# Benny the Badger and the Bedtime Bakery

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cozy burrow under the old oak tree lived Benny the Badger, the forest's only baker.

Every evening, Benny tied on his flour-dusted apron, warmed up his little stone oven, and baked honey buns for all his friends.

But oh dear — Benny's buns were never perfect. One bun leaned left. Another leaned right. A third had a bumpy bottom and a flat top.

"My buns are all lopsided!" sighed Benny, looking at the wobbly tray. "How can I take THESE to the forest bedtime picnic tonight?"

Just then, his best friend Moss the Mouse scampered in, sniffing the warm, honey-sweet air. Sniff, sniff!

"Benny! Are those honey buns? May I try one? Please, please, please?"

Benny handed her the bumpiest bun of all. Moss took one big bite — crunch! — and her whiskers trembled with joy.

"Benny!" she squeaked. "Crunchy outside, gooey inside, honey all the way through! This is the BEST bun in the whole forest!"

"But... but it leans to the left," said Benny.

"So lean with it!" laughed Moss, tilting her head to match the bun. "Perfect shapes are boring. Yours have character!"

That evening, Benny carried his tray of lopsided buns to the moonlit picnic. The rabbits nibbled. The squirrels munched. The old owl took three.

"Benny," hooted the owl, "these are wonderful! What is your secret?"

Benny smiled his biggest badger smile. "A spoonful of honey," he said, "and a whole heartful of love."

And from that night on, whenever anyone in the forest bit into a lopsided bun, they smiled and said, "Ah — Benny baked this one with love."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Made with love is the most perfect of all.*
