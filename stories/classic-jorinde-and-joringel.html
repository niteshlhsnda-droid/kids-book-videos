Jorinde and Joringel

A timeless Grimm tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once upon a time, at the edge of a deep green forest, there lived two best friends: a girl named Jorinde and a boy named Joringel.

Every morning, with their mothers' permission, they walked along the sunny path near their homes to pick wildflowers. They stayed on the path, just as they had promised, and they always came home before supper.

One golden morning, they wandered a little farther than usual, to where the trees grew tall and the air smelled of pine.

"Look at the red flowers!" cried Jorinde, pointing to a flowery clearing.

But in the middle of the clearing stood a tiny cottage, and in the cottage lived an old woman named Hilda. Hilda was lonely, and loneliness had made her grumpy. She did not like visitors at all.

When Jorinde stepped into the clearing, Hilda waved her hand — and POOF! — in a puff of soft purple smoke, Jorinde turned into a little nightingale!

The nightingale fluttered into a golden cage by the window, and sang a sad, sweet song.

"Jorinde!" cried Joringel. "Come back!"

But Hilda only shook her head, and Joringel had to walk home alone, his heart feeling heavy as a stone.

That night, Joringel had a dream. In his dream, a beautiful red flower with a pearl in its middle whispered to him, "Find me at sunrise, and I will undo the spell."

At sunrise, Joringel ran back to the forest. And there, right beside the path, grew a red flower with a pearl shining in its middle, just like in his dream!

He picked it gently and carried it to Hilda's cottage. He touched the golden cage with the flower — and POOF! — in a puff of soft golden light, the nightingale turned back into Jorinde!

"Joringel!" laughed Jorinde, hugging her friend tight.

Hilda watched them, and her grumpy face slowly softened. "I was lonely," she whispered. "That is why I kept the little bird to sing to me. I am sorry."

Jorinde smiled at her. "Then you must not be lonely any more," she said. "Come to our village picnic tomorrow. There will be songs and sandwiches, and a place saved just for you."

Hilda's eyes filled with happy tears. "I would like that very much," she said.

And so the next day, Hilda came to the picnic, and Jorinde sang her a happy song — not from a cage, but free in the sunshine, with her best friend beside her.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A lonely heart can be softened by a kind invitation.
