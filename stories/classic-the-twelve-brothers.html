The Twelve Brothers

A timeless Grimm's fairy tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in a castle on a green hill, there lived a king and a queen who had twelve sons.

The twelve brothers loved to play together in the castle garden.

One spring morning, a baby girl was born in the castle. She was tiny and sweet, with soft golden hair and a smile like sunshine.

"Our little sister!" cried the brothers, crowding round her cradle.

But that very same day, something strange happened. A soft wind blew through the castle windows, and a flock of dark feathers swirled in the air.

"Oh!" cried the queen. "Where have my boys gone?"

The twelve brothers had turned into twelve black ravens! They flapped their wings and flew away over the hills, calling softly to one another.

"Come back!" called the king, but the ravens flew on and on, until they were only tiny dots in the blue sky.

The little princess grew up kind and brave. Her name was Anika, and she loved stories about her twelve brothers.

"They are waiting for us to bring them home," her mother said gently one day. "They live beyond the hills, in a little house in the woods."

When Anika was old enough, she packed bread, cheese, and a soft blanket, then set off over the hills to find her brothers.

She walked through fields of yellow flowers and under tall trees, until at last she saw a little house in the woods.

Inside were twelve tiny beds, twelve tiny chairs, and twelve tiny cups.

"My brothers live here," whispered Anika. "I will make them a surprise."

She swept the floor, washed the cups, and sewed twelve little shirts from soft white cloth, humming a soft, happy tune as she worked.

Every evening, when the ravens flew home, they heard her gentle humming through the window. It made them feel warm and safe inside.

One morning, just as the sun began to rise, Anika finished the very last shirt. She laid all twelve shirts in a neat row on the table.

"Here you are, my brothers," she said softly. "One for each of you, sewn with love."

Suddenly, the room filled with golden light. The feathers floated away like autumn leaves, and there stood twelve boys!

"Look!" cried the tallest brother. "We are boys again! Our sister has brought us home!"

Anika laughed and hugged them all, one by one, and the brothers hugged her back.

"We missed you so much," said the smallest brother. "Your humming made us feel brave every day."

"And I missed you," said Anika. "Now we can be a family again."

The twelve brothers put on their soft white shirts, and they all walked home together, over the hills and back to the castle.

The king and queen ran out to meet them, crying happy tears. "Our children are home!" they cried.

That night, there was a grand feast in the castle, with cakes, sweet fruit, music, and dancing.

Anika sat in the middle of them all, smiling. She had brought her family home with patience, love, and a soft, happy song.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Love, patience, and a brave heart can bring a family back together.
