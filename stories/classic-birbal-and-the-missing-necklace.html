Birbal and the Missing Necklace

A timeless Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in a grand palace in Delhi, there lived a king named Akbar. In his court was a clever minister named Birbal. Birbal could solve any puzzle with his quick mind and his kind heart.

One day, there was a great fuss in the palace. The queen's favourite necklace had gone missing! It was made of shiny pearls, and the queen loved it more than anything.

"Where has my necklace gone?" said the queen sadly.

King Akbar called for Birbal. "Birbal, find this necklace. The thief must be someone right here in the palace."

"Do not worry, Your Majesty," said Birbal with a smile. "I will find it."

Birbal called all the palace servants into a big hall. He gave each one a wooden stick. Every stick was exactly the same length.

"Listen carefully," said Birbal. "These sticks are magic. Keep yours beside you tonight. In the morning, the thief's stick will have grown one inch longer."

The servants took their sticks and went away.

One of the servants was very frightened. He was the one who had taken the necklace and hidden it in his room! "Oh no!" he thought. "By morning, my stick will be longer, and everyone will know it was me."

That night, when everyone was asleep, he crept out of bed and cut a little piece off the end of his stick. "Now my stick will not look longer," he thought, and he went back to sleep feeling clever.

In the morning, all the servants gathered again with their sticks. Birbal measured the sticks one by one. Every stick was just as long as before — except one. One servant's stick was shorter than all the rest!

Birbal smiled gently. "My friend, your stick has grown shorter, not longer. Did you cut it in the night?"

The servant hung his head. Tears filled his eyes. "I am sorry," he said in a small voice. "I took the necklace. I feel so bad about what I did."

He ran to his room and brought the necklace back to the queen. The queen smiled and put it on, happy to have it back.

King Akbar clapped his hands. "Birbal, there is no puzzle you cannot solve!"

Birbal looked kindly at the servant and said, "Saying sorry takes great courage. Now work honestly again, and all will be well."

From that day on, the servant never told a lie, and everyone in the palace lived happily together. And when the moon came out that night, the whole palace drifted off to sleep in peace.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Truth and honesty always win.
