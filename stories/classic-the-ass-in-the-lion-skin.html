The Ass in the Lion's Skin

A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a sunny green meadow, beside a little red barn, there lived a small grey donkey named Dilly.

Dilly had long ears and a soft grey coat.

Every morning, Dilly carried baskets of hay for the farmer.

But Dilly had one big wish.

"I wish I were big and strong," he said to the morning sun. "Then everyone would look at me and say, 'Wow!'"

One morning, Dilly was trotting past the old shed when he saw something wonderful.

There, hanging on a peg, was a great golden lion's skin, soft and shaggy, with a big fluffy mane.

"Oh!" said Dilly, with wide eyes. "If I wear this, everyone will think I am a lion!"

He slipped the lion's skin over his back and shook the fluffy mane around his face. He looked in the puddle — and oh my! He looked just like a lion!

Dilly trotted into the meadow, walking like a lion.

The little sheep saw him and cried, "A lion!" and scampered away.

The rabbits hopped away, and even the big brown cow trotted off.

"Wow!" said Dilly, happily. "Everyone thinks I am a lion! This is the best day ever!"

He puffed out his chest and shook his fluffy mane. He felt big and grand.

All day, Dilly strutted around the meadow in his lion's skin, and no one came near him.

At first, that felt exciting. But soon, it felt lonely.

"I wish someone would play with me," said Dilly, softly. "But lions do not play with sheep."

Just then, his friend Fox came trotting down the lane. Fox stopped and stared.

"Hello, Mr. Lion," said Fox, politely. "What a fine mane you have!"

Dilly was so pleased that he forgot all about being a lion. He opened his mouth wide and brayed, "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

Fox's ears went up. He tilted his head and smiled a kind, clever smile.

"Ah," said Fox, gently. "That is not a lion's roar. That is Dilly's happy bray! Hello, Dilly!"

Dilly felt his cheeks go pink under the fluffy mane. "Oh no," he whispered. "You found me out!"

Fox laughed a warm, friendly laugh.

"Do not be sad, Dilly," said Fox. "You are not a lion — and that is wonderful! You are Dilly, the kindest donkey in the meadow."

Soon the sheep peeped out from behind the fence. "Is it really Dilly?" they asked.

"Yes, it is me!" said Dilly. He slipped off the lion's skin and shook his long ears. "It is just me — Dilly!"

The rabbits hopped back. The cow mooed happily. Everyone laughed kindly, and Dilly laughed too.

"Being a lion was exciting," said Dilly, "but being Dilly is better. My friends like ME — just as I am!"

That evening, Dilly munched sweet grass with his friends all around him, and Fox told a funny story.

And Dilly smiled his biggest donkey smile.

The moon rose, soft and silver, and Dilly closed his eyes, dreaming of sunny meadows and all his dear friends.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Being yourself is best of all — your friends love you just as you are.
