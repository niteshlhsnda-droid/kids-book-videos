# Nia the Nightingale and the Sleepy Star

*An original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High up in the night sky lived a tiny star named Twinkle.

Twinkle was the smallest star in the sky, and every night, just before bedtime, she got so excited that she could not fall asleep.

She twinkled and wiggled and spun in circles, and her light flashed — blink! blink! blink! — all over the sky.

"Oh dear," said the Moon, rubbing his sleepy eyes. "Twinkle, you need to rest. The whole sky needs its sleep!"

But Twinkle just giggled and spun faster.

Down in the garden below lived a nightingale named Nia, famous for the sweetest songs in the whole world.

One night, Nia heard the Moon's worried sigh. She fluttered up to the highest branch of the tallest tree and called up to the sky.

"Twinkle!" she sang softly. "Would you like a song to help you sleep?"

"Yes, please!" giggled Twinkle, still wiggling.

So Nia began to sing — not a fast, happy song, but a slow, soft, floating song.

"Twinkle, twinkle, little star, close your eyes, you have danced so far..."

Twinkle slowed down a little. Her blinking grew softer — blink... blink... blink...

Nia sang even softer. "Rock-a-bye, little star, the Moon will keep you safe where you are..."

Twinkle yawned a tiny star yawn. Her light dimmed to a gentle glow.

Nia sang softer still, barely a whisper now. "Hush-a-bye, sleepy star, dream of the places you have travelled so far..."

And with one last, happy little blink, Twinkle fell fast asleep, glowing softly in the dark sky.

The Moon smiled. "Thank you, Nia," he whispered. "You sang the sleepiest song in the whole world."

Nia smiled back. "Every star needs a song sometimes," she said. "And every singer needs someone to sing to."

And from that night on, whenever a little star could not sleep, Nia sang her soft, slow song, and the whole sky drifted off to dreamland together.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A gentle song at bedtime can calm even the wiggliest heart.*
