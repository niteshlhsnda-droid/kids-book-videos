# Flick the Firefly and the Friendly Glow

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet meadow, behind tall oak trees, there lived a little firefly named Flick.

Every night, when the sun went down, Flick joined his friends in the meadow. The big fireflies flashed bright, golden lights, and the small ones did too.

But Flick's light was different. It was tiny and soft, like a single sleepy star.

"My light is too small," Flick whispered to himself. "It will never help anyone."

One night, dark clouds rolled across the sky and covered the moon and the stars. The meadow grew very, very dark.

Flick sat on a leaf and watched his friends. "I wish I could shine brighter," he said. "Then I could help someone find their way."

Just then, he heard a small, frightened voice. "Hoo? Hoo? Where am I?"

It was Zuzu, a baby owl. She had flown out of her nest for the very first time, and now she could not find her way home.

"I am lost," she cried softly. "It is so dark, and I cannot see my tree."

Flick's heart beat fast. His light was small, but maybe it was enough for one little owl.

"Do not worry, Zuzu," he said kindly. "Follow my light. I will help you home."

Flick flew up into the dark sky and blinked his soft light. Blink. Blink. Blink.

Zuzu flapped her little wings and followed the tiny glow. "I can see you!" she called. "Your light is lovely!"

Flick flew slowly, so Zuzu could keep up. He blinked once, then again, then again. Each blink lit a small path through the dark.

"Almost there," said Flick. "Your tree is just past the tall oak."

Zuzu followed his glow around the oak tree, over a little stream, and up to a big, cosy nest.

"Look!" she cried happily. "There is my tree! There is my nest! There is my mummy!"

Mummy Owl spread her soft wings and hugged Zuzu close. "Thank you, little firefly," she said warmly. "Your small light brought my baby home."

Flick smiled. His tiny light felt warm and bright inside his chest.

"You are very brave," said Mummy Owl. "You flew through the dark to help a friend."

"I did not think my light was big enough," said Flick.

"It was just right," said Zuzu, snuggling into her nest. "It was soft and gentle, and I could follow it all the way home."

From that night on, Flick never worried about his light again. He knew that even a small light can guide someone home.

Every night, he flew through the meadow, blinking his soft glow. Blink. Blink. Blink.

And whenever a little creature felt lost or afraid, Flick was there, shining his friendly light, one soft blink at a time.

The fireflies in the meadow twinkled together, big lights and small lights, all shining happily in the dark.

Flick's light was still the smallest of all, but now he knew a wonderful secret.

A small light is not too small. A small light is just enough to help a friend.

The end. 🌙
