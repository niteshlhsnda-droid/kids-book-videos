The Clever Farmer's Daughter

A timeless German folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Once, in a kingdom of green hills, there lived a farmer and his clever daughter, Ria.

Ria was not the strongest or the tallest child in the village — but she was the cleverest. She could solve any puzzle, answer any riddle, and untangle any knot.

One day, the king himself came riding through the village. He had heard of Ria's cleverness, and he wished to test it.

"Come to my palace tomorrow," said the king. "But listen well — you must come to me neither on foot nor on horseback… neither dressed nor undressed… and neither by day nor by night. If you can do all this, I'll give you a special job at the palace — you'll be my helper, the one who solves the tricky problems."

The whole village gasped. "It cannot be done!" the villagers groaned.

But Ria only smiled. "Then I'll come," she said softly. That night, she sat by her little window, watched the stars come out, and thought and thought. Can you guess what clever plan she thought of? Hmm… listen, and I'll tell you. And by morning, she had a plan.

Ria set off at twilight — that soft purple time that is neither day nor night.

She did not walk. She did not ride a horse. She rode on the back of her pet goat, Bimble — clip-clop, clip-clop down the quiet road — and Bimble trotted along happily, as if he knew the riddle's answer too.

She was not dressed in a gown. She was not undressed either. She had wrapped herself all around in a fisherman's net — rough and ropey, full of little knots — just enough to be covered, and just enough to be clever.

So there she came to the palace gates: at twilight, on a goat, wrapped in a fishing net.

The guards stared. The palace helpers stared. And the king? He stared too — and then he laughed with delight.

"You came neither on foot nor on horseback!" laughed the king. "Neither dressed nor undressed! Neither by day nor by night! Every riddle — solved!"

True to his word, the king gave Ria a special job at the palace: helping him solve the tricky problems. And whenever a hard problem came to the palace, Ria would think, and smile, and find the cleverest answer of all.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Clever thinking can solve the hardest riddles.
