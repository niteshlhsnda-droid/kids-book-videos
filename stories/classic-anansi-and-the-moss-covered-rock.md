# Anansi and the Moss-Covered Rock

*An African world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Anansi the spider was clever — too clever, some said — and he loved a good trick.

One morning in the forest he found a big round rock, soft and green with moss. Anansi tapped it and grinned his widest grin. A plan was hatching!

He scampered to Elephant. "Have you seen the magic rock?" he whispered. "Look deep into the moss, and you will fall into the sweetest sleep!"

Elephant looked. Elephant yawned. Elephant flopped down — snore-snore-snore!

Quick as a wink, Anansi nibbled the bananas from Elephant's lunch basket.

Next came Zebra. "Look deep into the moss," said Anansi. Zebra looked, yawned, flopped — snore! And Anansi nibbled her sweet grass-cakes.

Then came Monkey, then Giraffe, then Warthog. Look, yawn, flop, snore! And Anansi's tummy grew rounder and rounder with everyone's lunch.

Little Rabbit Rosa watched it all from behind a bush. She twitched her long ears. "Hmm," she thought. "A rock that makes you sleepy? Let me look closer."

She hopped up to the rock and sniffed. Moss. Just moss! And a very full, very sneaky spider.

"Anansi!" Rosa called sweetly. "Your magic rock is wonderful! But have YOU ever looked deep into it?"

Anansi puffed up proudly. "Of course not! I am too clever to —"

"Oh, please try!" begged Rosa. "Just one little look?"

Anansi bent over the rock and looked deep, deep into the green moss. His eight eyes grew heavy. He yawned a great big spider yawn — and flopped over — snore-snore-snore!

Rosa giggled softly. Then she gathered up all the leftover lunches and shared them back with Elephant, Zebra, Monkey, Giraffe, and Warthog. Everyone woke up, rubbed their eyes, and laughed and laughed.

When Anansi woke, his cheeks burned red. "You tricked the trickster!" he said.

Rosa smiled. "Tricks come back around, Anansi. Next time, just ask to share — it tastes sweeter."

Anansi thought about that. Then he laughed too — a big, honest laugh. And from that day on, whenever there was a picnic, Anansi brought the juiciest mangoes for everyone.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Tricks come back around — sharing tastes sweeter than tricking.*
