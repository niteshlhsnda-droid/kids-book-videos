Holly the Hippo and the Hushed Lagoon

A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a wide green lagoon lived a young hippo named Holly.

Holly had soft grey skin, round ears, and a smile as wide as the lagoon itself.

All day long, the lagoon was full of noise and splashing.

The frogs croaked, the fish splashed, and the dragonflies buzzed.

Holly loved it all — splashing and playing and laughing with her friends.

But one evening, as the sun went down, something felt different.

The sky turned pink, then purple, then dark blue.

The moon rose, round and bright, and a soft hush fell over the lagoon.

The frogs stopped croaking, the fish stopped splashing, and even the dragonflies folded their wings.

"Hush," whispered the reeds. "Hush, hush. It is bedtime for the lagoon."

Holly blinked. She had never heard the lagoon so quiet.

"It feels strange," she whispered. "Where did all the noise go?"

Just then, Pip the frog hopped onto a lily pad.

"Shh," said Pip, in his tiniest voice. "The lagoon is getting sleepy. We must be soft and quiet now."

"But I love to splash!" said Holly, giving a little wiggle.

SPLISH! Her tail made a small wave.

Pip giggled. "A soft splash is fine," he said. "It is like a lullaby."

Holly smiled. A soft splash. She could do that.

She slid deeper into the water, making only the gentlest ripple.

"Look," said Pip. "Soft and slow, like the moonlight."

Then Lila the firefly floated down, glowing like a tiny lantern.

"Hello, Holly," she glowed. "I am lighting the way to bed for all my friends."

"How lovely," said Holly. "May I help?"

"Of course," said Lila. "Hum with me. A low, sleepy hum helps everyone drift off."

So Holly hummed.

Hmmmm. A low, warm hum, like a soft blanket of sound.

Pip closed his eyes and smiled. "That hum feels like a warm hug," he said.

Finn the little fish swam up and floated on his back.

"That hum sounds like the water rocking me to sleep," he yawned.

Hmmmm. Hmmmm.

The reeds swayed gently. The moonlight sparkled on the water like silver dust.

One by one, Holly's friends settled down for the night.

Pip tucked himself under his lily pad, pulling a leaf over him like a blanket.

Finn swam down to his cosy hollow under a smooth stone.

Lila dimmed her glow and rested on a reed.

Holly looked around at the hushed lagoon, her heart full of warmth.

"It is not strange at all," she whispered. "The quiet is a gift that helps everyone rest."

She floated on her back and watched the stars twinkle, one by one.

The moon smiled down at her.

"Goodnight, lagoon," whispered Holly. "Goodnight, Pip. Goodnight, Finn. Goodnight, Lila."

And as the last star blinked into the sky, Holly closed her eyes.

She hummed one last soft hum, and the lagoon hummed back, gentle and sleepy.

The water rocked her softly, like a cradle, and Holly drifted off to sleep, dreaming of moonlight and soft splashes.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Quiet can be a kindness — a soft splash and a sleepy hum help everyone rest.
