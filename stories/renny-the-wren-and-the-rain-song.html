Renny the Wren and the Rain Song

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a cozy hole in an old oak tree lived a tiny wren named Renny.

Renny was the smallest bird in the whole forest. Her feathers were soft brown, her beak was tiny, and her song was the sweetest — peep-peep-purr!

But Renny had one big fear: thunder.

Whenever dark clouds rolled in and the sky went RUMBLE-BOOM, Renny would dive into her hole, pull a leaf over her head, and shiver.

"Thunder is too loud! Thunder is too scary!" she would chirp.

One evening, the sky turned grey and heavy. Plip... plop... the first raindrops began to fall.

Renny's mama said, "Come inside, Renny. The storm is coming."

Renny zipped into the hole and hid under her leaf. RUMBLE... BOOM! She squeezed her eyes shut.

But then — through the rumble — Renny heard something new.

Pitter-patter-pitter... pit-pat-pit... pitter-patter-pitter-pat.

It was the rain! Dancing on the leaves, tapping on the bark, drumming on the stones — like a thousand tiny musicians playing a song.

And under the rain's song, Renny heard her mama singing softly: "Rain, rain, do not fear... Mama's song is always near..."

Renny peeked out from under her leaf. The whole forest was glistening. Every leaf wore a shiny raindrop hat. The stream was laughing as it ran.

"BOOM!" said the thunder — but this time, it sounded like a big drum in a band.

"Pitter-patter!" said the rain — like tiny bells.

And Renny — little scared Renny — opened her beak and sang: "Peep-peep-purr! Peep-peep-purr!"

Her song danced with the rain's song. The thunder drummed along. The whole forest was making music together!

When the storm passed, the sun peeked out, and a rainbow stretched across the sky like a smile.

Renny flew out and sat on the highest branch, her feathers all fluffy and proud.

"Mama!" she chirped. "The thunder wasn't scary at all! It was just the drums in the rain's band!"

Her mama nuzzled her. "You see, my little one? When you sing along, scary sounds become songs."

From that day on, whenever the clouds rolled in, Renny was the first bird to fly out and sing her rain song — peep-peep-purr! — and all the little birds who were scared sang along with her.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When you sing along, scary sounds turn into songs — courage can be musical.
