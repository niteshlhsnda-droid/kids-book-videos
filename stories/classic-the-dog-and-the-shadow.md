# The Dog and the Shadow

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

There once was a happy little dog named Moti.

One morning, Moti's master gave him a big, juicy bone for lunch.

"Thank you!" barked Moti, wagging his tail. "This is the best bone in the whole world!"

He picked up the bone in his mouth and trotted off to find a quiet spot to enjoy it.

Soon he came to a little wooden bridge over a sparkling stream.

As Moti trotted across the bridge, he looked down into the water — and stopped.

Down in the stream, he saw another dog! And that dog had a bone in its mouth too — a bone that looked even bigger than his own!

"Oh!" thought Moti. "That dog's bone is bigger than mine! I want that one too!"

So Moti opened his mouth wide and barked, "Woof! Give me your bone!"

But the moment he opened his mouth — splash! — his own bone fell out and plopped into the stream below.

The other dog was gone too. For of course, it was only Moti's own reflection in the water!

Moti stared at the ripples. His bone had sunk to the bottom of the stream, and the water was too deep to reach it.

"Oh dear," said Moti softly. "I had a lovely bone, and I lost it because I wanted another one that was never really there."

He walked slowly home, feeling very silly.

But when he got home, his master smiled and gave him a bowl of warm rice and milk for supper.

Moti wagged his tail. "You know what?" he said. "I already have everything I need right here."

And from that day on, whenever Moti saw his reflection in a puddle or a stream, he just wagged his tail and said, "Hello, my friend! I am happy with my own bone, thank you!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Be happy with what you have — chasing a shadow can make you lose something real.*
