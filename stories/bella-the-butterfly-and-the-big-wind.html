Bella the Butterfly and the Big Wind

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a sunny meadow full of marigolds lived a little butterfly named Bella.

Bella's wings were orange with black tips, and when she fluttered, they shimmered like tiny suns. Every morning she sipped nectar and danced from flower to flower.

One breezy afternoon, Bella flew a little too high, chasing a fluffy dandelion seed.

Suddenly — WHOOSH! A big, wild wind came tumbling over the hills!

"Whoa!" cried Bella. The wind grabbed her wings and spun her round and round — up over the meadow, past the tall mango tree, and far, far away to a garden she had never seen before.

When the wind finally set her down on a strange blue flower, Bella's wings were trembling.

"Oh no," whispered Bella. "Where am I? How will I ever get home?"

A plump bumblebee buzzed over. "Are you lost, little butterfly?"

Bella nodded, and a tiny tear rolled down her cheek. "The big wind blew me away from my meadow."

"Don't cry," said the bee kindly. "My name is Buzzy. The wind always blows toward the hills at sunset. If you fly with it — not against it — it will carry you home."

"But I'm too tired to fly that far," said Bella.

Just then, a ladybird landed beside her. "Then rest on my back!" said Dotty the ladybird. "I'll carry you to the edge of the garden."

So Bella rode on Dotty's shiny red back to the garden gate. There, a sparrow was waiting.

"Hop on, little one!" chirped the sparrow. "I'll give you a lift over the mango tree."

The sparrow carried Bella gently over the tall mango tree. And there — below — was her own sunny meadow, glowing gold in the sunset!

Bella fluttered down to her favorite marigold. "Home!" she cried happily.

The next morning, Bella did something wonderful. She gathered flower petals — pink, yellow, purple — and made a beautiful petal parade all across the meadow.

"What's this for?" asked Buzzy the bee, who had come to visit.

"It's a thank-you parade!" said Bella. "For you, and Dotty, and the sparrow. When the big wind blew me away, kind friends blew me home."

And every year after that, on the first windy day of spring, Bella holds her petal parade — to remind everyone that friends help you find your way.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When you are lost, kind friends will help you find your way — and gratitude keeps them close.
