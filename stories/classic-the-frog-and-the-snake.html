The Frog and the Snake

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Beside a big green hill there was a round, blue pond. The water was cool and clear, and soft pink flowers grew all around it.

In the pond lived a little frog named Freddie. He was small and green, with bright, round eyes. Freddie loved to hop on the lily pads and sing all day long.

But Freddie had one big worry. At the far end of the pond lived a big snake named Slinky. Slinky was long and shiny, and Freddie was afraid of him.

"I must stay far away from that snake," said Freddie. "Snakes are scary!"

So Freddie only swam on his side of the pond. He never, ever went near Slinky's side.

One morning, Freddie was singing his favorite song when he heard a sad, soft sound. It was coming from Slinky's side of the pond.

Freddie hopped a little closer. Then a little closer still. And there was Slinky, all alone on a rock, with his head down low.

"Why are you so sad?" asked Freddie, in a very small voice.

Slinky looked up with kind, gentle eyes. "I am sad because I have no friends," he said. "Everyone is afraid of me, and no one ever talks to me."

Freddie thought about that. Slinky did not look scary at all. He just looked lonely.

"Would you like to be my friend?" asked Freddie.

Slinky's eyes lit up like two little stars. "Oh, yes! I would love that!"

From that day on, Freddie and Slinky did everything together. Freddie taught Slinky his soft, happy song. Slinky learned it so well that soon the whole pond was humming along.

In the morning, they swam side by side. In the afternoon, they floated on the lily pads and watched the clouds drift by.

At night, when Freddie was fast asleep, Slinky swam slowly around the pond. He kept watch so that no harm could come to the little frog or any of the pond's tiny creatures.

"You keep the pond safe," said Freddie one morning, "and I will keep our song alive."

"And I will keep you safe every night," said Slinky with a smile.

Soon, all the other pond creatures saw how kind Slinky was. The dragonflies came to say hello. The little fish swam up to play. Even the shy turtle peeked out of his shell.

The pond became the happiest place in the whole green valley. And every evening, just as the sun went down, Freddie and Slinky sang their soft song together, and all the pond creatures sang along.

Then, when the stars came out, everyone tucked themselves in and drifted off to sleep, safe and warm, beside their very best friends.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness turns fear into friendship.
