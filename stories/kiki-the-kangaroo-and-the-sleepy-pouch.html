Kiki the Kangaroo and the Sleepy Pouch

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In the golden grasslands of Australia lived a little kangaroo named Kiki. She was the bounciest joey in the whole kangaroo family — boing, boing, boing! — from sunrise to sunset.

But when night came, Kiki had a problem. While all the other joeys snuggled into their mothers' warm pouches and fell fast asleep, Kiki's legs would not stop bouncing.

"Boing!" went her legs under the blanket of stars. "Boing! Boing!"

"Kiki," whispered her mother, Mama Roo, "your legs need to sleep too. What will help them rest?"

"I do not know," said Kiki, boing-ing softly. "They just want to keep jumping!"

Mama Roo thought for a moment. Then she had an idea. "Hop into my pouch, little one," she said. "I will teach you the Sleepy Pouch game."

Kiki curled into the warm, soft pouch. It smelled like sunshine and smelled like home.

"Now," said Mama Roo, "we are going to bounce — but only in our imaginations. Close your eyes."

Kiki closed her eyes.

"Imagine," said Mama Roo in her softest voice, "that you are bouncing on a cloud. Boing... so slow... so soft..."

"Boing," whispered Kiki, imagining it.

"Now the cloud is carrying you over the grasslands," said Mama Roo. "Below you, the wombats are yawning in their burrows. Yaaawn."

"Yaaawn," whispered Kiki.

"The kookaburras have tucked their heads under their wings. The emus are standing on one leg, fast asleep. Even the wind is getting sleepy — listen — shhhhh..."

"Shhhhh," whispered Kiki. Her legs were still now. Very, very still.

"And the stars," said Mama Roo, even softer, "are closing their eyes, one by one. Can you count them closing? One... two..."

But Kiki was already asleep — curled up tight in the sleepy pouch, dreaming of bouncing on clouds, with the softest smile on her little kangaroo face.

Mama Roo kissed the top of her head. "Goodnight, my bouncy girl," she whispered. "Even the bounciest legs need their rest."

And all across the grasslands, everything was quiet — except for the gentle shhhh of the sleepy wind.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Even the bounciest legs need rest — a quiet imagination is the softest pillow.
