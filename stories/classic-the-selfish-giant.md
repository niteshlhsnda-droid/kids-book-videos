# The Selfish Giant

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, there was a giant with a beautiful garden. It was the most beautiful garden you ever saw — soft green grass, peach trees heavy with fruit, and flowers that smelled like honey.

Every afternoon, when school was over, the children came to play in the garden. They laughed, they chased butterflies, and they climbed the peach trees. It was the happiest garden in the whole town.

But one day, the giant came home from a long journey. He saw the children playing and roared, "What are you doing in MY garden? Get out! This garden is mine, and mine alone!"

He built a tall wall all around the garden and put up a notice: "KEEP OUT!"

The children went away sadly. And do you know what happened next? Spring forgot the giant's garden.

Everywhere else, flowers bloomed and birds sang. But in the giant's garden, it stayed winter all year long. Snow covered the grass. Frost painted the trees. The north wind howled over the wall, and hail rattled on the roof.

"I cannot understand it," grumbled the giant, sitting alone in his cold, empty house. "Why is spring so late this year?"

One morning, the giant woke to the loveliest sound — a little bird singing outside his window. He jumped out of bed and looked out.

The children had crept in through a hole in the wall! They were playing in the garden again — and wherever they played, the snow melted, flowers opened, and spring came dancing back.

Only in one far corner, winter still lingered. There stood a tiny boy, too small to climb into a peach tree. He was crying softly.

The giant's heart, which had been hard and cold for so long, suddenly melted like the snow. "How selfish I have been!" he said. "I kept the children out, and I kept spring out too."

He tiptoed into the garden and gently lifted the little boy up into the branches of the peach tree. The boy threw his arms around the giant's neck and kissed his cheek.

All the other children laughed with joy. "The giant is kind now!" they cried.

The giant knocked down the tall wall with his own great hands. "From now on," he said, "this garden is YOUR garden too. Come and play here every day."

And from that day on, the giant's garden was the happiest garden in the whole town. Spring never forgot it again, and every afternoon the giant played with the children until the sun went down.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A garden — like happiness — grows lovelier when it is shared.*
