Birbal and the Lost Camel

A clever Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a busy town in India, there lived a kind merchant named Arjun.

Arjun sold soft shawls and bright scarves in the market. Every morning, he loaded his shawls onto his gentle camel, Kesar, and walked with her to his little shop.

Kesar was a lovely camel. She had soft brown fur, long eyelashes, and a very sweet smile. She loved to munch on mango leaves, and she loved Arjun most of all.

Arjun was showing a beautiful red shawl to a customer when — oh no! — he turned around, and Kesar was gone!

"Kesar? Kesar, where are you?" called Arjun, looking left and right. But Kesar was nowhere to be seen. She had wandered off into the crowd.

Poor Arjun did not know what to do. So he walked to the palace and asked to see Emperor Akbar.

"Your Majesty," said Arjun, bowing low, "my camel Kesar is lost. I have searched the whole market, but I cannot find her anywhere. Please help me!"

Emperor Akbar smiled kindly. He was a wise and gentle king, and he loved to help his people.

"Do not worry, Arjun," said the Emperor. "My friend Birbal will help you. Birbal is the cleverest man in all my kingdom!"

"Tell me, Arjun," said Birbal, "what does Kesar like to eat best of all?"

"She loves mango leaves," said Arjun. "She would walk a very long way for a fresh mango leaf."

"And where did you last see her?" asked Birbal.

"Near the mango seller's stall, by the old banyan tree," said Arjun.

Birbal nodded. "Then let us follow the clues," he said. "A clever search begins with clever questions."

So Birbal and Arjun walked back to the market together. Birbal looked down at the dusty ground, and there he saw them — big, round footprints, leading away from the mango seller's stall.

"Look!" said Birbal, pointing. "These are Kesar's footprints. See how big and round they are? Let us follow them!"

Then Birbal stopped and sniffed the air. "Do you see those nibbled leaves on the ground?" he asked. "Somebody has been munching mango leaves along this path!"

"That must be Kesar!" cried Arjun, clapping his hands. "Only she loves mango leaves so much!"

They followed the trail of nibbled leaves, step by step, until they came to a quiet garden at the edge of the town. And there, under a big shady mango tree, stood Kesar!

She was munching happily on the sweet green leaves, her tail swishing from side to side. When she saw Arjun, she gave a happy little hum and trotted over to him.

"Kesar!" cried Arjun, hugging her soft neck. "I have found you! Thank you, Birbal! You are the cleverest man in the whole kingdom!"

Birbal laughed. "I only asked questions and followed the clues," he said. "Your camel was never far away. She was just following her favourite snack!"

That evening, Arjun gave Birbal a beautiful blue shawl as a thank-you gift. And Kesar got a whole basket of fresh mango leaves, which she munched very happily indeed.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Clever questions and careful looking can solve the trickiest puzzles.
