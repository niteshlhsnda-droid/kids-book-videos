Tenali Rama and the Key to Heaven

A timeless Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long ago, in the village of Tenali, lived Tenali Rama — the cleverest man in the king's court. He could make the whole court laugh with his clever, funny jokes.

One sunny morning, a new man in a long orange robe came to the village. He settled into the cool shade of the big peepal tree, folded his hands, and closed his eyes.

"I am a great and holy man!" he announced in his grandest voice. "Bring me sweets and fruits, and I will pray for you."

Every morning and every evening, the man sat under the tree with his eyes closed. And every time, he said the SAME one prayer, over and over. "Om shanti, om shanti, om shanti…"

The villagers were amazed. They brought him laddus and ripe mangoes and bananas. "What a holy man!" they said.

But Tenali Rama watched from his shady tree. He stroked his chin and thought, "Hmm. A truly wise man would know more than one little prayer."

One evening, Tenali sat down beside the holy man, very close, and gave him a big, friendly smile. Then — plip! — Tenali gently plucked one loose hair from the man's beard. It didn't hurt a bit — but the holy man yelped, "Ouch!" as if it had.

But Tenali was already on his feet, holding the little hair high in the air. What do YOU think he did next?

"I HAVE IT!" shouted Tenali. "The key to heaven! This holy man is SO great that one hair from his beard will take me straight to heaven!"

The villagers gasped. Then, all at once, everyone rushed forward. "Me too! Me too!" they cried. "I want a hair too!"

The holy man opened his eyes. A whole crowd was running at his beard! "Oh no!" he squeaked. He leaped up, grabbed his long orange robe, and ran away down the road as fast as he could!

And he was never seen in Tenali again.

The villagers looked at one another. Then they giggled. Then they laughed and laughed. How silly they had been!

Tenali grinned. "Always use your own clever head, friends," he said. "Ask questions — that's what wise friends do!"

That night, the village was cozy and quiet. The lamps glowed softly. The peepal tree swayed in the breeze. And high above, the round moon seemed to be smiling down at Tenali, the cleverest, kindest joker of them all.

Sleep tight, little one. The moon is smiling at you too.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Use your own clever head — don't believe everything you hear, even from someone in a fancy robe.
