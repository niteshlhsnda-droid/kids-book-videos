# Sunny Says Goodnight

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High above the clouds lived Sunny the sun. Every morning she woke up bright and cheery, shining on the flowers and warming the playgrounds. She loved daytime more than anything.

But when evening came, Sunny did not want to set. "I am not tired!" she declared, feeling bouncy and wide awake, and she kept on shining and shining.

Down below, the world needed nighttime — baby birds needed to sleep, flowers needed to close their petals, and little children needed the dark to dream. But Sunny would not dim.

Mother Moon floated up beside her. "Sunny," she said gently, "even suns need sleep. Let me show you the goodnight stretch."

Sunny crossed her rays grumpily. "I do not want to stretch. I want to shine!"

"Just try," said Mother Moon. "First, stretch your rays out wide. As wide as you can."

Sunny stretched her golden rays until they reached across the whole sky. It felt rather nice.

"Now pull them in close," said Mother Moon. "Tuck them in like a blanket."

Sunny pulled her rays in, and the sky turned pink, then orange, then purple. A tiny yawn started tickling her.

"Stretch, yawn, goodnight," sang Mother Moon.

Sunny stretched once more and yawned a great golden yawn. Her light grew softer. Down below, the baby birds tucked their heads and the flowers closed their petals.

"Stretch, yawn, goodnight," sang Mother Moon again.

Sunny sank lower toward the sea, her light now just a warm glow. She felt drowsy and cozy. Being the sun was hard work — she had earned her rest.

"Stretch, yawn, goodnight," sang Mother Moon one last time.

Sunny slipped below the horizon, and the stars came out to take her place. Mother Moon smiled and began her night watch while Sunny dreamed sunny dreams under a blanket of ocean.

Down in the world, a little child looked out the window. "Goodnight, Sunny," she whispered. "Sleep well."

Sleep now, bright sun. You shone beautifully today. Rest now, and tomorrow you can shine again.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even the brightest and busiest need their rest — that's how they shine again tomorrow.*
