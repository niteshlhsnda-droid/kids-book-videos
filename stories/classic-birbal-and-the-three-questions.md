# Birbal and the Three Questions

*A clever Akbar and Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in India, there lived a great king named Akbar. And at his court lived his cleverest friend, Birbal, who could answer any question with a smile.

One morning, a proud scholar arrived at the palace. He wore a tall turban and carried a pile of big, heavy books.

"O great King Akbar!" he said, bowing low. "I have travelled to a hundred kingdoms, and no one has ever answered my three questions. If your Birbal cannot answer them, then I am the cleverest man in the world!"

King Akbar smiled. "Birbal," he said, "come and answer this scholar's questions."

Birbal stepped forward and bowed. "I am ready," he said.

The scholar cleared his throat. "Question one!" he said. "How many stars are there in the sky?"

All the courtiers gasped. How could anyone count the stars?

Birbal smiled. He pointed to a little goat standing in the palace garden.

"There are exactly as many stars in the sky," he said, "as there are hairs on that goat. Count them if you like — you will see I am right!"

The scholar opened his mouth. Then he closed it again. He could not count the hairs on a goat!

"Question two!" he said quickly. "Where is the very centre of the earth?"

Birbal picked up a thin stick from the ground. He pressed it into the earth, right where he was standing.

"Here," he said. "The centre of the earth is exactly here, under my stick. Measure it if you like — you will see I am right!"

The scholar blinked. How could he measure the whole earth?

"Question three!" he cried. "What am I thinking right now?"

Birbal looked at the scholar. He looked at his tall turban. He looked at his pile of big, heavy books. Then he smiled his widest smile.

"You are thinking," said Birbal, "that I am a fool who cannot answer your questions. But I just answered all three!"

The whole court burst out laughing — "Ha! Ha! Ha!" King Akbar laughed so hard that tears rolled down his cheeks.

The scholar laughed too. He bowed low to Birbal. "You are truly the cleverest man in the world," he said. "I came to show off — but you have shown me that a clever answer is kind, quick, and makes everyone smile."

King Akbar gave Birbal a bag of gold coins. And Birbal shared them with all the children of the palace, who clapped and cheered.

That night, as Birbal walked home under the stars, he looked up at the sky and smiled.

"One, two, three…" he counted softly. "Just as many as the hairs on a goat!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever mind answers with a smile — and a kind answer wins every heart.*
