# Anansi and the Tug of War

*A timeless Anansi tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In the warm lands of West Africa lived Anansi the Spider — small, clever, and always full of ideas. Anansi loved stories, and he loved a good laugh even more.

One sunny morning, Anansi was spinning his web between two mango trees when he heard two enormous voices arguing.

"I am the strongest creature in all the world!" trumpeted Ellie the Elephant, stamping her great foot.

"No, I am!" boomed Wally the Whale, splashing his great tail in the river. "I can pull a whole ship!"

Anansi scuttled down his web, chuckling to himself. "Two strong friends, both sure they are the strongest," he thought. "What a splendid game this could be!"

So Anansi tied one end of a long, strong vine around a tree near Ellie, and scuttled over to Wally with the other end.

"Dear Ellie," said Anansi politely, "Wally the Whale says he is stronger than you. Will you take one end of this vine and show him?"

"Certainly!" said Ellie, wrapping her trunk around the vine.

Then Anansi scuttled to the river. "Dear Wally," he said, "Ellie the Elephant says she is stronger than you. Will you take this end of the vine and show her?"

"Gladly!" said Wally, wrapping his tail around the vine.

"On the count of three," called Anansi, climbing to the top of a tall palm tree where he could see them both, "pull!"

"One… two… three… PULL!"

Ellie pulled with all her might. Wally pulled with all his might. The vine stretched tight — creak, creak, creak! — and neither Ellie nor Wally moved an inch!

Ellie pulled left. Wally pulled right. The vine went boing! And then — SPROING! — the vine slipped from Ellie's trunk, and Ellie sat down with a bump in a patch of soft grass. At the same moment, the vine slipped from Wally's tail, and Wally splashed happily back into the river.

Anansi laughed so hard he nearly fell off his palm tree. "Ha ha ha!" he cried. "You are both exactly as strong as each other!"

Ellie blinked. Wally splashed. Then they both began to laugh too.

"You know what, Wally?" said Ellie, shaking the grass from her ears. "You are a splendidly strong friend."

"And you are too, Ellie," said Wally, waving a friendly fin. "Let us never argue about it again. Let us have a picnic instead!"

So Ellie brought sweet mangoes from her tree, and Wally brought cool river water in a big leaf cup, and Anansi brought his stories. And the three of them laughed and picnicked together until the sun went down, golden and warm.

And that is how Anansi's tug of war ended — not with a winner, but with three good friends.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When friends stop arguing about who is strongest, everyone wins a picnic.*
