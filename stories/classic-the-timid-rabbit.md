# The Timid Rabbit

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once, in a green, green forest, under a tall mango tree, there lived a little rabbit named Chintu.

Chintu was a sweet rabbit, but he worried about everything. A shadow could make him jump. A falling leaf could make his heart go thump-thump-thump.

One warm afternoon, Chintu curled up for a nap in the cool shade of the mango tree. His nose twitched. His ears drooped. His eyes closed... and off he floated into a dream.

THUMP!

A ripe, round mango dropped from the tree and landed right beside his ear — THUMP!

Chintu's eyes flew open. "Oh no!" he squeaked. "That was so loud! The sky is falling! The sky is falling!"

He didn't stop to look. He tucked his ears back and ran — ran as fast as his little legs could carry him.

A second rabbit saw him running. "Why are you running?" she asked.

"The sky is falling!" cried Chintu.

The second rabbit didn't stop to ask more. "The sky is falling!" she cried, and off she ran too.

A deer saw the two rabbits running. "Why are you running?" asked the deer.

"The sky is falling!" cried the rabbits.

The deer didn't stop to ask more. "The sky is falling!" cried the deer, and away she bounded.

Then a monkey swung down from a branch. "Why are you running?" he asked.

"The sky is falling!" cried the deer and the rabbits.

The monkey didn't stop to ask more. "The sky is falling!" he chattered, and he swung away after them.

Then a big elephant heard the thumping feet. "Why is everyone running?" he rumbled.

"The sky is falling!" everyone cried.

The elephant did not stop to ask more. Off he thundered after them — THUMP, THUMP, THUMP!

Soon a whole parade was racing through the forest — rabbits, a deer, a monkey, an elephant, and more and more friends joining in. Dust rose. Leaves shook. Nobody had seen the sky fall — but everyone was running!

Then — a deep, calm voice called out, "Stop!"

It was the old lion king. He stood on a sunny rock, still and calm as deep water.

"My friends," said the lion gently, "why is everyone running?"

"The sky is falling!" they all cried.

The lion looked up. The sky was blue and quiet. Not a cloud was falling.

"Show me where the sky fell," said the lion.

So the lion padded back along the trail, and the whole parade tiptoed after him — quieter now.

They came to the tall mango tree. The air smelled sweet with ripe mangoes.

Can you guess what the lion found? There lay the ripe mango, round and golden. And there was the little dent in the soft earth where it had landed.

The lion picked up the mango with one great paw. "My friends," he said with a warm smile, "the sky did not fall. A mango fell — THUMP — and our little Chintu heard it."

Chintu's ears went pink. "It… was only a mango?" he whispered.

"Only a mango," said the lion. "Next time, when fear shouts, stop and look first."

Then — everyone laughed. A soft, kind, giggly laugh that floated up through the mango leaves.

Chintu laughed too, and his whole body felt light and warm.

Together they walked home under the calm blue sky — the rabbits hopping softly, the deer stepping gently, the monkey swinging slow, the elephant padding quietly, and the lion humming a low, sleepy song.

The leaves whispered. The breeze was soft. And little Chintu, not worried at all, drifted off to sleep right under his mango tree — where the sweetest dreams grow.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When fear shouts, stop and check the facts first.*
