# Didi the Duckling and the Silver Bell

The sun had dipped low behind the reeds, and the little pond turned the color of warm honey. Mama Duck gathered her ducklings close. "Tuck your beaks in," she quacked softly. "The stars are coming out to watch us sleep."

Didi was the smallest duckling, and she wore a tiny blue ribbon around her neck. As she paddled toward Mama, her foot bumped something smooth and round among the reeds. She dove her head under the water — and came up with a small silver bell on a frayed red string.

It was the prettiest thing Didi had ever found.

She slipped it under her wing and said nothing. All through supper, the bell felt warm against her feathers. That night, when everyone was asleep, Didi took it out and shook it very, very gently.

*Tink.*

The sound was dull and flat, like a pebble dropping into mud. Didi frowned. Perhaps it needed a morning shine, she thought, and hid it in her nest.

In the morning she polished the bell on her softest feather until it gleamed. *Tink.* Still dull. She showed it to Mama. "Where did you find it, little one?" asked Mama.

Didi looked down at her webbed feet. "In the reeds," she mumbled, which was true — but not all of it was true. Mama smiled, but her eyes were kind in a way that made Didi's chest feel tight.

All day Didi carried the bell. She shook it for the frogs and the dragonflies. *Tink. Tink.* Each ring sounded sadder than the last, as if the bell were homesick.

Then the evening fog came rolling across the pond, thick as cotton. Through the grey, Didi heard a worried voice. "Oh dear, oh dear," called Old Heron, poling his little wooden boat in circles. "My boat bell is gone! How will I call through the fog without my bell?"

Didi's heart went *thump-thump-thump*. She looked at the silver bell in her feathers — the frayed red string, the round silver sides. It was his.

Her wings felt heavy as stones as she paddled out into the fog. "Mr. Heron," she peeped, "I found your bell. I should have brought it back sooner."

Old Heron took the bell and tied it to the front of his boat. "Thank you, little Didi," he said, and his eyes crinkled with kindness. Then he rang it.

*Ting-a-ling! Ting-a-ling!*

The bell sang out clear and bright — the sweetest sound Didi had ever heard. It danced over the fog and came back as an echo, as if the whole pond were laughing with joy.

That night, Didi snuggled under Mama's warm wing. The heron's bell rang softly in the distance, guiding the fireflies home.

Sleep now, little Didi.

The end. 🌙
