Buzzy the Bee Finds Her Buzz

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Buzzy was a small honeybee who lived in a hive in the old apple tree. Every morning, the bees flew out together. Bzzzz! Bzzzz! Bzzzz! Their buzzing filled the air.

But one morning, Buzzy woke up quiet. She flapped her wings. No buzz. She flapped harder. Still no buzz.

"Oh no," whispered Buzzy. "My buzz is gone!"

She panicked, her wings trembling. "How will I fly with my sisters?" She felt sad and hid inside the hive.

Her friend Mona the moth fluttered over softly — moths are quiet and kind.

"Why are you hiding, Buzzy?" asked Mona.

"I lost my buzz," said Buzzy. "I am not a real bee without it."

Mona settled beside her. "Your buzz is not lost," she said. "It is just resting. Your buzz was inside you all along."

"Your buzz was inside you all along," Buzzy repeated softly.

"Your buzz was inside you all along." It became her hopeful hum.

"Your buzz was inside you all along."

Mona had a gentle plan. "Close your eyes," she said. "Think of the happiest thing you know."

Buzzy closed her eyes. She thought of sunny flowers. She thought of sweet nectar. She thought of her sisters dancing.

Her wings began to tingle. Tingle, tingle. Then, very softly: bzz.

Buzzy's eyes popped open. "Did you hear that?" she cried.

"Bzz!" said her wings again. A little louder. "BZZ!"

Mona clapped her wings. "Your buzz was inside you all along!"

Buzzy laughed. She felt relieved and joyful. Her buzz had been resting, just like Mona said.

Can you buzz like Buzzy?

Start soft: bzz.

A little louder: bzzz!

Now your biggest buzz: BZZZZ!

Buzzy flew out into the sunshine. Bzzzz! Bzzzz! She joined her sisters, and they danced the waggle dance together. They sipped nectar from apple blossoms. Sweet, sweet, sweet. Buzzy buzzed all day long, so happy to have her buzz back.

That evening, the hive glowed golden. Buzzy curled into her honeycomb cell while Mona rested on the hive's edge.

"Goodnight, buzzy bee," whispered Mona.

"Goodnight," buzzed Buzzy, soft and sleepy. Her buzz grew quieter. Bzz... bzz... bzz.

The apple tree swayed gently. The stars came out to listen. And Buzzy slept, buzzing softly in her dreams.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Sometimes what feels lost is only resting inside you.
