# Credits — Asha the Ant and the Singing Grasshopper

- **Original title:** "The Ant and the Grasshopper" (a fable from *Æsop's Fables: A Version for Young Readers*, by J. H. Stickney and Aesop)
- **Author:** Aesop (ancient Greek fabulist, c. 620–564 BCE); retold for young readers by J. H. Stickney
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *Æsop's Fables: A Version for Young Readers* (eBook #49010) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/49010
- **License:** Public domain (Aesop lived c. 620–564 BCE; the Stickney edition is listed as public domain in the USA on Project Gutenberg)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, expanded from the short fable into ten story beats — the sunny meadow, Asha's little yellow scarf and seed pantry, Gus and his brown fiddle, the invitation to sing, the golden autumn, the first snow, Gus's cold and hungry night, the soft knock, the open door and the fireside soup, and the shared goodnight; the ending is softened so the tale closes gently at bedtime, with the friends asleep together by the fire.
