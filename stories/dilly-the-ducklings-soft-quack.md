# Daisy the Duckling's Soft Quack

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Daisy was the smallest duckling in Bluebell Pond, in Willowmere Wood. Her brothers and sisters quacked very loud — QUACK QUACK QUACK! — at the sunrise and at the dragonflies. But when Daisy tried to quack, only a squeak came out.

"Squeak," said Daisy. She tried again. "Squeak."

Her face felt hot with embarrassment. "I cannot quack like everyone else," she whispered, hiding behind a lily pad. She did not want to try anymore.

Her friend Bobby the beaver swam over. He had been building a stick dam when he heard Daisy's tiny squeak. "What a sweet sound!" he said.

Daisy shook her head. "It is too small," she sighed. "It is not a real quack." Big tears wobbled in her eyes.

Bobby sat beside her. "Listen to my tail," he said, slapping the water softly. Plip. "Not every sound has to be big," he told her. "A soft quack is still a quack."

"A soft quack is still a quack." Daisy repeated it quietly. "A soft quack is still a quack." It became her brave little saying.

Daisy lifted her beak and let out her small sound. "Squeak quack," she said.

Bobby clapped his paws. "Beautiful!" he cheered.

Daisy tried again, a little braver. "Squeak quack! Squeak quack!" The dragonflies danced to her sound, and the tadpoles wiggled with joy. She felt light as a feather — her small quack was special.

Can you make a soft quack like Daisy? Try it: squeak quack! Now a little louder: squeak quack!

Daisy led a parade around Bluebell Pond with Bobby marching behind her, and her brothers and sisters joined in. They quacked loud, and Daisy quacked soft, and together it sounded like music. QUACK squeak QUACK squeak. Everyone smiled.

When the sun set, Daisy tucked herself under her mother's wing, tired and happy. Bobby floated nearby on a log. "Goodnight, sweet quacker," he whispered.

Daisy gave one last tiny quack. "Squeak quack," she yawned. Bluebell Pond grew quiet and still, and Daisy slept, soft and safe.

Sleep now, little Daisy.

The end. 🌙
