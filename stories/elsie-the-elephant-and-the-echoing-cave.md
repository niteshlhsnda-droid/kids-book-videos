# Elsie the Elephant and the Echoing Cave

*A sweet original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green jungle, beside a slow, brown river, there lived a young elephant named Elsie.

Elsie loved to trumpet. Oh, how she loved to trumpet! Every morning, she lifted her trunk high and blew, "Toot-too-too!"

But her trumpet was very, very loud. It shook the leaves. It rattled the branches. It woke the monkeys, the parrots, and even the sleepy old tortoise.

"Elsie, dear," said the tortoise one morning, rubbing his eyes. "Your trumpet is wonderful — but could you trumpet just a little more softly?"

Elsie felt sad. She loved her trumpet. But she did not want to wake her friends.

So she tiptoed away from the river, through the tall grass, and up the rocky hill. There, hidden behind a curtain of green vines, she found a cave.

Inside, the cave was cool and quiet. The air smelled of rain and stone. Little drops of water went, "Plip! Plop! Plip!" from the ceiling.

Elsie took a deep breath. She lifted her trunk — and she trumpeted, very softly, "Too-too-too."

And something magical happened. The cave answered her!

"Too-too-too!" echoed the cave, soft and sweet, like a song.

Elsie trumpeted again. "Too-too-too!"

"Too-too-too!" sang the cave.

Elsie laughed. Her trumpet sounded like music in the cave — gentle and round, like a lullaby.

Every day, Elsie came to the cave to practise. She learned to trumpet softly, then a little louder, then softly again — like a song that rises and falls. She counted the echoes — one echo, two echoes, three echoes — and each one sounded sweeter than the last.

Sometimes the little brown river-bird came to listen. Sometimes the green frog came to listen. And they all agreed: Elsie's soft trumpet was the sweetest sound in the whole jungle.

One evening, the moon rose, round and silver. Elsie invited all her friends to the cave.

"Come and listen," she said.

The monkeys came. The parrots came. The tortoise came, slowly, slowly. And Elsie trumpeted her softest, sweetest song.

"Too-too-too!" went Elsie. "Too-too-too!" sang the cave.

Then the monkeys hummed. The parrots chirped. The tortoise hummed a low, happy hum. And the cave sang it all back, soft and round, like a whole choir.

"It sounds like the whole jungle is singing!" cried the tortoise, smiling.

That night, Elsie and her friends lay down outside the cave, under the twinkling stars. Elsie trumpeted one last soft goodnight — "Too-too" — and the cave whispered it back — "too-too."

And they all fell fast asleep, dreaming of the echoing cave.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A big voice can be gentle too — and a song shared with friends sounds sweeter than a song sung alone.*
