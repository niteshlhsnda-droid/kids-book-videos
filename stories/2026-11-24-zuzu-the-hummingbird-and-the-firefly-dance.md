# Zuzu the Hummingbird and the Firefly Dance

In a garden full of pink hibiscus and sleepy marigolds, there lived a tiny hummingbird named Zuzu. Her wings were a blur of green, her throat shone ruby-red in the sun, and she could fly faster than any bird in the whole garden.

Zuzu loved to zip and zoom. She zipped to the tallest sunflower before breakfast, zoomed to the pond before lunch, and raced the wind all the way home before supper. "Slow is boring," she chirped. "Fast is fun!"

One evening, as the sky turned peach and gold, Zuzu zoomed past a big green leaf — and stopped. A little snail was sitting on the leaf, looking up at the sky with big, sad eyes.

"Hello," said Zuzu, hovering close. "I'm Zuzu. Why do you look so sad?"

"I'm Bibi," said the snail, in a small, soft voice. "Every night, the fireflies dance in the clearing across the garden. I have never seen them. I am too slow to get there before dark."

Zuzu looked at the far clearing. It was a very long way for a snail. But Zuzu had an idea — a slow idea, which was new for her. "Then I will stay with you," she said. "We will go together, one little slide at a time."

So Zuzu folded her fast wings and walked — well, hopped — beside Bibi. Bibi slid slowly along the leaf, and Zuzu pointed out all the things she had never stopped to see: a dew drop shining like a tiny moon, a ladybug tucking herself under a petal, a spider's web sparkling with evening light.

"Oh," whispered Bibi. "The garden is beautiful when you go slowly."

"It is," said Zuzu, surprised. She had zipped past these wonders a hundred times and never once seen them.

But the sky grew darker, and the clearing was still far away. Bibi's little shell drooped. "We will never make it," she sighed. "The dance will be over."

Zuzu looked up. The first stars were blinking awake. Then she had another idea. She took a deep breath and hummed — a soft, sweet, hummingbird hum, low and gentle like a lullaby.

One firefly drifted over, curious. Then another. Then ten more, then a hundred, until the whole garden around them glowed like a lantern. The fireflies danced in slow, golden circles — right there, around Bibi's leaf.

Bibi laughed, a tiny happy laugh. "They came to us!" she cried. "I can see the dance! I can see it!"

Zuzu watched the golden lights swirl, and her heart felt warm and full. She had not raced anywhere tonight — and yet this was the loveliest evening she had ever known.

When the fireflies drifted home and the moon climbed high, Bibi tucked herself into her shell, sleepy and smiling. Zuzu curled up on the leaf beside her, her fast wings folded at last.

Sleep now, little Zuzu.

The end. 🌙
