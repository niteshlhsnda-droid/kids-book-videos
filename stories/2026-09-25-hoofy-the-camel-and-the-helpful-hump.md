## Hoofy the Camel and the Helpful Hump

Long ago, at the edge of a wide golden desert, there was a little green oasis with tall palm trees and a cool, clear pond. Three good friends lived there — a Horse, a Dog, and an Ox — and every morning they worked together. The Horse carried bundles of grain. The Dog fetched cool water from the pond. The Ox pulled the wooden plow.

And right beside them lived a camel named Hoofy. But Hoofy never helped at all.

"Hoofy," said the Horse, "will you carry some of this grain with me?"

"Humph," said Hoofy, and he lay down in the warm sand.

"Hoofy," said the Dog, "will you fetch some water with us? The pond is far today."

"Humph," said Hoofy, and he yawned a great big yawn.

"Hoofy," said the Ox, "will you help me pull the plow, just for a little while?"

"Humph," said Hoofy, and he closed his eyes and dozed.

All day long, Hoofy did nothing but chew prickly desert plants and watch the clouds drift by. Whatever anyone asked, he only said, "Humph."

Soon the three friends were very tired, and their paws and hooves were sore. So one evening, they told their troubles to the Spirit of the Desert, the kind old keeper of all the animals in the dunes.

That night, while Hoofy dozed under the twinkling stars, the Spirit came to him, soft as a desert breeze.

"Hoofy," said the Spirit gently, "you have said 'Humph' for so long that you do not know what work feels like. From now on, you shall carry your own share — and a little extra besides."

And as the Spirit spoke, something soft and round began to grow upon Hoofy's back. It did not hurt, not one bit. It was a hump — a cozy little storehouse where a camel can keep food and water for long, hard journeys.

"That hump," said the Spirit, "will remind you to share your strength with others."

When Hoofy woke in the morning and felt the hump, he smiled a shy little smile. He walked to the Horse, the Dog, and the Ox, and said, "I am sorry I only ever said 'Humph.' May I help you today?"

"Of course you may," said the Horse warmly.

And from that day on, Hoofy carried bundles of grain, fetched cool water, and helped pull the plow. His hump held all the food and water he needed, so he never grew tired. And his friends loved him dearly — for a helper is always welcome.

And that is why, to this very day, every camel carries a hump upon its back — to remind us that helping others makes every journey lighter.

**Moral:** When everyone helps, no one's load is heavy.
