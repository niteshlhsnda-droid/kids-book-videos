# Leo Tells the Truth

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Leo was a little lion cub.

He had a big, bouncy ball.

One afternoon, he kicked it too hard.

BONK!

The ball hit Mama's clay pot.

CRASH!

The pot broke into pieces.

Leo felt scared.

His heart thumped fast.

His ears felt hot.

"What if Mama is angry?" he thought.

He wanted to say the wind did it.

He wanted to hide the pieces.

But then he remembered something.

Papa Lion always said, "Truth is brave, even when it shakes."

"Truth is brave, even when it shakes," Leo whispered to himself.

"Truth is brave, even when it shakes," he said again.

"Truth is brave, even when it shakes," he said one more time.

Leo took a deep breath.

He walked to Mama.

His paws felt wobbly.

"Mama," he said quietly.

"I broke your pot with my ball. I am sorry."

"I felt scared to tell you."

Mama Lion looked at the pieces.

Then she looked at Leo.

She hugged him tight.

"Thank you for telling the truth," she said.

"I am not happy about the pot."

"But I am proud of your honesty."

Leo felt surprised.

He felt relieved.

The shaky feeling melted away.

"Can we fix it?" he asked.

"Yes," said Mama.

They glued the pieces together.

The pot had golden cracks now.

"It looks special," said Leo.

"It tells our story," said Mama.

That evening, Leo played with his ball more carefully.

He kicked it softly.

Away from the pots.

At bedtime, Papa Lion nuzzled him.

"You were brave today," Papa said.

Leo smiled.

He felt scared earlier.

Now he felt honest and light.

The savanna wind sang softly.

Leo closed his eyes.

He was truthful.

He was loved.

Goodnight, Leo.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Telling the truth is brave, and it makes our hearts feel light.*
