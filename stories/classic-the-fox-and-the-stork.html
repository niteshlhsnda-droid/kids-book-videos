The Fox and the Stork

A timeless Aesop fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

One sunny morning, a clever red fox named Felix was cooking soup in his little kitchen. The soup smelled so good that Felix wanted to share it with someone.

"I know!" said Felix. "I will invite my friend Stella the Stork for dinner."

Stella had long, thin legs and a very long beak. She loved visiting friends, and she loved soup most of all.

That evening, Stella arrived at Felix's house, tapping her long legs happily on the path.

"Welcome!" cried Felix. "Sit down, sit down. Dinner is ready!"

Felix poured his lovely soup into two flat plates and set one in front of Stella.

"Mmm, thank you!" said Stella. She dipped her long beak into the flat plate. Peck, peck. But the soup was spread so thin that her beak could hardly pick any of it up.

Felix slurped his own soup with great big laps. "Delicious!" he said, licking his lips.

Poor Stella tried and tried, but all she got was a tiny taste on the tip of her beak. She smiled politely, because she did not want to be rude, but her tummy was still empty when she went home.

The next morning, Stella thought about it. "Felix did not mean to be unkind," she said to herself. "He just forgot that my beak is different from his mouth. I will show him, in a kind way, what it feels like."

So Stella invited Felix to dinner at her house.

"Welcome!" cried Stella. "Sit down, sit down. Dinner is ready!"

Stella poured her lovely soup into two tall, narrow jars and set one in front of Felix.

"Mmm, thank you!" said Felix. He pushed his nose toward the jar, but his wide fox muzzle could not fit inside at all. He licked the rim. He tilted the jar. Not one drop came out.

Stella sipped her soup easily with her long beak. "Delicious!" she said, smiling.

Felix looked at his jar. Then he looked at Stella. And slowly, his ears turned pink.

"Oh," said Felix quietly. "Now I understand. Last night, my flat plate was just as tricky for your long beak as this tall jar is for my wide nose."

"That is right," said Stella gently. "We both like soup. We just eat it in different ways."

Felix laughed. "I am sorry, Stella. Next time, I will think about what suits my guest, not just what suits me."

"And I will do the same," said Stella. "Now — shall we cook together?"

So the two friends cooked a fresh pot of soup together. They poured it into two wide bowls, and they both ate happily, spoonful after spoonful, until their tummies were full and warm.

From that day on, whenever Felix and Stella shared a meal, they always chose dishes that suited them both. And that is why their dinners were the happiest dinners in the whole meadow.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Think about what your friend needs, not just what you need — a good host makes everyone comfortable.
