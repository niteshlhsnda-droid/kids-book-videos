Wendy the Whale and the Sleepy Song

A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Deep, deep in the blue ocean, there lived a little whale named Wendy.

Wendy had a big, friendly smile, a smooth grey back, and the biggest heart in the whole sea. All the little fish loved her, because Wendy was gentle — she never splashed too hard, and she always said please and thank you.

But Wendy had one little problem. She could not sing the sleepy song.

Every evening, when the sun went down and the sea grew dark and quiet, all the sea animals sang the sleepy song together. The dolphins sang "la-la-la." The seals sang "ho-ho-ho." Even the tiny shrimp hummed, "hmm-hmm-hmm."

And the little fish would yawn, and close their eyes, and drift off to sleep, safe and warm.

But when Wendy opened her mouth to sing — all that came out was a great big BLOOP!

"BLOOP!" went Wendy. And all the little fish giggled — not to be unkind, because a bloop is a very funny sound, but Wendy felt a little shy all the same.

Wendy tried and tried. She practised behind a big rock, all by herself. "La-la-la," she tried. But out came — "BLOOP!" She tried again. "Ho-ho-ho!" But out came — "BLOOP!"

"Oh dear," sighed Wendy. "My voice only knows one sound."

One evening, Wendy swam to her best friend, Shelly the sea turtle.

"Shelly," said Wendy, "everyone can sing the sleepy song except me. I only go BLOOP!"

Shelly smiled her slow, wise smile. "Wendy, your bloop is not a mistake. It is YOUR sound. Have you ever listened to what your bloop does?"

Wendy blinked. "What it does?"

"Watch tonight," said Shelly.

That evening, as the sun went down, the sea animals began their song. "La-la-la," sang the dolphins. "Ho-ho-ho," sang the seals. "Hmm-hmm-hmm," hummed the shrimp.

And Wendy took a deep breath and sang — "BLOOP!"

And do you know what happened? The bloop rolled through the water like a soft, round bubble. It went bloop-bloop-bloop, gentle and slow, like a rocking cradle. And one by one, the little fish yawned — yawn! — and closed their eyes, and drifted off to sleep.

"Look!" whispered Coral the clownfish. "Wendy's bloop is the best part! It sounds like a lullaby hug!"

Wendy's eyes went wide. "Really?"

"Really!" said Shelly. "Your bloop is soft, and round, and slow — just right for sleepy little fish."

From that evening on, Wendy sang her bloop with pride. "La-la-la," sang the dolphins. "Ho-ho-ho," sang the seals. "Hmm-hmm-hmm," hummed the shrimp. And "BLOOP!" sang Wendy — soft and round and slow.

And all the little fish slept soundly, dreaming of bubbles and blue waves and a big, kind whale named Wendy.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Your own sound is the right sound — sing it proudly, and it will be just right.
