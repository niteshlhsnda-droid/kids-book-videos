# Rory the Jealous Raccoon

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Rory was a young raccoon who lived in Willowmere Wood, and his best friend was Daisy the duck.

One day, Daisy got a new red scarf. It was soft and bright. Everyone said, "What a pretty scarf!"

Rory felt jealous. His chest felt tight. His smile felt wobbly.

"I want a red scarf too," he thought. He did not feel happy for Daisy. He felt grumpy instead.

Daisy noticed. "Do you want to try it on?" she asked.

Rory shook his head. He sat under the bush alone.

His mama found him there. "I see you feel jealous," she said.

Rory nodded slowly.

"It is okay to feel jealous," Mama said. "Feelings are like clouds. They pass."

Mama sat beside him. "Jealous is just wanting. It does not make you bad," she said.

"Jealous is just wanting. It does not make you bad," she said again.

"Jealous is just wanting. It does not make you bad," she said one more time.

Rory breathed out. The tight feeling loosened a little.

"What can I do?" he asked.

"You can tell Daisy how you feel," Mama said. "And you can be glad for her too."

Rory walked back to Daisy. "I felt jealous of your scarf," he said, "but I am glad you have it."

Daisy smiled big. "Thank you for telling me," she said.

Then she did something kind. She gave Rory her old blue scarf. "It is yours now," she said.

Rory wrapped it around his neck. It was soft and warm. He felt happy — really happy.

That night, Rory wore his blue scarf to bed. He thought about Daisy. He felt glad in his heart — glad for his friend, glad for himself.

The moon glowed through the leaves. Rory closed his eyes. He was cozy. He was kind.

Sleep now, little Rory.

The end. 🌙
