# Teremok: The Little House in the Woods

*A gentle Russian folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in a quiet Russian forest, where the pine trees whispered and the snow fell soft as feathers, there stood a tiny little house.

It was called a teremok — a dear little house — with a crooked chimney, a round window, and a tiny door. And nobody lived in it. It stood empty and lonely among the tall trees.

One chilly morning, a little gray mouse came scurrying through the snow. Her name was Misha.

"Oh!" she squeaked, stopping in her tracks. "What a sweet little house! Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?"

No one answered. The little house was empty.

"Well then," said Misha, pushing open the tiny door, "I'll live here! It's warm and dry and just my size."

She swept the floor with her tail, hung a curtain on the round window, and made herself a cozy bed of soft moss. It was perfect.

The next day, hop-hop-hop came a frog through the snow. Her name was Frosha.

"Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?" she called.

"I do! Misha the mouse!" came the squeaky reply. "Who are you?"

"I'm Frosha the frog! May I live with you?"

"Oh yes! There's room for two!" So Frosha hopped in, and the two friends shared warm berry tea.

The next day, hippity-hop came a bunny. His name was Binky.

"Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?"

"I do! Misha the mouse! And I! Frosha the frog! Who are you?"

"I'm Binky the bunny! May I live with you?"

"Oh yes! There's room for three!" So Binky squeezed in, and the three friends sang songs together.

The next day, tip-tap-tip came a fox. Her name was Fifi.

"Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?"

"I do! Misha! And I! Frosha! And I! Binky! Who are you?"

"I'm Fifi the fox! May I live with you?"

"Oh yes! There's room for four — if we all squish a little!" So Fifi curled her fluffy tail around herself and squeezed in.

The next day, pad-pad-pad came a wolf. His name was Wally.

"Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?"

"I do! Misha! And I! Frosha! And I! Binky! And I! Fifi! Who are you?"

"I'm Wally the wolf! May I live with you?"

"Oh... yes! There's room for five — if we squish a LOT!" So Wally squeezed in sideways, and the little house creaked and groaned, but it held.

Then, on the sixth day — THUMP... THUMP... THUMP — came a great big bear. His name was Bruno.

"Teremok, teremok — who lives in the teremok?"

Five little voices answered, "We do! Misha and Frosha and Binky and Fifi and Wally! Who are you?"

"I'm Bruno the bear! May I live with you?"

The five friends looked at each other. The little house was already bursting at the seams!

"Well..." said Misha bravely, "there's always room for one more friend!"

So Bruno tried to squeeze in. He pushed and pushed... and then he decided to just sit on the roof to be cozy.

CREAK... CRACK... CRASH!

The little teremok squashed flat as a pancake!

Everyone tumbled out into the soft snow — Misha, Frosha, Binky, Fifi, Wally, and Bruno. For a moment, nobody spoke.

Then Bruno's lip trembled. "I'm so sorry," he rumbled. "I broke your beautiful house."

But Misha scampered up onto his big paw. "Don't cry, Bruno! It was an accident. And you know what? Now we can build a BIGGER house — one with room for everyone!"

So that's what they did. All six friends worked together — the mouse carried twigs, the frog mixed mud, the bunny gathered moss, the fox wove branches, the wolf lifted logs, and Bruno... Bruno did the heavy lifting!

And soon there stood a big, beautiful teremok — with six cozy beds, a round window for everyone to peek through, and a door wide enough for a bear.

That night, all six friends snuggled inside, warm and happy, listening to the pine trees whisper: shhh... shhh... shhh...

"Best house ever," yawned Misha.

"Best friends ever," yawned Bruno.

And they all drifted off to sleep — six friends in one big, cozy house.

Good night, little one. There's always room for one more friend.

---

*Moral: There's always room for one more friend — and together, we can build something bigger.*
