# Credits — Hoofy the Camel and the Helpful Hump

- **Original title:** "How the Camel Got His Hump"
- **Original author:** Rudyard Kipling (1865–1936)
- **Illustrator:** Fresh original illustrations generated for this adaptation (no source art reused)
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2781 (also https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/26223)
- **License:** Public domain in the USA (published 1902; author died 1936 — public domain worldwide)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm, bedtime-friendly language for ages 4–7. The long-winded Victorian asides were removed; the Djinn became the gentler "Spirit of the Desert"; the three animals were simplified to a Horse, a Dog, and an Ox; and the ending softens Kipling's punishment into a friendly reminder that helping others makes every journey lighter.
