# Ravi Helps Grandpa

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Ravi was a small boy, five years old.

He lived next door to his grandpa.

Grandpa had a little garden.

He grew tomatoes, beans, and one big mango tree.

One hot afternoon, Grandpa carried a heavy basket.

It was full of ripe mangoes.

His back felt achy.

His steps felt slow.

Ravi saw him from the gate.

He felt worried.

"Grandpa looks tired," he thought.

He ran over quickly.

"Grandpa, can I help you?" he asked.

Grandpa smiled.

"That would be wonderful, Ravi."

Ravi took one side of the basket.

Grandpa took the other side.

They carried it together.

Step by step.

It was still heavy.

But helping felt good.

Ravi felt helpful.

His chest felt warm.

His smile felt big.

Grandpa said, "Little hands make light work."

"Little hands make light work," he said again, patting Ravi's head.

"Little hands make light work," he said one more time.

Then they watered the garden together.

Ravi held the small watering can.

Grandpa held the big one.

Splash, splash went the water.

The tomato plants danced.

The bean plants shivered with joy.

Grandpa picked the ripest mango.

He cut it into pieces.

They sat under the mango tree.

They ate the sweet, juicy mango.

"This is the best mango ever," Ravi said.

"Because we grew it together," said Grandpa.

That evening, Ravi told his mama everything.

"I helped Grandpa today," he said proudly.

Mama hugged him tight.

"Helping others is a superpower," she said.

At bedtime, Ravi snuggled under his blanket.

He thought about Grandpa's smile.

He felt warm inside.

Helping had made two people happy.

The night was soft and quiet.

Ravi closed his eyes.

He was kind.

He was loved.

Goodnight, Ravi.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Helping someone we love makes our own heart happy too.*
