# Credits — "Goldie and the Three Bears"

- **Original title:** "The Story of the Three Bears"
- **Author:** Robert Southey (1774–1843)
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated in watercolor style for this project.
- **Source URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Treasury_of_Pleasure_Books_for_Young_Children%2FThe_Story_of_the_Three_Bears (Wikisource edition of Southey's tale, first published in *The Doctor*, 1837)
- **License:** Public domain (first published 1837; author died 1843)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (~450 words, bedtime tone). The visitor is the familiar little girl Goldie with golden curls; the three bears keep their great big / middle-sized / tiny wee sizes; the ending is softened so Goldie apologises, helps mend the chair, and is welcomed back — the lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
