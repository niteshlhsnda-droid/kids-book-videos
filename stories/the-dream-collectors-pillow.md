# The Dream Collector's Pillow

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Fable the fox kit had a problem: last night she forgot to dream. She just slept dark and empty, and she felt disappointed. Dreams were her favorite part of night.

She told her grandmother about it. Grandma smiled a wise smile. "Then tonight," she said, "we will visit the Dream Collector's pillow."

"The who?" asked Fable, curious.

Grandma led her to the attic, where an old, plump pillow sat in the corner. It was patched and soft, with a tiny pocket sewn on its side — and the pocket glowed faintly.

"This pillow collects spare dreams," whispered Grandma. "When someone has an extra happy dream, the pillow saves a little piece. For nights like yours."

Fable touched the pillow gently. It felt warm. She heard a faint giggle from the pocket… then a tiny snore. The dreams inside were alive!

"Soft pillow, hold my dream, keep it safe till sleep," sang Grandma.

The pocket glowed brighter, and a wisp of golden mist floated out. It swirled in the air, smelling like birthday cake and summer rain. Fable felt excited.

"Soft pillow, hold my dream, keep it safe till sleep," sang Grandma again.

The mist settled over Fable like a blanket, and she saw flashes of pictures — a carousel of cupcakes, a rainbow slide, a kitten wearing a crown. These were spare dreams, shared with love.

Fable hugged the old pillow. "Thank you," she whispered, feeling grateful and warm.

"Soft pillow, hold my dream, keep it safe till sleep," sang Grandma one last time.

Grandma carried the pillow downstairs and placed it beside Fable's bed. Fable snuggled under her blanket while the pillow sat close, humming softly.

Fable's eyes grew heavy as the golden mist wrapped around her. She dreamed of the cupcake carousel, rode the rainbow slide, and waved at the crowned kitten.

When she woke up, she remembered everything — the best dream ever. And she knew just what to do: that night, she would share a piece of her dream back with the pillow.

Sleep now, little fox. The Dream Collector is watching over you. May your dreams be golden and sweet.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Dreams are even sweeter when they are shared with love.*
