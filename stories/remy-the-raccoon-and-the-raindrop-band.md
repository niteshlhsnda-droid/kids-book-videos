# Remy the Raccoon and the Raindrop Band

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a forest where the trees grew tall and the mushrooms grew round, there lived a little raccoon named Remy.

Remy had a stripy tail, clever paws, and ears that could hear EVERYTHING. He could hear a beetle sneeze three trees away. He could hear a worm wiggle underground.

But most of all, Remy loved the rain.

Other animals ran for cover when the clouds rumbled. Not Remy. When the first drops fell — plip! plop! — Remy would run OUTSIDE and twirl in circles, laughing.

"Rain! Rain! My favorite music!" he would cry.

Because to Remy's wonderful ears, raindrops didn't just fall. They PLAYED.

One gray evening, the clouds gathered thick and soft, and the rain began — gentle at first, then steady and sweet.

Plip... plop... plip...

Remy scampered to his favorite spot: the old gardener's shed at the edge of the forest. Around it lay all sorts of wonderful things — tin pots, wooden buckets, big leaves, and hollow logs.

And as the rain fell, each thing sang its own note!

On the tin pot: TING! TING! — bright and silvery.

On the wooden bucket: DONG... DONG... — deep and round.

On the big leaves: pitter-patter-pitter — soft and whispery.

On the hollow log: boom-boom — low and cozy, like a sleepy drum.

Remy's eyes sparkled. "It's a BAND!" he whispered. "A raindrop band! And every band needs... a conductor!"

He climbed onto an upside-down bucket, picked up two twigs like batons, and raised his paws high.

"Ready, everyone?" he whispered to the rain. "One... two... three... PLAY!"

And the rain played! Ting-ting on the pot! Dong-dong on the bucket! Pitter-patter on the leaves! Boom-boom on the log!

Remy waved his twig batons — swish, swish — and the raindrops seemed to follow him. Faster! TINGTINGTING! Slower... ting... ting... Soft as a whisper... pitter... patter...

Soon, animals from all over the forest crept out to listen. The rabbits sat in a row, their ears swaying. The owls blinked sleepily on their branch. The hedgehogs curled into cozy balls. Even the grumpy old badger peeked out of his burrow and smiled.

"It's beautiful," sighed Mama Rabbit, cuddling her babies close.

Remy conducted more and more softly. His batons moved slower... and slower... like leaves floating down to the ground.

Ting... dong... pitter... boom...

The rabbits' eyes drooped. The owls tucked their heads under their wings. The hedgehogs breathed slow and deep. One by one, the whole forest began to yawn — big, soft, sleepy yawns.

Remy smiled. He lowered his batons very, very gently.

And the rain, as if it understood, grew softer too — until it was just a whisper on the leaves. Shhh... shhh...

"Good night, band," Remy whispered. He curled his stripy tail around himself like a blanket, right there on his bucket podium.

And the last thing he heard before sleep was the sweetest sound in the world: the rain playing its softest song — ting... dong... pitter... boom — a lullaby for the whole forest.

Good night, little one. Listen to the rain... ting... dong... The band is playing softly, just for you.

---

*Moral: Music is everywhere — you just have to listen for it.*
