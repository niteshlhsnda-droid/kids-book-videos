# Hattie the Hamster and the Hidden Acorn

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a cosy little burrow under the roots of a big oak tree, there lived a small hamster named Hattie.

Hattie had round cheeks, bright eyes, and a very important job: getting ready for winter.

All autumn, she gathered nuts and seeds and carried them home in her cheeks, which puffed up like two little balloons. Pop! Pop! Pop!

One golden afternoon, Hattie found the biggest, shiniest acorn she had ever seen. It was smooth and brown, with a tiny cap like a little hat.

"Oh, what a beautiful acorn!" said Hattie. "This one is too special to eat. I will hide it somewhere safe, and keep it for the coldest winter day."

So Hattie hid the acorn… somewhere very secret. She was so clever about hiding it that she hid it too well — because the very next morning, she could not remember where she had put it!

"Oh no!" cried Hattie. "Where is my beautiful acorn?"

She looked under her bed. No acorn.

She looked behind the little door. No acorn.

She looked inside her teacup, under her blanket, and even in her left shoe. No acorn anywhere!

Just then, her friend Milo the mouse came squeaking down the tunnel. "Good morning, Hattie! Why do you look so worried?"

"I hid my special acorn," said Hattie, "and now I cannot find it!"

"Do not worry," said Milo. "Two heads are better than one. Let us look together!"

So Hattie and Milo searched the whole burrow. They looked in the pantry, and under the table, and behind the picture of the sunflower.

Then Milo had an idea. "Hattie," he said, "where would YOU hide something precious? Somewhere soft and warm?"

Hattie thought and thought… and then her eyes lit up. "Under my pillow!" she squeaked. "I hid it under my pillow, so I could dream about it!"

She lifted her pillow — and there it was! The big, shiny acorn, safe and sound, smiling up at her.

"Hurray!" cried Hattie and Milo together.

But then Hattie looked at the acorn, and she looked at her friend Milo, and she had a new idea — an even better idea.

"Milo," she said, "let us not hide this acorn at all. Let us plant it outside, under the autumn sun. Then one day it will grow into a big oak tree — and there will be acorns for everyone!"

So together, Hattie and Milo dug a little hole, tucked the acorn in, and patted the earth down gently.

"Goodnight, little acorn," whispered Hattie. "Grow tall and strong."

And that night, Hattie dreamed of a great green oak tree, full of acorns — enough for every hamster and mouse in the whole forest.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A hidden treasure is only fun when it is found — and better still when it is shared.*
