Pippa's Big Rainy Day

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Pippa was a little green parrot with a very loud laugh. Ha-ha-ha! Hee-hee-hee!

Every morning, Pippa swooped out of his nest and played all day. He raced the butterflies. He splashed in the puddles. He nibbled sweet, sweet mangoes.

But one morning, Pippa woke up to a strange sound.

Pitter. Patter. PLOP.

Rain! Big, splishy-splashy rain.

"Oh no!" cried Pippa. "I cannot play outside. My wings will get all soggy!"

He sat in his nest and pouted. He pouted and pouted until his beak hurt.

Then — knock, knock, knock! — someone tapped on his door.

It was Tia the tortoise, hiding under a giant leaf like an umbrella.

"Pippa," she said softly, "I am bored too. Can I come in?"

Pippa opened the door. "There is nothing to do," he sighed.

Tia smiled. "Then let us make something to do!"

First, they built a blanket fort — a big, cozy, wobbly blanket fort.

Then, they told each other silly stories. Pippa told a story about a dancing mango. Tia told a story about a sleepy cloud. Ha-ha-ha! Hee-hee-hee!

Then, Pippa remembered the mango muffins his mama had baked — warm, yummy, sweet-smelling mango muffins.

"One for you," said Pippa, "and one for me."

They munched and crunched. Yum, yum, yum!

When the rain finally stopped, the sun peeked out — peep! — and the whole forest sparkled.

"That was the best rainy day ever," said Tia.

Pippa grinned his biggest grin. "Rainy days are not so bad… when you share them with a friend."

And from that day on, whenever the rain came — pitter, patter, PLOP — Pippa smiled, because he knew just what to do.

The end. 🌧️

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Sharing and friendship can turn a gloomy day into a happy one.
