Anansi and the Pot of Wisdom

A timeless Anansi tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a warm green forest full of chattering birds and rustling leaves, there lived a clever little spider named Anansi.

Anansi loved wisdom. He loved good ideas, clever answers, and wise words. And one day he had a big idea of his own.

"I will gather up all the wisdom in the world," said Anansi, "and keep it safe in a pot, just for me!"

So Anansi went from friend to friend, collecting wisdom. From the elephant, he learned to be patient. From the tortoise, he learned to take his time. From the little hummingbird, he learned that small wings can still fly far. He tucked every wise word carefully into a big clay pot.

Soon the pot was full to the brim with wisdom. It was very heavy!

"I must hide it where no one else will find it," said Anansi. "I know — at the very top of the tallest tree!"

Anansi tied the pot to his tummy with a vine and began to climb. But the pot bumped against the tree trunk at every step. Bump! Bump! Bump! Anansi could hardly climb at all.

His little son, Kofi, was watching from below. "Papa," called Kofi, "why don't you tie the pot on your back instead? Then it will not bump the tree!"

Anansi laughed. "What a clever idea, my little one! That wisdom came from you!"

So Anansi tied the pot on his back, and up, up, up he climbed, quick as a wink, all the way to the top of the tallest tree.

But just as he reached the top branch — OH NO! — the vine slipped! The pot tumbled down, down, down, and CRASH! It broke into a hundred pieces on the forest floor!

Out poured all the wisdom, sparkling like golden dust in the sunlight. It floated over the trees and rivers, over every home and hollow, and settled gently into the hearts of everyone in the forest.

The elephant felt patient. The tortoise felt calm. The hummingbird felt brave. And even the tiniest ant felt wise enough to find her way home.

Anansi climbed down and looked around in wonder. "Well," he said, scratching his head and laughing, "it seems wisdom was never meant to stay in one pot. It belongs to everyone!"

And from that day on, whenever anyone in the forest had a good idea, they would smile and say, "Thank you, Anansi — and thank you, little Kofi, too!"

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Wisdom grows when it is shared with everyone.
