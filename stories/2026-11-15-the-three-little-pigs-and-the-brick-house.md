# The Three Little Pigs and the Brick House

In a green valley, where buttercups nodded, there lived three little pigs — Pip, Posy and Plum. One bright morning, their mother said, "My dears, it is time for each of you to build a house of your own."

Pip was the quickest. He met a man carrying straw. "Please, give me that straw to build my house," said Pip. Pip built his house in no time. Then he danced and sang, "My house is done!"

Posy met a man carrying sticks. "Please, give me those sticks," said Posy. Posy built her house of sticks in a wink. Then she joined Pip, and the two of them danced and sang together.

Plum met a man with red bricks. "Please, give me those bricks," said Plum. Plum set to work. He laid each brick straight. It took two whole days. "Come and play!" called Pip and Posy. But Plum said, "Soon. A house should be strong enough to keep us safe."

A grey wolf came down the lane. He stopped at the straw house and called, "Little pig, little pig, let me come in!" "No, no, not by the hair of my chinny-chin-chin!" cried Pip. "Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" said the wolf. And he huffed, and he puffed, and down it came. Pip ran to Posy's house.

Then the wolf knocked on Posy's stick house. "Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!" "No, no, not by the hair of our chinny-chin-chins!" cried Pip and Posy. "Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" said the wolf. And he huffed, and he puffed, and down it came. They ran to Plum's house.

The wolf knocked on the red door. "Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!" "No, no!" cried the pigs. "Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" cried the wolf. So he huffed and puffed — but the brick house did not move at all.

"Little pig," said the wolf, "I know a fine apple tree. Meet me tomorrow at five." "Very well," said Plum. But Plum got up at four and picked the apples. When the wolf came, Plum tossed an apple far away, and while the wolf fetched it, Plum ran home.

The wolf was cross now. He climbed onto the roof to come down the chimney. But Plum heard him, and put a pot of water by the fire. Down came the wolf — splash! — right into the pot. He leapt out and ran away, and he never came back again.

"Plum," said Pip, "will you help us build brick houses too?" "Of course," said Plum. So they built two more brick houses, brick by brick, until all three stood strong. That night, when the moon rose, they snuggled into bed. The house was warm and safe, and the three pigs dreamed of apples and sunshine.

Sleep now, little Plum.
