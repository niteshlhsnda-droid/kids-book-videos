# The Tortoise and the Hare

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Hare was the fastest runner in the whole meadow. Zoom! He could run like the wind.

Tortoise was the slowest walker in the whole meadow. Plod... plod... plod.

One day, Hare laughed at Tortoise. "You are so slow!" he giggled. "I could run to the big oak tree and back before you take ten steps!"

Tortoise smiled a slow, calm smile. "Let us have a race," he said. "You and me. To the big oak tree."

All the animals gathered to watch. "Ready... steady... GO!"

Zoom! Hare shot off like an arrow.

Plod... plod... plod... Tortoise started walking, slow and steady.

Hare ran so fast that soon he could not even see Tortoise behind him. "Ha!" he said. "I have plenty of time. I will take a little nap under this shady tree."

He curled up in the soft grass and closed his eyes. Zzz...

Meanwhile, Tortoise kept walking. Plod... plod... plod... past the giggling squirrels. Plod... plod... plod... past the sleeping Hare. Plod... plod... plod...

He did not stop. He did not hurry. He just kept going, one slow step at a time.

At last, Tortoise reached the big oak tree!

The animals cheered. Hare woke up with a start. "What? How?" He ran as fast as he could — zoom! — but it was too late.

Tortoise had already won.

Goodnight, slow and steady Tortoise.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Slow and steady wins the race.*
