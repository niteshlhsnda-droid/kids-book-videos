# The Monkeys and the Firefly

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One cold winter night, in a big green forest, five little monkeys sat close together under a tall mango tree.

Brrr! The wind was cold. The monkeys shivered and rubbed their little hands together.

"We are so cold," said Momo the monkey, with a shiver. "I wish we had a warm little fire."

Just then, something twinkled in the dark.

Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle! A tiny light danced among the leaves, soft and golden.

"Look!" cried Momo, pointing with one cold finger. "A little fire! A little fire, glowing in the leaves!"

The five monkeys jumped up with joy.

"Warm hands at last!" said Momo, with a big smile. "Let us sit close and warm our cold little hands."

So the monkeys crept closer and closer to the twinkling light. They stretched out their hands, waiting for the lovely warmth.

But the light was not warm at all.

"Why is our fire not warm?" asked little Mimi, the smallest monkey. She tilted her head, wondering.

Just then, a kind little bird flew down from a high branch. Her name was Bella, and she had soft brown feathers and a gentle voice.

"Dear friends," chirped Bella, softly. "That is not a fire at all. That is a firefly, a tiny friend with a light of her own."

"A firefly?" said the monkeys, all together. Their eyes grew round and wide.

"Yes," said Bella, with a smile. "She glows to light her way in the dark. She is not hot, and she cannot burn you. She is just a small, shining friend."

The monkeys looked again and saw her tiny wings and soft, golden glow.

"Oh!" said Momo, softly. "We thought you were a fire. We are sorry, little firefly. We did not mean to crowd around you."

"Do not worry," she seemed to say, in her twinkling way. "I am happy to meet you."

Then Bella had a kind idea. "Little firefly," she chirped, "will you light the way home for my friends? The path is dark."

The firefly twinkled twice, bright and happy. Yes, yes, yes!

So the firefly flew ahead, slow and gentle, lighting the dark path with her soft golden glow.

The five monkeys followed her, step by step, holding hands so no one would trip.

Momo hummed a soft, sleepy song as they walked.

Soon they reached their warm little home in the hollow of the old mango tree.

Inside, there were soft leaves to curl up on, and a cozy corner for each little monkey.

"Thank you, little firefly," said Momo, with a happy smile. "Thank you for your light, and thank you for being our friend."

"Thank you, Bella," said Mimi, sleepily. "Thank you for telling us gently, and for helping us learn."

The firefly twinkled one last soft goodnight outside their little home, then floated away to light another dark path.

The monkeys curled up close together, warm and safe and happy. One by one, their little eyes closed, and they dreamed of twinkling lights dancing softly through the trees.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Ask gently before you decide — and a kind friend can light the darkest path.*
