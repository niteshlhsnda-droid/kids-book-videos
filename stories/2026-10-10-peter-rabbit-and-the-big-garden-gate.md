# Peter Rabbit and the Big Garden Gate

Under a big old fir tree, at the very edge of a sunny meadow, there lived a rabbit family in a cozy burrow. Mama Rabbit took care of her four little bunnies — Flopsy, Mopsy, Cottontail, and Peter.

Peter was the smallest bunny, and the most curious. His whiskers twitched whenever something new happened, and his fluffy tail bobbed like a tiny white pom-pom.

One bright morning, Mama Rabbit called her little ones. She had a basket on her arm, and her kind eyes rested on each of them in turn.

"Now, my dears," she said softly, "I am going to the meadow to gather sweet clover. You may play in the field or go down the lane. But you must not go into Mr. McGregor's garden. It is not a safe place for little rabbits."

Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cottontail promised right away. Peter promised too... but his long ears were already leaning toward the tall garden gate.

As soon as Mama Rabbit hopped away, Peter's little heart began to race. Through the bars he could see rows of the greenest lettuces, the roundest radishes, and the plumpest French beans, all shining in the sun.

"I will just have one tiny peek," Peter whispered. And he squeezed under the gate.

Oh, what a feast! Peter nibbled lettuce leaves until his tummy felt round as a ball. He crunched crisp radishes and munched sweet French beans. Everything tasted better than anything he had ever eaten before.

Then — thump, thump, thump — heavy footsteps came down the path.

Mr. McGregor was coming!

Peter's heart beat like a drum. He turned to run, but his blue jacket caught on a branch. He tugged and tugged, but the jacket stayed behind. His little shoes slipped off too, and away he ran — fast as the wind, with nothing but his round tail bobbing behind him.

"Stop, you rascal!" shouted Mr. McGregor, waving his rake. But Peter was too quick. He slipped back under the gate and ran all the way home, his feet pattering over the soft grass.

Mama Rabbit was waiting at the burrow door. She did not scold him — she just wrapped him in a warm blanket and tucked him into his little bed.

Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cottontail had bread and milk and sweet blackberries for supper. But Peter was so very tired — and his tummy felt funny — that Mama Rabbit brought him a warm cup of chamomile tea instead.

As he sipped it, Peter thought about his mama's gentle words that morning, trying to keep him safe all along.

Mama Rabbit kissed his forehead and pulled the blanket up to his chin.

Sleep now, little Peter.

The end. 🌙
