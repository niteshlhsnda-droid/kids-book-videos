Toby the Toucan and the Twilight Song

An original bedtime tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a lush rainforest, where the leaves were big as umbrellas, lived a young toucan named Toby.

Toby had a magnificent beak — bright orange, yellow, and red, like a sunset you could wear! But Toby had one worry: he could not sing. Whenever he tried, all that came out was a squeaky "Kraa! Kraa!" while the nightingales sang beautiful tunes all around him.

Every evening, at twilight, the birds of the rainforest gathered for the Twilight Song, when each bird sang its own special part. Toby always sat at the back, quiet as a mouse, hiding his big beak behind a leaf.

One twilight, the old parrot, Professor Polly, noticed Toby hiding. "Why so quiet, young Toby?" she asked.

"Because my voice is not beautiful," mumbled Toby. "Kraa is not a song."

Professor Polly chuckled. "Who says a song must sound like a nightingale? The Twilight Song needs every voice — high, low, sweet, and squeaky! Tomorrow, will you try just one little kraa?"

Toby gulped. "Just one?" he said. "I suppose I can try just one."

The next twilight, the birds gathered as the sky turned pink and gold. The nightingales sang, "La-la-la!" The hummingbirds hummed, "Hum-hum-hum!" And then there was a tiny pause — and Toby took a deep breath and sang, "Kraa!"

It was a small kraa. A shy kraa. But it was a start!

"Again, Toby!" chirped the birds. "Again!"

Toby sang again, a little louder: "Kraa! Kraa!" And then something wonderful happened — the nightingales wove their la-la-la around his kraa-kraa, the hummingbirds hummed underneath, and together they made a brand-new Twilight Song, richer and happier than ever before!

"Listen!" cried Professor Polly. "That is the sound the forest was missing! Every voice matters, Toby — especially yours!"

From that twilight on, Toby never hid at the back again. He sat right in the middle, beak held high, singing his proud "Kraa! Kraa!" as the sun went down.

And every night, as the little animals of the rainforest drifted off to sleep, they listened to the Twilight Song — la-la-la, hum-hum-hum, kraa-kraa — and they knew the forest was singing just for them.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Every voice matters — even a squeaky one — when we sing together.
