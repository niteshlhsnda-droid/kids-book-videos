# Credits — Mole and Ratty and the River Picnic

- **Original title:** "The Wind in the Willows" (Chapter 1, "The River Bank")
- **Author:** Kenneth Grahame (1859–1932)
- **Original publication:** 1908
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #289 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/289
- **Illustrator:** None — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated watercolour-style art; no source artwork was reused.
- **License:** Public domain (author died 1932; work published 1908; listed as public domain in the USA by Project Gutenberg)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a ~450-word bedtime story. The long opening chapter is shortened to one gentle morning: Mole leaves his spring-cleaning, meets Ratty by the river, wobbles while learning to row, and shares a picnic on the bank. Toad, Badger, the motorcars and the Wild Wood are left out. The story closes with a warm goodnight. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
