# Asha and the Sleepy Sunflower

*A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Asha lived in a little house with a sunny garden. In the very middle of the garden grew a tall, golden sunflower named Sunny.

Every morning, Sunny stretched up toward the sky and smiled at the sun. "Good morning, sun!" Sunny would whisper. And Asha would laugh, because it really did look as if the sunflower was smiling.

Asha watered Sunny every day. She talked to Sunny while she watered. "Grow big and strong," she said. "You are the tallest flower in the whole garden."

One warm evening, as the sun began to sink low, Asha noticed something strange. Sunny's big golden head was drooping down, down, down. His petals looked tired, and his smile had gone sleepy.

"Oh no!" said Asha. "Are you poorly, Sunny?"

A little bee named Bina buzzed over. "He is not poorly," she buzzed. "He is just sleepy. Flowers get tired too, after a whole day of smiling at the sun."

"Then Sunny needs a lullaby," said Asha.

So Asha sat down on the soft grass beside Sunny. She sang the softest, sleepiest song she knew — the song her grandmother sang to her every night.

"Sleep, little flower, close your petals tight. The moon will watch you all through the night…"

Bina the bee stopped buzzing and settled on a leaf. A ladybird tucked herself under a petal. Even the butterflies stopped fluttering and folded their wings.

Sunny's head drooped a little lower. His golden petals curled in like a cosy blanket. And then — soft and slow — Sunny gave a tiny, happy yawn of sleep.

"Shhh," whispered Asha. "Good night, Sunny."

She tiptoed back into the house, brushed her teeth, and climbed into her own bed. Outside her window, the moon rose over the garden, and the stars twinkled down on one sleepy sunflower.

The next morning, Asha ran into the garden first thing. And there was Sunny, stretching up tall and smiling brighter than ever!

"Good morning, sun!" whispered Sunny. And Asha was quite sure that Sunny was smiling especially at her.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Everyone needs rest — even the brightest, sunniest flowers — and a kind song helps us all sleep well.*
