# The Bat and the Weasels

*A timeless Aesop's fable for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a quiet old barn, lived a little bat named Boris.

Boris was not quite a bird, and not quite a mouse. He had soft furry ears like a mouse, and wide wings like a bird. He slept upside down all day, and fluttered about at night, eating tiny moths.

One evening, Boris fluttered out of the barn to look for supper. But who should be waiting in the dark but Wilma the weasel!

Wilma was quick and sharp-eyed, and she loved to chase little creatures.

"Got you!" said Wilma, pouncing. "You are a mouse, and mice are my supper!"

"Oh no, please!" squeaked Boris. "I am not a mouse at all! Look at my wings! Mice do not have wings. I am a bird, and birds are not weasel supper!"

Wilma looked at Boris's big wings. She scratched her head.

"Hmm," said Wilma. "You do have wings. And mice certainly do not have wings. Very well, little bird — off you go."

And she let him go.

Boris fluttered away, very pleased with his quick thinking. "My wings saved me!" he said to himself.

But the very next evening, as Boris fluttered out again, who should be waiting in the dark but Wesley the weasel, Wilma's brother!

"Got you!" said Wesley, pouncing. "You are a bird, and birds are my supper!"

"Oh no, please!" squeaked Boris. "I am not a bird at all! Look at my ears! Birds do not have soft furry ears like these. I am a mouse, and mice are not weasel supper!"

Wesley looked at Boris's soft furry ears. He scratched his head.

"Hmm," said Wesley. "You do have furry ears. And birds certainly do not have furry ears. Very well, little mouse — off you go."

And he let him go.

Boris fluttered home to his barn, safe and sound. He hung upside down, tucked his wings around himself, and smiled a sleepy little smile.

"I am a little bit of both," said Boris, "and that is just right. My wings saved me once, and my ears saved me once. Being a bat is the best thing of all."

And he drifted off to sleep, dreaming of moths and moonlight.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A quick, kind thinker can find a gentle way out of trouble.*
