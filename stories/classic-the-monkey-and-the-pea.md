# The Monkey and the Pea

*A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a green forest full of tall trees and whispering leaves, there lived a little monkey named Golu.

Golu had bright eyes, a curly tail, and a very busy mind. He loved to run, to jump, and to do everything as fast as he could.

One sunny morning, Golu's grandmother gave him a little cloth pocket full of sweet green peas.

"These peas are for you, my dear," said his grandmother, with a warm smile. "Take your time, and enjoy them one by one."

"Thank you, Grandmother!" cried Golu, hopping up and down with excitement. He tucked the pocket under his arm and ran off into the forest.

As he swung from one branch to another, plip! One little pea slipped out of his pocket and fell to the soft ground below.

He bent down quickly to pick it up. But as he bent, his pocket tipped over — and plip, plip, plip! All the other peas tumbled out too!

"Oh no, oh no, oh no!" cried Golu, holding his empty pocket. "My peas are everywhere! I dropped one pea, and then I lost them all!"

Just then, his friend Rikki the squirrel came scampering down from her tree. She had a fluffy tail and kind, bright eyes.

"What is the matter, Golu?" asked Rikki, sitting down beside him.

"I was in such a hurry," sniffed Golu, "that I dropped one pea, and then I dropped them all. Now my peas are everywhere, and I do not know where to begin."

Rikki smiled gently. "You do not need to pick them all up at once," she said. "Just slow down. Take your time. Pick up one pea, and then the next pea, and then the next."

Golu took a deep breath. In through his nose, and out through his mouth. He felt his busy mind grow quiet, like a still pond.

"One pea," he said, picking up the pea by his foot.

Riya the little blue bird fluttered down to watch them.

Slowly, one by one, Golu gathered his peas back into his pocket. It took a little while, but it was not hard at all. And as he worked, he began to feel calm and happy.

When Golu had gathered almost all his peas, he counted them carefully. There were still plenty left in his pocket — more than enough for one little monkey.

But Golu looked at his friends, and he had a wonderful idea.

"Would you like to share my peas?" he asked Rikki and Riya. "I have plenty, and sharing makes them taste even sweeter."

"Thank you, Golu!" chirped Riya, fluttering her wings.

So the three friends sat together on the mossy rock, under the tall green trees, and shared the sweet green peas, one by one. They laughed and chatted, and nobody hurried at all.

"Thank you for helping me slow down," said Golu, with a happy smile. "When I rush, I drop everything. When I take my time, I can enjoy everything."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: When we slow down and take our time, we do things better and enjoy them more.*
