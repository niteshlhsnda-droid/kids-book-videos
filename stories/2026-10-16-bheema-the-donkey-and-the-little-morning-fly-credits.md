# Credits — Bheema the Donkey and the Little Morning Fly (2026-10-16)

- **Adapted from:** "Bheema, the Sleepyhead" (English translation of the Hindi original 'भीमा गधा')
- **Original title:** *Bheema, the Sleepyhead*
- **Author:** Kiran Kasturia
- **Illustrator:** none reused — all illustrations generated fresh for this story (original illustrations by Shweta Mohapatra, CC BY 4.0, not reused)
- **Translator:** Rajesh Khar (English translation, © Pratham Books, 2012)
- **Source URL:** https://freekidsbooks.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Bheema-the-Sleepy-Head-FKB-Stories.pdf (verified CC BY 4.0 attribution page; book first published on StoryWeaver by Pratham Books, storyweaver.org.in)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 (Pratham Books, 2012 — story and illustrations released under CC BY 4.0; reuse permitted with attribution, even commercially)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in warm, child-friendly language for ages 4–7, expanded from the short picture-book text to about 430 words with a gentle bedtime closing. The folk-plot is kept (friends try to wake Bheema — cow, dog, rooster, crow — and a tiny fly succeeds with a tickle-sneeze); all dialogue is newly written. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
