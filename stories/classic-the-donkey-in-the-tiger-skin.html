The Donkey in the Tiger Skin

A clever Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

At the edge of a sleepy village, there lived a donkey named Dudu.

Dudu had soft gray fur, long floppy ears, and a very loud voice. "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!" he sang every morning, and the whole village woke up smiling.

One day, Dudu was trotting past the old barn when he saw something strange lying in the straw — a big, stripy tiger skin!

"Oh!" said Dudu. "I wonder what I would look like wearing this?"

He pulled the tiger skin over his back. He tucked his long ears underneath. He looked at himself in the pond — and gasped.

"ROAR!" he tried. It came out a little squeaky, but in the striped skin, he looked very, very tigerish.

That evening, Dudu padded softly into the meadow where the animals grazed.

The rabbits froze. The goats gulped. The sheep huddled together.

"A t-t-tiger!" squeaked a little rabbit.

Dudu felt very important. He ate the sweetest grass. He drank first from the cool stream. Nobody pushed past him. Nobody said, "Move, Dudu!"

For three whole days, Dudu the "tiger" got the best of everything.

But on the fourth night, the moon rose big and round and silver, and Dudu's heart filled up with song.

He lifted his nose to the moon and sang his favorite song — "HEE-HAW! HEE-HAW! HEE-HAW!"

The rabbits peeked out. The goats giggled. The sheep burst out laughing.

"That is no tiger!" chuckled the old goat. "That is Dudu! No tiger ever sang hee-haw to the moon!"

Dudu's cheeks turned pink under the tiger skin. He wriggled out of it — pop went one ear, pop went the other! — and stood there, just Dudu, soft and gray and a little bit silly.

"I am sorry I tricked you," he said. "I only wanted the sweetest grass."

The old goat smiled and pushed the sweetest grass toward him. "You never needed stripes, Dudu. Your morning song is the sweetest thing in this meadow."

And from that day on, Dudu sang his hee-haw song every morning louder and happier than ever — and the animals sang along, each in their own voice.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: You don't need to pretend to be someone else — being yourself is the sweetest song.
