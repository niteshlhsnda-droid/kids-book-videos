# Anaya and the Midnight Library

*A gentle original tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Anaya loved books more than anything in the world. She had books about cats and books about stars, books about cakes and books about trains.

But the book she loved most was the one her grandfather read to her every night — a big blue book of bedtime stories, with gold stars on the cover.

One night, Anaya could not sleep. She heard a soft, silvery sound — ting, ting, ting — like tiny bells. She tiptoed out of bed and followed the sound down the stairs.

And there, in the living room, was the most wonderful thing she had ever seen. The big blue book was glowing! Its pages were fluttering gently, all by themselves, and out of the pages floated a tiny librarian no taller than a pencil.

"Hello, Anaya," said the little librarian, tipping her tiny hat. "I am Lila, keeper of the Midnight Library. Once a year, on the quietest night, the library opens — and you are invited."

Before Anaya could say a word, the room stretched and shimmered, and suddenly she was standing in a grand library with shelves that went up, up, up to the stars. The books on the shelves were whispering to each other in soft, sleepy voices.

"Shhh, shhh," whispered the books. "We are telling each other our stories before bedtime."

Lila led Anaya to a cosy corner with a velvet chair and a lamp shaped like a moon. "Every book here is looking for a reader," said Lila. "Would you like to read one before you sleep?"

Anaya chose a small green book called "The Little Bear Who Found the Moon." She read it aloud, softly, while Lila listened and the whispering books listened too.

When the story was over, all the books sighed happily. "Thank you, Anaya," they whispered. "A story read aloud is a story that dreams well."

Lila smiled. "Now it is time for you to sleep, little reader. But you may come back — in your dreams, the library is always open."

Ting, ting, ting went the little bells. Anaya blinked — and she was back in her own bed, tucked under her blanket, with the big blue book glowing softly on her bedside table.

She closed her eyes, and in her dreams she walked through the Midnight Library once more, where all the books whispered, "Shhh, shhh… sleep well, little reader."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A story shared at bedtime is a gift — for the reader, and for everyone who listens.*
