Mimi the Mouse and the Moonlight Picnic

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

In a cosy little house at the edge of the wood, behind the kitchen wall, there lived a tiny mouse named Mimi.

Mimi loved three things more than anything in the world: the moon, her friends, and picnics.

One evening, as the round silver moon rose over the wood, Mimi had a wonderful idea.

"I am going to have a moonlight picnic!" she said. "A picnic under the stars, just for my friends!"

First, Mimi packed a little basket. She put in a crumb of cheese. She put in three fat blackberries. She put in a tiny crust of bread. And she put in a thimbleful of sweet honey.

Then she wrote three tiny invitations on three tiny leaves, and she delivered them herself.

"Dear Flick," read the first one, "please come to my moonlight picnic tonight, under the old oak tree. Love, Mimi."

"Dear Dottie," read the second one, "please come to my moonlight picnic tonight, under the old oak tree. Love, Mimi."

"Dear Ollie," read the third one, "please come to my moonlight picnic tonight, under the old oak tree. Love, Mimi."

When the moon was high and round, Mimi spread a little red cloth under the old oak tree. She set out the cheese and the blackberries and the bread and the honey. She lit a tiny candle in a jam jar, and it glowed like a little star.

Then her friends arrived!

Flick the firefly came first, glowing and twinkling. "What a beautiful picnic!" he said, lighting up the whole table with his glow.

Dottie the dormouse came next, yawning a big yawn. "I almost fell asleep," she said, "but I would not miss your picnic for the whole world!"

Ollie the owl came last, swooping down ever so softly. "Hoo-hoo!" he said. "What a lovely night for a picnic!"

They all sat on the little red cloth and ate the cheese and the blackberries and the bread and the honey. It was the tastiest picnic ever!

Then Flick the firefly told a story about the time he lit up a whole field of fireflies. Dottie the dormouse told a story about the softest bed she had ever slept in. And Ollie the owl told a story about the stars, and how each one has a name.

Mimi listened and smiled. The moon shone down, round and silver. The candle glowed in its jam jar. And the four friends sat together, happy as could be.

At last, Dottie the dormouse yawned the biggest yawn of all. "I think it is time for bed," she said sleepily.

"Me too," said Flick the firefly, his glow growing soft and sleepy.

"Me too," said Ollie the owl, blinking his big round eyes.

Mimi packed up her little basket and blew out the tiny candle. "Thank you for coming to my moonlight picnic," she said. "It was the very best picnic ever."

And as they all tiptoed home under the moonlight, Mimi whispered to the moon, "Thank you for shining on our picnic."

And the moon seemed to shine just a little bit brighter, as if it were smiling back.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: The best picnics are the ones you share with friends.
