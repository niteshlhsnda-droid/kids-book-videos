Teddy's Midnight Picnic

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Buttons the bear cub woke up at midnight with moonlight streaming through his window. He felt hungry — his tummy rumbled like a tiny drum.

Then he heard a small voice. "I am hungry too," it squeaked. It was Teddy, his stuffed bear — and Teddy's tummy rumbled just like Buttons's!

"What should we do?" Buttons whispered. He felt a flutter of worry. "It is the middle of the night."

"Let us have a picnic," said Teddy. "A tiny midnight picnic, right here."

Buttons giggled. A midnight picnic sounded wonderful! He tiptoed to the kitchen and packed a small basket — two oat cakes and a cup of warm moon-milk. (Moon-milk is just warm milk, but it tastes like magic at midnight.)

Back in bed, Buttons spread a napkin, set out the oat cakes, and poured the moon-milk into two tiny cups — one for him, and one for Teddy.

"Nibble, nibble, sleepy time," sang Buttons.

They nibbled the oat cakes, crumb by crumb, and sipped the warm moon-milk, sip by sip. Buttons felt his tummy growing full and happy, and Teddy looked full and happy too.

"Nibble, nibble, sleepy time," sang Buttons again.

They finished the last crumbs, and Buttons packed the basket away. He wiped Teddy's face with the napkin. Teddy's button eyes looked drowsy now.

Buttons crawled under his blanket and hugged Teddy close. Teddy smelled like oat cakes and love, and Buttons felt warm all over.

"Nibble, nibble, sleepy time," sang Buttons one last time.

His voice was very quiet now, and his eyes felt heavy. The moon watched over their picnic spot. Everything was still and peaceful.

Buttons's breathing grew slow and deep, and Teddy's head rested on his paw. They had shared a secret picnic — now they could share secret dreams.

Sleep now, little bear. Sleep now, little teddy. Full tummies and happy hearts make the sweetest dreams.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A small, gentle snack and a good friend can chase midnight hunger away.
