The Toothbrush Boat

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Otis the otter did not like brushing his teeth. It was boring, it took too long, and every night his lip stuck out in a grump when his mother handed him the toothbrush.

But tonight, something was different. His toothbrush glowed faintly blue, and when Otis picked it up, it felt warm. A tiny voice squeaked, "All aboard!"

Surprise jolted Otis. "Who said that?" he asked.

"I did!" said the toothbrush. "I am Captain Brush. Tonight we sail the Tooth Islands. Climb aboard!"

Otis giggled. A talking toothbrush! He squeezed toothpaste onto it, and the paste foamed up like sea spray. Otis opened his mouth wide, and the little boat sailed in.

"Brush, brush, sail away, clean the islands every day!" sang Captain Brush.

The boat sailed to Molar Mountain, a big tooth at the back. Brush-brush went the bristles as they scrubbed the mountain clean. Otis felt the tickle and giggled with his mouth full of foam.

"Brush, brush, sail away, clean the islands every day!" sang Captain Brush again.

Next they sailed to Canine Cove, a pointy tooth by the side. Swish-swish went the bristles as they polished the cove until it shone. Otis's grumpiness was melting away — this was actually fun.

"Brush, brush, sail away, clean the islands every day!" sang Captain Brush one last time.

The voyage lasted two whole minutes. Captain Brush checked every island, and every tooth sparkled. "Voyage complete!" he announced. "The Tooth Islands are clean!"

Otis rinsed his mouth. His teeth felt smooth and fresh, and he smiled at the mirror — his smile shone bright.

He set Captain Brush in the cup. "Same voyage tomorrow?" asked the captain.

"Same voyage tomorrow," Otis promised, his chest swelling with pride. Brushing was not boring at all. It was an adventure.

He climbed into bed with a sparkly smile, his teeth clean and his heart happy. His eyes grew heavy.

Sleep now, little otter. Your Tooth Islands are clean and safe. Dream of sailing seas of minty foam.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: With a little imagination, everyday jobs turn into grand adventures.
