Tenali Rama Counts the Crows

A clever Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in the kingdom of Vijayanagara, there lived a clever man named Tenali Rama.

Tenali Rama made the king laugh every day with his quick answers. King Krishna loved his jokes more than gold.

One sunny morning, the king and Tenali Rama were walking in the palace garden. Crows were everywhere — cawing on the rooftops, hopping on the walls, and pecking at crumbs on the path.

"Caw! Caw! Caw!" went the crows.

The king stopped and smiled a tricky smile.

"Tenali Rama," he said, "you are so clever. Tell me — how many crows are there in my whole kingdom?"

All the courtiers held their breath. What a question! Who could ever count all the crows?

One courtier scratched his head. Another counted on his fingers and gave up. A third just stared at the sky and said nothing at all.

Tenali Rama did not look worried. He looked up at the crows and smiled, as if they were old friends sharing a secret.

Tenali Rama did not look worried at all. He looked up at the sky and counted on his fingers, just as if he were doing a very hard sum.

"One, two, three…" he murmured. Then he nodded and said, "Your Majesty, there are ninety-nine thousand, four hundred and seventy-three crows in your kingdom."

"Ninety-nine thousand, four hundred and seventy-three!" cried the king. "Are you sure? How do you know?"

"Oh, I am quite sure," said Tenali Rama, folding his arms. "I counted them all this morning."

"But what if someone counts them tomorrow and finds more?" asked the king, raising one eyebrow.

"Then, Your Majesty," said Tenali Rama with a bow, "it will mean some crows have come to visit their cousins in our kingdom."

"And what if someone finds fewer?" asked the king.

"Then it will mean some of our crows have gone to visit their cousins in another kingdom," said Tenali Rama. "Crows love visiting their cousins, you know."

The king threw back his head and laughed and laughed. "Ha! Ha! Ha!" The courtiers laughed too. Even the crows seemed to laugh — "Caw! Caw! Caw!"

"Tenali Rama," said the king, wiping tears of laughter from his eyes, "you have answered a question that has no answer. That is the cleverest answer of all."

And the king gave Tenali Rama a bag of gold coins and a big bowl of his favourite sweets.

That night, as Tenali Rama walked home under the stars, a little crow landed on his shoulder.

"Caw!" it said, as if to say thank you for the kind answer.

Tenali Rama smiled. "You are welcome, little friend," he said. "Now go home to your cousins — wherever they may be."

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A clever answer is kind, quick, and makes everyone smile.
