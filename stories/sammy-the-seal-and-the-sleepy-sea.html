Sammy the Seal and the Sleepy Sea

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Far away, where the sea meets the sky, there lived a little gray seal named Sammy.

Sammy loved two things: splashing — SPLOSH! — and singing. Every evening, as the sun turned the waves gold, Sammy would float on his back and hum a soft, round song. Hmm-hmm-hmm.

All the sea babies loved Sammy's song. Pip the puffin chick would tuck his head under his wing. The baby turtles would close their eyes. Even the waves seemed to grow quieter — shush... shush... — to listen.

One evening, the wind came howling over the sea. WHOOSH! It whipped the waves into big, grumpy humps. The sea was NOT sleepy at all. It tossed and turned and splashed — crash! crash! crash!

"Oh no!" cried Pip the puffin chick, bouncing on a wave. "The sea is too wild to sleep!"

The baby turtles clung to a rock. "We can't close our eyes!" they squeaked. "It's too bumpy!"

Sammy bobbed up on a wave, his whiskers dripping. "Don't worry, little ones," he said. "The sea just needs a lullaby. A BIG one."

Sammy took a deep breath and began to hum — hmm-hmm-hmm — louder than ever before. He clapped his flippers — pat, pat, pat — keeping time with the waves.

"Come on, everybody!" he called. "Hum with me! Hmm-hmm-hmm!"

Pip the puffin chick hummed — hmm! The baby turtles hummed — hmm-hmm! A sleepy old whale floated by and joined in with a deep, low HMMM that rumbled through the water like a warm blanket.

Hmm-hmm-hmm went Sammy. Hmm-hmm-hmm went the puffin and the turtles. HMMMM went the whale.

And slowly — so slowly — the wild waves began to soften. The big grumpy humps grew smaller... and smaller... and smaller...

The wind heard the lullaby and grew curious. It stopped howling and listened. Then it began to hum too — a soft, sighing whoo-oo-oo — rocking the waves gently, like a mother rocking a cradle.

Shush... shush... went the sea. Shush... shush...

One by one, the sea babies drifted off to sleep. Pip tucked his head under his wing. The baby turtles floated like tiny boats. Even the whale closed one great, gentle eye.

Sammy floated on his back under the twinkling stars, humming softer... and softer... and softer...

Hmm... hmm... hmm...

And the whole sleepy sea dreamed sweet, salty dreams until morning.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A gentle song can calm even the wildest waves — kindness soothes everything.
