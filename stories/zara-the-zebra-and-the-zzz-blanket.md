# Zara the Zebra and the Zzz Blanket

*A gentle bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a quiet green valley, where the grass was soft and the stars came out one by one, lived a little zebra named Zara.

Zara had black-and-white stripes, soft ears, and sleepy brown eyes. But tonight, her sleepy brown eyes were wide open.

"I cannot sleep," said Zara with a big yawn. "The night feels too noisy and too bright."

The crickets were chirping — chirp, chirp, chirp. The wind was rustling through the tall grass — shhh, shhh, shhh. And the moon was shining so brightly that Zara could see her own shadow on the grass.

Zara tossed and turned. She counted her stripes. Still, sleep would not come.

"Grandma," she called softly, "the night will not let me sleep."

Just then, Grandma Zebra came padding over, carrying something soft in her mouth. It was a blanket — the softest blanket in all the valley, stitched all over with little yellow stars.

"This is your Zzz Blanket," said Grandma Zebra, laying it gently over Zara. "I stitched it myself, one star at a time, when your mama was small. And every star knows a tiny lullaby."

Zara snuggled under the blanket. It felt soft and warm, like a hug made of cotton.

"Close your eyes, little one," said Grandma Zebra, "and listen to the stars."

Zara closed her eyes. And guess what? The first little star began to hum — a tiny, sleepy tune, soft as a sigh.

"Hush-a-bye, little zebra, the night is soft and slow. The moon is watching over you, wherever you go."

Then the second star hummed its tune. Then the third. Each star sang a little softer than the one before, like a row of sleepy candles going out, one by one.

Grandma Zebra showed Zara her gentle bedtime routine.

First, Zara sipped a small cup of warm milk, slow and sleepy.

Then she washed her face and her stripy hooves in cool, clear water.

Then she took three slow, sleepy breaths — in through her nose, and out through her mouth. Breathe in, breathe out, breathe in, breathe out.

"Now," whispered Grandma Zebra, "pull your Zzz Blanket up to your chin, and let the stars sing you to sleep."

Zara pulled the blanket up to her chin. The stars hummed their tiny lullabies. The crickets chirped, but now their chirping sounded like a soft, sleepy song. The wind rustled, but now it sounded like a gentle hush.

Zara's ears went droopy. Her legs went floppy. Her sleepy brown eyes closed, and stayed closed.

"Hush-a-bye, little zebra," hummed the last little star, softer than a whisper. "The night is soft and slow."

And Zara the zebra drifted off to sleep, warm and cosy under her Zzz Blanket, dreaming of soft grass and sweet stars and Grandma Zebra's loving smile.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A gentle bedtime routine helps sleep come softly.*
