# Cora the Little Crab and the Gentle Wave

In a little rock pool by the sea lived a tiny crab named Cora. She was small and soft-orange, with big round eyes and little clicking claws. She loved her rock pool — the pink shells, the tickly seaweed, the warm sun on the rocks.

But Cora was afraid of the big waves. Beyond the rocks, the sea rolled and rolled, and the waves went crash, crash, crash. "Too big, too loud," Cora would whisper, tucking herself under a shell. "I will stay right here, where it is safe."

One evening, as the sky turned peachy-pink, the tide went out. Her pool grew low and still. Then — tap, tap, tap — a tiny starfish washed up onto the wet sand. "Oh!" he cried. "I am lost! The sea has gone away!"

Cora peeked out from under her shell. The little starfish was small, orange as a sunset, with five tiny arms. "I am Sandy," he said in a wobbly voice. "I need the sea, or I will dry up in the sun." She was afraid of the waves — but Sandy needed help.

"Do not worry," said Cora, clicking her claws bravely. She tucked a wet shell over Sandy to keep him cool and scooped water over him with a little leaf. "I will stay with you," she said. "We will wait for the sea together."

But the sun sank lower, and the sand grew dry. Sandy grew quiet. Then Cora heard it — a faraway rumble. A big wave was coming over the rocks. "Oh no," whispered Cora, her claws trembling. "It will wash us both away!"

The wave rolled in — and it was not a crash at all. It was a soft, slow, gentle wave, curling over the rocks like a kind hand. It lifted Cora up, light as a feather, and Sandy too. "Hold on!" cried Cora, holding Sandy's tiny arm tight.

The gentle wave carried them over the rocks and out into the blue water, rocking them gently. Cora opened one eye... then the other. The sea was warm and blue, and little fish darted past like silver ribbons. "We are swimming!" laughed Sandy. "We are flying!"

The wave set them down softly by a bed of oysters, where Pearl the little oyster waited. "Sandy!" cried Pearl. "You came home!" Sandy wriggled happily. "Cora brought me," he said. "She was brave, even though she was scared." "Then the sea is your friend too, Cora," smiled Pearl.

Cora looked back at her rock pool, twinkling in the moonlight, then out at the wide, gentle sea. She was still a tiny crab, but she was not afraid anymore. The waves were not too big — they were just the sea, saying hello. Cora waved a little claw at the moon.

Sleep now, little Cora.

The end. 🌙
