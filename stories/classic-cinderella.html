Cinderella

A timeless fairy tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 5 minutes.

Once upon a time, there lived a kind girl named Cinderella.

She lived with her stepmother and two stepsisters, who were not kind at all. They made her do all the sweeping and scrubbing, and at night she slept by the warm fireplace, among the cinders. That is how she got her name — Cinderella.

But Cinderella stayed kind. She sang while she swept. She hummed while she scrubbed. The little birds were her friends, and they sang along.

One day, a letter came from the palace. The prince was holding a grand ball, and every girl in the land was invited!

Cinderella's stepsisters squealed with joy. "New dresses! New shoes!" They twirled around the room.

"May I go too?" asked Cinderella softly.

Her stepmother laughed. "You? With no dress and no shoes? Someone must stay home and clean."

On the night of the ball, Cinderella sat by the fireplace, and a tear rolled down her cheek.

Just then — POOF! — a swirl of sparkles filled the room, and there stood her fairy godmother!

"Do not cry, my dear," she said with a warm smile. "You shall go to the ball!"

With a wave of her wand — swish! — a pumpkin became a golden carriage. Four little mice became four white horses. And Cinderella's old dress became a gown of shimmering blue, with two sparkling glass slippers.

"But remember," said the fairy godmother, "the magic ends at midnight. Be home before the clock strikes twelve!"

At the ball, the prince saw Cinderella and his eyes went wide. "Will you dance with me?" he asked.

They danced all evening, twirl and swirl, and Cinderella was the happiest she had ever been.

Then — BONG! — the clock began to strike twelve!

Cinderella ran down the palace steps. Trip! One glass slipper slipped off her foot. But she could not stop — she ran all the way home, just as the magic faded.

The next day, the prince searched the whole land for the girl whose foot fit the glass slipper. At last, he came to Cinderella's house.

The slipper fit perfectly!

Cinderella was kind to everyone, always. And she lived happily ever after.

Goodnight, Cinderella.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A kind heart shines brighter than the finest gown.
