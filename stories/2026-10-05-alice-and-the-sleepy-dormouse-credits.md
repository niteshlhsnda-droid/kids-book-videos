# Credits — "Alice and the Sleepy Dormouse"

- **Original title:** *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, Chapter VII — "A Mad Tea-Party"
- **Author:** Lewis Carroll (Charles Lutwidge Dodgson) — first published 1865
- **Illustrator:** none (all illustrations generated fresh for this adaptation)

- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 (~420 words). The mad tea party episode is distilled to its sleepiest thread: Alice, the Hatter, the March Hare, and the Dormouse who naps through everything. Where the original treats the Dormouse as a joke, this telling shows Alice choosing gentle patience — no shouting, no tickling, just quiet kindness — with a goodnight closing ("Sleep now, little Alice.").
