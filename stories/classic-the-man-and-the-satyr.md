# The Man and the Satyr

*A timeless Aesop tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in a quiet forest lived a kind satyr named Sammy.

Sammy had curly brown hair, two little horns, and furry goat legs that went clip-clop on the forest path. He lived in a cosy hut with a warm fire and a big pot of soup.

One cold winter evening, a traveller came walking down the forest path. His name was Arjun. His coat was thin and his hands were red with cold. Brrr!

"Good evening!" called Sammy from his doorway. "You look frozen, my friend! Come in, come in, and warm yourself by my fire."

"Thank you," said Arjun, with a grateful smile. "You are very kind."

Sammy bustled about, happy to have a guest. He piled more logs on the fire until it crackled and glowed.

But first, Arjun held his hands up to his mouth and blew on them. Whooo! Whooo! Warm breath on cold fingers.

Sammy watched with wide eyes. "Why do you blow on your hands?" he asked.

"To warm them up, of course!" said Arjun, smiling. "My fingers are cold as ice. A good blow warms them."

Sammy nodded slowly. He had never seen a person do that before. It seemed a very clever trick.

Then Sammy ladled out the soup — a thick, steaming soup, smelling delicious. He set a bowl in front of Arjun.

"Careful," said Sammy. "It is very hot!"

Arjun looked at the steaming bowl. He picked up his spoon, blew gently on the hot soup — Whooo! Whooo! — and took a little taste. "Mmm! Perfect!" he said.

Sammy stared. His mouth fell open.

"Wait a moment!" cried Sammy. "First you blew on your hands to make them HOT. Now you blow on your soup to make it COLD. How can one mouth blow both hot and cold? I do not understand it at all!"

Arjun saw Sammy's puzzled face and began to laugh — a warm, friendly laugh, like sunshine.

"Oh, Sammy!" said Arjun. "It is the same warm breath both times! My breath is warm. When it lands on my cold hands, my hands feel warmer. When it blows across the hot soup, it pushes the steam away and helps the soup cool down. One breath can do both — warm and cool — depending on what it touches!"

Sammy blew on his own hands — Whooo! — and felt them warm. Then he blew on his soup — Whooo! — and watched the steam drift away.

"Wow!" said Sammy. "Your breath is warm, and the warm breath warms the cold and cools the hot! That is the cleverest thing I have ever heard!"

"Not clever," said Arjun, smiling. "Just the way breath works. And thank you for the soup — it is the best soup I have ever tasted."

Sammy grinned a great big grin. "And thank you for teaching me something new! I thought I knew everything about the forest, but I learned something tonight."

So the two friends ate their soup together until the fire burned low and the stars came out. Arjun stayed the night in Sammy's cosy hut, wrapped in a soft blanket, warm and safe.

"Come again soon!" called Sammy. "Next time, I will blow on my soup too!"

And they stayed the best of friends — the satyr who learned something new, and the traveller who explained it with a smile.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: The same breath can warm and cool — asking questions helps us understand each other.*
