Beauty and the Beast

A timeless fairy tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a small village at the edge of a green forest, lived a kind girl named Belle.

Belle loved two things more than anything in the world: books and roses. Every morning, she sat by her window with a book in her lap, and her garden was full of pink, yellow, and red roses that smelled sweet.

Belle lived with her father, a gentle man who travelled to faraway towns, and he always brought her a gift when he came home.

One cold winter evening, her father was travelling home through the dark forest when snow began to fall, and soon he could not see the road.

Then, through the trees, he saw warm light in the windows of an old castle.

"Thank goodness," he said, and knocked on the big wooden door. The door opened — all by itself!

Inside, a warm fire crackled, and a table was laid with bread and soup. "Hello?" he called. No one answered, but he was so tired and cold that he ate the soup and fell fast asleep by the fire.

In the morning, he walked through the castle garden and saw the most beautiful rosebush he had ever seen, covered in red roses.

"One rose for my Belle," he whispered, and picked a single red rose.

Just then, a soft voice said, "You picked my rose." Her father turned around, and there stood a great Beast, tall and furry, with kind, sad eyes.

"I am sorry," said her father. "My daughter loves roses, and I only wanted one for her."

The Beast looked at him for a long moment. "Stay and be my guest," he said in his low, gentle voice, "and the rose is yours."

So her father stayed. But he missed Belle so much that at last he sent her a message, telling her where he was.

When Belle heard, she packed a small bag and set off at once. "Father!" she cried, running into his arms. "I came to be with you."

Then she turned to the Beast in the doorway and smiled. "Thank you for being kind to my father," she said.

The Beast bowed his big furry head. "You are welcome here," he said softly.

Belle stayed at the castle, and she and the Beast became friends. Every evening, they ate supper together and told each other stories. Every night, before bed, she read aloud to him, and the Beast listened, his eyes soft and bright.

"You are a good friend," the Beast said one evening. "No one has ever been so kind to me."

"And you are a good friend too," said Belle.

Then one morning, something wonderful happened. As the first golden light touched the castle, the Beast began to glow softly, like a star waking up, and when the light faded, a kind young prince stood there, smiling.

"Your kindness has broken the spell," said the prince. "A spell turned me into a Beast, and only a true friend's kindness could set me free."

Belle laughed with joy, and her father laughed too. From that day on, they lived happily together, and every morning, Belle still picked a red rose from the garden.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Kindness is the truest magic.
