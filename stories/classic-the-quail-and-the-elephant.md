# The Quail and the Elephant

*A timeless Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a wide green meadow, beside a tall shady tree, lived a tiny quail named Queenie. Her nest was a soft cup of grass, tucked safely among the roots of the tree. In the nest lay three speckled eggs, warm and snug.

Every morning, Queenie sang a sweet little song to her eggs. "Sleep tight, little ones," she chirped. "Soon you will hatch, and we will peck seeds together in the sun."

One day, a great big elephant named Ella came stomping through the meadow. Ella was so big that her feet went thump, thump, THUMP on the ground. She did not see the tiny nest at all — and CRASH! Her huge foot knocked the nest right over, and the three little eggs rolled away into the grass.

Queenie fluttered down in a fright. "Oh no, oh no!" she cried. "My nest! My eggs!"

But the eggs were not broken. They had rolled softly into a patch of clover, safe and sound. Queenie gathered them up and tucked them back into her nest. Then she looked up at Ella, who had stopped and was looking very sorry indeed.

"Forgive me, little quail," said Ella in her deep, gentle voice. "I am so big, and your nest is so small. I did not mean to knock it over."

Queenie took a deep breath. "I know you did not mean it," she said. "But please, dear Ella, will you watch where you step from now on? Little nests matter too."

"I promise," said Ella, and she bowed her great grey head. "From today, I will tiptoe through the meadow."

And Ella kept her promise. Every day after that, she tiptoed — thump… thump… thump, very softly — around Queenie's tree. She even brought Queenie cool water in her trunk on hot days, and shaded the nest with her big ear when the sun shone too bright.

Soon the three eggs hatched into three fluffy chicks, and they chirped happily in the shade of the great, kind elephant.

Queenie smiled up at her big friend. "Thank you for being careful, Ella," she said. "Big or small, we can all look out for one another."

And from that day on, Ella never forgot to tiptoe — and Queenie never forgot to sing her morning song, now with three tiny voices joining in.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Even the biggest friend can be gentle, and even the smallest voice deserves to be heard.*
