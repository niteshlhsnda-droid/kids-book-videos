Birbal and the Crows

A timeless Akbar-Birbal tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in India, there lived a great king named Akbar.

In his court lived Birbal, the cleverest man in the whole kingdom. Akbar loved to ask Birbal hard questions, and Birbal always had a clever answer ready.

Every morning, Birbal fed the palace crows a handful of grain from the kitchen. The crows knew him well. They would fly down, cawing happily, and eat right out of his hand. So Birbal knew the crows of the city better than anyone.

One fine morning, Akbar was sitting on his balcony, eating sweet, juicy mangoes. All around the palace, black crows were flying and cawing — "Caw! Caw! Caw!"

The king watched the crows and smiled. Then a naughty idea came into his head.

"Birbal," said the king, "tell me — how many crows are there in my city?"

Birbal did not even blink. "Your Majesty," he said, "there are exactly one lakh crows in the city."

"One lakh!" laughed the king. "Exactly one lakh? Are you sure?"

"Quite sure, Your Majesty," said Birbal, bowing low.

"And if I count them," said the king, "and there are more than one lakh?"

"Then, Your Majesty," said Birbal with a twinkle in his eye, "the extra crows must be visitors. They have come from other cities to see your beautiful palace!"

The king laughed again. "And if I count them, and there are fewer than one lakh?"

"Then, Your Majesty," said Birbal, "some of our crows must have gone visiting other cities, to tell their friends about your beautiful palace!"

The king stroked his beard and thought. Then he clapped his hands. "Let us count them!" he cried.

So his servants counted the crows all afternoon — one, two, three — all the way to ninety-nine thousand, nine hundred and ninety-eight!

"There are only 99,998 crows, Your Majesty!" said the servant.

"Ah," said Birbal with a smile, "then two of our crows must have gone to visit the next city, to tell their friends about your beautiful palace!"

The whole court burst out laughing — even the king laughed so hard that mango juice ran down his chin!

"Birbal, Birbal!" cried the king. "You always have an answer! You are the cleverest man in my kingdom!"

Birbal bowed and smiled. And do you know what? From that day on, whenever the king saw a crow, he would say, "Ah! One of my one lakh crows — or perhaps a visitor!"

And the crows would caw back, "Caw! Caw!" as if they were laughing too.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A clever answer can turn any hard question into a happy one.
