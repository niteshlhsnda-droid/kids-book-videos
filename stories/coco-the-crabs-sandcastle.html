Coco the Crab's Sandcastle

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On the warm, golden beach of Coral Bay lived a little crab named Coco.

Coco loved to build. Every morning, she would scuttle sideways across the sand — clickety-click, clickety-click — because crabs never, ever walk straight! — and build the grandest sandcastle you ever saw.

This morning's castle was the biggest and best one yet. It had five tall towers, tiny shell windows, and a flag made from a pink feather. The feather waved in the sea breeze.

Coco's whole face lit up in a big smile as she stepped back to admire it.

All the beach friends came to look. The seagulls cried, "Beautiful!" The little fish in the rock pool splashed, "Splish-splash wonderful!" Even old Tommy the turtle nodded slowly and said, "Very fine indeed."

Coco beamed. She sat beside her castle all afternoon, watching the flag flutter.

But as the sun began to sink, the evening tide came rolling in — shhhh… shhhh… Can you whisper shhhh, too?

The waves reached the first tower. Then the second. Then, softly, the whole grand castle melted back into the smooth sand.

Coco's shoulders drooped, and her little claws went still. "My castle," she whispered. "It's gone."

Old Tommy the turtle paddled over and sat beside her. "Coco," he said gently, "where did the castle go?"

"Into the sea," sniffed Coco.

"And where did all the happy making go?" asked Tommy. "The towers, the shells, the flag — who got to enjoy all of that?"

Coco thought. She remembered the proud feeling. The clicking sand. The pink feather waving. The smiles of all her friends.

"Me," she said at last, and a small smile came back to her face. "Building it was the happy part — even if I can't keep it."

That night, Coco curled up in her cozy sandy corner, listened to the soft waves — shhhh… shhhh… — and drifted off into sleepy, sandy dreams.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Building it was the happy part — even if you can't keep it.
