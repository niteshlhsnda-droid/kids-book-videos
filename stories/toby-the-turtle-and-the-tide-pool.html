Toby the Turtle and the Tide Pool

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On a sunny beach where the waves went shush-shush, there lived a little green turtle named Toby.

Toby was slow. Very, very slow. While the crabs scuttled — skitter-skat! — and the seagulls swooped — whoosh! — Toby plodded along — plod... plod... plod.

"Hurry up, Toby!" laughed Sandy the crab. "The tide will come in before you take your next step!"

Toby just smiled his slow turtle smile. "I like slow," he said. "Slow lets me see everything."

One morning, Toby was plodding along the rocks when he heard tiny voices — "Help! Help!"

In a little tide pool, the water was getting lower and lower. The morning sun was hot, and poor Marina the minnow, Shelly the shrimp, and baby Ollie the octopus were splashing in the last bit of water.

"The tide is going out!" cried Marina. "Soon our pool will be dry!"

"Don't worry," said Toby. "I will help you. But first —" he looked around slowly, "— we need shade."

Toby plodded — plod, plod, plod — to a big piece of driftwood. It was far too heavy for little Marina or Shelly. But Toby tucked his head down, pushed with his strong turtle legs — heave! — and dragged it all the way back. Plod... plod... plod.

He laid the driftwood across the tide pool like a little roof. Cool shade fell over the water.

"Hurray!" cheered the tide-pool friends. "The sun can't dry us up now!"

But Toby wasn't finished. He plodded — plod, plod, plod — to the seaweed patch and gathered big, wet ribbons of seaweed. He draped them over the driftwood. Drip, drip, drip — cool drops fell into the pool.

Then Toby did the slowest, kindest thing of all. He sat beside the pool and fanned the water with his wide flippers — swish, swish — keeping it cool until the tide came back.

It took a long, long time. The sun climbed high. But Toby stayed — plod-slow and steady.

At last — shush-shush! — the waves rolled back in. Cool seawater splashed over the rocks and filled the tide pool to the brim!

Marina the minnow did a happy flip. Shelly the shrimp danced sideways. Baby Ollie the octopus waved all eight arms at once!

"Toby! You saved us!" they cheered. "And you're not slow — you're just the right speed for helping!"

Sandy the crab scuttled over, quite out of breath. "Toby," she said softly, "I'm sorry I laughed at slow. Slow saved the day."

Toby smiled his slow turtle smile. "Slow lets me see who needs me," he said.

And that evening, as the sun melted into the sea, all the tide-pool friends floated in the cool water and watched the stars come out — slow and beautiful, one by one.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Slow is not weak — taking your time lets you see who needs your help.
