Ollie the Octopus and the Underwater Lullaby

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Deep under the sea, in a soft coral reef, lived a little octopus named Ollie.

Ollie had eight wiggly arms and one very sleepy problem.

Every night, when it was time to sleep, the reef was too noisy!

The crabs clicked. The fish swished. The waves whooshed and splashed above.

"How can anyone sleep in all this noise?" Ollie sighed.

He tucked himself into his cosy shell bed and closed his eyes. But click, click went the crabs. Swish, swish went the fish.

Ollie opened his eyes wide. "I need a quiet song," he said. "A soft, sleepy song."

So Ollie swam out into the blue night water to find soft sounds.

First, he found a whispering shell lying on the sand. Ollie held it close and listened.

"Shhh... shhh..." whispered the shell, soft as a secret.

"What a lovely sound," said Ollie. He tucked the shell gently under one arm.

Next, he met a little seahorse, humming as she swayed in the sea grass.

"Hmm... hmm..." hummed the seahorse, slow and sweet.

"What a lovely sound," said Ollie. He held the humming seahorse close with another arm.

Then, near the rocks, he found a sleepy old turtle. The turtle blew slow, round bubbles that floated up like tiny moons.

"Blub... blub..." went the bubbles, slow and calm.

"What a lovely sound," said Ollie. He gathered the bubbles softly with a third arm.

Now Ollie had his soft sounds. On the way home, a sleepy starfish waved one slow arm. "Sing softly," it whispered. Ollie nodded and sang even softer. The moonlight danced on the water above, silver and still.

He swam back to his shell bed, curled up his eight wiggly arms, and began to sing.

"Shhh... hmm... blub..." sang Ollie, soft and slow.

The whispering shell whispered. The humming seahorse hummed. The sleepy turtle's bubbles rose like tiny moons.

The little song floated through the reef, gentle as a wave.

The crabs stopped clicking. The fish stopped swishing. The waves above seemed to hush and listen.

One by one, the whole reef drifted off to sleep.

And Ollie, the little octopus, smiled in his shell bed. His eyes closed. His arms went still.

The lullaby had worked — and it had sung Ollie to sleep too.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A soft song and a kind heart can bring quiet to everyone.
