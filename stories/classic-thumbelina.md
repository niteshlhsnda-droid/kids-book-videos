# Thumbelina

*A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Once upon a time, there was a kind woman who lived in a little cottage with flowers in every window.

She longed for a tiny child of her very own. One day, a fairy gave her a curious striped seed. The woman planted it in a flowerpot, watered it, and waited.

Pop! Up grew a beautiful tulip, pink and gold. And inside the tulip, fast asleep on a soft petal, lay the tiniest little girl you ever saw — no bigger than your thumb!

"Thumbelina!" laughed the woman, and that became her name.

Thumbelina slept in a walnut shell, sailed in a tulip petal boat, and sang songs so sweet that all the birds stopped to listen.

But one night, while Thumbelina slept, a great big toad hopped through the window. "A wife for my son!" croaked the toad, and she carried Thumbelina away to a lily pad in the middle of a pond.

"Oh dear," whispered Thumbelina. "I do not want to live in a muddy pond."

The kind fishes heard her and nibbled the lily stem, so the leaf floated away down the stream, carrying Thumbelina with it. A beautiful white butterfly tied his silken thread to the leaf and pulled it along like a little boat.

"Thank you," said Thumbelina, and she sang him a song.

But soon a clumsy beetle buzzed down, grabbed Thumbelina, and carried her up into a tree. "What a funny little creature!" buzzed the beetles. "She only has two legs!"

Thumbelina felt sad. She slipped away into the forest, where she lived all summer among the flowers, drinking morning dew and sleeping in soft clover.

When winter came, cold and white, Thumbelina shivered. She knocked on the door of a field mouse, who took her in, gave her warm soup, and sang her to sleep.

"You must marry my neighbour, the mole," said the field mouse one day. "He has a warm house underground."

But the mole never came out into the sunshine, and Thumbelina loved the sun. On their last walk through the mole's tunnels, Thumbelina found a poor, cold swallow lying still on the ground.

She tucked him into a blanket of dry leaves and sang to him every day. Slowly, the swallow opened his eyes. "Thank you, little friend," he whispered. "When spring comes, I will fly again — and I will take you with me."

When spring came at last, the swallow spread his wings. "Climb onto my back!" he said. And up, up, up they flew, over fields and rivers, to a land where flowers bloomed all year long.

There, in a white blossom, stood a tiny prince with golden wings. "Who are you?" he asked, smiling.

"I am Thumbelina," she said.

"Then you are the princess of the flowers," said the prince. "Will you stay and sing with us?"

Thumbelina smiled the biggest smile of her life. "Yes," she said. "Yes, I will."

And from that day on, Thumbelina lived among the flowers, singing her sweet songs, with the swallow visiting every spring.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Kindness in hard times comes back to you — and the place where you belong is the place where you are loved for who you are.*
