# Tenali Rama and the Palmist

*A clever Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In the grand kingdom of Vijayanagara lived King Krishnadeva Raya, and his wittiest minister, Tenali Rama, who could make the whole court laugh with one clever sentence.

One day, a famous palmist arrived at the palace, wearing a tall turban and carrying a shiny crystal ball.

"O King!" he announced in a deep, gloomy voice. "I have read the stars, and I must warn you — your birthday feast next week will bring you very, very bad luck!"

The king's smile faded. The queen looked worried. The whole court began to whisper and fret.

"Oh dear," sighed the king. "Should we cancel the feast? I was so looking forward to the mango pudding!"

Tenali Rama stepped forward with a twinkle in his eye.

"Respected palmist," he said politely, "you can see far into tomorrow. But can you see the small things right in front of you?"

"Of course!" huffed the palmist.

"Then tell me," said Tenali, "what did I eat for breakfast this morning?"

The palmist blinked. "Er… rice? No… bread?"

"Wrong!" laughed Tenali. "I ate idlis! Now tell me — where is my left shoe right now?"

The palmist looked under the table. He looked behind the curtains. "I… I cannot say."

Tenali Rama turned to the king with a big smile. "Your Majesty, here is a man who cannot see my breakfast or my shoe, yet he claims to see your whole future! Should we let his gloomy guess steal our happy feast?"

The king thought for a moment — and then he laughed, long and loud!

"You are right, Tenali!" he cried. "Let the feast go on!"

And what a feast it was! There was mango pudding and coconut sweets, music and dancing. The queen clapped her hands with joy, and the courtiers cheered, "Long live the king!" The palmist — who had quietly slipped away — was not missed at all.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Do not let scary guesses steal today's happiness — enjoy the good moments while they are here.*
