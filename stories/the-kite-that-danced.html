The Kite That Danced

An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Jai got a new kite for his birthday — shaped like a butterfly, with blue wings, yellow spots, and a long curly tail. He felt thrilled.

On Saturday, the wind was just right.

"Let's fly it!" said his big brother, Arjun.

They ran to the top of the hill.

"Hold the string tight," said Arjun.

Jai held on while Arjun threw the kite into the air. Up it went! Higher and higher.

"Look at it go!" shouted Jai, joyful.

The kite did not just fly. It danced.

It swooped left, swooped right, looped and twirled.

"Dance, dance, dance," sang Jai. That was three times he sang it, and he felt amazed.

The butterfly kite seemed alive. It dipped down to say hello to the grass, rose up to tickle a cloud, and spun in a happy circle.

"Whee!" laughed Jai, feeling dizzy just watching it.

Then the wind grew stronger, and the string tugged hard. Jai felt worried.

"Don't let go!" said Arjun.

Jai held on with both hands and planted his feet. He was strong.

The wind calmed down and the kite steadied. "Phew," said Jai, proud. He had not let go.

They flew the kite until the sun turned orange. The butterfly danced slower now, swaying gently, like it was getting sleepy.

"Time to come down," said Arjun.

They wound the string. Down came the butterfly, soft as a feather.

Jai hugged his kite. "Best dance ever," he whispered.

That night, Jai dreamed of flying. He soared over hills with butterfly wings.

Sleep now, little flyer.

Dream of dancing kites in a breezy blue sky.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Hold on tight, and you can weather any gust.
