Willow the Whale and the Moonlit Song

In the deepest, bluest part of the sea, where moonbeams melted into the water like silver honey, lived a little whale named Willow.

Willow was the smallest whale in her pod, and she had the softest voice in the whole ocean. While the other whales sang grand, rolling songs that echoed for miles and miles, Willow's song was no bigger than a hum.

"My song is too small," Willow sighed one evening. "Nobody will ever hear it."

That night, the moon was round and white as a pearl, and the sea lay calm as a blanket. Willow floated near the surface, humming her tiny song to herself, when she heard a small sniffle nearby.

"H-hello?" she called softly.

It was Taro, a baby sea turtle no bigger than a teacup. "I'm lost," Taro whispered, his little voice wobbling. "I was following the moonlight home, but a cloud swam over the moon, and now I cannot see the way."

Willow's heart thumped like a soft drum. All around them the water was dark, and the moon was hiding. "Hold my fin," she said gently. "I cannot see the way either... but I can sing it."

So Willow began her little song, the softest hum the ocean had ever heard. Hmmmm... hmmmm...

And as she sang, something wonderful happened. The tiny silver fish, who had been hiding in the dark, peeked out one by one and began to dance to her tune. Their scales caught the last sliver of moonlight, and together they twinkled like a trail of stars through the water.

"Follow the twinkles!" Willow sang. Taro paddled after the shimmering line of fish, and Willow swam slowly beside him, humming all the way. Step by splash, the little trail of light led them through the dark — past the sleepy coral, around the swaying kelp — until at last they saw it: the warm, golden glow of Turtle Bay, where Taro's family was waiting.

"Taro! There you are!" cried his mother, wrapping him in a flipper hug. "How did you find your way home in the dark?"

Taro grinned up at Willow. "Willow sang us home. Her song may be small, but it led the whole sea."

That night, the great whales gathered around Willow. "Teach us your song," they rumbled kindly. And under the round white moon, the whole ocean sang Willow's gentle hum, soft as a lullaby, until every little creature drifted off to sleep.

Sleep now, little Willow.
