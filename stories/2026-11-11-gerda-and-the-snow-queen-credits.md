# Credits — Gerda and the Snow Queen

- **Original title:** "The Snow Queen" (Danish: "Snedronningen")
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen (1805–1875)
- **Original publication:** 1844
- **Source:** *Andersen's Fairy Tales*, Project Gutenberg eBook #1597 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1597
- **Illustrator:** None — all illustrations for this retelling were freshly generated watercolour-style art; no source artwork was reused.
- **License:** Public domain (author died 1875; work published 1844)
- **How it was adapted:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7 as a ~450-word bedtime story. The long seven-part original is shortened to one gentle journey: the mirror-shard plot is left out, the Snow Queen is lonely rather than frightening, and Gerda's love — not a battle — melts the cold. The story closes with a warm goodnight. The lesson is shown through the story, never stated.
