# The Mouse Who Became a Girl

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, beside a slow, silver river, there lived a wise old holy man with a beard soft and white as clouds, in a little hut that smelled of warm earth.

One day, a tiny mouse came tumbling down, down through the soft blue sky — can you see her twirling? — right to the holy man's feet. An eagle had dropped her, and flown far, far away!

"You poor little thing," said the holy man gently. And with a wave of his hand, he whispered soft magic words — rustling like wind through dry leaves — and turned the tiny mouse into a baby girl. A real, crying baby girl!

The holy man and his wife gave her warm milk, sang her soft lullabies, and told her bedtime stories. They named her Chuhiya, and she grew up bright-eyed, quick-footed, and kind.

When Chuhiya was grown, the holy man said, "My dear, the time has come to find you a husband — the strongest one in all the world!"

"Then I will marry the Sun!" said Chuhiya. "Nothing is stronger than the Sun!"

The holy man called the Sun — big and golden, warming their faces. But the Sun sighed, "The Cloud is stronger than me — he puffs himself up all grey and covers my face whenever he wants!"

So they asked the Cloud. But the Cloud sighed, all soft and drift-y, "The Wind is stronger than me — he just… whooshes me away, all over the sky."

So they asked the Wind. But the Wind huffed and puffed, "The Mountain is stronger than me — he just stands there and will not move, not one bit!"

So they asked the Mountain. Now — who do YOU think is stronger than a mountain? … The Mountain smiled and said, "The little Mouse is stronger than me — he nibbles holes in my feet all day!"

Chuhiya smiled a soft, sleepy smile. "Then I will marry the Mouse," she whispered. "He is small like me, quick like me — and he was my very first friend."

The holy man waved his hand and whispered his soft words once more — and slowly, gently, Chuhiya felt a tiny tickle — her whiskers were back! She was a little mouse again.

So the clever little mouse became her husband, and they curled up in their cozy hole under the holy man's hut — nibbling grain, telling soft stories, till the moon came up.

Goodnight, Chuhiya. The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Be true to yourself — happiness is being who you really are.*
