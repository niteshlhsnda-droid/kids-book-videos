# Dancing with Fireflies

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

At dusk, when the sky turned purple, the fireflies woke up.

Their leader was a firefly named Glow.

She was the brightest dancer of all.

"Time to dance!" she called.

The fireflies stretched their tiny wings.

They felt excited.

One by one, they lit their lanterns.

Blink.

Blink, blink.

The meadow sparkled.

Little Tia watched from her porch.

She felt amazed.

"They are dancing!" she whispered.

Glow twirled in the air.

She drew a loop of light.

A loop-de-loop.

Then another firefly joined.

Then another.

Soon fifty fireflies were dancing.

Can you count some with Tia?

One, two, three, four, five.

Five bright dancers!

Glow had a pattern.

Blink, pause, blink-blink.

"Follow me!" she said.

The fireflies followed.

Blink, pause, blink-blink.

"Dance, dance, dance," sang Tia softly.

That was three times she sang it.

She felt joyful.

The fireflies danced over the flowers.

They danced around the old oak.

They danced in swirls and zigzags.

Their lights looked like floating stars.

Tia clapped quietly.

She did not want to scare them.

She felt gentle.

Then Glow noticed Tia.

She danced closer.

Blink, pause, blink-blink.

It looked like she was saying hello.

"Hello, Glow," whispered Tia.

She felt special.

The dance went on as the sky grew dark.

The stars came out above.

The fireflies danced below.

It was a sky of lights, top and bottom.

Slowly, the dance grew slower.

And slower.

And slower.

One by one, the lanterns dimmed.

"Goodnight, dancers," whispered Tia.

"Goodnight, goodnight, goodnight."

That was three times she whispered it.

She felt sleepy.

Glow gave one last blink.

Then the meadow was dark and quiet.

Sleep now, little watcher.

Dream of dancing lights in a purple dusk.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Joy is brightest when it is shared.*
