# Credits — The Fisherman and the Jinni

- **Original title:** "The Fisherman and the Jinni" (from *The Book of the Thousand Nights and a Night*, Vol. 1, 1885)
- **Author:** Anonymous (traditional tale from the Arabian Nights); translated by Sir Richard Francis Burton (1821–1890)
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *The Book of the Thousand Nights and a Night*, Vol. 1 (eBook #51252) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/51252
- **License:** Public domain (Burton translation first published 1885; Burton died 1890)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, shortened to ten story beats from the original tale — the fisherman's four casts, the brass jar sealed with lead, the jinni's grumpy hundred-year sleep, the clever question that sends the jinni back into the jar, the three promises, the jewel-bright pond, and the warm family supper; the ending is softened into a cosy moonlit goodnight so the tale closes gently at bedtime.
