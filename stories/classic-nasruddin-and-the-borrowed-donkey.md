# Nasruddin and the Borrowed Donkey

*A timeless Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a little village with white walls and red roofs, there lived a kind and clever man named Nasruddin. Nasruddin had a warm smile, a long beard, and a donkey he loved very much.

The donkey was small and grey, with soft ears and a gentle walk. Nasruddin called him Dodo. Every morning, Nasruddin brushed Dodo's coat and gave him fresh hay and a crunchy carrot.

One sunny morning, there was a knock at Nasruddin's door. It was his neighbour, Mr. Karim, who was in a big hurry.

"Nasruddin, my friend!" said Mr. Karim. "I need to carry a heavy sack of flour to the market. May I please borrow your donkey, just for today?"

Nasruddin scratched his beard and thought for a moment. He loved Dodo very much, and Dodo was tired after a long walk yesterday.

"I am sorry, my friend," said Nasruddin with a kind smile. "My donkey is not at home. He is away on a long trip."

Mr. Karim looked a little sad. "Oh, what a shame," he said.

But just then, from inside the stable, came a loud sound. "Hee-haw! Hee-haw!"

It was Dodo! The donkey was right there, calling out in his big, loud voice.

Mr. Karim's eyes went wide. "But Nasruddin! I can hear him! Your donkey is right there in the stable!"

Nasruddin did not look worried at all. He smiled his big, warm smile and put his hand on his heart.

"My dear friend," he said, "who do you believe — me, or my donkey?"

Mr. Karim looked at Nasruddin. He looked at the stable. Then he laughed and laughed until tears ran down his cheeks.

"Oh, Nasruddin!" he said, still laughing. "You are the funniest man in the whole village!"

Nasruddin laughed too. Then he said, "Come, my friend. Sit with me and have a cup of tea. We will carry your sack together, you and I, on our own two feet."

So the two friends walked to the market together, carrying the sack between them, and they chatted and laughed all the way there.

And Dodo? Dodo stayed home in his stable, munching his hay and listening to the birds sing. He was happy, and Nasruddin was happy, and Mr. Karim was happy too.

That night, when the stars came out, Nasruddin patted Dodo's soft nose and whispered, "Good night, my little friend."

And Dodo gave a soft, sleepy "hee-haw," closed his eyes, and drifted off to sleep.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A kind heart and a little humour make every day brighter.*
