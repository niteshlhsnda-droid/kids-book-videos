# Yuki the Snow Bunny and the Starlight Sled

*A brand-new bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High up on a snowy mountain, in a little burrow under a pine tree, there lived a small white bunny named Yuki.

Yuki loved the snow. She loved to hop through it, to roll in it, and to make tiny snowballs with her soft paws.

But most of all, Yuki loved her little wooden sled. It was painted blue, with silver stars on its sides. Her grandmother had made it for her, long ago.

"This is a starlight sled," her grandmother had said. "It only moves when you sing it a song."

One cold winter evening, the snow was falling softly — flake, flake, flake — and all the little animals were getting sleepy.

Yuki pulled her sled to the top of the hill and climbed in. She took a deep breath and sang her softest, sleepiest song:

"Twinkle, twinkle, little star,
Time to sleep, wherever you are…"

And guess what? The sled began to glow! Its silver stars twinkled, and it slid gently down the hill — swoosh! — smooth and soft as a dream.

At the bottom of the hill, Yuki found a little squirrel, shivering in the snow.

"Come and ride with me," said Yuki. "I will take you home."

The squirrel climbed in, and Yuki sang her song again. The sled glided on through the snow — swoosh!

Soon they found a sleepy hedgehog, curled up under a bush.

"Come and ride with me," said Yuki. "I will take you home."

The hedgehog climbed in, and Yuki sang again. The sled glided on — swoosh!

Then they found a little fox cub, yawning by the frozen pond.

"Come and ride with me," said Yuki. "I will take you home."

The fox cub climbed in, and Yuki sang her song one more time. The sled glided through the snowy trees, its silver stars twinkling like real stars.

One by one, Yuki took each friend home — the squirrel to his tree, the hedgehog to his burrow, the fox cub to his den. Each one waved goodbye and whispered, "Thank you, Yuki. Sweet dreams!"

At last, Yuki was alone on her sled. The snow was falling softly — flake, flake, flake — and the stars were shining brightly above.

She sang her song once more, very softly, just for herself:

"Twinkle, twinkle, little star,
Time to sleep, wherever you are…"

And the starlight sled carried her gently home, all the way to her little burrow under the pine tree.

Yuki curled up in her warm bed, pulled her blanket up to her chin, and closed her eyes.

"Good night, snow," she whispered. "Good night, stars. Good night, sled."

And she fell fast asleep, dreaming of silver stars and soft, soft snow.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A kind song and a helping hand can carry everyone safely home.*
