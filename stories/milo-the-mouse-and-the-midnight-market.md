# Milo the Mouse and the Midnight Market

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a tiny house at the edge of a quiet garden, there lived a little mouse named Milo.

Milo had soft grey fur, round pink ears, and a long, curly tail. He loved cheese more than anything in the whole wide world. He kept his very favourite piece of cheese, round and golden, in a little blue box beside his bed.

One night, when the moon was high and the stars were twinkling, Milo heard a soft, tinkling sound outside his window. Tinkle, tinkle, tinkle, like tiny bells.

Milo peeked out of his window and gasped. Down in the garden, under the moonlight, there was a market! A tiny, twinkling midnight market, just for little creatures.

Fireflies flew above the little stalls, their lights glowing like golden lanterns. The air smelled sweet, like honey and warm berries.

"Oh, how wonderful!" whispered Milo. "I must go and see!"

Milo put on his little red scarf, tucked his favourite piece of cheese into his pocket, just in case, and tiptoed down the garden path.

At the first stall, a kind firefly was selling moonbeam jam in tiny jars that glowed soft and silver.

"Moonbeam jam!" called the firefly. "Sweet as a lullaby!"

At the next stall, a busy bee was selling star sugar, sparkling like tiny stars in little paper bags.

"Star sugar!" buzzed the bee. "One sprinkle makes everything taste like a dream!"

Milo smiled and waved at everyone. He loved the midnight market. The music was soft, the lights were warm, and all the little creatures were smiling.

Then Milo saw someone sitting all alone on a little stone. It was his friend Pippa the field mouse. Pippa was hugging her knees, and her tummy was rumbling softly.

"Hello, Pippa," said Milo. "Why are you sitting here all alone?"

"I came to see the market," said Pippa, "but I have no coins, and I am very hungry."

Milo thought for a moment. Then he smiled and reached into his pocket. He took out his very favourite piece of cheese, round and golden.

"Here, Pippa," said Milo. "This cheese is for you. It is my favourite, and I would love to share it with you."

Pippa's eyes went wide. "For me? Really?"

"Really!" said Milo. "Sharing makes it taste even better."

Pippa took a little bite, and her whole face lit up. "Mmm! It is the yummiest cheese I have ever tasted!"

Just then, the firefly fluttered down with a tiny jar of moonbeam jam. "For you, Milo," she said. "For sharing so kindly."

And the bee buzzed over with a little bag of star sugar. "And this is for you too," she said. "Kind friends deserve sweet treats."

Milo and Pippa spread the moonbeam jam on the cheese and sprinkled it with star sugar. They shared it with the firefly and the bee, and they all sat together under the twinkling lights, munching happily.

When the market began to close and the fireflies dimmed their lights, Milo and Pippa walked home together, warm and full and happy.

"Thank you, Milo," said Pippa. "Tonight was the best night ever."

"You are welcome, Pippa," said Milo. "The market is even more magical when it is shared."

Milo climbed into his cosy bed, pulled his blanket up to his chin, and closed his eyes. He dreamed of moonbeam jam, star sugar, and a tiny market that twinkled under the moon.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing what you love makes it taste even sweeter.*
