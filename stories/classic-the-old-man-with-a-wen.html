The Old Man with a Wen

A timeless Japanese folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a little village in Japan, there lived a kind old man with a round, smiling face and a little round lump on his cheek, called a wen.

It was soft, it did not hurt, and the old man did not mind it one bit.

What he loved most in the world was to sing and dance. Every day, as he gathered wood in the hills, he sang a sweet little song — "La, la, la! Tra, la, la!" — and danced a happy little dance: one step, two steps, three steps, turn!

The birds sang along, and the bamboo swayed to his song.

One evening, as he walked home through the bamboo forest, the moon rose, round and bright — and he heard music!

"Pling, pling!" went a little flute. "Tum, tum!" went a little drum.

He peeped through the bamboo. There, in a moonlit clearing, the mountain spirits were dancing — round and jolly, with rosy cheeks and twinkling eyes, laughing and clapping as they danced round and round.

His feet began to tap all by themselves. "One step, two steps, three steps, turn!" He danced out of the bamboo and joined them, singing his sweetest song.

"What a kind, happy dancer!" cried the spirits, clapping. "You dance with such a joyful heart! We must give you a gift!"

And with a soft, gentle touch, they lifted the little lump from his cheek, and it floated away like a dandelion puff.

"Oh!" cried the old man, touching his smooth cheek. "Thank you! Thank you!"

He danced all the way home, singing his sweetest song. And when his wife saw his smooth, smiling cheek, she laughed with joy.

Now, the old man had a grumpy neighbour who also had a lump on his cheek. When he heard the story, he stomped into the forest.

"Give me a gift too!" he demanded. "Take my lump away!"

But he did not sing. He did not dance. He only stamped his feet and shouted, "Hurry up! Hurry up!"

The spirits shook their heads. "A gift is given to a kind heart," they said. And — pop! — they gave him the old man's lump as well, so now he had two, one on each cheek!

The grumpy man went home, very cross. But that night, he thought about the old man's happy dance. The next evening, he went back — and this time, he sang a soft little song and danced a gentle little dance.

"La, la, la!" he sang, smiling for the very first time. "Tra, la, la!"

"Now you dance with a kind heart!" cried the spirits, clapping. And they lifted both lumps from his cheeks, and they floated away like dandelion puffs.

From that day on, the two old men sang and danced together every evening, and the mountain spirits danced along under the bright moon.

That night, the kind old man lay down and counted the moonbeams on his wall — one, two, three, four, five — until his eyes grew heavy.

And under the twinkling stars, he fell fast asleep, smiling.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: A kind heart dances the sweetest dance — and kindness is the best gift of all.
