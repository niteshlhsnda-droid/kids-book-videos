The Patient Buffalo

A gentle Jataka tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a green valley with a slow, silver river, there lived a big, kind buffalo named Bruno.

Bruno had wide, curved horns and soft brown eyes. Every morning he waded into the river, swished his tail at the flies, and munched the sweet grass on the bank.

One morning, a mischievous little monkey named Milo swung down from the trees. Milo loved teasing more than anything in the world.

"Good morning, slow-poke!" Milo chattered. He tugged Bruno's ear. Boing!

Bruno blinked his soft brown eyes and said nothing. He went on munching his grass.

Milo swung onto Bruno's back. He pulled Bruno's tail. He tapped Bruno's horns — tok, tok, tok — like little drums!

Still Bruno said nothing. He stood calm and still, like a great, kind rock in the river.

Day after day, Milo teased and teased. He chattered rude words. He dropped twigs on Bruno's head. He swung from one horn to the other, shouting, "Wheee!"

And day after day, Bruno stayed patient and calm.

One evening, another monkey, old Aunt Lulu, swung by. She watched Milo pull Bruno's ear and saw Bruno just smile a slow buffalo smile.

"Milo!" scolded Aunt Lulu. "Why do you tease Bruno so? And Bruno — why do you never stop him?"

Milo stuck out his tongue. But Bruno spoke at last, in his deep, rumbly voice.

"Little Milo is young," said Bruno. "His teasing cannot hurt me. My horns are strong, my skin is thick — and my heart is at peace. Why should I spend my anger on a game?"

Aunt Lulu nodded wisely. "And if a tiger came and teased you?" she asked.

"Ah," said Bruno, and his soft eyes twinkled. "Then you would see how fast these old legs can run, and how sharp these old horns can be! I save my strength for when it truly matters."

Milo's cheeks turned red. He slid down from Bruno's back and hung his head.

"I'm sorry, Bruno," he whispered. "I teased you because you never got angry. I thought it was funny."

Bruno nuzzled the little monkey gently with his big, warm nose. "It takes a brave heart to say sorry," he rumbled. "Shall we be friends instead?"

From that day on, Milo rode on Bruno's back — not to tease, but because they were friends. He picked the tastiest leaves for Bruno, and Bruno gave him rides across the river, splashing through the silver water.

And whenever Milo felt grumpy, he remembered Bruno's calm eyes, took a deep breath, and let the grumpiness float away like a leaf on the river.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Patience is true strength — the calm heart is stronger than the angry shout.
