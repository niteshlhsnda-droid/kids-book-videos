# The Mouse and the Cat

*A timeless Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a sunny garden behind a little cottage, there lived a small grey mouse named Mini. Mini loved to nibble fallen grains and dance among the marigolds. In the same garden lived a big orange cat named Billu, who loved to nap in the warm sun and chase butterflies — never mice, for Billu was a gentle cat with a kind heart.

One morning, Mini heard a sad sound near the rose bushes. "Mew... mew... oh dear, oh dear!"

Mini peeped through the leaves. There was Billu, tangled in an old fishing net that had blown into the garden! The net's strings were wrapped around his paws, and try as he might, he could not get free.

"Please help me, little mouse," said Billu softly. "I am all tangled up, and I cannot even reach my water bowl."

Mini's whiskers trembled. A cat asking a mouse for help! But Mini remembered her grandmother's words: "When someone needs help, help them — no matter who they are."

"Do not worry, Billu," said Mini bravely. "My teeth are small, but they are very sharp. I will nibble through these strings, one by one."

Nibble, nibble, nibble! Mini worked all morning. She nibbled the first string, then the second, then the third. At last, with one final nibble, the whole net fell away!

"Free!" laughed Billu, stretching his paws in the sunshine. "Thank you, Mini! You are the bravest little friend in the whole garden."

From that day on, something wonderful happened. Billu never chased Mini. Instead, when it rained, Billu curled up beside the marigolds to keep Mini dry, and when Mini found extra grains, she always saved some for Billu's supper.

One evening, the two friends sat together under the stars, sharing warm milk and crunchy grains.

"Who would have thought," purred Billu, "that a cat and a mouse could be the best of friends?"

"Anyone who shares kindness," squeaked Mini, "can be friends with anyone at all."

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Kindness turns even unlikely strangers into the best of friends.*
