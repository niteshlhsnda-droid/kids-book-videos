# Tenali Rama and the Thieves

*A clever Tenali Rama tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Tenali Rama, the cleverest minister of King Krishnadevaraya, had a beautiful garden behind his house. In the middle of the garden stood his favorite plant — a young mango tree, just beginning to grow.

One evening, Tenali Rama heard a whisper at his gate. Two sneaky thieves were peeking into his garden.

"Look," whispered the first thief, "Tenali Rama is rich. He must have buried gold in his garden!"

"Tonight," whispered the second thief, "we will dig up his garden and find the gold!"

But Tenali Rama had heard every word. He smiled his slow, clever smile and called out loudly — just loud enough for the thieves to hear.

"My dear wife," he said, "I am so glad I buried our gold pot deep under the mango tree. No thief will ever find it there!"

That night, the two thieves crept into the garden with big shovels. Dig, dig, dig! They dug all around the little mango tree, deeper and deeper, looking for the gold pot.

But there was no gold pot. There was only earth, and roots, and more earth.

"Hmm," said the first thief, wiping his forehead. "Perhaps we must dig deeper."

Dig, dig, dig! They dug until the moon was high and their arms ached. Still no gold.

At last, tired and dusty, the thieves gave up and crept away empty-handed.

The next morning, Tenali Rama walked into his garden and saw the deep, soft earth all around his little mango tree. He clapped his hands with delight.

"Wonderful!" he cried. "My mango tree has been dug around so deeply — its roots can breathe, and the rain will sink right in! Thank you, kind thieves, wherever you are!"

And do you know what? That mango tree grew faster than any tree in the whole kingdom. By summer, it was covered in sweet, golden mangoes, and Tenali Rama shared them with all his neighbors.

When the two thieves heard about it, they scratched their heads and said, "We came to steal gold, and instead we planted a mango tree!"

And Tenali Rama just smiled his slow, clever smile.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever mind turns trouble into treasure — even a thief's digging can help a garden grow.*
