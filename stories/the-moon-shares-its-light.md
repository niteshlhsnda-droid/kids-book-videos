# The Moon Shares Its Light

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

The moon loved its light. It kept every beam for itself. It shone bright and proud all alone. It felt happy being the brightest thing in the sky.

But down below, the night animals stumbled in the dark. Tilda the turtle bumped into a rock. Bodhi the bunny tripped over a root. They felt frustrated. It was too dark to see.

"Excuse me, Moon," called Tilda politely. "Could you share a little light? We cannot see our way."

The moon felt unsure. "But it is MY light," it said. "If I share it, I will have less."

Just then, a little firefly floated up. Its glow was tiny. "I share my light every night," it said. "And I never run out. Sharing makes it grow."

The moon felt curious. It had never thought of that. It broke off one small beam. It was just a sliver of silver. It gave it to Tilda the turtle.

"Share a little light, and it shines twice as bright," sang the firefly.

Tilda glowed softly. She could see the path now. She felt happy and safe. The moon watched. It did not feel dimmer at all!

The moon broke off another beam. It gave it to Bodhi the bunny. Bodhi's ears glowed pink. He hopped home safely.

"Share a little light, and it shines twice as bright," sang the firefly again.

The moon felt wonderful. Sharing was fun! It gave a beam to the sleepy owl. It gave a beam to the tiny mice. Soon the whole meadow glowed gently.

"Share a little light, and it shines twice as bright," sang the firefly one last time.

The moon looked down. The meadow was beautiful. Every animal had a little glow. And the moon? It shone brighter than ever. The firefly was right. Sharing had made its light grow.

The animals looked up. "Thank you, Moon!" they called. The moon felt warm inside. It had never felt this happy alone.

One by one, the animals fell asleep in the gentle glow. The moon watched over them proudly. It was no longer just bright. It was kind.

Sleep now, little moon. You shared your light beautifully. Rest knowing the meadow dreams in your gentle glow.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Sharing what you have makes it shine even brighter.*
