# The Cloud Shepherd

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

High above the hills lived an old cloud shepherd named Nimbus.

Every morning he gathered his flock of little clouds. "Come along, clouds," he called. "Time to drift." The little clouds floated after him in a fluffy line, and Nimbus felt proud.

One little cloud named Puff was very slow, and she lagged behind the others.

"Hurry, Puff," said Nimbus.

"I'm trying," said Puff, worried she might get lost.

Nimbus had an idea. He tied an invisible string to Puff — not a real string, but a shepherd's string, made of care. "Now you can drift at your own pace," he said, "and you will never get lost."

Relief washed over Puff. She drifted slowly behind the flock, watching the world below — green fields, a winding river, and tiny houses with tiny gardens.

"So pretty, so pretty, so pretty," she whispered. That was three times she whispered it, and she felt happy.

At noon, the flock stopped over a thirsty farm. "Rain time," said Nimbus. The big clouds let their rain fall — pitter-patter on the dry fields — and the farmer waved up at the sky. "Thank you!" he called.

Puff wanted to help too. She squeezed out three little drops. Plip, plip, plip.

"Well done, Puff," said Nimbus, and Puff felt proud.

As evening came, Nimbus led his flock home while the sun painted them pink and gold. Puff drifted slowly, as always — but she was not worried now. She had her shepherd's string, and she had her place in the flock.

One by one, the clouds settled into the night sky. Puff found a soft spot near the moon. She yawned a fluffy yawn.

Sleep now, little cloud.

Dream of drifting slow over quiet fields.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Going slowly is just fine when someone cares about you.*
