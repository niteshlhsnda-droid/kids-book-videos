# Nasruddin and the Echo

*A merry Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a little town between green hills, there lived a funny, kind man named Nasruddin.

Nasruddin wore a big round turban, a long coat, and a smile that made everyone smile too. He rode a small gray donkey everywhere he went — clip-clop, clip-clop.

One sunny morning, Nasruddin rode his donkey up into the hills to pick wildflowers for his wife.

At the top of the hills was a deep, round valley. Nasruddin had never been there before. He looked down into it and called out cheerfully, "HELLO-O-O!"

Back came a voice from the valley: "HELLO-O-O!"

Nasruddin blinked. "Who said that?" he asked.

"WHO SAID THAT?" answered the valley.

Nasruddin scratched his turban. "Is somebody down there copying me?"

"COPYING ME?" said the valley.

Nasruddin frowned. "Stop copying me, you rude fellow!"

"RUDE FELLOW!" said the valley.

Now Nasruddin was cross. He shook his fist. "Come up here and say that to my face!"

"TO MY FACE!" said the valley.

Nasruddin huffed and puffed. He was about to climb down into the valley and have a stern word with this rude fellow — when his little donkey gave a loud, cheerful HEE-HAW!

And the valley answered at once: "HEE-HAW!"

Nasruddin stared at his donkey. The donkey stared back and twitched his long ears.

Slowly, a big grin spread across Nasruddin's face. "Oh!" he laughed. "Oh, you clever valley! There is no rude fellow down there at all — it is only my own voice, bouncing back to say hello!"

He cupped his hands around his mouth and sang, in his happiest voice, "LA-LA-LA, WHAT A LOVELY DAY!"

And the valley sang back, "LA-LA-LA, WHAT A LOVELY DAY!"

Nasruddin sang a whole song, and the valley sang along — his very own echo choir! He danced on the hilltop, his coat flapping, his donkey braying along — HEE-HAW, HEE-HAW!

When he rode home that evening, his basket was full of wildflowers, and his heart was full of song.

"Where have you been all day?" asked his wife, laughing at his dusty coat.

"I found a new friend in the hills," said Nasruddin with a wink. "He repeats everything I say — but I don't mind, because today he only repeated my singing!"

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: What you send out comes back to you — so send out songs, not shouts.*
