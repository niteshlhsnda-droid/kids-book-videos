# Credits — Jojo the Jackal and the Thumping Drum

- **Original title:** "The Jackal and the War Drum" (Book I, Tantra I)
- **Author:** attr. Vishnu Sharma (traditional)
- **Source work:** The Panchatantra, c. 3rd century CE
- **Source URL:** https://en.m.wikisource.org/wiki/Panchatantra (Arthur William Ryder's 1925 translation; the "Jackal and Drum" tale appears in Book I)
- **License:** Public domain (ancient text; translation 1925, pre-1929)
- **Illustrator:** Fresh watercolor-style illustrations generated for this adaptation; no source art reused.
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm bedtime language for ages 4–7. The jackal becomes a young pup named Jojo; the war-drum discovery is kept as the gentle climax; the lesson (scary sounds are often harmless — a brave peek turns fear into a giggle) is shown through the story and never stated. Added a cozy den, a mother's lullaby memory, and a goodnight closing.
