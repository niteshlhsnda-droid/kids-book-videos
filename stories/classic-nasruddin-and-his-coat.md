# Nasruddin and His Coat

*A giggly Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

One day, Nasruddin was invited to a grand feast at the house of a rich merchant.

Nasruddin was in a hurry, so he went just as he was — in his old patched coat, with flour on his sleeves from the morning's bread.

At the merchant's door, the servant looked him up and down. "Hmm," said the servant, and led Nasruddin to the very last seat, in the very back corner, behind a big potted plant.

No one offered Nasruddin any food. No one asked him his name. The rich guests in their silky clothes laughed and chatted, and nobody noticed Nasruddin at all.

Nasruddin stroked his beard and thought for a moment. Then he smiled, stood up, and walked all the way home.

He washed his face. He combed his beard. And he put on his very best coat — deep blue, with shiny brass buttons.

Then he walked back to the feast — knock, knock, knock!

This time the servant bowed low. "Welcome! Welcome!" he cried, and led Nasruddin to the seat of honor, right beside the merchant himself!

"Try the pilaf! Taste the kebabs! Have some honey cake!" Everyone wanted to serve him. Everyone wanted to chat with him.

Nasruddin smiled politely. Then he picked up a spoonful of pilaf — and tucked it into his coat pocket!

He poured a little gravy into his sleeve. He stuffed a honey cake into his collar.

"Eat, coat, eat!" he said to his coat. "Drink, buttons, drink!"

The whole table went silent. Then the merchant blinked. "Nasruddin... what are you doing?"

Nasruddin grinned. "When I came in my old coat, nobody fed ME. Now that I wear this fine coat, everyone feeds ME. So clearly, it is the COAT you invited — not me! Eat, coat, eat!"

There was a moment of hush. Then the merchant's face turned red — and then he burst out laughing. And the whole table laughed with him!

"You are right, Nasruddin," said the merchant, wiping his eyes. "I was foolish. From today, I will welcome the PERSON — not the coat."

And to make up for it, the merchant served Nasruddin the biggest plate of all — and this time, Nasruddin ate every bite himself.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: Welcome the person, not their clothes.*
