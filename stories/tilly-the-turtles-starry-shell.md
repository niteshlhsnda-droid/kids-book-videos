# Tilly the Turtle's Starry Shell

*An original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

In a green pond under a wide, wide sky lived a little turtle named Tilly.

Tilly loved bedtime best of all. Not because she was sleepy — oh no! Because at bedtime, the sky turned dark, and out came the STARS.

"Oh, look at them!" Tilly would whisper, floating on the cool water. "Thousands of twinkling stars! I wish, I wish my shell sparkled like that."

For you see, Tilly's shell was plain and brown — smooth and round, but plain. And Tilly wanted it to shine.

So one sunny morning, Tilly had an idea. Near the pond she found some silver paint, left behind by a painter. And with the tip of her tiny tail, she painted little silver stars all over her shell. Star, star, star!

"Now I am a starry turtle!" she said proudly, and she swam in happy circles.

But then — plip, plop! — the rain came. Pitter-patter, pitter-patter. And when the rain stopped, Tilly looked down. Oh no! Her silver stars had all washed away!

Tilly let out a long, sad sigh. But she was not done trying.

That evening, she saw the fireflies dancing over the pond — tiny lanterns, blinking on and off. "Dear fireflies," Tilly called, "will you sit on my shell? Then I will sparkle like the stars!"

The fireflies giggled and landed on her shell — one, two, three! Blink, blink, blink! Tilly glowed and shimmered! "I am starry! I am starry!"

But oh — their tiny feet tickled! Tilly wiggled. She giggled. She wiggled some more — and off flew the fireflies, laughing, "Too ticklish! Too ticklish!"

Tilly's smile faded. She pulled her head into her plain brown shell and felt very, very sad.

"Why can't I be starry?" she whispered.

Just then, the sky rumbled. RUMBLE! Dark clouds rolled in. The wind howled. The rain poured down — splash, splash, SPLASH! It was a big, big storm!

But Tilly? Tilly tucked herself snugly INSIDE her strong, plain, brown shell. It was warm in there. It was dry in there. It was safe in there. And as the rain drummed softly on her shell — tap, tap, tap — it sounded like a lullaby.

Tilly closed her eyes and listened… and soon she was dreaming of stars.

When she woke, the storm was gone. The sun was peeping up, pink and gold. And on Tilly's shell sat a hundred tiny dewdrops — little drops of morning water — and every single one caught the sunlight and TWINKLED!

Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle! Tilly's shell was sparkling like the night sky!

Tilly smiled the biggest smile of her life. "My shell was starry all along," she whispered, "in its own way."

And from that day on, whenever Tilly looked at the stars, she smiled — because she knew she was wonderful, exactly as she was.

Now you close your eyes too, little one… and dream of twinkling stars… twinkling… twinkling…

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: You are wonderful exactly as you are.*
