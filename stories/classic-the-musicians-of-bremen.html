The Musicians of Bremen

A timeless world folktale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a green valley, there lived an old grey donkey named Dara.

Dara had carried heavy sacks for many years, and now his legs were slow and tired.

One morning, Dara said to himself, "I can still sing! I will go to the town of Bremen and become a musician!"

So Dara tied a little red scarf round his neck and set off down the dusty road, singing a happy hee-haw song.

Soon he met a big brown dog named Bruno, sitting by the road with droopy ears.

"Why are you so sad, Bruno?" asked Dara.

"I am old now," sighed Bruno, "and nobody wants my bark any more."

"Come with me!" said Dara. "We are going to Bremen to be musicians! You can bark in tune!"

Bruno wagged his tail, jumped up, and trotted along beside Dara, barking happily.

A little further on, they met a soft grey cat named Sona, sitting on a stone wall and washing her face.

"My purr has gone quiet," said Sona, "and nobody listens to my songs any more."

"Come with us!" said Dara. "You can sing the sweet high notes!"

Sona purred a happy purr, jumped down from the wall, and padded along after them, humming a soft mew-mew tune.

Then, near a farm gate, they heard a loud cock-a-doodle-doo! A proud red rooster named Chintu stood on a fence, flapping his wings.

"I love to sing!" crowed Chintu. "But nobody here wants to hear my morning song."

"Then come with us!" said Dara. "Bremen needs a fine loud singer to lead the band!"

Chintu flapped his wings with joy and marched along behind them, crowing his bright cock-a-doodle-doo.

Now the four friends walked together — clip-clop, trot-trot, pad-pad, flap-flap — all the way down the long road.

But soon the sun went down, and the sky turned soft and purple.

Just then, Dara saw a warm yellow light twinkling through the trees.

"Look!" he whispered. "A little cottage! Let us peep inside."

They crept close and peeped through the window.

Inside were three grumpy thieves, counting shiny coins and eating bread and cheese.

"Oh dear," whispered Sona. "They do not look friendly at all."

"Then let us sing!" said Dara with a brave smile. "Our music will chase their grumps away!"

So Dara stood up tall, Bruno jumped on his back, Sona climbed on Bruno, and Chintu perched on top.

Then they all sang together — hee-haw! woof-woof! mew-mew! cock-a-doodle-doo!

They sang so loudly and so merrily that the whole cottage shook with music!

The thieves jumped up in fright. "What is that noise?" cried one. "A monster with four heads!" cried another.

And off they ran, out of the door and into the dark forest!

The four friends laughed and laughed. Then they walked inside the warm little cottage.

They ate the bread and cheese and curled up by the fire.

"This is the best home ever!" said Chintu, tucking his head under his wing.

And every morning after that, the four friends sang their happy song together.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When friends sing together, even a scary night turns into a happy home.
