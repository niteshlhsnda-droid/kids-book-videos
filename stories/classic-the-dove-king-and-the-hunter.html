The Dove King and the Hunter

A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

High in a tall peepal tree lived a flock of soft grey doves.

Their king was Dove King Greywing, the kindest dove in the whole forest. Every morning he led his doves out to find seeds, and every evening he counted them all — one, two, three — to make sure every dove was home safe.

One sunny morning, the doves saw something wonderful in a clearing below.

Golden grains of rice were scattered on the green grass, sparkling like tiny suns!

"Breakfast!" cooed the young doves, and down they fluttered.

But the moment their little feet touched the grains — SWISH! A great net fell over them all!

The doves flapped and fluttered, but the net held them tight. The young doves trembled — but Greywing's calm voice kept them brave.

"Do not be afraid!" called Greywing. "Flap together! Up, up, up — all at once!"

The doves took a deep breath. One… two… three… FLAP!

Together they rose into the sky, carrying the whole net with them, like a great grey cloud.

Far away lived Greywing's dear friend, Nibbles the mouse, who had the sharpest little teeth in the forest.

"Nibbles!" called Greywing, landing with a soft thump. "Please help us!"

Nibbles did not waste a moment. Nibble, nibble, nibble — his tiny teeth worked the ropes until — SNAP! The net fell open, and every dove fluttered free.

Just then, the hunter who had set the net came running through the trees.

But when he saw the doves flying in a happy circle around Greywing, and Nibbles waving his tiny paw, his frown melted away.

"Such friendship!" he whispered. "I will never set a net again."

And he never did. Instead, he scattered rice for the doves every morning, and they sang him the sweetest evening songs.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: When friends work together, no trouble is too big — and kindness can change any heart.
