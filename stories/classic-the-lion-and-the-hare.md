# The Lion and the Hare

*A gentle Hitopadesha tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Long, long ago, in a green forest full of tall trees and soft grass, there lived a big proud lion named Raja.

Every morning Raja roared, "I am the king of this forest! One animal must visit me every day!"

The animals did not like this rule at all. They were busy gathering food and playing with their friends, and nobody wanted to wait outside the lion's cave all day.

One morning it was the turn of a clever little hare named Molu. She had soft grey fur, long ears, and very quick feet.

"I do not want to walk to the lion's cave," said Molu to her friend Meera the deer. "And I do not want anyone else to have to walk there tomorrow either."

"What can we do?" asked Meera. "Raja is so big, and we are so small."

"We can be clever," said Molu, with a twinkle in her eye. "Big is not the same as clever."

So Molu took her time. She stopped to smell the flowers and to watch a butterfly dance in the sunshine.

By the time she reached the lion's cave, the sun was high, and Raja was very cross.

"Why are you so late?" roared Raja, shaking his great mane. "I have been waiting and waiting!"

"Oh, great king," said Molu in her smallest, politest voice. "I was on my way to you, but another lion stopped me on the path."

"Another lion?" roared Raja. "There is no other lion in my forest!"

"He is even bigger than you," whispered Molu. "He lives in the deep well by the old mango tree, and he says he is the true king."

"Show me this lion at once!" roared Raja. Molu hopped along the path, and Raja stomped after her, all the way to the deep old well.

"There he is!" said Molu, pointing down into the dark water. "Look at his big face!"

Raja leaned over the edge and looked down. There, in the still water, he saw a big lion's face staring back at him.

"ROAR!" shouted Raja. "ROAR!" shouted the lion in the water, right back at him.

"How dare you!" cried Raja. "I am the king of this forest!" The lion in the water shook his mane too, and looked just as cross.

Raja was so angry that he forgot to think. He gave one great leap — and SPLASH! — he jumped right into the well!

Cold water splashed everywhere. At last Raja climbed out, wet from his nose to his tail, with his mane hanging down in drips.

"Oh dear," said Molu, trying not to giggle. "That lion was only your own reflection in the water, great king."

Raja sat down on the soft grass and shook the water from his ears. Then, to everyone's surprise, he began to laugh.

"Ha! Ha! Ha!" laughed Raja. "I have been very silly. And you, little Molu, have been very clever."

From that day on, Raja changed his ways. He no longer asked the animals to visit him, and he shared the forest kindly with them all.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: A clever mind can solve what strength alone cannot.*
