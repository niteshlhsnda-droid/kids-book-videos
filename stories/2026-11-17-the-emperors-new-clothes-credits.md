# Credits — The Emperor's New Clothes

- **Original title:** "The Emperor's New Clothes" ("Keiserens nye Klæder", 1837)
- **Author:** Hans Christian Andersen
- **Illustrator:** None reused — all illustrations were freshly generated for this retelling in a watercolor picture-book style.
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg, *Andersen's Fairy Tales* (eBook #1597) — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1597
- **License:** Public domain (Andersen died 1875; first published 1837)
- **Adaptation note:** Retold in simple, warm language for ages 4–7, shortened to ten story beats with a gentle bedtime ending; the vain emperor, the two weavers and the honest child are kept, and the tale's trick is softened so the laughter is kind and shared.
