# The Four Friends and the Hunter

*A timeless Panchatantra tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Deep in a green forest, beside a sparkling stream, lived four best friends.

Kiki was a clever crow with shiny black feathers. Minti, the tiny mouse, had whiskers that twitched whenever an idea popped into her head. Then there was Titu — a slow, kind tortoise with a strong, round shell. And Dina, a gentle deer with the softest brown eyes.

Every morning, the four friends met under the big peepal tree to play and share stories. Titu's strong shell was Minti's favorite umbrella in the rain, and Dina's soft tail swished the flies away from Kiki's feathers — different in every way, and they loved each other dearly.

One sunny morning, Dina did not come to the peepal tree.

Kiki flew up, up, up to look for her — and what she saw made her heart thump. Can you guess what it was? … Dina was caught in a hunter's net, spread across the grass!

"Oh no!" cawed Kiki, flying back as fast as her wings could carry her. "Our Dina is caught! Come quickly!"

Minti, Titu, and Kiki hurried to the spot. The hunter was still far away, but he was walking closer.

"We need a plan!" said Minti, her whiskers twitching fast. "Kiki — you fly down to Dina and peck her ear softly — tap, tap — so she knows we're here and stays brave. Titu — you walk, slow and brave, right across the hunter's path. He'll stop to look at you. And I — I'll nibble through the ropes with my sharp little teeth!"

Kiki nodded and flew to Dina's side. She pecked softly — tap, tap — at Dina's ear.

Minti scampered up to the net and began to nibble — nibble, nibble, nibble — at the ropes.

Brave Titu stepped forward, one slow step at a time, right into the hunter's way.

The hunter stopped. "Well, well," he said, bending down toward the tortoise. "What have we here?"

At that very moment — snap! — the last rope broke. Dina sprang to her feet!

Dina scooped Minti onto her back. Kiki flew down and tapped Titu's shell — "Hurry, slow friend, hurry!" Then all four darted into the deep, safe forest.

The hunter looked up. The net was empty. The deer was gone. Even the tortoise had vanished into the bushes!

He scratched his head and walked home with nothing at all, while under the peepal tree, four best friends hugged each other tight.

The end. 🌙

---

*Moral: True friends stand by each other — and clever teamwork can solve any problem.*
