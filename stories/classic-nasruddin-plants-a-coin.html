Nasruddin Plants a Coin

A funny Mullah Nasruddin tale for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

Long, long ago, in a sunny little village, there lived a funny, kind man named Nasruddin.

One bright morning, Nasruddin was sitting in his garden, looking at a shiny gold coin in his hand.

"Oh, what a beautiful coin!" he said to himself. "I wish I had a whole tree full of coins. Then I could buy sweets for all the children in the village!"

Then Nasruddin had a very funny idea. He clapped his hands and laughed out loud.

"I know what I will do!" he cried. "I will plant this coin in my garden, just like a seed. I will water it every day, and a money tree will grow!"

So Nasruddin dug a little hole in the soft brown earth, right in the middle of his garden. He placed the shiny coin gently into the hole, and he covered it up with soil, as carefully as if it were a tiny seed.

His neighbour Ayesha was hanging her washing on the line next door. She saw Nasruddin watering the bare earth, and she smiled kindly.

"What are you planting today, Nasruddin?" she asked.

"A money tree!" said Nasruddin proudly. "I planted a shiny coin, and soon it will grow into a tree full of coins!"

His neighbour Farhan was mending his fence nearby.

"What are you growing, my friend?" asked Farhan.

"A money tree!" said Nasruddin, beaming. "Just wait and see!"

Every morning, Nasruddin watered his coin. Every evening, he watered it again. He sang to it. He talked to it. He even read it a bedtime story!

At last, after a whole week, Nasruddin scratched his head. "Hmm," he said. "Perhaps my coin needs a little more time. Or perhaps I should take a tiny peek."

So he dug very gently into the soft earth — and there was his shiny coin, just as bright as the day he had planted it. It had not grown at all!

Nasruddin looked at the coin. Then he looked at his garden. Then he burst out laughing — a big, happy, belly laugh that echoed all down the street!

"Ha! Ha! Ha!" laughed Nasruddin. "What a silly idea! Coins do not grow on trees! I should have planted real seeds!"

Ayesha and Farhan heard his laughter and came over to see what was so funny.

"And I have some pumpkin seeds you can have," said Farhan, holding out a little paper bag. "Pumpkins really do grow!"

So Nasruddin planted the pumpkin seeds in his garden. He watered them every day, just as he had watered the coin. And this time, something wonderful happened.

First, a tiny green shoot poked out of the earth. Then it grew leaves, big and round. Then it grew flowers, bright and yellow. And then — oh, joy! — it grew pumpkins! Big, round, orange pumpkins, one after another!

"Come and share my pumpkins!" he called to everyone in the street. "There are plenty for us all!"

"Money does not grow on trees," said Nasruddin, with a happy grin, "but pumpkins do — and sharing them is worth more than gold!"

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Real seeds, patience and sharing grow something far better than wishing for money.
