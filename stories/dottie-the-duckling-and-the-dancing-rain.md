# Dottie the Duckling and the Dancing Rain

*A gentle original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.*

---

Down by the little blue pond, in a cosy nest, lived a tiny yellow duckling named Dottie.

Dottie loved sunny days — waddling after her mother, nibbling green grass, and peeping at the butterflies. But there was one thing Dottie did not love at all.

Rain.

Whenever the sky turned grey and the first raindrop fell — plip! — Dottie would run and hide under the biggest leaf she could find. She would tuck her little head under her wing and wait, and wait, and wait for the rain to stop.

One morning, the sky went grey, and the clouds rolled in like fluffy grey pillows. Plip! Plop! The rain began to fall.

"Come on, Dottie!" quacked her mother. "Come and see the rain!"

But Dottie shook her head and hid under her big leaf. "No, thank you, Mama. The rain is too wet and too noisy."

Her mother smiled a warm, gentle smile. "Oh, my little duckling," she said. "Come and look. Just one little peek. The raindrops make the puddles dance!"

Dottie peeked out from under her leaf. She saw the rain falling on the pond — plip, plop, plip, plop — and each drop made a tiny ring on the water, like a little silver crown.

"See?" said her mother. "The puddles are dancing! Plip, plop, splish, splash!"

Dottie took one brave little step out from under the leaf. A raindrop landed on her beak — plip! She giggled. It tickled!

She took another brave step. The rain pattered softly on her feathers — pit-pat, pit-pat — like tiny fingers tapping a tune.

"Plip, plop, splish, splash!" sang her mother, waddling into a puddle. She stamped her webbed feet, and the water splashed up in sparkly drops.

Dottie watched. Her little heart went thump, thump, thump. Then she took a deep breath, and — splash! — she hopped right into the puddle!

The water was cool and lovely. She stamped her feet — splish! She wiggled her tail — splash! She flapped her tiny wings, and the raindrops danced all around her.

"Plip, plop, splish, splash!" sang Dottie, laughing.

Soon her duckling friends came waddling over — Bubbles and Pop and little Peep. They jumped into the puddles too, and they danced and splashed together, round and round, quacking and laughing in the rain.

They splashed in the big puddle. They splashed in the little puddle. They spun in circles, and the raindrops fell on their feathers like tiny silver stars.

Then, slowly, the rain began to stop. The clouds drifted away, and the sun peeked out, warm and golden. And there, arching over the pond, was the most beautiful rainbow Dottie had ever seen — red and orange and yellow and green and blue and purple!

"Look, Mama!" cried Dottie. "A rainbow!"

Her mother nuzzled her softly. "You see, my brave little duckling? Rainy days can be happy days too."

That evening, Dottie waddled home, tired and happy, her feathers still a little damp. She snuggled into her cosy nest, yawned a big, sleepy yawn, and closed her eyes.

And in her dreams, the raindrops were dancing — plip, plop, splish, splash — under a bright, beautiful rainbow.

The end. 🌙

*Moral: Rainy days can be happy days too.*
