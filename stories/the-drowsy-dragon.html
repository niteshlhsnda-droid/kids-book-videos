The Drowsy Dragon

A cozy original bedtime story for ages 4–7. Reading time: about 4 minutes.

On a quiet little mountain, inside a cave that smelled like warm earth, with dry grass rustling under her toes, lived a tiny dragon named Dara. She was no bigger than a cushion, and her scales were the soft blue of the evening sky just before the stars come out.

Every night, Mama Dragon tucked Dara into her nest of moss and feathers. "Time to sleep, my little spark," Mama would say softly. And every night, Dara would try. Really, she would.

But her feet felt wiggly. So did her tail. And her whole body, like there was a bouncy little toy inside her tummy that would not sit still. And whenever the wiggles came back, her tummy would rumble — grumble, grumble, grrrooo — and the tiniest warm flame would puff out of her nose.

Poof!

It tickled her nose, so she giggled. And giggling woke her right back up again — wide awake. That was the trouble. Can you guess why?

"Oh no," whispered Dara, trying not to wiggle. "My flame won't let me sleep."

Mama Dragon curled around her, soft and warm as a blanket. "Your flame isn't the problem, little one," she said. "It's just excited, like you. Shall I teach you what my mama taught me? It's called dragon breathing."

Dara nodded, and her tummy gave another little rumble. Grumble, grrrooo. Poof!

"First," said Mama, "we breathe in slowly through our noses. Like smelling the sweet honeysuckle at the cave mouth. Shall we try together?"

Dara breathed in... slooowly... through her nose. The night air felt soft inside her.

"Now," whispered Mama, "breathe out softly through your mouth. Like blowing a tiny bubble. Very, very gently."

Dara breathed out... soft, soft, softer... And instead of a flame... something wonderful happened. What do you think floated out of her mouth? ... A tiny golden sparkle, drifting up, up, up — like a little firefly going home.

"Again," said Mama. "Slow in through the nose... soft out through the mouth..."

So Dara breathed in, slow and deep. She breathed out, soft and warm. And with every breath, the flames grew smaller and smaller, turning into golden sparkles instead. Poof... became sparkle, and grumble... became glow.

Soon each breath stretched longer, slow and warm. The sparkles floated up like tiny lanterns, twinkling gently, one by one, until the whole cave glowed with soft golden light.

Her wiggly feet stopped wiggling. Her wiggly tail stopped, too. The bouncy little toy in her tummy settled down at last. She tucked her nose under her tail and breathed in... and out... slow and warm.

"In through the nose," she breathed, so sleepily. "Out through the mouth..."

The golden sparkles drifted down softly and settled around her, and Dara breathed in... and out... and the drowsy dragon finally... finally... fell asleep.

💛 Tonight's gentle lesson: Slow, sleepy breaths can calm any wiggly feeling.
